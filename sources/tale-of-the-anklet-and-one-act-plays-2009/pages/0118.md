# Scan 118

Printed page: 84

## Publication text

Kannagi (crying out): No, my Lord, you should not go-- they will kill you there! (Talking to herself) Yes, they have done him to death and that too, after calling him thief... My Lord! You, a thief! ... ... No, No...

She runs out in a frenzy, toppling the oil-lamp as she rushes past. Madhari and others follow her. In the empty hut, the flame from the oil-lamp licks a wooden post and leaps upward. Soon the roof is ablaze. Fire spreads through the deserted hamlet and fanned by a strong wind, it races towards the city of Mathurai, as if at the bidding of Kannagi the symbol of chastity.

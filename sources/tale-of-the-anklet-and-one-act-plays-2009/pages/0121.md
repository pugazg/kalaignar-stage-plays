### 35 Volcano in Court

Kannagi at the entrance to the palace. Her appearance strikes fear in the hearts of the guards.

Kannagi (ordering the guards): Go forthwith and tell your Monarch that a woman who moves, though lifeless, has come to meet him, with tears in her eyes and a single anklet in her hand...

One of the guards hurries in to convey the message to the king.

* * *

Court of the Pandya king. A guard enters and bows low to the Throne.

Guard: A woman is at the entrance, your Majesty, and she is in tears. She insists on seeing you.

Nedunchezhian: Admit her.

Kannagi enters, like a thunderstorm and volcano. The courtiers gasp at the sight of her.

Nedunchezhian (kindly): Lady in great grief, who are you?

Kannagi (fearless): O Monarch who acts in haste! (the courtiers are shocked at the form of address; the king, however, is impassive) I come from where a ruler of yore wrote himself into history by offering his own flesh in order to save a dove in distress; and where, a successor of his trussed up and laid his son on the ground and ran the royal chariot over the little prince who had caused the death of a calf under the wheels.

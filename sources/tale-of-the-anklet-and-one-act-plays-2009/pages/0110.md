### 30 Anklet for Sale

Broad street in Mathurai city. Kovalan is in conversation with a prosperous-looking citizen, who is examining the anklet, in unconcealed admiration.

Citizen: What a marvellous piece! If you bring the twin of this anklet too, I am willing to take both and you can name the price.

Kovalan [trying to avoid any reference to the second anklet]: Don’t you know anyone, sir, who would be willing to buy this anklet for the rubies in it, which could be taken out for the purpose of making any other ornament?

Citizen [returning the anklet]: The appropriate man for you would be the Chief Goldsmith of the palace who might be able to make use of the parts of this single anklet.

Kovalan: Thank you, sir.

He leaves.

Citizen [talking to himself]: Seems to belong to a noble family . . . must have fallen on evil days . . .

Kovalan, looking a little tired and very self-conscious, meets two other local citizens.

Citizen [looking at the anklet]: What superb craftsmanship! I think you better take the anklet to the Chief Goldsmith of the palace.

Citizen’s friend: Think of him and he is there! Here comes the Chief Goldsmith himself.

# Scan 104

Printed page: 70

## Publication text

Ayyai: There . . . mother is coming . . . [calling] Mamma, mamma!

Madhari [seeing Kaunthi Adigal first]: Welcome, Adigal, my obeisance to you.

Kaunthi Adigal: Madhari, I have brought two guests with me and they have become my children in the course of our journey. [Introducing] Kovalan and his wife Kannagi. They are of such prominent stock that if it comes to be known in Mathurai that they are here, they would be accorded an opulent reception by some of the wealthiest houses in the city. But I have brought them here because you are nobler than the noblest and also because you prefer to stay in a quiet place. You must keep them here and look after them until they are on their own.

Madhari: It is a privilege for me, Adigal, to do your bidding. [To Kovalan and Kannagi] Welcome, friends, this is your home now. Kannagi, my daughter Ayyai will be your constant companion.

Kaunthi Adigal: My responsibility is over now. Let me take leave of you all.

Kannagi [in tearful gratitude]: Mother, you have been such a source of strength to us ...

Kaunthi Adigal: And now Madhari will take my place. [To Madhari] Madhari, I am committing Kannagi to your care; [emphasising] she is your ward and I shall hold you personally responsible for her welfare.

They bid Adigal an affectionate farewell.

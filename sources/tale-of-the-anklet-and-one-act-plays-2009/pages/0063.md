# 10 Madhavi Conquers — continued

take long for Kovalan to realise that she is actually portraying her own travails, from the time she saw him first in the Hall of Pearls. Dance and music rise to supreme heights as her grief mounts and even the creeper-plants in the hall and the pillars of rock seem to respond to the unconsoled melody of her mood.

Kovalan’s heart beats fast as the music rises to a crescendo and she swirls to a stop prior to writhing as in agony, on the marble-floor.

His restraint breaking at last, Kovalan cries ‘Madhavi’ and rushes to her. Placing her head on his lap he seeks to bring her back to consciousness by sprinkling water on her face. Taking a fruit from a bowl nearby he squeezes it and tries to pour the juice into her mouth.

Kovalan [caressing her]: Madhavi!

She opens her eyes slowly and gazes into his down-turned face. Tears well up in her eyes and she sobs her heart out.

Kovalan [with great feeling]: Why these tears, Madhavi? Why do you wear yourself out in such a frenzy of dance?

Madhavi [her voice tear-choked]: That was because it was my last dance.

Kovalan [shocked]: No, no! You should not even think on such lines.

Madhavi [ruefully]: What is there for me to live for, my Lord? These last few days I have been clinging to my life, only because I wanted my Art to come to fruition before the eyes of one who alone deserves the privilege of witnessing it. Tonight my mission is fulfilled and I shall drift away like a tired leaf. [She closes her eyes and opens them again after a pause. Her eyelids flutter.]

Kovalan [seeking to resuscitate her will to live]: If you do anything hasty, the best in the Art of the Chola Kingdom would be extinguished.

Madhavi: My Lord, can Art flower in a heart drained of joy and enthusiasm?

Kovalan: Why not, Madhavi? Even the floods of sorrow could energise the waves to rise high in the ocean of Art.

Madhavi: Maybe, my Lord. But in a land where everyone seeks inner peace and joy, why should Madhavi alone make a cult out of grief and spread it through the medium of melody?

Kovalan: You talk as if some great sorrow has enveloped your life!

Madhavi [alert, with hope kindled]: Does it mean that the shadow of grief has been swept away from my life? Tell me, my Lord, tell me; has the kuyil* alighted on the branch of my tree, to herald a fresh lease of joy for me?

*Kuyil = a singing bird of the nightingale species.

## Publication text

### 31 Sceptre Bends

Sanctum in the Pandyan Palace. King Nedunchezhian is all dressed and ready to go to Court. Kopperundevi is still on the couch, a sullen queen.

Nedunchezhian: It is getting late, dear, and you will have to hurry.

Kopperundevi [rebelling]: I am not coming to Court, for I dare not be seen in public, without my anklet.

Nedunchezhian: But have I not told you that I want to examine the suspects in the Court today and apprehend the culprit?

Kopperundevi [sardonic]: And by the time you complete all formalities the anklet would have travelled a long distance. You are so preoccupied with writing poems and studying military science and attending to public affairs that you do not have the time to bother overmuch about the ankle of your consort staying deprived of its ornament.

Nedunchezhian [sternly]: Do not forget, Kopperundevi, that the country is above even you or me. For a trivial anklet, should a queen throw a tantrum like this?

Kopperundevi [raging]: Yes, a trivial anklet indeed! That is because I have myself become trivial to you. [She is near tears.]

Nedunchezhian [unmoved]: Once again I am asking you, are you coming to Court or not?

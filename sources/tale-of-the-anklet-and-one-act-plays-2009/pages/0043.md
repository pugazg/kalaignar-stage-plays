# Tale of the Anklet — Section 4 continued

**Ilango [smiling sweetly]:** Not fate, mother. Destiny dictated that I should be king but I put it to rout through straightforward strategy.

**Senguttuvan [breaking down]:** I will not allow this sacrifice, Ilango. You who should come in procession in the streets of this capital city ought not to spend your life in the wilderness of austerity. The kingdom is yours, Ilango, take it if you have some consideration for us, your near and dear.

**Neduncheraladhan:** Unparalleled is the glory of your renunciation, my son, but don’t you owe a duty to us, your old parents? Can you find it in your heart to reduce us to agony like this?

**Narchonai:** My son, my son, do not drive a thousand spears into our hearts.... *(pointing out Venmaal who is steeped in speechless grief)* See the scalding tears of your sister-in-law, the writhing agony of your aged father and the pain you have inflicted on your brother. I gave birth to you, my son, but now the pangs are a thousandfold. Even if you will not obey me, you cannot reject my prayer....

**Ilango (in steeled detachment):** Let no one seek to change the course of the life I have chosen for myself. If we are content to put our faith in Fate, then Tamilnad would soon turn into a dungeon of despair. I am here to defy Fate and I shall do so till my last breath. The magnitude of the very first victory has heartened me in my mission and I beg of all of you to bless me and bid me leave.

**Senguttuvan (alarmed afresh):** Bid you leave! Ilango, where indeed are you going?

**Ilango:** Don’t be afraid; I will not leave Tamilnad. My residence is only the monastery on the outskirts of this city and I shall certainly be seeing you all from time to time.

*He takes leave of them and walks away with footsteps firm. They watch him through tears, but he does not look back, even once.*

*As Ilango the monk descends the steps of the palace, guards stand motionless, like graven images. Citizens of all walks of life have gathered in their thousands in front of the palace-gates and a hush falls on the vast concourse as Ilango moves through and away. The calm expression on his face shows that his inner eyes have spotted the goal and he is determined to reach the destination.*

*Soon the crowds are left far behind and Ilango is all alone when the Astrologer crosses his path.*

**Astrologer (surprised):** Is it Ilango? What a morbid transformation, Prince!

**Ilango:** Oh, it only marks the downfall of your deity called Fate. You are best-equipped to be my first convert and so, Master-Astrologer, why don’t you follow me?

**Astrologer (confused):** What, me? To follow you?

# 8 Kannagi in Wedded Bliss — continued

**Masathuvan [remorseful]:** I have been hasty, son. and I am sorry that I allowed gossip to get the better of me. When the truth is known about how you rescued Madhavi, the world will praise me for being the father of such a noble son. That tribute is enough for me, Kovalan. [*He embraces his son.*]

**Manaykan’s wife [to her daughter]:** Kannagi, thrice-blessed are you to be daughter-in-law in such a family.

*Kannagi sheds tears of joy.*

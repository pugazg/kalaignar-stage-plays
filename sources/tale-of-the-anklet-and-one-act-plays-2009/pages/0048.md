# Tale of the Anklet — Section 6 continued

All the streets and homes are decorated today and gaiety is the keynote of all activity. The air is charged with the sweet music of Tamil.

(Song)

Praise unto the sun! Praise unto the sun!
For like the golden wheel of Chola’s prowess
The sun too circles the land!

Praise unto the moon! Praise unto the moon
For like the Umbrella of the Lord of the Cauveri,
The moon too showers grace!

Praise unto the cloud! Praise unto the cloud!
For like the compassion of the great King Chola,
The cloud too enriches the land!

Praise unto Puhar! Praise unto Puhar!
For the capital city of the Chola kingdom
Nurtures world peace and prosperity!

**Sathanar [continuing]:** On a mound of white sand on the seashore stands a young woman of ravishing beauty. She is scanning the waves and awaiting the arrival of her beloved from across the high seas. The woman of chiselled features and inherent charm is Kannagi the daughter of Manayakkan, the merchant-prince who is renowned for his philanthropy.

Presently a ship nears the shore and Kannagi’s face lights up with joy. The ship is anchored and alighting into small boats, the passengers travel towards the shore. There are Greeks and Romans among them. In one of the boats is a strong young man, with soulful eyes and next to him is an elderly Greek gentleman. The young man is Kovalan, Kannagi’s husband. On spotting his boat, Kannagi wades through the shallow waters to reach it quicker.

**Greek gentleman [to Kovalan]:** I wonder who that angel of a girl is! Her courage matches her good looks, for see, how she defies the waves!

**Kovalan [in excitement]:** She is my wife Kannagi.

**Greek gentleman:** I envy you, my boy. In my motherland Greece, there is not a woman comparable to the divine beauty who is your helpmeet.

*Smiling in pride, Kovalan leaps into the sea and swims to meet Kannagi. They embrace each other and even the waves seem to rejoice at their union.*

**Kovalan [in emotion]:** Kannagi!

### 26 Queen’s Ornament

As the king and the queen walk through the corridor towards their private apartments, one of the queen’s anklets slips off and falls to the ground with a musical clatter. The queen picks up the ornament, examines it and shows it to her consort who has stopped for her.

Queen: The clasp is broken, my Lord.

King [calling]: Hey, there! [An officer of the palace hastens in, followed by a maid. Both of them bow and await for orders] Take this anklet straightaway to the Chief Goldsmith and order him to mend it. [To the Queen] Why don’t you give the other anklet too?

Queen: I wouldn’t like to be without an anklet, my Lord, and anyway, it is only a minor repair.

The officer takes the anklet to be mended and bows again, as the king and the queen leave.

The maid notices that the officer is gazing at the anklet avidly and she rags him.

Maid: Do not swallow it, sir, it would prove hard in digestion!

Officer [startled]: What a dangerous joke? Do you think I want to risk my head?

He goes off, with the ornament in his tight grasp.

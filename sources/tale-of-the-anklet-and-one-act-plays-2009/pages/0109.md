# Scan 109

Printed page: 75

## Publication text

Kannagi: Yes, my Lord, I am convinced about that.

Having unfastened one anklet and given it to him, she is about to remove the second anklet too, when he stops her.

Kovalan: Let it be there, Kannagi; the proceeds from one anklet would be adequate for us. And the single anklet still with you will chime and tinkle to hammer a lesson into my conscience, so that I may never prove false to you again, even in the region of thought! . . . Bid me leave, Kannagi, and wish me well in my venture.

Kannagi: In my mind I already feel the effulgence of the future, my Lord! [Bidding him leave] You must be very careful in this unfamiliar city and you must also return soon.

Kovalan [boyish]: You are instructing me as if I am a child, Kannagi!

Kannagi [shyly]: Is it not time that I trained myself?

Kovalan pats her affectionately. Chorus music is heard, indicating the commencement of the folk dance.

Kovalan [stepping out]: Be of good cheer, Kannagi. I will come back very soon with the capital for our new life.

He leaves. Ayyai and her friends enter and take Kannagi with them to the dance.

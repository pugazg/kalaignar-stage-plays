# Scan 113

Printed page: 79

## Publication text

Kopperundevi: Without my anklet, I will not take one step from this place.

Nedunchezhian goes out. The scene has upset him and he is in a black mood.

* * *

Nedunchezhian is walking along the corridor and the officer follows him at a respectful distance. The Chief Goldsmith comes from the opposite direction and bows low to the king.

Nedunchezhian: Yes? . . .

Chief Goldsmith [exultantly]: The man who stole the Queen’s anklet has been apprehended, Your Majesty.

The officer is in mortal fear.

Nedunchezhian: Where is he now?

Chief Goldsmith: Having mended the clasp, he was trying to dispose of the anklet. I have kept him in a safe place and come straight to report the matter to you.

Nedunchezhian: A thief in my land! [To the officer] Konru varugha (which is Tamil for “kill and return”.)

The officer bows and sets off with the Chief Goldsmith.

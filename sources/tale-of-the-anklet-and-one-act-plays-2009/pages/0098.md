# Scan 98

Printed page: 64

Running head: `Tale of the Anklet`

## Transcription

children . . .

As they cross a field, an over-energetic fish, leaping too high from the irrigation canal, lands on the ground and squirms helplessly. Kaunthi Adigal hurries to its aid.

Kaunthi Adigal: This is how some people too behave. Without assessing the possible effect, they get into action and then find themselves marooned.

Kovalan [with wry humour]: It is my melancholy privilege, Mother, to be the living example of all your observations during our journey.

Kaunthi Adigal [smiling, but not giving any other indication of having taken notice of Kovalan’s remark]: What a lovely fish! It is the emblem of the Pandyas and that is perhaps why it knows not fear.

She restores the fish to water and it swims away, in the exultation of freedom.

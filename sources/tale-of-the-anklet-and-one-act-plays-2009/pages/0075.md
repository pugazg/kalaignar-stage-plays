# Scan 75

Printed page: 41

Header: *Tale of the Anklet*

*The brahmin and his wife with the babe-in-arms take leave of Kovalan and Madhavi. After they have left, Madhavi gazes at Kovalan thoughtfully.*

Madhavi: Your counsel to the brahmin was appropriate, my Lord, that he should not desert his wife. Even if you are to be displeased with me, I must tell you that the advice applies to you also in equal measure and that you should lose no further time in visiting your home where my sister Kannagi must be pining for you. How many times have I sought to persuade you in the matter?

Kovalan: You are right, Madhavi, but I would feel so ashamed to face her.

Madhavi [cornering him in argument]: You mean you are ashamed of being in love with me!

Kovalan [confused]: Not that, Madhavi!

Madhavi: What else could it be? Either you have to admit you are ashamed of our love or you should agree to go and meet Kannagi. I want the issue to be settled before I budge from here, for I would not like you to be bracketed with those who only believe in preaching to others, without themselves following any of the teachings. Not only that, my Lord, I am genuinely frightened whether the tears shed by Kannagi would affect the child in my womb.

Kovalan [closing her mouth with the palm of his hand]: You don’t have to threaten me, Madhavi. I promise to carry out your bidding . . . There is a chill in the air, dear, let us return home.

*They leave the grove as dusk falls.*

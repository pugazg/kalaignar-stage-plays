# 5 Birth of Anklet's Tale

*Mountainous region overlooking the River Periyar. A track winds through the heights and slopes of rugged beauty. Ballads native to the hills are heard and as the music gathers volume a group of tribal folks appears on the scene. The carefree travellers are laden with gift-articles, animate and otherwise, ranging from tiger-cubs to sandalwood. As they reach the bottom of the hill, they meet Ilango and prostrate before him.*

**Ilango:** My blessings to you, friends. You seem to have undertaken a festive journey from the hills to the bank of the River Periyar. May I know the occasion for the travel?

**One of the tribesmen:** Don't you know, sir? Your brother Senguttuvan and Sathanar the poet are camping on the bank of the river and we are going there to pay our respects to them and present a few articles.

**Ilango [pleased]:** Is that so? Then let me also join you, for it is quite some time since I saw my brother.

*The tribesfolk resume their journey*

* * *

*Bank of the River Periyar. King Senguttuvan, Venmaal and Sathanar are in a relaxed mood as they survey the natural scenery. Over their pavilion is hoisted the Chera flag with the emblem of the bow. Ilango and the tribesfolk arrive and at the sight of his brother, Senguttuvan's eyes light up with joy. He rises and walks a few steps to meet Ilango, with outstretched arms. The brothers embrace each other.*

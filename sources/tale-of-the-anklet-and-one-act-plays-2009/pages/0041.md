# Tale of the Anklet — Section 3 continued

**Astrologer:** It was not I who spoke, Your Majesty, it was the voice of Destiny. In law and practice, the Crown is inherited by the eldest son, no doubt, but the ways of Providence are inscrutable and Fate is no respecter of customs and traditions. It is decreed that your younger son Ilango and not Senguttuvan shall rule this land.

*Pindrop silence in the court.*

**Neduncheraladhan [shocked]:** Could it be so?

**Courtiers [in confused whispers]:** How can the younger prince supersede his elder brother? ... Fate, the Astrologer says... What tidings these, to be received on a day of rejoicing!...

*For some time, Ilango had been watching his brother’s face which loses colour when the import of the prediction sinks deep into his heart.*

**Ilango [firm]:** No, no! It is my brother who is born to rule and nothing shall change that position.

**Neduncheraladhan [desperate]:** Who can defy Destiny, Ilango? Our Astrologer’s prediction is bound to come true.

**Ilango:** That might be so for those with ears of superstition but not for me, Sire, since my ears are tuned in to the gospel of truth and truth alone. The forecast is bound to fail and I accept the challenge of the so-called Fate which has got to turn tail when confronted with determined endeavour, as Valluvar has inspired us to believe.

**Astrologer [irked]:** Haven’t you read the epics, Ilango? Could anyone alter the decree of Destiny that Rama the eldest brother should go into exile and Bharata rule in his place?

**Ilango [arguing]:** The epic I read says authoritatively that neither Bharata nor Rama ascended the throne but it was the sandal that reigned supreme. *[Sarcastic]* Revered Master-Astrologer, please do not dangle the epics before us in order to prop up a false case. Here and now I swear to create an epic new that shall smash your prediction to smithereens. My brother and brother alone shall rule this land and the miracle that brings this about will not originate from your chart-caged planets but from the effulgent sun of honest intellect.

*Bowing to the Throne, Ilango leaves the hall. The Assembly is plunged in bewilderment and the King and the Queen glance at each other, thoroughly mystified by the rapid pace of events.*

# Scan 106

Printed page: 72

## Publication text

Acquire the priceless
treasure of learning
through timely acts of
aid to the preceptor
and by offering
material wealth
and through service
steady and sincere ....

A maid enters in some agitation and interrupts.

Maid: Your Majesty, the Chief Goldsmith of our palace seeks an urgent interview.

Nedunchezhian: Well . . . [He gets up and goes to the doorway.]

Chief Goldsmith [in trepidation]: Obeisance to you, sire!

Nedunchezhian [solicitously]: Why are you trembling, my man? Anything that matters?

Chief Goldsmith [crying out]: Mercy, Your Majesty, mercy . . .

Nedunchezhian: Compose yourself, Goldsmith, and let me know what ails you.

Chief Goldsmith: Oh, Your Majesty, I am not to blame. I had taken all precautions to keep the anklet in a secure place. I myself deposited it in an iron-safe, locked the safe and kept the key next to my heart. And yet, when I unlocked the safe this morning, the anklet was not to be found. [Wailing] I am undone, oh, I am undone...

Nedunchezhian [perturbed]: What, is the Queen’s anklet lost? If you had kept it safe, how could it have disappeared without your knowledge? Goldsmith, answer me straight, “Whom do you suspect of the felony?”

Chief Goldsmith: Whom shall I point out, Your Majesty? A hundred assistants work under me.

Nedunchezhian [thoughtful]: Well, assemble all the hundred goldsmiths in our Court tomorrow. I will myself conduct an inquiry and apprehend the culprit.

Chief Goldsmith: I will carry out the order, Your Majesty. Once again I pray for forgiveness and mercy . . .

The Chief Goldsmith retires, leaving Nedunchezhian pensive and frowning.

# Scan 128

Printed page: 94

## Publication text

Bhairavan and heading the mob are the Kanaka-Vijayas. From the plains of the Ganga have they dared the might of the Cauvery, and on behalf of Tamilnad I say ‘The challenge is accepted’. The fight shall be to the finish and the menace of their bravado has to be eradicated once and for all!

Tumultuous applause. As it subsides, the Chief Priest rises to ask a question.

Chief Priest (to Senguttuvan): Your Majesty, the challenge is to the Chola and the Pandya kings too and so, do you have to shoulder the entire burden of the expedition?

Senguttuvan: It is the honour of Tamilnad that has been challenged and an immediate answer is called for in terms of action. My brother-kings you have mentioned might not be in a position to react militantly just at present, but we shall not make that an excuse to postpone the day of reckoning.

By doubting the valour of the Tamils, our foes have held out an affront to all that lends substance to our culture and civilization. They have sought to denigrate Valluvar, the author of our code supreme, poets Sathanar and Ilango and Paranar here in this Assembly whose works shall outlive time and the innumerable artists of peace and war and wisdom who enable us to live in dignity. Let us march to strike the words of scorn from the lips of the detractors. ‘Victory or death’ is our motto, my men, for honour is dearer to us than life...

The warriors respond with full-throated battle-cries and the great army sets out.

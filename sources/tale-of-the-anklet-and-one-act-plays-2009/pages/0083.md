## Transcription

Vasantamalai [from a distance]: I will attend to the work straightaway, my Lady!

Kovalan and Madhavi drive through the streets in the chariot drawn by dark-brown stallions. The buildings on both sides stay illuminated and the lighthouse adds to the magnificence of the landscape. It being the last day of the Indra Festival, people are afoot although it is not yet dawn and most of them are proceeding to the seashore. Kovalan and Madhavi arrive at the spot reserved for them and are pleased to find that it faces the rendezvous of the Cauveri and the sea.

Madhavi is radiant and her happiness transcends the need for spoken words. She plays the harp and the soft melody captures all the beauty of the morn as the sun peeps out of the horizon and floods the sea and the earth with fiery gold. The waves too seem to be tamed as the melody flows. Kovalan sits entranced.

Presently, Madhavi stops playing and offers the harp to Kovalan.

Madhavi: Your turn first, my Lord, for the promised lyric.

Kovalan [not taking the harp, but caressing her fingers]: Soft as anicham flowers are your fingers, that they cannot cause a scratch even if I prick my eyes with them. The hardness of my touch might prove too much for the strings of the harp, Madhavi, that have been accustomed only to tenderness.

Madhavi: If only this harp were animate, my Lord, it would weave out a poem in melody in order to express the thrill of your touch. Please do not tarry, for my ears are eager to share the joy of my eyes.

Kovalan takes the harp and plays as he sings.

[Song]

With a mere sweep  
of his Sceptre bright,  
Your Lord and Master  
the Chola Monarch  
has won and wed  
Ganga there  
in the distant North,  
and Kumari too  
in the extreme South;  
And yet, and yet,  
O Cauveri,  
you’re kind and constant  
to your Lover Royal!

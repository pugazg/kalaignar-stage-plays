Printed page: 81

## Publication text

Kovalan [shocked]: Thief! . . . Master-Goldsmith, what is the meaning of all this? Is it proper to foist a baseless accusation on me?

Chief Goldsmith: Was it proper on your part to steal the anklet of the Queen?

Kovalan [angered]: Who stole the anklet of your Queen? Lacking the capability to trace the culprit, should you, in your monumental inefficiency, go about searching for scapegoats?

Officer: Hold your tongue, fellow. Our king is distressed beyond words to learn that one of the five deadly sins has sneaked into his land in your person.

Kovalan: Then let me meet the king and tell him that I am no thief; that I am not a sinner to any except my devoted wife whom I set adrift in the sea of tears when I strayed from her to liquidate all my wealth—silver and gold and gems in heaps; and, that, taking refuge here in Mathurai in order to make a fresh beginning in life, I had to stoop to the extent of selling my wife’s anklet. I am a devoted child of Tamil and my thoughts are charged with the lofty melody of Tamil; living on others is foreign to the traditions of our family and do you feel I would ever covet another’s property? I am confident of securing justice from the renowned King Nedunchezhian and so, sirs, please lead me to his presence now itself!

Officer: Do you think the King is your bodyguard to be at your beck and call?

Kovalan: He is Guard to the land, for he guards justice and righteousness, the rule of law and the tenets of equity; and above all, he guards Tamil, the language of love and beauty and truth. I have heard it said in foreign countries too, that Nedunchezhian cannot countenance an injustice of the size of an atom even. Let such a king look at my face and say whether he finds there a trace of felony; he is an expert in physiognomy and I shall abide by his verdict. Take me to him, for I cannot bear to be under a cloud, even for a moment.

Officer: Let me tell you, veteran thief, that our Monarch will not deign to look at the face of a sinner like you!

Kovalan [retorting]: After looking at your face, I am sure that the King would only be too glad to see any other face!

Chief Goldsmith: Impertinence will take you nowhere, man. Having been caught with the goods on you, why don’t you realize that discretion lies in being humble and come out with the truth?

Kovalan: Master-Goldsmith, I am putting to you a straight question, ‘Am I a thief’?

Chief Goldsmith: And I am giving you a straight answer, “Yes, you are!”

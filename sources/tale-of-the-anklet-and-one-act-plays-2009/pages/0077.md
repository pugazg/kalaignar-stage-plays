# Scan 77

Section 16: **Birth of Manimekalai**

Madhavi’s villa. Madhavi is a mother now. The angelic child is being cradled and the celebration is on a lavish scale.

Madhavi takes the child from the cradle and brings it to Kovalan, who kisses the infant in pride and affection.

Madhavi: You should name the child, my Lord.

Kovalan [gazing at the babe]: You are the synthesis of all that is sweet, my child, like milk and honey and sugar-candy. You are the zephyr from the south, bearing the pollen of all the flowers fragrant. The magic of your slightest smile puts to shame the melody of the flute and the harp. You are a jewel to womanhood, my daughter, and every gesture of yours is an expression of Art supreme. So I name you Manimekalai and bless you, my child.

Those present: Long live Manimekalai!

Alms-giving is an important aspect of the celebration, which Kovalan himself is supervising when he hears a cry for help from the street. He runs to the street and there he sees that an elephant running amuck has caught hold of a brahmin and the panic-stricken victim is squirming in the noose of its trunk. The animal is in the act of raising its victim high, with the intention of dashing him to the ground when Kovalan distracts its attention and challenges its might.

# Scan 94

Printed page: 60

Running head: `Tale of the Anklet`

## Transcription

am responsible for the flight, accompanied by wife and unknown even to fond parents. Hapless am I, with no cure to offer for a lacerated heart and I can only beg to be forgiven, again and again.

Your Beloved.

As Kovalan reads through the epistle, tears course down his cheeks.

Kovalan [to Kausikan]: Tell Madhavi from me that I am filled with remorse for the wrong done to her. [Scanning the message once again] I find that this epistle contains what all I would like to convey to my parents and so, sir, will you please take it back with you to Poompuhar and give it to my father?

Kausikan [sad that Kovalan is not returning]: Yes, Kovalan.

Kausikan leaves, with the epistle.

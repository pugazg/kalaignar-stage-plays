And here I am now, deluded and deserted once again, and should I not at least know the reason, my Lord, why I am being chastised for no fault of mine? Did you not say but the other night that I was the pure gold of your life, the pearl, the fragrance flawless and the source of light; was it all only an exercise in imagery which evaporated when the stars set and the sun rose, like the dew-drops on grass? Speak to me but once, my Lord, just once, won’t you? ...

[Rising like an enraged tigress] Since you will not speak, I will force the whole world to speak for you. The world has got to accept that you were not a thief and the Pandya king who wields a sceptre bent will have to bow his head in shame. The savages who shed your blood must be crushed and the city of Mathurai with a blot on its emblem should be wiped off the face of the earth. That shall be my sole mission from this moment and this I swear, my Lord, on your blood. [She dips her finger in Kovalan’s blood and applies a tilak on her forehead.] [In laughter terrible] Blood for blood, blood for blood....

She rushes out as she came, like a gale. Unfastening the single anklet and taking it in her hand, and with tresses unloosed she flies in the direction of the Pandya palace.

# 8 Kannagi in Wedded Bliss — continued

**Kannagi [assertive, in spite of her confusion]:** Oh, don’t think ill of him; he is my God.

**Masathuvan:** He may be God to you but he is my son. If he wants to bring discredit to our family and marry that dancer Madhavi, it will be over my dead body.

**Kannagi:** Whoever said that he will marry her?

**Masathuvan:** Why, it is the talk of the entire town that they exchanged garlands in public. I provided for him to set up house with you, but if he is inclined to stray I will see that he does not escape the consequences.

**Kannagi:** Oh, why don’t any of you have the patience to find out the truth?

**Masathuvan’s wife:** The truth is there plain as a pikestaff, Kannagi, but now that we are seized of the matter, you need no longer worry about it. Your uncle can be depended upon to discipline Kovalan and restore him to you.

**Manaykan’s wife [butting in]:** Look at my daughter... her eyes are red with weeping. Is it to see her in this state that I gave birth to her! [*She sobs.*]

**Masathuvan [growling]:** I will go straightaway and deal with Kovalan...

*Kovalan comes out of the bedchamber, smiling.*

**Kovalan [to Masathuvan]:** Father, where are you going to search for me?

**Masathuvan [staggered]:** Kovalan, were you here all the time?

**Kovalan:** Of course, where else should I be?

**Masathuvan [perplexed]:** What all I heard about you, son! I suppose some mischief-mongers started a rumour. [*His anger changing direction*] Wait until I meet them and they wouldn’t be recognised by their own kith and kin!

**Kovalan:** Don’t be in a hurry, father. What all you would have heard was true in details but not in conclusion. I parted with a necklace in exchange for a garland so as to rescue Madhavi from the clutches of an old reprobate and there the matter ended as far as I was concerned. If you had heard anything beyond that, it was an extension in gossip which deserves to be dismissed with disdain.

**Manaykan’s wife [chiding her daughter]:** Kannagi, why could you not explain all this before, instead of keeping us in agony?

**Kannagi:** So now you turn on me! Doesn’t it strike you all that you never allowed me to speak?

**Manaykan [still in some doubt]:** And so...

**Kovalan [reassuring him]:** Kannagi and I are two persons but with one mind, uncle, and my responsibility is even more than yours in safeguarding her happiness.

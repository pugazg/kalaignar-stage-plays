- Work: `Tale of the Anklet`

## Transcription

Kannagi [at a loss]: Then . . .

Kovalan [brightening up somewhat]: Why not we leave this city, dear? We could go to Mathurai, the capital of the Pandya kingdom where the anklets will fetch a good price and where no one will be able to identify us!

Kannagi: Yes, my Lord. I am ready for the journey.

Kovalan: Come, then, for wherever I am, you have got to be by my side, till death does part us, Kannagi! Let us leave this city at dead of night so that we may not be noticed in our present state of penury and distress.

They cross the courtyard and go in.

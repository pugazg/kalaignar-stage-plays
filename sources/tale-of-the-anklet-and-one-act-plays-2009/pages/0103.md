## Publication text

### 27 Nobility in Simplicity

Kovalan and Kannagi have arrived at the outskirts of Mathurai, accompanied by Kaunthi Adigal. A pastoral song is heard being rendered in chorus and presently a group of boys and girls appears, herding cows and singing with gusto. The leader of the group, a very smart girl with bright eyes, identifies Kaunthi Adigal even from a distance and she comes running ahead of her friends.

Girl [to Kaunthi Adigal]: Our respects to you, Mother!

Kaunthi Adigal: My blessings, Ayyai. Where is your mother Madhari?

Ayyai: She has gone to offer rice to the monks and she will be back in a few moments. Let us go home, Mother, and . . . [looks enquiringly at Kovalan and Kannagi.]

Kaunthi Adigal: They are our guests, Ayyai. [Introducing Kovalan and Kannagi.

Ayyai: Welcome to Mathurai, dear guests!

Kaunthi Adigal [to Kovalan and Kannagi, as they follow Ayyai and the group of cowherds]: Ayyai’s mother Madhari is a pious and hospitable woman and you would be delighted to meet her . . .

* * *

Madhari’s dwelling. Though modest, the place is spick and span and it is easy to see that the family inhabiting the abode is dedicated to plain living as an outcome of noble thinking. Kaunthi Adigal, Kovalan, Kannagi and Ayyai are awaiting Madhari’s return from her mission of feeding the monks.

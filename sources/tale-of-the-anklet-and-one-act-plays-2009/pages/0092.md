# 22 Journey to Mathurai

Rugged road to Mathurai city. Kovalan and Kannagi find it hard going, particularly since the stretch is thoroughly desolate. They meet Kaunthi Adigal*, an elderly Jain nun.

Kovalan: Our respects to you, Mother. [Introducing] Kannagi, this is Kaunthi Adigal whose name would be familiar to you.

Kannagi: We are honoured to meet you, Mother.

Kaunthi Adigal: My blessings to you, children. May I know . . .

Kovalan [anticipating her question]: We are from Poompuhar-on-Cauveri, that harbour-capital of the Chola land which exports gold and pepper, rubies and pearls and ivory and sandalwood. My name is Kovalan and this is Kannagi my wife.

Kaunthi Adigal: Kovalan, are you not the son of that merchant-prince Masathuvan? What calamity befell you, boy, that you have to trek in this manner?

Kovalan [bitterly]: I turned false to Kannagi, the light of my life and it is meet that I should be chastised. I am a sinner twice over, Mother, for now I am taking her along this road of suffering!

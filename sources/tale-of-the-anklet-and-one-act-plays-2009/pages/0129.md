## Publication text

### 38 Mission Accomplished

North India. Senguttuvan the Victorious surveys the scene of battle. His commander-in-chief Villavankothai is by his side.

Senguttuvan (exultant): We have made short work of them, Villavan. What is this place called?

Villavankothai: It is known as Kuyilaluvam, Your Majesty.

Senguttuvan: Kuyilaluvam shall be enshrined in military history. What surprises me, Villavan, is that Kanaka and Vijaya are not to be found anywhere. I shall hate to think even of our enemies that they fled from the field of battle rather than embrace a heroic end.

Villavankothai: It is fact that they have escaped in disgrace, Your Majesty. Reports indicate that they might have done so, disguised in saffron robes.

Senguttuvan: What a shame! And they were the brave who rated our valour low!

A group of Chera warriors drag Kanaka and Vijaya to the presence of Senguttuvan. The captive kings are in the clothing of monks.

Senguttuvan: Our thunderous opponents seem to have achieved renunciation in the heat of the battle... Well, ye valiants, you must be familiar with all the ancient legends; will you please answer a few questions on the subject? To start with, could you tell me the duration of the war between the devas and the asuras?

## Publication text

### 37 March to the North

On the bank of the River Periyar,

Senguttuvan with Venmaal.

Senguttuvan (in angry laughter): Did the Kanaka-Vijayas dare to deride Tamilnad, the homeland of such a heroic woman as Kannagi! What audacity and hardened villainy on their part! Here was Nedunchezhian who sacrificed his life at the altar of justice, and his Queen Kopperundevi who followed his lead even beyond death. Shall I rest, while aliens scoff at the land of unparalleled valour? No, a hundred times, no! Let those kings of the North get ready for a showdown on the field of battle. I shall conquer them and march them to the South, with the rock for the statue of Kannagi, loaded on their heads. Let the war-drums roll and the call go forth to all the warriors of our land...

The scene shifts to the Chera Capital Vanchi.

Senguttuvan: Glory to you, my men, who have stepped forth to teach a lesson to the enemies of Tamilnad. The challenge is an invitation to us to demonstrate our valour and the North shall tremble under the tread of our legions. Ignorant of the alphabet of heroism, they have gone to the extent of mocking at our emblems of the bow and the tiger and the fish, engraved there on the rock of the Himalayas.

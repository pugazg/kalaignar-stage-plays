# 10 Madhavi Conquers

Madhavi’s villa is being hurriedly decorated for Kovalan’s visit. As Kovalan’s chaise stops at the gate of the compound, dancing beauties are ready there to accord him a magnificent welcome. Kovalan is overwhelmed by the opulence of the reception and as he walks towards the house like one in a dream, Madhavi meets him and seeks his blessings by bowing low. With her lovelorn gaze fixed on his face she rises and leads him on. A thin stream of water from the garden crosses their path as they approach the building and lest Kovalan’s feet should get wet, Madhavi takes off her cape, folds it and places it on the ground so that it may act as a bridge for his feet. Kovalan hesitates to step on the silk but not having the heart to reject the pleading in her eyes, he yields to her solicitude and walks on.

As Kovalan mounts the stairs, Chitrapathi accords a traditional welcome with aratti. Madhavi takes a garland and steps forward but Kovalan gently prevents her from garlanding him. Seeing the anguish in her eyes, however, he yields to the extent of receiving the garland in his hands.

Kovalan is led to the seat of honour in a miniature auditorium. Filled with wonderment at the splendour of the reception, he watches a group dance, the sheer brilliance of which drugs his senses. Madhavi is the centre of the programme. The group gets reduced as the programme advances and at last Madhavi alone is left on the dais.

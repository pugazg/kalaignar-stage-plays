# Scan 108

Printed page: 74

## Publication text

Kannagi: Leave off such sombre meditations, my Lord, so that the past may not be a drag on the future. Food is ready and it is cooked all by myself. It seems ages since I served you. Please come, my Lord, I am so eager to resume my duty . . .

Kovalan takes food and the happiness of their early days is recaptured almost in full, for the couple.

Kannagi: How was the cooking, my Lord?

Kovalan [cheerfully]: More wonderful than ever, Kannagi, perhaps because this was the last meal you have cooked for me.

Kannagi [startled]: Why so, my Lord?

Kovalan: Once I re-establish business, do you think I will allow you to go near the oven? We shall have servants for all that work and your sole duty shall be to remain my queen and bride all the time.

Kannagi [playfully]: What extremes in mood? Only a few moments ago you were so dejected, but now you feel strong enough to conquer the world, my Lord! [Offering neatly folded betel-leaves to him] When the anklets are sold, my Lord, remember to purchase some silk-garments and ornaments for yourself; I must see you again decked in finery . . .

Ayyai comes from the front room.

Ayyai: Brother, brother , . ,

Kovalan: Yes, child . . .

Ayyai: Please don’t take sister Kannagi with you to the city. We are arranging a folk-dance here in our hamlet and we are eager that sister should witness it.

Kovalan: Go ahead, Ayyai. Kannagi is not coming with me.

Ayyai runs away, delighted.

Kovalan: So, shall I start for the city, Kannagi? [Kannagi stoops to unfasten her anklets. His resolution gives way at the sight] No, my dear, I would rather be a labourer and spend the rest of my life in the hut of austerity than convert your anklets into coins and build a fortune on that base.

Kannagi [determined, looking up]: Haven’t I told you, my Lord, that we would be able to secure any number of such anklets, once your business is resumed ?

Kovalan: Wait, Kannagi, for the last time let me see you walking with the anklets on your feet . . .

She obliges and Kovalan struggles with his emotions.

Kannagi: How much you scare me, my Lord, by using expressions like ‘last meal’ and ‘last time’!

Kovalan [embracing her]: Don’t be frightened, Kannagi. Haven’t you said, again and again, that good times are just round the corner for us?

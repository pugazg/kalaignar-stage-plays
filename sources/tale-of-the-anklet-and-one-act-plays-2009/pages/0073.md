# Scan 73

Printed page: 39

Header: *Tale of the Anklet*

*She moves away from him and her eyes are moist.*

Kovalan [coming to her]: Why this sorrow, dear?

Madhavi [accusing him]: Did you not sneeze, a while ago?

Kovalan: I did; and you wished me a long life. But how does it account for your tears?

Madhavi: You thought about someone other than me and that was the cause of the sneeze!

Kovalan: When you pervade my entire being, how could I have any thought of anyone other than you? Now I see—women are apt to get truculent on such occasions.

Madhavi [persistent]: What such occasion, my Lord?

Kovalan [hedging]: Occasions such as cause fatigue when you dance.

Madhavi: Tell me, Master-physician, what is the cause of the fatigue?

Kovalan: So you have come back to the starting point of the cross-examination!

Madhavi: You want to cling to your secret and so let me not disturb you, sir. [Once again she moves away, pouting.]

Kovalan: Stop, Ma..dha...vi!

Madhavi [turning]: Why do you lengthen my name?

Kovalan: Because you are en route to becoming - ‘madha’*. That is why you feel fatigued when you put yourself to physical strain. How do you relish my secret?

Madhavi [blushing in innocent pride]: I... I... [too shy to express her delight, she covers her face with both hands.]

Kovalan [taking her in his arms]: Madhavi!

Madhavi: My Lord!

*Their rapture is cut asunder by a lamenting voice from somewhere outside the grove.*

Voice: Oh God, for what sin of mine in the previous birth am I put to this suffering? Why should I live at all, when my husband has deserted me? My husband is hard-hearted but cannot my cry of anguish touch Your heart even, Oh God?

*Kovalan and Madhavi rush in the direction of the voice, to find a brahmin-woman with a babe-in-arms crying her heart out and gazing wildly at a boat in the Cauveri going away from the bank.*

# Scan 71

Printed page: 37

Header: *Tale of the Anklet*

Kannagi: Whose wealth is it, father? Is it not the earning of his industry and that too accumulated through hazardous journeys on the high seas? What better use can I put it to than by giving it away, to his satisfaction?

Manaykan: Wealth is like wax that melts rapidly if you do not conserve it, Kannagi. Wealth is not an ocean that remains the same in size even as you draw from it.

Kannagi: If wealth dwindles, not much is lost, father, for we could always acquire more wealth through industry. But if the love which my husband has for me shrinks ... [she is choked].

Manaykan [bitter]: So you are still under the delusion that Kovalan’s love for you is intact. In that case why should he be infatuated with that sorceress Madhavi who is his queen and goddess now?

Kannagi: I would not approve of your reviling Madhavi. She is far superior to me, not only in beauty but in love too. He has parted from me but I am still alive, whereas Madhavi will not be able to survive the pangs of separation. That is the reason why he has stayed there. Not that he loves me less; only he is more compassionate towards Madhavi and he knows full well that of the two she is more gentle and sensitive and hence is in greater need of his protection.

Manaykan [lamenting]: What nobility in this daughter of mine! Kovalan, how did you have the heart to fling this bouquet in raging fire!

*The strain of the discussion has been too much for Kannagi. Tears flow down her cheeks and she makes no effort to restrain her grief.*

Kannagi: Do not worry, father; it will take us nowhere.

Manaykan’s wife: We both came here to console you but it is you who are giving us strength. Deserting a paragon like you, how could that savage . . .

Kannagi [incensed]: Mother, measure your words. If you want to curse my husband and call him names, then I would not like you to visit this house. Whoever disrespects my husband cannot be my friend or relative. Now leave me alone, leave me alone . . .

*Her sobs are heartrending and the parents watch helplessly.*

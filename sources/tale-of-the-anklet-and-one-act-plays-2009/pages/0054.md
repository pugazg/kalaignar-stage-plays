# 7 Queen of Artists — continued

**Kovalan [not noticing her fear]:** Come on, dear, we have nothing else to do here. [*He hustles her and they leave the auditorium*]

*The hall is soon empty and the frustrated Greek too has left with the rest of the audience. Madhavi and her team are, however, still on the dais. Madhavi has recovered her poise.*

**Madhavi:** Vasantamalai, he took my garland, but why has he gone away?

**Vasantamalai:** That is an odd question, Madhavi. Why should he stay?

**Chitrapathi [to Vasantamalai]:** Simpleton, don’t you follow her train of thought? She wants to meet him again. [*Turning to her daughter*] Am I not right, Madhavi?

*Madhavi blushes and averts her eyes in affirmation.*

# 8 Kannagi in Wedded Bliss

*Bedchamber in Kovalan’s house. Fragrance of jasmine. Moonlight filters through the casement. Kovalan and Kannagi are seated on the couch, close to and facing each other.*

**Kannagi:** My Lord, what a panic I got into, there in the Hall of Pearls when you offered your necklace to Madhavi!

**Kovalan:** Tell me, dear, could I have done anything else to save Madhavi?

**Kannagi:** I know, my Lord, but I was scared all the same. Only, even in the grip of fear, faith glowed at the bottom of my heart, like the pearl on seabed.

**Kovalan:** That only goes to show that all women are suspicious by nature. What surprises me is that my Kannagi too has this common failing.

**Kannagi:** Not that I suspected you, my Lord. It was the sudden turn of events that unnerved me somewhat. [*Clinging to him*] Don’t I know that I have absolute claim over you!

**Kovalan:** I am your willing slave, Kannagi. For without you by my side, my life feels drained of purpose.

**Kannagi [telling more]:** I shuddered to think whether my priceless treasure would slip into the hands of Madhavi and whether the religious rights performed at the time of our wedding would be deprived of their efficacy to hold us together in union.

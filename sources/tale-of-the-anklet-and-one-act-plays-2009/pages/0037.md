# 1 Vanchi Prepares for Festival

*A broad street in Vanchi, the capital of the Chera kingdom. A royal messenger on elephant-back beats a drum to draw the attention of citizens. The elephant is awe-inspiring in grandeur and the silk draped over its sides carries the imprint of a bow which is the emblem of the Cheras.*

*The messenger rounds off his drum-beating with a flourish and as the echoes fade, silence descends in a rush, to be slowly replaced by the eager whisperings of the expectant crowd.*

**Messenger (in strident tones):** Eternal life to Tamil! Victory to Tamilnad! Rejoice, ye citizens, and celebrate in splendour this, the birthday of our valiant prince Senguttuvan, the worthy son of the brave and noble Neduncheraladhan who led an army to the North and engraved our emblem of the bow on the rock of the towering Himalayas. Let the bells ring in gay abandon and let the music of revelry fill the air, as the great people of this vast land send their felicitations to the lion-hearted prince. Long live Senguttuvan,! Long live Cheraland! Eternal life to Tamil! Victory to Tamilnad!

*The drum resumes its vigorous music as the elephant moves down the street and the citizens disperse in small groups, chatting happily.*

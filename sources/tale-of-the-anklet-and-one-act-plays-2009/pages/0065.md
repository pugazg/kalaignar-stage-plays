# 11 Bravery in Grief

Kovalan’s house. Kannagi is standing near the doorway, waiting for her husband. The chaise returns on weary wheels. She eagerly looks for Kovalan to alight and she is alarmed when the driver alone comes and stands irresolute.

Kannagi: Where is your master?

Driver [with head bowed]: Master has not returned...., he has also postponed his journey to Rome.

Kannagi is dazed. The driver goes away. Just then, Kannagi’s friend Devanti comes in and is surprised to see her at the doorway, as in a trance.

Devanti: Kannagi, what is the matter? Why are you looking like that?

Kannagi [recovering]: Oh, it is nothing, Devanti.

Devanti: Then why are your eyes wet? Where is your husband?

Kannagi [temporising]: He must have gone to prepare for his journey to Rome.

Devanti: And that is why you have been crying—in anticipation of the parting! Really, you must learn to be a little less emotional, Kannagi.

They go into the house.

Devanti: I heard Madhavi was keeping on sending envoys to your husband. Has she stopped now?

Kannagi [steeling herself]: Of course, she has.

# Scan 96

Printed page: 62

Running head: `Tale of the Anklet`

## Transcription

Masathuvan: Although it had been taken without your knowledge, this pile of gold was my son’s gift to you, Madhavi, and it is a rule in our house that we never take back anything given once.

Madhavi: When I have lost the treasure of my love, what use is this transient gold to me, sir! Oh, please say once that you have forgiven this sinner!

Masathuvan [moved to tears]: Madhavi, my beloved daughter!

Madhavi [breaking down]: Manimekalai, my child, see for yourself what a noble family your mother has wrecked . . .

Masathuvan [consoling her]: Weep not, Madhavi . . . Let me see the child.

He takes the child in his arms and the thought that he is now a grandfather tickles him in spite of his grief. Manimekalai smiles up at him. Reminded poignantly of Kovalan’s smile, his eyes fill with tears once again . . .

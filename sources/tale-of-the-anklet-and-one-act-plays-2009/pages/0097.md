# 24 Near Mathurai

One way to Mathurai. The track winds through paddy-fields against the distant backdrop of blue hills. A thorn runs into Kannagi’s foot and she gasps in pain.

Kovalan [removing the thorn]: There seems to be no end to the misery I am causing to you, Kannagi. [Staring at the thorn] I am far more callous than this thorn, for it only sought to hurt you in the foot when you stepped on it, whereas, without any provocation whatsoever, I inflicted a wound on your heart and that too, a wound which even Time cannot heal . . . Rather than see my Kannagi suffering like this, I would prefer to be blind and this timely thorn shall do me a service . .

He raises the thorn to his eye.

Kannagi [crying out and catching hold of his hand]: Oh, my Lord, what a terrible thing are you attempting? The only antidote to my sorrow is your comely grace and do you want to rob me of that also? Don’t thrust me deeper in the slough of despond, my Lord, don’t!

Kaunthi Adigal: Kovalan, how could you forget the maxim of the Thirukkural that the best weapon against adversity is a courageous smile? A hero is one who stands up to vicissitude, and why do you lose heart when you are so near your destination?

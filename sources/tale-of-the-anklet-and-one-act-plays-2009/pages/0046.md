# Tale of the Anklet — Section 5 continued

**Senguttuvan:** Welcome, Ilango!

**Ilango:** Greetings, brother. *[To Venmaal]* How are you, sister? *[She smiles by way of answering ‘quite well’]* *[To Sathanar]* What a pleasure to meet you, comrade!

**Sathanar:** The pleasure is mine and also the privilege.

**Ilango [to Senguttuvan]:** Brother, your subjects from the hills have come to greet you.

**Senguttuvan:** I am indeed glad to see them.

*The tribesfolk prostrate before him and offer the gifts they have brought.*

**Senguttuvan:** Thanks, my friends. May you all live long and in prosperity. Now, is there any news of importance in this part of the land?

**One of the tribesmen [he is obviously their chosen spokesman]:** Yes, sir. A lady with unloosed tresses, whose name, we ascertained, was Kannagi, stood as in penance for very many days right under the vengai tree yonder. She had lost her husband but her faith was profound that he would return to her. One day she was not to be seen and we know not where she went, sir. Judging from the ardour of her devotion, we are inclined to believe that she succeeded in rejoining her husband; and this we can affirm, that never before have we seen a woman of such divine grace and never before have we seen such a picture of sorrow!

**Senguttuvan [repeating the name to himself as if trying to recollect something]:** Kannagi!

**Sathanar:** Yes, yes, Kannagi, the Queen of Chastity. Her life is an epic and only Ilango has the art and style to record it for posterity.

**Senguttuvan:** Ilango will fulfil your desire, Sathanar, and so let us inaugurate the writing straightaway.

*Fondling a tiger-cub, Sathanar commences to relate the story. His resonant prose is transformed into rich poetry as it passes through Ilango whose stylus races over the palm-leaves (which were the equivalent of writing paper in those times.) The scene changes to the rhythm of Sathanar's voice and a flag bearing the tiger-emblem of the Cholas flutters high against the backdrop of the blue sky with cumulus clouds.*

**Sathanar:** Poompuhar, the City Beautiful on the banks of the River Cauveri where it meets and merges with the billowing sea. It is the capital city of the Chola kingdom...

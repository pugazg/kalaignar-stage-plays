# 13 Kannagi in Loneliness

Kovalan's house. Masathuvan and his wife are there to console Kannagi.

Masathuvan: Kovalan is a traitor to my house, Kannagi. He is like a fish in Madhavi's net and alas, you are like a goldfish thrown on hard ground.

Kannagi [putting on a brave face]: Things have not gone that far, uncle. [Playing the hostess, as a ruse to conserve her self-control] How selfish of me to forget even elementary courtesies! Let me go in and bring some fruits for you. [Without giving them time to restrain her, she goes in, wipes her eyes and returns with a bowl of fruits which she sets before them.]

Masathuvan: Forgive me, Kannagi, for having brought forth a son who is callously indifferent to the feelings of your tender heart. When I look at you in your present state, I feel like rushing to Madhavi's house, putting Kovalan in irons and dragging him here.

Kannagi [alarmed, for she knows her father-in-law is impetuous and is capable of carrying out his threat]: No, no, uncle, it would only aggravate the trouble. After all, the fact is there that it was I who despatched him to Madhavi's villa and if you were to go there and humiliate him, I would become a traitor to my husband.

Masathuvan is cornered and he keeps quiet.

Masathuvan's wife: How I wish, Kannagi, that you had been my daughter rather than daughter-in-law!

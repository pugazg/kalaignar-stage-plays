## Transcription

The music and dance outside is cacophony to her ears and memories of the Festival in the years past weigh her down. In utter despair, she stands in front of Kovalan’s portrait. That spot to her has become a sanctuary from the rest of her own home.

Kannagi: My Lord, I am certain that you would be in the midst of the celebrations now. I have stayed cloistered within these four walls from the day you left me for Madhavi, but I can bear the agony no longer. I have got to see your face, my Lord, at least from a distance, or my life would ebb away. I am coming, my Lord, to seek you out…

She unbolts the frontdoor and rushes to the street, as in frenzy. Devanti is alarmed and she sets out to follow Kannagi.

On the seashore, Kovalan and Madhavi are on a stroll. They walk close to the water-edge and the waves caress their feet from time to time.

Kannagi too has reached the seashore but she is too far away from the lovers to see them or to be seen by them. Devanti overtakes her at last and grips her shoulders, none too gently.

Devanti [gasping on account of exertion]: What a fall have you registered, Kannagi, by coming out like this! Even if you wanted to bring disgrace to your family, could you not choose a less auspicious time than when the land is celebrating the Indra Festival?

Kannagi [bridling]: Is it a disgrace, Devanti, if I come to see my husband?

Devanti: At this time of the day and looking the picture of misery, should a respectable woman be seen running in frenzy? What would people think of you, Kannagi, and how would Kovalan himself feel if he were to see you here? [Kannagi is silent] You have so far put on a brave front before your parents and in-laws and won’t they sink in despair if they learn of this? Coming from a noble family you are daughter-in-law of an equally noble house; recollect your background, Kannagi, and do not give way to such despair. [Kindly] After all, why should you want to see your husband in person when he is all along dwelling in your heart? Let us return home, my friend, before we are noticed here.

She takes Kannagi by the arm and almost drags her along.

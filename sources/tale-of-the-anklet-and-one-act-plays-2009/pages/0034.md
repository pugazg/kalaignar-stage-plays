# Preface — concluded

My thanks are due to Mr T. G. Narayanaswamy who has rendered the play into English, to the management of the B. N. K. Press Private Ltd. who have printed the book, to the artists Amudhone and Vijaya, to Mr R. Nagaswamy, Director of Archaeology, Government of Tamilnad, who has helped with the photographs in the book and to my close friend and comrade-in-literary-studies Mr Anandam who, assisted by K. Gopalaswamy and G. Dhinakaran, has organised the publication of this work.

I shall ever be beholden to my revered leader Anna, Chief Minister of Tamilnad, who has so graciously consented to contribute the ‘Foreword’ to this book.

Madras  
9 January ’68

**M. Karunanidhi**

# Scan 111

Printed page: 77

## Publication text

The Chief Goldsmith comes, accompanied by two persons.

Citizen [accosting]: Master-Goldsmith, here is someone with a magnificent anklet for sale.

Chief Goldsmith [startled]: Anklet? [opening his eyes wide on seeing the ornament, he addresses Kovalan] I might be able to help. You don’t belong to Mathurai, do you?

Kovalan [pleased, that he has found an opening]: No, I come from Poompuhar.

Chief Goldsmith: Actually, I was looking for just such an anklet. Will you kindly wait with the anklet in this hall here, while I discuss the matter with the king and return? [To the two persons who came with him] Keep him company until I am back.

The two persons get the import of his special gesture and they nod in comprehension.

The Chief Goldsmith hurries away. Kovalan settles down to wait.

# 2 Senguttuvan’s Birthday — continued

**Senguttuvan:** Oh, don’t blame me for the delay. It is your sister-in-law who is still giving the finishing touches to my decoration.

**Ilango [appealing to Venmaal]:** Sister, do please release him quick. I will wait outside. [*He turns to go.*]

**Senguttuvan:** Don’t be so very arch, Ilango. You may stay here itself and take me with you. [*Turning to Venmaal*] My dear, do I have your leave to go to the Court with my brother? [*Her eyes convey the message of her lips and responding in like manner, Senguttuvan strides from the chamber, accompanied by Ilango. Submerged in love, Venmaal is a picture of thoughtful beauty and grace.*]

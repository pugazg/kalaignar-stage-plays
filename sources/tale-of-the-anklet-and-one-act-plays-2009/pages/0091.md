# Scan 91

Printed page: 57

Running head: `Tale of the Anklet`

## Transcription

Madhavi [scolding her]: So you too have come! Like the hunchback in Ramayana who sought to wreck the country, you are here to bring this house to rack and ruin.

Hunchback [arrogant]: Hold your tongue, Madhavi!

Madhavi: Why should I? Without my knowledge and making use of his name, you plundered his wealth. What depraved creatures you all are! Did you think, you schemers, that I loved him for his riches?

Chitrapathi [callously]: You never loved him at all. We allowed you to make love to him so that we could find the means to live in comfort and style.

Madhavi: You are not my mother but a vampire living on my blood; and that Hunchback by your side is Evil incarnate whose mission is to destroy this ancient city of Puhar. It is a sin to be near you even . . .

They go away in dudgeon.

Madhavi [to Manimekalai]: And now, child, we are sole support to each other.

She weeps bitterly and the child watches her, in uncomprehending grief.

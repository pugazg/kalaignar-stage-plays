# 19 Melody Breaks

Bedchamber in Madhavi’s villa. An oil lamp burns in a corner. Through the casement is seen the grey of the pre-dawn. Kovalan and Madhavi are in sound slumber.

A cock crows and somewhere a conch is blown to herald the break of day. Madhavi awakes and gently calls ‘My Lord’. Kovalan is still asleep. She takes a harp and runs her fingers over its strings. The soft melody awakens Kovalan and he gazes at her face in speechless adoration.

Madhavi: The Indra Festival is to conclude today, my Lord. Shall we go to the seashore to witness the majesty of sunrise?

Kovalan: As you please, my dear, but there you must play the harp and sing a seaside lyric for me.

Madhavi: I agree, my Lord, on condition you also respond with a lyric of your own.

Kovalan: If the theme is Madhavi, I will compose and sing any number of lyrics but if you want a routine seaside song, I am out of the deal.

Madhavi: Have it your way, then. Let us hurry, my Lord, or we would miss the golden splendour of the rising sun. [Calling] Vasantamalai, we are going to the seashore and so see to it that a settee is arranged there for us, on the white sand, in the shade of a flowering tree and curtained with silk drapes. Order the chariot to be at the gate in the next few moments and keep my harp tuned and ready.

# Tale of the Anklet — Section 9 continued

Kovalan [pointing at Vasantamalai]: Nothing but this. She has come for the hundredth time to persuade me to marry Madhavi. [In disgust] The sheer impertinence of the idea!

Vasantamalai: How long am I to run up and down? Cannot you come but once and give your answer in person to her?

Kannagi: There is some sense in what she says. Why don't you meet Madhavi once, my Lord, and convince her that she is yearning for the impossible?

Kovalan [surprised not a little]: Kannagi, to give a negative answer in person would be adding insult to injury!

Kannagi: Not exactly, my Lord. Advice from one she has so much regard for might make her see reason. Is it not worth a try anyway?

Kovalan [very reluctant]: Well, Vasantamalai, go and tell your mistress that I am coming to confirm my 'no' to her. [She curtsies and hurries away.]

Kannagi brings his silk wrap and sandals.

Kovalan [as she ties the sandals to his feet]: I don't have to go at all, but for your insistence. What a melancholy mission and that too when we two should be in each other's company...

Kannagi: Madhavi is a soft-hearted girl and I want her to be happy. Tell her that you yourself would find a suitable husband for her.

Kovalan: All right, Kannagi, I will be back in a few moments.

Kannagi: I have myself asked you to go but now I find even this brief separation unbearable. [Her eyes filling with tears] How shall I bid you leave, my Lord, when you set out for Rome?

Wiping her tears once again, Kovalan gets into his chaise and drives away. With a vague sense of fear haunting her all of a sudden, Kannagi keeps gazing at the vehicle until it disappears round the bend.

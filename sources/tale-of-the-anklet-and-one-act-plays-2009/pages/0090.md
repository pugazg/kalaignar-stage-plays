# 21 Madhavi in Loneliness

Madhavi’s villa. Madhavi, anguished and worn out, is seated near Manimekalai. Vasantamalai enters.

Madhavi: Could you meet him, Vasantamalai? How did he receive my message?

Vasantamalai: It is too late, Madhavi. In spite of my warning you played with fire and now you have lost him. His house is uninhabited and all I could learn from Devanti was that Kovalan and Kannagi have left for Mathurai.

Madhavi [losing all hope]: Shall I ever see him again? [To the child] Alas, Manimekalai, your father has left the city even without taking leave of you and me! Calling you by a hundred endearing names he used to shower kisses on your cheeks and he was looking forward to hearing you utter your first word. Even before you could learn to call me ‘Amma’* and call him ‘Appa’+ how could he have the heart to tear himself away from you and from me your hapless mother!

Chitrapathi enters.

Chitrapathi [sternly]: Madhavi, What a dirge are you raising, my daughter? As if you were his lawfully wedded wife and he has deserted you!

Madhavi [turning on her mother]: Enough, Mother, I have borne enough for the misfortune of being your daughter!

Hunchback [coming in]: What is the matter, Chitrapathi?

---

* Amma = ‘ma for mamma’, + Appa = ‘pa for ‘papa’.

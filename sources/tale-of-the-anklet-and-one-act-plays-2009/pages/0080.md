# Scan 80

Section 18: **Two Sides of the Festival**

Yet another Indra Festival in Poompuhar. The tiger-flag is hoisted to the cheers of the populace and the city is brighter and gayer than ever before. Since the last Festival, statues have been installed of the renowned Chola ancestors, Indra and Karikalan.

There is a march-past by the army; and at the harbour, the royal ships and barges present a formation.

Madhavi and Kovalan are apparelled in finery and they stop by Manimekalai’s cradle to fondle the child.

Kovalan: See the beam of the child’s smile, Madhavi. Manimekalai is a replica of yourself in grace and cheer.

Madhavi [without inhibition]: But the consensus of opinion among the ladies here is that Manimekalai resembles my sister Kannagi. I have myself got into the habit of considering the child to be Kannagi’s daughter rather than mine.

Kovalan [embarrassed, closing the topic]: Let us go, Madhavi.

They kiss the child and go out. Manimekalai waves to them from the cradle while Vasantamalai and others pamper her with their attentions.

Kovalan and Madhavi go round the city participating in the best of the celebrations.

* * *

Kovalan’s house, now dilapidated and shorn of light. The tumult of the Festival in the city plunges Kannagi in agony. By closing the casement she tries to shut out the flash of fireworks but the bright lights do filter in, through the chinks.

### 34 Kannagi Swears Vengeance

Hall in Mathurai City where Kovalan stayed last. Kannagi rushes in like a gale and falls on the blood-spattered body of her husband.

Kannagi: Have they reduced you to a corpse, my Lord! When I bid you leave and sent you to the city with my anklet, I predicted that good times were just round the corner for us. I had no means of knowing then that untimely death was lying in ambush for you! How did you allow yourself to be abducted by death, my Lord, and that too with such a terrible allegation branded on your forehead? There in Madhari’s hamlet I was watching a tableau on grief while here in this hall of injustice you were the hero of a tragedy, preparing to set me adrift once again in the sea of sorrow, with my heart breaking into myriad bits. Did not the halo of integrity around your head, the light of courage in your eyes and the smile of truth on your lips convince your enemies that you were innocent beyond the shadow of doubt? Was it in anticipation of your end that you asked to see me walk, wearing both the anklets? Combing your hair in the aura of akhil, I added the fragrance of sandalwood to the gold of your complexion.

### 36 Mathurai in Flames

Kannagi (her fury still unabated): Blood for blood! Death and destruction! The city of Mathurai which could not prevent the murder of my husband, let it be razed to the ground. Let mansions crumble and mounds arise. Let the very earth split and devour the sinful. Let dust get churned up and blot out the sun. Let fire spread and leave nothing behind, except its own spark - the spark of justice that shall glow through time!

The laughter of her vengeance rises above the roar of the raging fire which threatens to burn up even the skies.

People run for their lives but the fire catches them up and they go down with hideous cries. The Chief Goldsmith, the officer who stole the Queen’s anklet, the guardsmen who surrounded and killed Kovalan - all of them are swallowed up by the flames.

Kannagi, still with tresses unloosed, seems to lead the fire mile upon mile, until she leaves the boundary of Mathurai...

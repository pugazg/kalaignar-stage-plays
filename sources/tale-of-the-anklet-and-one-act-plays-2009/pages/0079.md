# Scan 79

Section 17: **Valour Seen through Tears**

Kovalan’s house. Devanti is relating how Kovalan controlled the wild elephant and saved the life of the poor brahmin. Kannagi is listening, in deep concern.

Devanti [concluding]: They say that even battle-scarred warriors could not control the elephant and that your husband literally achieved the impossible.

Kannagi [in tears and in smiles]: Oh, he is as valorous as he is compassionate. Are you certain he was not hurt even a little?

Devanti: What solicitude, Kannagi? [Light-heartedly] Even if he had received a bruise or two, Madhavi is there to tend him.

Kannagi: Yes, you are right; Madhavi is there. My sister is a blessed girl!

*She looks up at Kovalan’s portrait and ministers to him in her fancy.*

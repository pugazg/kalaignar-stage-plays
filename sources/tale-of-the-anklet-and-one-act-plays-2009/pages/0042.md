# 4 Ilango’s Renunciation

*Palace of the Chera king. Neduncheraladhan, Narchonai, Senguttuvan and Venmaal are engaged in anxious discussion. Senguttuvan has overcome his disappointment and his face is now clear and bright.*

**Senguttuvan:** Who is Ilango after all? Is he not my younger brother? All you have to do, Sire, is to forget for a moment that I was born, and conduct the coronation of Ilango. Is not that the simplest and the best solution?

**Neduncheraladhan:** That will not be proper, son.

**Senguttuvan:** Why quarrel with Providence, father ? Let me not come between Ilango and the throne ordained for him. Let him rule the land; and as Commander-in-Chief I will ever be by his side and be a sword and shield to him.

*Ilango’s voice is heard, calling “Brother”. They all turn in the direction of the voice and Ilango appears, in the robes of a monk.*

**Senguttuvan [shocked]:** Ilango, what have you done, my boy?

**Ilango:** Nothing, brother, except taking to the path of renunciation so that the firstborn in the royal family may fulfil the role he is groomed for. Ilango is no longer a prince; he is a king in his own right in that he owns nothing material and is only an ascetic.

**Narchonai [sobbing]:** Ilango, my son! What quirk of Fate is this, that I should live to see you in this apparel!

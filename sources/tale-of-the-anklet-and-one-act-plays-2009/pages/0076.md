# Scan 76

Section 15: **Return Thwarted**

Kovalan’s house. Austerity all round. Kannagi is a picture of grief as she moves from chamber to chamber. The lamp is unlit and the oil is being drained, drop by drop. The front-door is ajar and noticing that a rooster is trying to sneak in, she slams the door shut, in mechanical action.

Outside, Kovalan, who has arrived in trepidation and stepped on the stair just then, receives a rude shock when he sees the door literally slammed against his face. For a moment he stands, head bowed, and then he turns and goes away.

Unaware of her husband’s arrival and departure, Kannagi is standing in front of Kovalan’s portrait.

Kannagi: My Lord, will you never return? Why did you direct the anger of the volcano on my humble self and why did you select my heart to test the point of your spear? Your embrace is denied to me and my ears are deprived of the poetry of your speech. With bleary eyes I keep vigil, through day and night, hoping against hope and battling against despair. Will my devotion be in vain, my Lord, just as my love has been? Won’t you come but once, so that I may have a brief glimpse of the face of my God?

*Her gaze is fixed on the portrait as she is tossed about in a sea of memories.*

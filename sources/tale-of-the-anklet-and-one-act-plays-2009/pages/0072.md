# Scan 72

Section 14: **Madhavi’s Magnanimity**

A verdant grove on the bank of the Cauveri. Madhavi and Kovalan are at the heights of happiness. She sings and dances, then suddenly sits down fatigued.

Kovalan [concerned]: Why, Madhavi, this fatigue?

Madhavi: I don’t know, my Lord, but these days I am subject to sudden spells of giddiness.

Kovalan: Let me make a resolution here and now, that I should not ask you to dance.

Madhavi: Are you displeased with me, my Lord?

Kovalan: Certainly not, my dear, but I know that in your present state you should not dance much.

Madhavi: Why so?

Kovalan: I won’t tell you, for you would feel shy.

Madhavi: You have got to tell me, for I cannot bear the thought that you are holding back anything from me.

Kovalan: On second thoughts, I feel it will be better if I don’t tell you.

Madhavi [sulking]: That is all right then. You can enjoy your secret on your own.

*She prepares to leave the place. Just then Kovalan sneezes.*

Madhavi [involuntarily]: May you live a hundred years!

# 12 Kovalan in Newfound Joy

Madhavi’s villa. Madhavi is stringing flowers and Kovalan is engrossed in the deft movements of her fingers.

Madhavi [happily]: What is engaging your attention, my Lord?

Kovalan: Only the artistic movements of your expressive fingers. You are created for Art, Madhavi; I see it in every gesture of yours.

Madhavi [with conviction]: No, my Lord, I was created only for you and that was why I could meet you and win you too.

Kovalan: Coming to think of it, you could not have been created at all! Where in the universe could there be an artist and sculptor to fashion a dream-like you? The gold of your complexion and the rain-cloud of your tresses, the ruby of your lips and the pearl of your teeth, the magic in your eyes and the drug in your speech – which creator could have assembled them all and having assembled, parted with the fruit of his labour?

Madhavi: It is so easy for women to string flowers, my Lord, but I see that it is far easier for men to string garlands of praise for women in their presence.

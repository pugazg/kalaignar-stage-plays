# 7 Queen of Artists — continued

**Madhavi [in a tear-choked voice]:** I have decided to remain a virgin.

**Chitrapathi [her anger mounting]:** I cannot dance to your tunes all the time. [*Menacingly*] Give the garland or else...

**Kannagi [to Kovalan]:** My Lord, I cannot bear to see Madhavi’s plight. Please do something to rescue her from a sentence worse than death.

**Madhavi [losing all hope and falling at the feet of the Greek gentleman]:** Sir, have pity on me!

**Greek gentleman [querulous]:** What is all this drama? A promise was held out in public that whoever pays 1008 *kazhanjus* of gold for the garland could claim Madhavi and now there seems to be a conspiracy to back out of the deal.

*Kovalan strides towards the dais.*

**Kovalan [firmly, to Greek gentleman]:** Enough of this. You will get neither the garland nor Madhavi. Collect your gold and come out please.

**Greek gentleman:** Kovalan, so you are also in the conspiracy. Wonderful are the standards of justice in Tamilnad!

**Kovalan [pricked]:** It is not considered proper in Tamilnad that an old man should yearn for a young bride.

**Greek gentleman [not budging]:** Then why was the offer made here in such specific terms?

**Kovalan:** Madhavi’s mother stipulated the condition that whoever can afford to buy the garland could claim her daughter. My interpretation and verdict is that he who bids for the garland should be worthy of Madhavi. Is that clear? Let us go now; you have caused enough anguish to everyone here.

**Greek gentleman [adamant]:** Madhavi is mine and I shall not leave without her. You have an agile brain and a glib tongue, Kovalan, but all your oratory cannot cover the fact that no one here could compete with me and produce 1008 *kazhanjus* of gold.

**Kovalan [stung to the quick]:** Dare you imply that we are bankrupts here in Poompuhar? [*Unclasping the pearl necklace he is wearing*] Here is something worth a lot more than 1008 *kazhanjus* of gold. I will take the garland in exchange for this and thereby rescue Madhavi from the clutches of this old sinner.

*Impulsively he hands over his pearl necklace to Madhavi and takes the garland from her hands. Kannagi in the audience is taken aback by the turn of events. His mission of rescuing Madhavi accomplished, Kovalan walks off the dais and comes straight to Kannagi.*

**Kovalan:** Kannagi, let us go.

**Kannagi [apprehensive]:** But...

# 7 Queen of Artists — continued

*Chitrapathi whispers something in the ears of the hunchbacked old woman. Hunchback takes the garland from Madhavi’s hand and goes to the dais.*

**Hunchback [to the audience]:** Gentlemen, Chitrapathi’s daughter Madhavi provided a feast to your eyes and ears and hearts so far and now we shall move a stage ahead. Here is an opportunity for anyone eligible in your midst to seek her hand. [*Holding up the garland*] He who is able to pay 1008 *kazhanjus* of gold for this flower-garland shall be Madhavi’s life-companion.

*The magnitude of the price stuns the audience.*

**Greek gentleman [rushing towards the dais]:** Here is the gold.

*Chitrapathi and the hunchback exchange glances of delight.*

**Madhavi [alarmed]:** Vasantamalai!

**Vasantamalai [hurrying close to Madhavi, in a gesture of defence]:** Madhavi!

*The Greek gentleman unties a bag and pours out the gold for all to see.*

**Greek gentleman:** This gold I had brought to buy pearls from Tamilnad but now I have got something more valuable. This is not the price of the garland but of Madhavi the queen of my heart. [*To Hunchback*] Here, take the gold and give me the garland.

*As Hunchback is about to conclude the transaction, Madhavi grabs the garland from her hand and rushes sobbing to her mother Chitrapathi.*

**Chitrapathi [chiding her daughter]:** What is all this, Madhavi? Are you still a child? Give the garland at once.

**Vasantamalai [to Chitrapathi]:** Madam, for the sake of 1008 *kazhanjus* of gold do you want to throw away your daughter on this 108-year-old bidder? [*Pleading*] Listen to me, Madam, and postpone the selection to a more opportune occasion.

**Chitrapathi:** Hush, Vasantamalai, it shall not be said that Chitrapathi went back on her word. Madhavi belongs to this gentleman and I will not be challenged on this.

**Vasantamalai [wringing her hands and appealing to the audience]:** Is there no one here to come to the rescue of Madhavi? [*Getting no response, she turns to the Greek gentleman himself*] Sir, I appeal to your good sense! Oh, do please save Madhavi!

**Greek gentleman [in mordant wit]:** I will, for the rest of her life and mine— I have sufficient riches for the purpose.

*Vasantamalai sinks in despair. In the audience Kovalan and Kannagi stand rooted to the spot, not knowing what to do, to help Madhavi.*

**Chitrapathi [grim]:** Madhavi, give the garland. Did you not say that you would marry and settle down in life? And now that I have yielded to your whim and arranged the match, why do you want to humiliate me like this?

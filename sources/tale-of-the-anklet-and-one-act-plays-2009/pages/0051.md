# 7 Queen of Artistes

*Auditorium of Pearls. Madhavi’s dance is in progress. The Chola King and his family are in the front rows of the audience. Near the entrance to the dais and down below are seated Madhavi’s mother Chitrapathi, her agent, a very old hunchbacked woman, and Madhavi’s companion and maid Vasantamalai.*

*Kovalan, Kannagi and the Greek gentleman enter the hall and take their seats.*

*Madhavi’s artistry keeps the audience spellbound. The Greek visitor, in particular, is overwhelmed.*

**Greek gentleman:** Kovalan, is it a portrait come alive that is dancing there?

**Kovalan [ragging him]:** It must be so, for nothing else could rekindle the embers in your ancient heart. The portrait is Madhavi and I myself have known her only through reputation, till this evening.

*The programme concludes, to the applause of the audience. Madhavi comes from the dais and bows before the king.*

**King:** My blessings to you, Madhavi. In the field of dancing, you are the foremost in our land. May your genius flourish!

*Saying which, he presents her a flower-garland, a baton (as a mark of her supremacy in Fine Arts) and a silk-purse containing 1008 kazhanjus* of gold. He then leaves the hall, with his retinue.*

---

*kazhanju = a measure of gold by weight

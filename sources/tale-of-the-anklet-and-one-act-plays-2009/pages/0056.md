# 8 Kannagi in Wedded Bliss — continued

**Kovalan:** The vow I took at the altar when we stood hand in hand under the canopy of flowers shall remain immutable, dear.

**Kannagi:** So I trust, my Lord, and that is the staff of my life.

**Kovalan:** Kannagi, in future I will not talk of remembering you, for that would imply I had forgotten you, which is an impossibility for me. You are the pure gold of my life and you are the pearl, the fragrance flawless, the sweetness of honey and the source of light. You are the gem which the earth could not have yielded and you are the melody which no harp can compose. I can chant your praise till eternity and even then I would not have done justice to my theme.

**Kannagi [moved]:** My Lord...

**Kovalan [softly]:** Kannagi...

*As if in magnified echo, someone outside calls ‘Kannagi’ in a loud and perturbed voice. That upsets the magic for the lovers. They draw apart and recovering herself, Kannagi unbolts the door and goes outside while Kovalan stands irresolute for a moment and then smiles to himself as he sits on the couch again.*

*Kannagi comes to the courtyard and she is surprised to see her parents standing there grief-stricken.*

**Kannagi’s mother [rushing to Kannagi and holding her in embrace]:** My daughter, what harm have I done that such a fate should befall you! [*She weeps.*]

**Kannagi [mystified and perturbed]:** Mother, what are you talking about?

**Kannagi’s father Manaykan [putting on a brave face]:** Fear not, my daughter, everything will work out well. [*His pose giving way*] Till yesterday I could never have believed that any shadow would fall on your conjugal bliss, but today... [*his voice fails him.*]

**Kannagi [in acute suspense]:** What has happened, father? Please tell me that.

*Hurrying footsteps are heard and Kovalan’s parents arrive on the scene.*

**Kovalan’s father Masathuvan [in great anger]:** Kannagi, where is Kovalan?

**Kannagi:** Uncle...

**Manaykan [interrupting her and addressing Masathuvan]:** See what your son has done! A portrait in cheer she was when I gave her in marriage to Kovalan but now he has reduced her to tears.

**Masathuvan:** Worry not, my friend. I will myself teach him a lesson. [*To his daughter-in-law*] Kannagi, leave the matter to me and trust me when I say that I will never allow sorrow to come your way.

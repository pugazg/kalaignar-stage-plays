# 9 Parting Sparks Separation — continued

**Kovalan:** No, my dear! [*She is startled.*] For, as I said once before, if I have to remember you, I should have forgotten you in the first instance and that is of course impossible. [*He wipes her tears.*]

**Kannagi [still sulking]:** They say Rome is a beehive of beauties.

**Kovalan [promptly]:** Then I would be lame for the duration of my stay there, so that I cannot reach up to the hive. Anyway, what honey can be sweeter than the nectar you give me? In my dreams I will feast on your beauty...

**Kannagi [interrupting him]:** That way you are more fortunate, my Lord, since I will not be able to see you in my dreams.

**Kovalan [startled in his turn]:** Why so, dear?

**Kannagi:** Because dream presupposes sleep and my eyelids do not meet when you are away from me.

**Kovalan:** I will be back in sixteen weeks, Kannagi, and that shall be my last trip abroad. Never again shall I be away from you, even for a single day...

*Persistent knocking is heard at the front-door. Kovalan frowns in annoyance.*

**Kovalan:** Let me go and find out who it is, at this time of the night. You wait here, Kannagi.

*He unlatches the door, crosses the courtyard and goes to the main door.*

*Kovalan opens the front-door and is mightily displeased to find Vasantamalai without.*

**Kovalan [harsh]:** Is it you, again?

**Vasantamalai [hurt by his tone]:** I only came to inform you, sir, that Madhavi is preparing to sacrifice her very life at the altar of unrequited love.

**Kovalan [unmoved]:** Whatever be your mode of assault, my answer is the same— I did not buy Madhavi’s garland to marry her.

**Vasantamalai:** So you are determined to pass death sentence on her.

**Kovalan:** I admit the fault is mine, in having intervened. I should have allowed the old Greek to take her away with him!

**Vasantamalai:** Will you at least fulfil Madhavi’s last wish, sir, and let her know for what flaw in her that you reject her so thoroughly? Do you find her lacking in beauty or grace? Is her proficiency in Art not up to the mark?

**Kovalan:** Tell her from me that her beauty and accomplishments are of such a high order that the genius is yet to be born who can be her helpmeet!

*The chime of anklets is heard and Kannagi comes to the doorway.*

**Kannagi:** I came to see what was detaining you for such a long time.

## Publication text

### 32 Virtue Mowed down

The Chief Goldsmith, the officer and a group of guardsmen arrive at the hall where Kovalan is waiting.

Kovalan: Master-Goldsmith, was your mission successful?

Chief Goldsmith: Of course. But your mission, alas, has failed.

Kovalan [his face has fallen]: So the king does not need the anklet?

Chief Goldsmith [flippantly]: Why should the king want an anklet at all? It is the queen who requires it!

Kovalan [confused]: Master-Goldsmith . . .

Chief Goldsmith [interrupting]: Hand over the anklet to him [pointing at the officer].

Kovalan does so.

Officer [interrogating Kovalan]: How did you come by this anklet?

Kovalan: It is my wife’s.

Officer: What is the reason for your selling it?

Kovalan: The buyer does not require that information.

Officer [flushing]: A competent thief indeed!

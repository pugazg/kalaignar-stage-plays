# Tale of the Anklet — Section 6 continued

firm. *[Humorously]* All that is grey is not old, Kannagi, and our friend here has the heart of youth.

**Greek gentleman [in mock anger]:** How dare you call me old, Kovalan? Let me appeal to the comely judge here; tell me, damsel, am I an old man?

**Kannagi [prankish]:** Not at all, *thatha*¹!

**Greek gentleman:** That is right, bright girl! Kovalan, what does ‘thatha’ mean in your language?

**Kovalan:** It is the equivalent of Apollo in Greek, sir!

**Greek gentleman [delighted]:** See! She is as wise as she is beautiful!

**Kovalan [explaining]:** Kannagi, he is learning Tamil only from me.

**Kannagi:** So, he would have by now learnt two of the three sections in the Tamil language, prose and melody.

**Greek gentleman:** But the theatre art of Tamilnad is still a sealed book to me.

**Kannagi [to Kovalan]:** My Lord, today Madhavi is giving a dance programme in the Hall of Pearls. Why not we take our friend there to witness the performance?

*Kovalan agrees and he and Kannagi go home accompanied by the guest.*

---

¹ *thatha = grandfather*

# 20 Penitent Return

Kovalan’s house. Almost demented with grief, Kannagi stands near the casement and then walks slowly to the front-door. She opens the door aimlessly and a rooster enters as if it had been waiting for the opportunity. She does not have the energy or the interest to chase it away. Apparently surprised at the absence of objection to its entry, the rooster moves forward and pauses, surveying the terrain.

Lost in mournful thoughts, Kannagi does not at first notice that someone is standing at the doorway. When she does, she is not sure whether it is not the apparition of Kovalan come to mock her futile devotion.

She stands gazing at him, in disbelief. Kovalan is all contrition and he waits in the agony of suspense for her reaction. Presently Kannagi falls at and clings to his feet. He raises her and both stand united in the cyclone of sorrow unchecked.

Kovalan: Forgive this hardened sinner, Kannagi, he who left the spring-shade of your foliage for the mirage of glamour.

Kannagi [restraining him]: No one should revile my Lord and Master, not even he himself!

Kovalan: You are the gem of womanhood, Kannagi, and you are the embodiment of all that is the noblest and the best in Tamil culture. Deserting you I was lost to the world and my existence was circumscribed by the tinsel charms of an enchantress.

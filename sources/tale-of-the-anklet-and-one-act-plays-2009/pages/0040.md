# 3 Celebration Takes a Turn

*Royal Court. King Neduncheraladhan and his queen Narchonai are on the throne. A brisk and intricate folk-dance is in progress in the hall. The dancers carry an artistic bow and arrow each and as Senguttuvan and Ilango enter, the dancers set up a waving arch with their bows. The two princes walk up to the throne, pay obeisance to their royal parents and take their seats on either side of the throne. All the while the dance continues and when it concludes, Neduncheraladhan speaks.*

**Neduncheraladhan:** Beloved courtiers, my heart is brimming with pride and joy as I convey my blessings to Senguttuvan on his birthday. Senguttuvan has the material in him to consolidate the prosperity of our land and expand the frontiers of its renown. My younger son Ilango is his able lieutenant and companion and hence I am doubly proud and happy. On your behalf, on behalf of the Queen and on my own behalf, I wish many happy returns of the day to Senguttuvan and I hereby proclaim that in the very near future he shall ascend the throne and take charge of the sceptre from me.

**Courtiers [applauding]:** Long live Senguttuvan! Long live Senguttuvan!

*As the cheers taper off, the astrologer rises with an ominous glint in his eyes.*

**Astrologer [in a loud voice]:** Long live Prince Senguttuvan! And long live Ilango, the future king of our land!

**Neduncheraladhan [puzzled]:** What is the meaning of this, astrologer?

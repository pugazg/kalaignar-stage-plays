# 9 Parting Sparks Separation

*Bedchamber in Kovalan’s house. Two oil-lamps are burning steadily. Kannagi garlands Kovalan and touches his feet with her hands. Kovalan raises her and as she looks into his eyes, her face reflects a trace of sorrow.*

**Kannagi:** Are you holding back anything from me, my Lord?

**Kovalan [taken aback by her perspicacity]:** I cannot, my dear, even if I want to. I was only thinking how to break the news to you, that I have to proceed on a brief trip to Rome in the near future. [*Talking fast, to forestall her objections*] I would have sent someone else in my place, Kannagi, but the cargo is very valuable and the responsibilities of shipping it are heavy. Fifty thousand pearls have to be delivered at the palace of Count Augustus and I have to be personally in charge of the transaction from start to finish. Allow but this one trip, dear, for the business means so much to us in material terms, not to mention the fact that it would add to the standing of Tamilnad in the field of international trade.

**Kannagi [in tearful resignation]:** As you are on the high seas with the precious cargo, will you remember the lamp flickering here, my Lord?

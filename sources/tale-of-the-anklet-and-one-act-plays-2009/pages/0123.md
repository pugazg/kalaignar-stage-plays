# Scan 123

Printed page: 89

## Publication text

Nedunchezhian: Kannagi, the anklet in your husband’s hand was my wife’s.

Kannagi: It was not. The anklet in my husband’s hand was mine and mine alone.

Nedunchezhian: I do not believe you.

Kannagi: Who are you to believe me, and did anyone ask you to do so? I have come to this Court with a case against you and since you are the accused, you have no manner of title or right to sit in judgement. The noble Elders who have formed the Assembly here, they shall be judges now and let them give the correct verdict. (addressing the House).

Revered Gentlemen
of upright conduct,
I address my plea
to each conscience.
Take the oath
on your mother dear,
on Tamil pure
and on the people of the land.
Swerve not
from the path of Justice
out of fear
of the might of the King,
this Pandyan here
who put to rout
the Aryan hordes.
And draw not
the curtain of his fame
over the guilt
I charge him with.

The allegation, gentlemen, was that my husband Kovalan had stolen the anklet of Queen Kopperundevi and the sentence, of death, has already been pronounced and carried out.

My case is that it was a hasty verdict, delivered without inquiry or investigation or trial; and I hold Nedunchezhian here responsible for it.

Seniormost minister: Are you submitting any evidence in support of your contention?

### 33 “My Husband a Thief ?”

Madhari’s hamlet. The concluding sequence in the folk dance is a tableau on grief. Kannagi, in the front row of the audience, is lost in the eloquence of the theme.

Madhari rushes in, like a storm.

Madhari (crying out): Kannagi! Kannagi!

Kannagi (shocked out of her meditations): Ah...

Madhari (wailing): They have branded Kovalan a thief and killed him too, my child!

Kannagi swoons.

* * *

Madhari’s dwelling. Kannagi opens her eyes and sits up, without being conscious of her own movements. Scalding tears from her eyes fall to the ground but she is not conscious of that either.

She looks around but does not recognize Madhari or Ayyai or others. She looks beyond and keeps staring at the image of the recent past, of Kovalan taking leave of her, to go to the city with her anklet in his hand....

Kovalan’s voice: Shall I start for the city, Kannagi?

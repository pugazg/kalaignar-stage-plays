# 10 Madhavi Conquers — continued

Kovalan [overwhelmed]: Madhavi!

Madhavi: I seek not gold, my Lord, nor the other riches of the world. The patronage of monarchs holds no attraction for me. All I ask is that you should be by my side for, apart from you, I have no life nor has my Art any meaning.

Kovalan: Madhavi!

Madhavi: Let me be the vine, my Lord, clinging to you the staff of my life. Let me be a bond-slave, my Lord, tip-toeing in your footsteps. Promise me a spark, my Lord, and I shall ask nothing more.

Kovalan: Madhavi!

Madhavi [her voice tapering off]: I know why you are withholding your grace. Is it not because you judge me by my clan and suspect that I too am trained in the craft of seduction?

Kovalan [mortified]: No, no, dear, you do me an injustice!

Madhavi [in exultation]: So my God has relented at last!

Kovalan [holding her close to his heart and whispering in her ear]: Madhavi, my beloved!

Drinking in the nectar of his love, Madhavi presently sleeps, with her head on his lap. Kovalan gazes in rapture at the childlike innocence mirrored by her face.

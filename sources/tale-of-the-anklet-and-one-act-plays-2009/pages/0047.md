# 6 Beauteous Pride of Poompuhar

*Poompuhar unfolds itself in all its opulence and majesty as Sathanar speaks.*

**Sathanar:** Poompuhar the City Beautiful on the banks of the River Cauveri where it meets and merges with the billowing sea! It is the capital city of the Chola kingdom. The Indra Festival is celebrated there annually in all pomp and pageantry and it is in honour of the memory of an ancestor of the Cholas called Indra. Poompuhar is a city of added attractions during the Festival and foreign tradesmen too join in the celebrations. Fine arts claim pride of place in the agenda; and music and dance programmes are arranged in all the three districts of the city, namely, Pattinappakkam, Maruvoorppakkam and Nalangadi.

Here is the Grand Arch which the King of Avanti constructed as a gift to Karikaal Chola....

This is the Auditorium of Pearls, a present from the Monarch of Vajra...

The Pavilion before us is a tribute from the Ruler of Maghada....

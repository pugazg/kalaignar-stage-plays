# 12 Kovalan in Newfound Joy — continued

Kovalan: My dear, when I seek to describe you and chant your praise, it is the Tamil language that gets enriched.

Madhavi: And likewise, when I garland you, may the flowers be flattered!

This time there is no resistance to her garlanding. Her conquest is complete.

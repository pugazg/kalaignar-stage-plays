Kannagi: Yes, the anklet I hold here in my hand. It was the twin of this anklet that my husband brought to the city for sale and some evil men murdered him and abstracted the ornament, at the behest of the accused here on the throne.

Nedunchezhian: Then, what of the anklets worn now, by the Queen?

Kannagi: Of the pair she is wearing now, one anklet is mine without doubt. O Pandya King who have defiled this court of law, a thousand questions you are aching to ask, in order to wash the blood clean from your hand! Had you but shown a fraction of this care to cross-examine my husband, would you not have retained your title for evenhanded justice? And would not my husband be still in the land of the living?

Seniormost minister: Are you in a position to prove that one of the anklets worn by the queen now belongs to you?

Kannagi: Is there any use, my proving it? Would you be able to fit the severed head of my husband on to his shoulders again and would you be able to bring him back to life? You cannot, I know. Hear me further. My husband’s head fell, only on his death; but your heads shall hang down even while you are alive, and that I shall see to, by making you realize that you are deprived of your honour, that you have taken leave of righteousness and that your justice is in tatters. Now, answer this question, O Pandya of Mathurai! What form the contents of your wife’s anklets – pearls or rubies?

Nedunchezhian: Pearls, from Korkai.

Kannagi: My anklets contain rubies.

Nedunchezhian: Here, let me see... (he turns to the queen who unfastens her anklets and hands them to him. He dashes one of the anklets against the floor. The anklet breaks, to scatter pearls.)

Nedunchezhian (holding high the second of the anklets): This was the anklet stolen by Kovalan.

As before, he breaks the anklet. Rubies, red as blood-drops, roll on the floor.

Nedunchezhian (dazed): Ah...

The courtiers too are in consternation.

Kannagi: Are you still in doubt? Here is my second anklet.

In a fury of vengeance, she hurls the anklet on the floor with all the strength at her command. There is a shower of rubies.

Kannagi: What else have you to say, Pandya? Ministers of the Council and Elders of this Court, what, is there no answer to this from any of you?

## Archival status

This is a **published English secondary witness**, kept separate from the independently produced English translation under `works/silappathikaram-nataka-kappiyam/translations/en/`.

It must never silently override the controlling Tamil scan or the verified Tamil archival text.

## Transcription method

- one Markdown record per physical scan page under `pages/`;
- printed text transcribed as printed, including spelling, punctuation, capitalization and visible page numbers;
- library labels, stamps, handwritten accession marks, barcodes and other copy-specific marks are recorded separately from publication text;
- photographs, logos and decorative artwork are described rather than converted into invented literary text;
- uncertain readings remain explicitly marked rather than guessed;
- no modernization or editorial correction is introduced silently.

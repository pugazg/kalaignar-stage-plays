## Review method

Every physical scan must be visually compared against its repository page record. Parsed/OCR text is assistance only; source pixels control. Check printed wording, spelling, punctuation, capitalization, speaker labels, stage directions, headings, page numbers, quotations, repetitions, line/paragraph boundaries where meaningful, blank pages, and copy-specific stamps/handwriting. Never silently modernize or repair the published text.

Review outcomes per scan: `passed`, `corrected-and-passed`, or `unresolved` with an explicit source obstruction/ambiguity note.

# Scan 137

Printed page: 103

## Publication text

**Raja:** Son of Akbar! Lord of the world!

**Anar:** Ah!

**Raja:** Saleem! Saleem! Saleem!

**Anar:** Saleem! Saleem! Saleem! No, no. He is not Saleem. Not the son of the Emperor! A mere sepoy! He’s only a sepoy! He who has endeared my heart will not tell me lies!

**Raja:** Do not pretend! I know very well that a wandering dancer *(geisha girl)* will not cast an enticing net to capture an ordinary soldier! Enamoured of the king’s court, regal bedchamber, royal procession through the streets of the kingdom - in the hope of acquiring all these things for a glance. Only with this end in view did you aim at enthralling the son of the crowned monarch!

**Anar:** Enough, stop it! Don’t sting like a thousand scorpions at my heart which is already ablaze! Anarkali showed love only to a sepoy, and not to the sceptred son !

**Raja:** Still feigning, Anarkali!

**Anar:** It is not me who dissembled! It is the son of your crowned monarch! Knowing that I do not respect the enthroned, Prince Saleem has told me such a big lie!

**Raja:** Let bygones be bygones; let what is yet to happen be good. Look here, Anarkali! I’ll give you one lakh gold coins. Take that and get away from here to some place. Let the prince forget you!

**Anar:** If I loved him for gold I’d go now! Why talk of lakhs, there are a number of rich men who are ready waiting to pour money in crores at my feet. Be it they, or you - or the gold you offer me – All these are like sand to me – all these equal sand.

*(She picks a handful of sand and shows to him)*

**Raja:** *(quietly)* Think over it a little in quietude! Then give your reply! You showered your love on him thinking that he was a mere soldier! Once you knew that he belonged to the ruling class you ought to have abandoned his love in consonance with your principles! Young woman! Were I in your place I would certainly choose to forget Saleem!

**Anar:** You would forget – because – you are not Anarkali!

**Raja:** All right ! What do you say as your final decision!

**Anar:** You have no right to intercept my opinions!

**Raja:** What?

**Anar:** Anar’s ‘ liberty to love ’ will not yield to Delhi imperialism!

**Raja:** Let me see its ending too.

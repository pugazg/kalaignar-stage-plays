## Scene – 3

### Anarkali’s house

*(Anar is weeping – Saleem’s sweet voice calling her ‘Anar’ is heard. She is startled, and springs up like a snake trod upon.)*

**Anar:** Welcome, welcome prince who has made a maid’s heart languish!

**Saleem:** Anar! You are cross with me because I did not come here at the appointed time?

**Anar:** Does time matter to men of stately court? Or even the thought . . . ?

**Saleem:** Man of majesty! Oh! How wrathful! Anar! In your anger you tend to heighten my status! . . . . Do you make fun of me, a poor soldier?

**Anar:** I know! The warrior who talks like the breeze ! I know you are not a warrior but the son of Emperor Badsha!

**Saleem:** Ah!

**Anar:** I have understood! The cloud of smoke you raised has cleared! Truth shines like the rising sun!

**Saleem:** Anarkali! No . . . No . . . .

**Anar:** *(bursting into tears in anger)* Yes. That’s it. You are not Saleem! An ordinary sepoy! You are not zari (disco) flower! Ordinary yellow *samanti* flower! You are not the flamboyant silver flower of the royal palace! Fragrant jasmine flower! The thief of my heart! You are not the brilliant gold flower of the court! But the pleasant smelling rose dripping dew drops! Not the young prince! But the prince of my heart! Isn’t it so Saleem? This is how that I must dupe myself!

**Saleem:** Anar! Please listen to me!

**Anar:** I have listened several times! Listened umpteen number of times to your declamatory false declaration “I am a soldier going to the warfront carrying a lance and a shield. Then, I was not aware of the great dimension of differences in status. Had I known it then this lotus in the little pond would not have fondly blossomed for the sun in the sky! You the son of the king carrying the flag imprinted with a crescent! The future protector of Delhi imperialism! Why do you stare like that?

The honeyed feast tasted behind the screen! Love ambrosia that should be thrown out after getting cloyed! Are you worried that before fulfilment of the need it stands interrupted? You the son of the Badsha of armed forces! Enough you have baited me with the gait of a warrior! Please go away!

**Saleem:** Anar! I never thought of cheating you! I declared myself a soldier fearing that the truth that I am royal by birth might frighten you.

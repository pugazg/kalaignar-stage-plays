#### Scene – 4

**Kuyilaluvam**

(In the battle fought against Kanakan and Vicayan, Senguttuvan emerges victorious)

Cheran: Villava! What is the name of the place where drums of victory sound?

Villavan: Kuyilaluvam.

Cheran: Kuyilaluvam . Note it down. All these rocks are pages in history marked with the valour of the Tamils! Victory alone is not enough, Villava! We should not leave the defeated just like that.

Villavan: Kanakan and Vicayan are reported to have escaped disguised in saffron, like rishis!

Cheran: Ha. . .ha. . .! That is nothing new. . . .That is the only characteristic trait of them!

(Kanakan and Vicayan are brought on the stage dressed in saffron)

Cheran: Who are these?

Villavan: They are Kanakan and Vicayan.

Cheran: Kanakan and Vicayan! Kanakan and Vicayan! Ha. . .ha. . ha . . .! You the bravest of the brave! Very good is your war strategy! To retreat showing your back . . . hiding yourselves in clothes . . . ambushing and attacking. . . .disguising as sadhus. Ha. . .ha. . ha . . .!

Disguised as rishis! Answer my question! How many days did it take for the war between Devas and Asuras to end?

# Scan 145

Printed page: 111

## Publication text

He that vowed on the marriage platform

Decked with mango-leaf festoons, “We

Are united for life, now bears the lance

His chest and has fallen dead!

After the soul left the body lived

The lady – like a moulded doll –

Wailed aloud – The family was

Like a bunch of flowers!

She cursed the merciless Death!

Decried death! What remains yet?

The fruit has grown rotten, she yelled.

Eyes brighter than snow, speech

Sweeter than palmyra candy

Ditties of love through nuptial nights –

Are all gone? Turned to dust?

Henceforth these are sleepless eyes!

Bemoaned! And prayed in the direction

Of her fallen lord!

No sooner did the victors’ drumbeats fall

on her ears, though she’s in a swoon, than

she sprang up and said, “ What was lost is lost.

Let my entire family be sacrificed! Will I

Live for my own home? Rather, I will die

For my glorious country.” –

The Tamil woman, capable of telling time

by looking at the sinking slotted platter,

suddenly remembered her son, lovingly reared

in a cradle, now sent to learn from the teacher

seated under the banyan tree!

She went there – the boy sprang to her calling

“ Mother” lisping the word in sweetness!

“Has the dad or grandpa returned home?” said he.

### 2 Cheran Senguttuvan

Before the play begins a voice is heard. It is a golden leaf from the epic written by Elango, brother to King Cheran Senguttuvan.

It is the story about bringing a stone from northern India to carve the statue of Kannaki to be consecrated in Tamil Nadu!

Heroic lines that depict the golden glory of the historic past of Tamil Nadu.

A day in the distant past! When we were seated on the throne, ruling over the country with infantry, cavalry, and chariot divisions!

Then in the court of rulers known as Kanakan and Vicayan in the north . . . .

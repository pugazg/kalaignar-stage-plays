# Scan 149

Printed page: 115

## Publication text

Kanakan: Eighteen years!

Cheran: The war between Rama and Ravana . . . ?

Kanakan: Eighteen months!

Cheran: the War in the Mahabharata . . . ?

Kanakan: Eighteen days!

Cheran: Warriors, remember this. Cheran Senkuttuvan’s war fought in the north lasted only eighteen ‘nali’ [1 nali equals 24 minutes].

Kanakan: O Lord ! Forgive us, we surrender to you!

Cheran: You mean ‘Forgive’? You come to Tamil Nadu and go down on your knees. Let her forgive you!

Villavan: My lord, should we not return to Tamil Nadu? It is already thirty-two months since we started!

Cheran: Yes, let us set out . . .Villava, before that we are to choose a rock fit for sculpting Kannaki statue, aren’t we?

Villavan: Yes.

Cheran: We have now got the right bullocks to carry the stone. Kanakan and Vicayan – you hurled a word at the Tamils--- now carry the stone on your heads!

# Scan 144

Printed page: 110

## Publication text

‘I’ll pound the foes to a hundred pieces!

Without saving nation, homeward I won’t be bound!

But the young lady

With a smiling face said she,

“March forward, my lord!”

Maybe I’ll come back or not

So turn round I will kiss you now”

Gave her – then he shot arrows

At the foes!

War! War! War!

Thus sounded the drums!

Look! Look! Look at her man

Sweep with valour through the army band!

It’s how his friends of the fighting class

Acclaimed with applause!

The beautiful lady smiled through her eyes

The more she heard of her man’s feat and

Increasing accolade on the battlefield!

Forts were retrieved! Enemy horses’ legs broken!

Foes with Tiger flags defeated! Lances broken!

The distant drums of our victor king

Are heard like summer’s thundering!

She jumped for joy! Sang to new tunes!

Soldiers came –said “Success is your man’s”

King’s messengers came – paid their homage-

Neighbouring women-friends came –

Delighted to read aloud citations! The dame

Shed joyful tears! - Why then

Came the man to break heart-rending news?

“My dear young lady!

Do not shed tears!

Latest news from warfront, listen!

Alas!

My dear Athenians! Now Socrates is going to die quite happily! The Grecian Court dumped on him baskets full of charges, and also ordained him to drink a cup brimming with poison which is meant to drink his life! The lips that disseminated his thoughts are going to kiss the brim of the cup containing poison! Just a couple of minutes more! Then the bond between the body and the soul would snap, and put a full stop to the serial called life! Farewell Greece!...... Farewell! My dear Athens, farewell! The broad and beautiful streets of Athens, which helped me to run round in joy and deliver my emotional outbursts, Farewell! Shades of trees along the streets! Platforms in the front of the houses! This aged man takes leave of all of you! Proud young men that walk upright! Young brothers and protectors of our nation! Dearest friends! Old men and mothers! The cubs of the country! May the last tender respects Socrates tenders all be yours! If there be any who truly and whole-heartedly still believes and contends that this old man corrupted the Greek youth, let them forgive me! Let them forgive me! Let me go. Bye! Fail not to think! Eh, world! Fail not to think! Know yourself! Know yourself!

(Socrates gulps down the poison; walks till his legs become unsteady; then lies down. The body becomes chill! The guard pulls the cloth over his face. A few seconds later, Socrates lifts the cloth from his face)

Socrates: Crito! I forgot to tell you one thing.... I owe a cock to Asclepius; will you remember to pay the debt? Forget not to return it to him.

(With that the great man of Greece breathed his last. Crito cried. Some time later, Greece bathed his grave with tears)

# Scan 139

Printed page: 105

## Publication text

**Anar:** Not that I would fear, but that I might reject! I might not yield! Saleem Badsha has done a feat even a commoner would not dare do! The reason is that he has so much love for Anarkali! It is like the tiger that has grown fond of a deer which appeared before the tiger which is disguised as a goat, with a view to nurturing love and friendship! Your story is similar, isn’t it, Badsha?

**Saleem:** Anarkali! Enough. Stop it! Saleem is neither a tiger, nor did he dissemble! But any amount of my argument is not going to convince you; nor are you going to believe me. The tall walls of the fortress prevent you from hearing the soft melodies of immaculate love that spring from my heart! The curtain that ‘ I am the son of Akbar’ interrupts the view of my heart where stands painted the picture of my love queen Anarkali! So my sighs and my arguments won’t reach your ears! I shall demonstrate right now that Saleem desires to die and lie like a corpse at the rosy feet of Anarkali ! Look here Anar! Look here!

*(Saleem draws his sword and attempts to take his life)*

**Anar:** *(Rushes forward to him)* Saleem! Forgive me!

**Saleem:** Anar! *(They embrace each other)*

*(Soldiers enter)*

**Soldier:** Emperor Akbar commands that you two be arrested!

*(Raja Mann Singh stands at a distance)*

**Saleem:** Ah! . . . Raja Mann Singh.

**Raja:** *(standing at a distance)*

Yes. That’s how Akbar’s court decrees.

*(Saleem and Anarkali are taken away)*

*(A voice from behind the curtain):*

Saleem and Anarkali are accused. Akbar Badsha orders the execution of both – Raja Mann Singh persuaded the king to pardon Saleem; lest the lineage of the king should get broken, and secured Saleem’s release! It was further ordered that Anarkali should be entombed alive! Hearing the news Saleem rushed to save his lady love! What awaited him when he reached there? Alas! Before he could reach, Anarkali’s tomb had been sealed.

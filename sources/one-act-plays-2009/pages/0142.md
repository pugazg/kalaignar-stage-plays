# Scan 142

Printed page: 108

## Publication text

A king: Well, now that both of you have come together! If one of you were chosen how would you both then share one woman?

Tamil Poet: It would then be another Mahabharatham.

Vicayan: Who are you, man? What do you speak?

Tamil Poet: Tamil! Pure Tamil! It was born with the world! Born at a time before stone was formed and sand was formed! Tamil is older!

Vicayan: What business have you at the elective wedding hall?

Tamil Poet: Hm! Strange question, King! Strange to say that only old people like you have business at the swayamvar hall and young men like us do not have any reason to be here!

Vicayan: No need to fool! Say who you are!

Tamil Poet: I am a Tamil poet – come on a pilgrimage to north India.

Kanakan: A poet? Do you know how to compose poems?

Tamil Poet: Tamil Nadu has not yet come across a poet who could not write poems – nor a soldier who could not wield a sword.

Kanakan: Let us see. Come on, tell us a poem!

Tamil Poet:

Fought on the banks of the Ganges – The Tamils

Brought fame to the banks of the Cauvery;

Ruled over the rolling waves of the seas – The Tamils

Hailed by the world as the greatest!

Kanakan: Stop! Stop!

Vicayan: Stop that nonsense!

Kanakan: Crazy fellow! The Tamils battled on the banks of the Ganges! They would indeed fight! Had they come to fight when Kanakan and Vicayan are in power we would have broken their legs! Be careful!

Kings: Ha, ha, ha!!!

Vicayan: What Kanakan said deserves a reward of a thousand lakh! When your kings imprinted their national emblems of the tiger, the bow and the fish – perhaps there were no kings like us!

Tamil Poet: Why? To fall prostrate at your feet and pay homage? You that have bats in your belfry; listen! I will return to the south today itself. I shall report to King Cheran of the undiplomatic talk of loose-tongued Kanakan and Vicayan at the feast, in the presence of other kings, denigrating the Tamil language, exposing their ignorance of the rich language. You haughty and headstrong people! Tamil valour has not died yet! We will meet again! The ruling monarch is Cheran Senguttuvan! Cheran Senguttuvan!!

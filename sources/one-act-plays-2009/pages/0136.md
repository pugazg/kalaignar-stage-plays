## Scene – 2

### Anarkali’s house

*(Forgetful of the world, Anarkali keeps moving towards her house in great joy. As she enters, she is startled to find Raja Mann Singh standing there.)*

**Anar:** Who is it?

**Raja:** Raja Mann Singh!

**Anar:** Raja Mann Singh!

**Raja:** Yes. . . . Minister to Emperor Akbar; Administrator; General of the Armed Forces.

**Anar:** *(cautiously, yet mischievously)* Brother-in-law to Akbar! I know, my Lord! The cottage of this poor maid is greatly graced by your visit.

**Raja:** Royal dancer! You say your cottage is honoured by my visit. I say that the court itself should be honoured by your visit.

**Anar:** I am after all a dancer ! What greatness lies with me that could bring pride to the nation?

**Raja:** At the king’s court. . . .

**Anar:** You mean to say that the country becomes reputed because of my dance, don’t you?

**Raja:** No. . . . You must dance only at the king’s court.

**Anar:** What?

**Raja:** You should not dance in the heart of Prince Saleem!

**Anar:** What ? Saleem! He has a heart ! And there . . . my dance?

**Raja:** And a garden to project it.

**Anar:** What do you mean ?

**Raja:** Why do you hush up, Anarkali? . . . . I rushed here only after I witnessed you both enjoy singing like a pair of pigeons in the garden!

**Anar:** All right! Has there been any proclamation in Akbar Badsha’s regime that to love is a grievous wrong. . . ?

**Raja:** Not made yet. . . however. . . . There is reason to deliberate on those lines, considering the status of such lovers!

**Anar:** Status for love! Should not a dancing girl and a patriotic warrior fall in love?

**Raja:** He is not a soldier, but a ruling prince.

**Anar:** Ah!

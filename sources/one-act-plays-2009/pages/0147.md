#### Scene – 3

**King Cheran’s Court**

Cheran: Tigers of Tamil Nadu, holding lances and upholding country’s fame! Here’s a call for all of you! Here is yet another good opportunity to prove and establish our valour in the north! It is said that rulers of north India cast aspersions on the ancient and historic imprints we have made on the Himalayan mountains! Simpletons are slandering! Those who have never set their feet upon warfields are scoffing at the Tiger! The worthless fellow has inveighed against our Bow and Arrow. He who has not heard of our exploits laughs at our Fish. Uttiran – Vicittiran – Chittiran – Swethan – Bairavan – Don’t blink like that! These are names of humans! To all these frog-headed kings, Kanakan and Vicayan are the heads! The rulers of the Gangetic plains throw a challenge to the Cauveryites! Friends! Let’s teach them a lesson – let’s start!

A Minister: Why do you get so angered, Lord, when Kings Chola and Pandiya remain unperturbed?

Cheran: Because I’m a Tamil – and because my self-respect has not diminished. Chola and Pandiya might be my enemies; for that reason I cannot, till my death, permit north Indian rulers to tease us! They have disparaged my Tamil language! Ah, the author of Thirukkural, the north Indians have insulted you! You are despised, Satthan, who have explicated the meanings of poetry! The ocean that abounds in pearls, the gardens that yield jasmine, you are despised! Oh the seat of the Lion where drums are beaten on all eight directions, you are despised! My mother Narconai, the conqueror of the Himalayas Senguttuvan, son of Netunceralatan, you are despised! You great poet Ilanko, you are despised! My loving brother! Pandiya who sacrificed his life for justice, you are despised! Nedunceliyan, who defeated the Aryans, you are despised! The rightful leonine inheritors of Classical Tamil, you are despised! Scholars!

## Publication text

# 1 Anarkali

## Scene – 1

### Garden

*(A love duet by Anarkali and Saleem. From a distance observes Raja Mann Singh.)*

**Saleem:** Anarkali! I look for a means of not parting from you even for a moment. I don’t find it!

**Anar:** You are a warrior! What, don’t you know? Only when permanent peace has come to stay in the world forever, and when conditions so improve that white doves, instead of vultures, start hovering around can there be uninterrupted love between lovers in the homes of warriors. Isn’t it so, my darling?

**Saleem:** Anarkali! Night takes leave without telling us; it kills us without truly killing. My love! . . . Shall I take leave?

**Anar:** Yes, duty calls you back!

**Saleem:** Your magnetic eyes prevent me. I feel that too. But . . . .

**Anar:** It is courtesy which compels my mouth to bid you farewell, while at the same time comforting my heart with the hope of meeting you tomorrow.

**Saleem:** This humble sepoy (soldier) should not be a cause for a trifling error to happen in the reign of Akbar, my dear!

**Anar:** (embracing) Dear mine!

**Saleem:** See you later, my dear. . . ! *(exeunt)*

*(Raja Mann Singh leaves the place with a resolution in his mind)*

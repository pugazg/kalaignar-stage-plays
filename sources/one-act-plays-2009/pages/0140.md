Did the stone-hearted men bury you in a heap of stones, not willing to destroy the fullness of your smiling face by setting fire to it? My love! Anarkali! Anarkali!

Sinners! You have murdered Anarkali! You have put out the flame of my heart! Broken the divine Veena! Buried the deathless heroine of the epic of love in a heap of stones! Hidden her eternal beauty! But, you silly fools, none of you have the courage to pluck her from Saleem’s heart! None has the skill!

It is said that she is a nomad! And I am born to rule over the country! People in all the eight directions would obey the intent of the movement of even my little finger! Different armies I have! And I have the capacity to cross many a hurdle! A palace as big as a town! A huge fortress! Servants, run-errands, weaponry, army, all I have in abundance! Many are there at my beck and call! Should I need pleasant breeze? And there is a garden! Should I swim? There is a stream! Many to bewail if my feet get hurt while walking on a path covered with silk! A thousand people are there to ponder whether it is better to sprinkle my bed with jasmines – mullai or malli?

What though I have all these? What of all these joys? To me who have no right to claim my love who has filled my heart! To me who have not the liberty to live with the one I loved? What if I had all these or not? Why this palace? Why this luxury? Why this royal life? Why a thousand to run errands at my command? Why all these? Why? Why . . . ? Anarkali! Why all these to one who could not live with you? Why this kingdom to a slavish prince? In a world without you why should I live at all?

Akbar’s son should not fall in love with a dancing girl! Oh tomb that encloses my most beautiful love, tell me! You have devoured her because she did not have royal status. Akbar Badsha, a Muslim can marry a Rajput lady Jyothi Bai. But the son of that Rajput woman should not love a dancing girl! Oh, tomb that has swallowed my love, tell me! Is the judgment proper! You won’t answer! You know only to devour life! Come on, do it now. You are used to doing it!

*(Saleem prepares to die. – – but his country did not need his death)*

### 3 Socrates

- Socrates was the greatest philosopher Greece gave. The largest treasure house of wisdom Athens delivered.
- His product, Plato, remains to this day the father of many political ideologies.
- Communist ideology evolved after long cogitation by Lenin and Stalin, the products of Marx and Engels evolved from the intellectual smithy of Plato.
- Hegel, a German philosopher, used Plato’s smithy and signalled the emergence of his faith in the stale Nazism. Mean-minded Hitler and Mussolini turned it to their advantage and took the axe to behead people.
- This is how Plato has been responsible in supplying ingredients for good as well as bad deeds; that Plato was the product of Socrates!........
- As history records it, Socrates was not only ‘a man unafraid of death’ but also the author of certain misdeeds!
- Some might argue that some of his ideas are contrary to our own ideas. But the world of rationality is not willing to throw aside the genius on account of these trivial reasons.
- A great thinker like Rajaji in his book entitled ‘Cokkiratar’ has presented Socrates like another Sankaracharya steeped in orthodoxy.
- But I have attempted in this short play to present Socrates as an intellectual who enjoyed his life.
- Prof. Sabin wonders how such a terrible punishment could have been meted out to a great thinker like Socrates in Athens, which is accepted as the cradle of democracy and civilization. He even suspects there could have been some secret plotting behind the curtain for the death of Socrates.

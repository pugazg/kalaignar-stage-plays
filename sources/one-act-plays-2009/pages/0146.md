## Publication text

“Yes, returned, and went away to the land of Death!

Come along my dearest. . . . !” said she.

“It’s a matter of honour, honour, deathless honour!

My son, come along to taste it,” – said she.

He came forward, the tender shoot of that family,

The light of home, with brightened face; said she,

“Because of enemy’s invasion – our family

stands now denuded, my boy!

No worries ---

They went to the warfront and died.

But they earned the name that “They saved our honour!”

And it will remain till the end of the world.

My son! You must also fight the enemy

As long as there’s strength in your body.

Sing the war-song! This is your Motherland –

Take sword and advance!” Saying thus

She hugged the little boy, and combed his hair,

Adorned him, handed him a sword turned

Brown with the stain of blood! And cried,

“Go you my son marching to the battlefield

And come back victoriously!” She blessed him.

Is there any in the world who doesn’t glorify

This wonderful scene, Dear mother! . . .

Poet: (interposing) Yes! Yes ! There is.

Cheran: What?

Poet: Such thick-skinned fat souls are there! There are people who slight and slander our valour!

Cheran: Ah!

Poet: Please do not be shocked at my act of insolence, intruding into your privacy when you and the queen are together. I am going to tell you something more shocking.

Cheran: Poet, what do you mean?

Poet: Yes, my lord! Our valour has been slandered by the weeny brained!

Cheran: Are there slanderers around?

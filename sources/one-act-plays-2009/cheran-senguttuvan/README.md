The independently translated `சேரன் செங்குட்டுவன்` was completed and Tamil→English reviewed before this witness was opened for comparison.

Durable comparison:

- `../../../works/cheran-senguttuvan/translations/en/SECONDARY_WITNESS_COMPARISON.md` — **PASS / COMPLETE**.

The comparison covers the pre-play framing and all four scenes. It records broad structural agreement alongside published-witness normalization, explanatory additions, poetic rewriting, compression and material divergences.

The independent English was drafted/reviewed only from verified Tamil; M. D. Jayabalan's 2009 published-English witness was opened only afterward as a separate comparison layer.

The Socrates witness comparison records broad structural agreement but a freer published style and several material divergences, including intro `பல தகுதிகளுக்கும்` → `certain misdeeds`, `அறிவுலக ஜோதியாக` being replaced by `an intellectual who enjoyed his life`, normalization of protected Scene-4/5 forms, a published `281...221` variant, conversion of `நாழிகை` into minutes, and final `அங்கிளிப்பியசு` / `கோழிக் குஞ்சு` being replaced by the familiar `Asclepius / cock` formulation. No verified Tamil or independent English wording was changed by the comparison.

The 2009 published-English one-act-play source remains a **secondary witness**. It must not reconstruct Tamil, and its wording must not silently control independent translations.

The exact next activity is the **independent English translation of `சேரன் செங்குட்டுவன்`** from its verified Tamil scene assemblies. Its complete 2009 published-English witness must remain unconsulted for wording until the independent translation and Tamil→English fidelity review are complete.

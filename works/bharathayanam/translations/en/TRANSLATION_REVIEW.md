## Source-sensitive terminology decisions

- `பரதாயணம்` → *Bharathayanam*: title retained rather than flattened into an explanatory title.
- `காலட்சேபம்` → *kalakshepam*: retained as the performance genre.
- `உற்சவ விக்கிரகம்` / `மூல விக்கிரகம்` → `festival idols` / `principal idols`: temple-image metaphor retained.
- `கன்யா சுல்கம்` / `கன்யா சுல்க` → `kanya sulkam` / `kanya sulka`: the source's Sanskritic legal/ritual register remains perceptible.
- `பராக்!` → `Parak!`: retained as a performance/heraldic cry because the source alone does not justify a more specific English expansion.
- `பாதுகை` → `sandals`: readable English used consistently while preserving their political-symbolic role in the plot.
- `அனுபவ பாத்தியதை` → `prescriptive right arising from enjoyment of rule`: keeps the property-law register behind the joke.
- source `அண்டமா முனிவரெல்லாம்` is an unusual expression. The translation uses the deliberately non-specific `all the great sages`; no etymological or historical explanation is asserted from the source.
- `மோக்ஷம்` / `மோட்சம்` / `மோசம்`: the English preserves the punchline using `moksha`, `motcham`, `mosam`, followed by semantic guidance because the Tamil near-homophony cannot be reproduced directly.
- `நமப் பகுத்தறிவுபதே!` → *Nama Pagutharivupathe!*: retained as a hybrid mock-liturgical formula; the translation note explains the rationalist substitution without replacing the source phrase.

## Source anomaly control

The verified Tamil assembly preserves scan 15's physical `நீயல்ல` / `வர்` as `நீயல்லவர்`, not the semantically expected `நீயல்லவா`.

The English therefore deliberately gives the awkward literal line:

`Those who are not you are gods!`

This is not presented as smooth English. It is an archival translation decision made to avoid silently repairing a verified source anomaly. The translation note makes the issue explicit.

The scan 15→16 physical split `பாதுகாப்` + `பாக` is correctly understood through the verified Tamil assembly as `பாதுகாப்பாக`; no spurious speaker turn appears in English.

## Interpretive separation

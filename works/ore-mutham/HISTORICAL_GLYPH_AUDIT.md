Historical Tamil type must be decoded by character identity before transcription into modern Unicode. Visual resemblance to a modern glyph is not sufficient evidence.

## Mandatory families

Every processed scan is checked for:

`ணா / ணை / ணொ / ணோ / லை / ளை / றா / றொ / றோ / னா / னை / னொ / னோ`

A family may be absent on a page; that absence does not remove the requirement to inspect the page.

## Permanent rules

1. source scan controls;
2. read character identity, not familiar modern appearance;
3. do not modernize spelling, grammar, sandhi, punctuation, labels, names or numbers;
4. no global replacement of historical shapes;
5. same-edition comparison only from positively verified examples;
6. context/dictionary/proper-name familiarity cannot override uncertain pixels;
7. unresolved source evidence remains explicit `needs-review` rather than guessed;
8. donor/library/handwritten marks are not literary text;
9. scene headings and speaker labels receive the same glyph scrutiny as body text;
10. a page is `verified` only after both visual fidelity and the glyph gate pass.

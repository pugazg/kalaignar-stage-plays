# Scene 27

Location: A palace street in Inbapuri.

(The Maharaja is on an upper floor. People are in the street.)

A Man: Do you know the verdict?

Another: What? What?

A Man: The Prince has been sentenced to death by hanging.

Another: Ah!......

(The two run off.)

(Two women.)

One: Hanging for the young prince? What injustice!

The Other: Injustice? A time of calamity has arrived......

(They run.)

(A man returning from the fields carrying a plough hears the cry, “The Prince is to be hanged,” throws down the plough and runs. A large crowd rushes along in agitation. Mahaveerar and Vibhishanan watch this.)

Vibhishanan: They are all going to the execution ground.

Mahaveerar: Why?

Vibhishanan: Some revolt may break out.

Mahaveerar: A revolt?

Vibhishanan: They may free them by force.

Mahaveerar: Ah!

Vibhishanan: Then they may enter the palace too. Maharaja! Even in this small revolt they could seize our throne.

Mahaveerar: This must be plucked out in the bud. Vibhishanan, make the necessary arrangements.

Vibhishanan: What arrangements? Show them the point of a spear and all this revolt will boil away. First I will send the troops.

Mahaveerar: Hmm. Send them.

Vibhishanan: I must follow them too.

Mahaveerar: Very well. Leave.

(Vibhishanan departs swiftly.)

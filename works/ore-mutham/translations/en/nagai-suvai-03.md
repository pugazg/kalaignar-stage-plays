# Comedy Section — Scene 3

Location: Sandhegam's house.

(A large grain bin, a chicken, a plough, a ploughshare, and stacked *viratti* implements. Holding the chicken, Samayochitham and Adhirshtam sing, to the tune of “Pura Pattu,” about escaping thanks to the chicken. While they are singing, Sandhegam knocks on the door and calls out. Samayochitham hides Adhirshtam inside the grain bin and opens the door.)

Sandhegam: Savitri!

Samayochitham: Why, Satyavan?

Sandhegam: Didn't you say we should sell this grain bin and buy a bigger one!

Samayochitham: Yes!

Sandhegam: I've settled the price for this one. The buyer is standing outside. Now we have to lift it out.

Samayochitham: Before you did, I sold it to someone else. He's inside checking whether there are any rotten places! (To the grain bin) Sir! Are you still looking?

Adhirshtam: No... I've looked. (Coming out) The grain bin is very convenient. Won't it be very difficult for you without it?

Samayochitham: Yes! (Speaking to him) Very convenient indeed! The opening is very small. Only if it were bigger could one person climb inside without difficulty.

Sandhegam: There's no difficulty getting in. I've climbed in many times to put paddy inside.

Adhirshtam: You can climb in to put paddy...

Sandhegam: Look here! See how freely one can get inside!

(He climbs in. As soon as Sandhegam is inside the grain bin, Adhirshtam embraces Samayochitham.)

Sandhegam: (From inside) See what the grain bin is like—smooth, isn't it?

Adhirshtam: (Stroking Samayochitham's cheek) Yes, like glass!

Sandhegam: (From inside) You can't find a single fault with it.

Adhirshtam: (Holding her) No, no. Anyone who finds fault is a fool.

Sandhegam: A lucky piece of property. Whatever price you pay will be worth it. Priceless—see how compact and how handsome it is?

Adhirshtam: Yes. It's exactly this beauty that attracts my heart.

Sandhegam: However much paddy you take from this grain bin, it never seems to diminish. Friends like you absolutely ought to have one.

Adhirshtam: That's exactly why I came. If this is in our house, there'll be no worry afterward.

(Samayochitham moves toward him.)

Sandhegam: Then you'll take it yourself, won't you?

Adhirshtam: Ah, ah! When you yourself are giving it...

Sandhegam: Just don't reduce the price of the grain bin.

Adhirshtam: Forget the price, sir, forget the price! As long as one gets the thing one desires!

Sandhegam: Then lift it up and take it!

(Adhirshtam lifts Samayochitham and, lost in delight, dances. Both are in bliss. Sandhegam sticks his head out from inside the grain bin.)

[In the end Samayochitham develops leprosy. She regrets her disgraceful conduct.......... She meets Chithra, who has become an ascetic....... She follows her path.]

# Scene 16

Location: The same place where Ponni and Puthan met before.

(Puthan comes searching for Ponni. Ponni too comes with Puthan in her thoughts. Neither notices the other. Ponni [Source-held: one small lexical cluster in the opening stage direction remains unresolved] stands where Puthan had stood and lets out a deep sigh. Unexpectedly they see each other and become embarrassed by what they were doing.)

Puthan: Ponni!

Ponni: What brings you this far?

Puthan: Nothing. I came running, mixed up in a dream.

Ponni: A dream!

Puthan: Yes. A terrible sight—as though those thugs were carrying you away again, and you were calling out to me!

Ponni: Ayyo! The dream has come true. There they are again!

Puthan: (Startled) Where?

Ponni: There—they're coming, hiding and hiding. (She points toward the bushes. Puthan runs toward them. Ponni laughs because she has fooled him. From where he stands, disappointed—)

Puthan: Ponni! Your game has gone to waste. See—I am aiming a spear at my chest. I am signalling that if I move, I will be killed.

Ponni: Ah! Run here. (She suffers anxiously.)

Puthan: [Source-held: the first interrogative word in this line remains unresolved] Ponni! I will die taking the spear in my chest. I will not show my back—(toward the bushes) Hmm.... stab! Why are you frightening me? Your spear will split my heart, that is all! Hmm...... throw it. Let me die as a warrior. (Ponni, startled, cries “Ayyo!” and runs to seize Puthan; Puthan bursts into thunderous laughter.)

Puthan: One lie has sprouted into three lies!

Ponni: What was the first lie?

Puthan: The dream I said I had!

Ponni: Are all your words like this?

Puthan: Only at playtime. Ponni! What if they truly had stabbed me with a spear....?

Ponni: Before the blood on that spear dried, it would plunge into my chest!

Puthan: Why should you worry if I die, Ponni?

Ponni: Why should you worry if they carry me away?

Puthan: It is natural for the eyelids to protect the eye;

Ponni: And natural for a spear striking the eyelid to strike the eye too.

[Source-held: a small bracketed stage/song cue on scan 61 remains unresolved at exact-word level.]

Puthan: Ponni....

Puthan: Ponni! No one can ever separate us, can they?

Ponni: They can. There is a terrible instrument that can separate us.

Puthan: An instrument?

Ponni: Yes. It can separate my very life from this world.

Puthan: What is it, Ponni? What instrument is so terrible?

Ponni: Your forgetfulness.

Puthan: Forgetfulness! If daylight forgets to come to the land, if a great warrior forgets to carry his sword, if jasmine forgets to give fragrance—then Puthan will forget Ponni!

Ponni: Beloved! You will never leave me, will you?

Puthan: Leave you? Can sound leave the bell? But only now, there is an urgent task.

Ponni: You have started already!

Puthan: No. I will return as soon as I go.

Ponni: What is so urgent?

Puthan: Government business.

Ponni: Government! You....?

Puthan: A prince.

Ponni: A prince! (To herself) A dangerous love. Without knowing the depth, I have stepped in—a prince.....

Puthan: (Disturbed) Ponni! Why such disgust merely because I am a prince?

Ponni: No. I fear that my love may dissolve like morning mist.

Puthan: So, in madam's opinion, a prince does not know how to love—is that all?

Ponni: He knows how to abandon as much as he knows how to love.

Puthan: You must have heard such stories, Ponni! Even if time keeps flooding over its banks, it cannot dissolve the dam of our love.

Ponni: What if a crack called status appears in that dam?

Puthan: We can seal it with sacrifice. In sacrifice made for love, lovers may die if need be. Love does not die. Ponni! We are the veena and its music.

Ponni: What if the string called love snaps, beloved?

Puthan: We can live like the water of a pond and its clear stillness.

Ponni: What if time throws a stone into it, my dear?

Puthan: Here. This ring is the sign of our love. Even if mountains roll down against us, our love will not be shaken. I swear upon my crown. (He puts the ring on her finger.)

Ponni: Has this become the story of Shakuntala?

Puthan: Am I blind like Dushyanta? Or a traitor? Even if the ring disappears, you are still my beloved. I am going now, Ponni!

[Puthan parts from Ponni at the edge of her sari.]

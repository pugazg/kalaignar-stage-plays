# Scene 25

Location: Vibhishanan's palace.

(Ponni and Puthan are tied up. Vibhishanan stands there. Yaaliththathar is brought in by several soldiers.)

Vibhishanan: Yaaliththathar! Welcome, welcome.

Yaaliththathar: (Seeing Puthan) Ah! Prince! Who committed this injustice? Vibhishanan! What disaster is this?

Vibhishanan: Wait. Those who endure will rule the earth. I waited and endured. Now I am going to rule the earth.

Yaaliththathar: I have no time to listen to your talk. (He draws his sword. Soldiers surround and seize Yaaliththathar.)

Vibhishanan: I too have no time to keep talking. Hmm. Quickly! (Yaaliththathar is tied to a pillar. Vibhishanan takes a letter, extends it to him.) Yaaliththathar! I will keep dictating. You must keep writing.

Yaaliththathar: A rat commands a tiger. Traitor to the king! On hearing your words, not even my finger will move.

Vibhishanan: Watch me move your very soul. (To the soldiers) Hey! Each of you take a bow. The moment I say “Hmm,” Puthan's head must roll. (The soldiers stand with drawn swords pointed directly at Puthan's head.) What do you say now, Yaaliththathar? The Prince's life rests on the movement of my lips. If I speak—my stubbornness will make his life fly away. Write quickly, or consent to Puthan's death!

Yaaliththathar: (Stunned) Vibhishanan! In the name of justice I ask you—is this righteous?

Vibhishanan: Ask in whatever name you like! I do what occurs to my mind.

Yaaliththathar: Do not go wherever the mind happens to wander.

Vibhishanan: I do not intend to hear advice. This is an urgent moment. Carry out my order. Hmm—write!

Yaaliththathar: Write what?

Vibhishanan: I will tell you.

To the Prince of the Inbapuri Empire, abundant greetings...

Write. (He writes.)

I received your letter and was pleased. If Maharaja Mahaveerar is to be overthrown, he must be killed.

[Yaaliththathar stops writing, stunned.]

Vibhishanan: Hmm. (The soldiers raise their weapons over Puthan; Yaaliththathar resumes writing.)

It is a terrible task. Even so, it can be done carefully. If poison is mixed into the food and the task completed....

Yaaliththathar: Ah!

Vibhishanan: Hmm. Do not open your mouth.

You will be Maharaja of Inbapuri, and Ponni's condition will also be fulfilled. Nothing must be done openly. That would endanger us. Vibhishanan, who interferes in our conspiracy, must also be finished. You must not come in person. Do not forget that the minister's post in Inbapuri is for me.

Greetings,
Yaaliththathar.

Hmm. Do not hesitate! A head will fall there.

Yaaliththathar: O conscience! You have disappeared. O beats of my heart! Could you please stop?

[Source-held: compact stage direction immediately after `நின்று விடக்கூடாதா?` on scan 98; the source visibly begins with `இளவரசன்`, but its full exact wording remains unresolved.]

Vibhishanan: Puthan! You write! If you care for Ponni's life, write!

(A sword is held directly at Ponni's head. Puthan begins writing.)

Written to Yaaliththathar, Royal Representative of Poonthottam.

It appears that the Maharaja's death will not occur soon. I am growing older. At what age will I become Maharaja? Under the plan we made before, conspiracy and invasion are now impossible. Reason: Vibhishanan is watching us. Our attempt to bring down Vibhishanan himself also failed. Even so, I am still trying for it. My beloved Ponni, whom I mentioned before, says she will marry me only if I become king. She wants to become Maharani. Write at once of some way to make this happen. The Maharaja too must be removed. At the same time, we must retain a good name among the people. Keep this in mind.

Yours affectionately,
Puthan.

Vibhishanan: The work is done. Victory is mine. To Maharaja Vibhishanan!

Soldiers: Jai! Jai!

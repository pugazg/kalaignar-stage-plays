# Scene 1

Location: A street in Inbapuri.

(A procession celebrating Prince Puthan's birthday approaches. People run eagerly to catch sight of Puthan.)

“Long live the Prince!”
“Where is the Prince?”

(The people come shouting. In the surge of their joy they bump into the aristocrats, who react with disgust and arrogance.)

An Aristocrat: Idiots! Beat them and drive them away!

(A heavy blow lands on an old man's head. He cries out.)

Puthan: Stop! What injustice is this?

Aristocrat: I was the one who told them to drive them away. They come crashing into us like rabid dogs.

Puthan: Does that mean we too must become like that? And on a joyful day such as this!

Aristocrat: An ordinary subject, against the nobles of the principality......

Puthan: An ordinary subject! It is that subject's blood and sweat that make the lords' mansions, the lords' pleasures, the lords' lives! Is this the gift for this worker who came running eagerly to see me? (He goes near the old man with compassion.) Elder, do not grieve. It happened without thought. Keep this. (He gives him a pearl necklace.)

A Man in the Crowd: They did beat you—but what luck struck you at that very auspicious moment, Grandpa!

Old Man: Blessed one! What compassion, what mercy, what kindness! May you live long!

(The procession moves on again with music.)

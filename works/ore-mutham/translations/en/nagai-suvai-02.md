# Comedy Section

## Scene 2

Location: The fields.

(The farmers are eating. Sandhegam sits exhausted and hungry.)

Karuppan: Hey, Sandhegam! Why are you still sitting without eating?

Sandhegam: Food has to come before I can eat.

(Karuppan and the others get up and leave.)

Sandhegam: (To himself) Why is she still nowhere to be seen? Maybe the curry is taking time? Hmm. Hunger is pinching my belly! Hey, miserable stomach! Stay quiet for a little while. Is she cooking? Or is she sitting before Panchali Amman singing?

Karuppan: (Returning) Hey, still no food?

Sandhegam: No food. Only death is going to come.

Karuppan: Maybe your wife is talking with someone?

Sandhegam: There isn't even another house nearby. What woman could she talk with?

Karuppan: Does it have to be a woman?

Sandhegam: What did you say?

Karuppan: I said your wife talks with men too.

Sandhegam: My wife? Don't say that about a chaste woman, sinner! Your tongue will rot.

Karuppan: Chaste woman! (Laughing) Hey, Sandhegam! I'm telling you because you're my mate. I shouldn't say it. Still, I'll say it. Your wife and another man are close.

Sandhegam: Ah! Really?

Karuppan: Yes.

Sandhegam: Don't say that! I think of her like Panchali Amman. However you look at it, she's always doing penance before the goddess!

Karuppan: It's all disguise! You're a gullible fool. You trust her and get cheated.

Sandhegam: Hey! Tell me the truth! I can't believe it!

Karuppan: Don't believe me. Test her yourself and find out. Only your name is Sandhegam—Doubt! You don't have the habit of doubting at all.

Sandhegam: Let her come today. I'll try doubting her.

Karuppan: Do it! Do it!

(He leaves.)

Sandhegam: (To himself) Pretending to be a chaste wife, that wretch has cheated me! Let her come. I'll see.

(He sits. Samayochitham arrives carrying the covered vessel. Sandhegam looks her up and down.)

Samayochitham: What are you looking at?

Sandhegam: I'm doubting you. Stand properly. Chandala woman! You cheated me!

Samayochitham: What are you saying?

Sandhegam: What am I saying? Why did it take so long to bring the food? Answer. I'm asking because I doubt you.

Samayochitham: The rice refused to cook!

Sandhegam: Why? What is the rice angry about? I'm asking because I doubt you—tell me!

Samayochitham: What a troublesome doubt!

Sandhegam: Were you talking with a man? I'm asking because I doubt you. Tell me!

Samayochitham: I was.

Sandhegam: Hmm...... that's what I asked! When were you talking?

Samayochitham: In the morning.

Sandhegam: Hmm. His name?

Samayochitham: I mustn't say!

Sandhegam: Say it, you trickster!

Samayochitham: May I say your name?

Sandhegam: I'm not asking that, chatterbox! Do you have another man as a lover or not?

Samayochitham: A man? I've never even looked at the face of another man...

Sandhegam: Don't pretend!... I'm asking because I doubt you. Answer straight and fast!

Samayochitham: For your sake I do penance before the goddess night and day. I pray, “Let Adhirshtam come, let Adhirshtam come.” Panchali Amman! Such sinful words are falling into my ears!

Sandhegam: Disguising dog! Prostitute!

Samayochitham: Apart from two faces, I've never looked at another face! Ayyo, God!

Sandhegam: Two faces? Ah! Ayyo! My murderous pride is gone!

Samayochitham: One is your face. One is the goddess's face.

Sandhegam: You think this is a joke? Look here! I know all about you now. Tell the truth and save yourself. Otherwise I'll split your skull with this wooden implement itself! (He raises it.)

Samayochitham: O Panchali Amman! Make my husband tell the truth! If it is true that I am a chaste wife, may the rice I cooked turn back into raw rice and may the chicken curry turn back into a live chicken!

(He flings the vessels down. Rice spills out. The chicken flies away.)

# Scene 13

Location: In the street.

(Two of Vibhishanan's guards walk along, drunk and bumping into each other.)

Guard 1: Hey! If we don't catch one today, sir will get very angry.

Guard 2: Hey, just because we've caught a woman, can it be any old piece of goods? He wants one with every feature full and rounded, a neat-looking woman, like a beautiful red lotus. What are we supposed to do?

Guard 1: Can we keep catching a new one every single day? At this rate, for this whole area, it feels like we ourselves may have to become women!

Guard 2: Hey, fool! Can a man become a woman? You're babbling in your drunkenness.

Guard 1: Get lost! Lord Mahavishnu himself became a woman. He and Lord Shiva even had a child!

Guard 2: They're gods, man! They'll turn into women, they'll even bear children!

(Guard 1 sings the story of the gods Vishnu and Narada becoming women and bearing children; Guard 2 sings the refrain, and they leave frolicking.)

Guard 2: Hey! There's a light in that hut over there!

Guard 1: That's a brothel, man.

Guard 2: Then that's fine. We can get some merchandise for our Vibhishanan. Come on, come!

(They leave.)

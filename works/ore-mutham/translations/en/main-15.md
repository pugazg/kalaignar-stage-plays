# Scene 15

Location: Vibhishanan's hall.

[Chithra is present.]

Vibhishanan: Would a warrior's wife return empty-handed? Can the mate of a tiger be a goat?

Chithra: That is what you said.

Vibhishanan: Who said what?

Chithra: The Prince said, “A snake's wife is a snake!”

Vibhishanan: The Prince! That boy does not know this snake's nature. Watch what happens to the man who comes to catch the snake.

Chithra: I have kept my word, haven't I?

Vibhishanan: Only your word? You have saved my life, the aim of my life—everything.

Chithra: My love!

Vibhishanan: Chithra! Why the hesitation? What do you want to ask?

Chithra: A few questions. I want answers.

Vibhishanan: Are you setting me an examination? Ask.

Chithra: Indra is a scoundrel! The reason?

Vibhishanan: They would say lust.

Chithra: The name of Ravana's younger brother?

Vibhishanan: Kumbakarna.

Chithra: The one younger than him?

Vibhishanan: Vibhishanan!

Chithra: Some people hate him. The reason?

Vibhishanan: They say he betrayed his elder brother.

Chithra: No. They say he betrayed something greater—his splendid country.

Vibhishanan: Chithra! That is quite a heavy preamble!

Chithra: My love! If a crooked turn comes into your life, won't my life wither?

Vibhishanan: A good question. A good answer. Chithra! Say briefly what you came to say.

Chithra: At least from now onward......

Vibhishanan: Upward or downward?

Chithra: Isn't treachery a sin?

Vibhishanan: Hmm..... a Vedantic inquiry! What next?

Chithra: What if this terrible conspiracy ends by becoming a danger to us ourselves?

Vibhishanan: Conspiracy—Chithra! Marching an army for a throne and causing the ruling king to perish is not conspiracy; it is proper statecraft. What I am doing is right. What name do you give to the Pandavas wandering as ascetics and then taking the Kauravas' country?

Chithra: It was their own country.

Vibhishanan: After losing it in gambling, to keep celebrating ownership—is that proper gambling? Chithra! Even by your argument, this country belongs to everyone. Everyone is a king of this country. Why should Mahaveerar alone have the title Maharaja?

Chithra: And isn't that very title of Maharaja what you want?

Vibhishanan: Chithra! Be quiet. You do not have enough experience in political matters.

Chithra: If you act wrongly, am I not allowed to say so?

Vibhishanan: No. Look at Draupadi. They wagered her herself in the game of dice. Knowing that if they lost she would become Duryodhana's slave, she remained silent.

Chithra: No stories. Listen to what I am saying.

Vibhishanan: Do not speak! Next month you will be the Princess's Maharani, and I will be Maharaja!

“Imaya, paraak!”

[At the servant's announcement, Chithra leaves.]

Imaya: Vibhishanan! A remarkable woman has come. Ah! What wonders she performs.

Vibhishanan: Who is she?

Imaya: Fine beauty, fine face, fine body!

Vibhishanan: Where is she?

Imaya: Her speech is the sound of a veena! Her glance is a cool stream!

Vibhishanan: Where is she now?

Imaya: When she walks, she has exactly the grace of a peacock.

Vibhishanan: Enough! Where on earth is she?

Imaya: I forgot to tell you one more thing. A very important matter.

Vibhishanan: What is it?

Imaya: While dancing, that queen among women keeps scooping wine into a cup and serving it.

Vibhishanan: Imaya! I cannot bear it any longer. I must see her. Answer in one word: where is she?

Imaya: She will be here now. (To a servant) Hey! Bring her in. (Before the servant can begin to leave, Prince Puthan, the warriors and Kumari enter.)

Imaya: (Startled) Prince! Sit, sit.

Vibhishanan: Sit. (They are received hospitably.)

Imaya: Prince! What brings you on this sudden visit?

Puthan: I came to invite you to a banquet at the palace.

Imaya: A banquet? (Eagerly.)

Puthan: Yes. A banquet arranged to praise your method of rule.

Imaya: Does the Prince himself have to come for that? Send a palm-leaf message and I will come running.

Puthan: Should Royal Representative Imaya be invited with such neglect?

Imaya: Hmm...... Ah! What fortune—what fortune is mine.

Puthan: Fortune you sought for yourself. Hold out your hand.

(He signals to a soldier; Imaya sees the shackle.)

Imaya: Ah! What is this?

Vibhishanan: Prince, you are joking!

Puthan: Yes. This too is one of your playthings! Put it on; let him have one as well. I have arrested both of you.

Vibhishanan: Both of us?

Imaya: Both of us? Vibhishanan alone is guilty.

Vibhishanan: (Glaring at Imaya) Prince! What drama is this?

Puthan: Not a drama, actor! This is the special chapter of the Vibhishanan Purana.

Vibhishanan: No mockery. Answer the question.

Puthan: An answer! The corpses of the workers massacred under your terrifying rule will answer! The pitiful faces of tender children who cried themselves to death beside their dead, milk-giving mothers will answer! The conspiracy letters you have piled up for your plan to rule the world, though you are unfit even to rule a town and are drunk on hunger for office, will answer!

Vibhishanan: Fine imaginings. Imaginings that never happened in this country. Prince! Do not heap false accusations on us. Do not fling wicked words that do not befit the Lord.

Puthan: The Lord—do not draw that curtain over your crimes! It is a torn curtain. Enough of tempting innocent Imaya and making him dance in a political puppet show.

Imaya: Prince! It was all his arrangement. I know nothing about it.

Vibhishanan: Hush.... coward! Do not speak.

Imaya: I am not speaking.

Vibhishanan: Tender children, workers, pity, conspiracy, work! New disguises upon new disguises against these innocents.

Puthan: Innocents! A tiger that has tasted raw flesh speaks Vedanta with a blood-red tongue! Vibhishanan! You who ordered that those who did not pay tax should not be allowed to live—you are innocent!

Vibhishanan: What evidence is there that I am guilty?

Puthan: Evidence? I will provide it in the hall of inquiry. Hmm..... Take them away!

(The warriors drag them away.)

Puthan: (To Kumari) Kumari! You go ahead. I will come later.

Kumari: Alone?

Puthan: Yes.

(They leave.)

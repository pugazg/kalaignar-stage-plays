# Scene 10

Location: The slopes of Pavalamalai.

(Puthan sits deep in thought. Warriors are with him.)

A Warrior: Still no sign of her.

Warrior 2: What if some danger has occurred?

Warrior 3: You said before leaving that if she did not return by morning, it would be a sign of danger!

Warrior 1: Prince! We must set out at once and search.

Puthan: Ah! Ponni...... fool!

Startled Warrior 1: I am speaking about our matter, Prince. We must save Lady Kumari.

Puthan: (Coming to himself) Yes. We must not delay. Kumari will be struggling in distress, waiting for us.

(A warrior arrives.)

Puthan: Any new information?

Arriving Warrior: Imaya's men have burned an entire village.

Puthan: Ah!

Arriving Warrior: The people were burned alive.

Puthan: Why?

Arriving Warrior: They did not pay the tax.

Puthan: They did not pay the tax! Warriors, I no longer intend to let those deceivers live. I will no longer allow the blood of innocent people to flow over the sacrificial altar of power. Cruelty in the Inbapuri Empire! Leave now. Put that merciless man in chains.

Warrior 1: If we are to arrest him, we need evidence, Prince.

Puthan: Ah! Though we have seen with our own eyes the monstrous dances of these criminals, we have found no evidence with which to place them before a court of justice.

Kumari: (Entering) Why not? Here it is.

Puthan: Kumari! Nothing dangerous happened to you?

Kumari: A parrot before a cat—can there be no danger?

Puthan: Were you caught?

Kumari: I was caught. Chithra freed me.

Puthan: Chithra?

Kumari: Yes. A breeze whose husband is a cyclone. If she had not been there last night, I would have had to kill myself. Chithra realized that I had been caught in Vibhishanan's net. From the moment I went there, she treated me with affection. At first I thought she too was an accomplice of that cruel man. Her speech was syrup-sweet, but I strongly suspected that poison might be mixed into it. Yet last night, when I was trapped between Vibhishanan's poisonous fangs, she freed me with great cunning!

Puthan: Leave that aside. What about the task you went for?

Kumari: A success, Prince! This is a letter written by Madhavan. (Two of Vibhishanan's guards notice this.)

Puthan: (After reading) Madhavan—a human beast who mortgages his conscience. He eats the Maharaja's food and yet thinks of betraying him.

Kumari: This is Singanagan's letter.

Puthan: Written in the stride of a raging tempest. These tigers have forgotten that Puthan exists.

Warrior 1: Prince! Powerful evidence.

Puthan: Yes! Kumari deserves a proper reward.

Kumari: I did not work for a reward.

(In a secluded spot, the guards whisper.)

Guard 1: Prince Puthan!

Guard 2: Yes! Who is that street dancer?

Guard 1: It is all disguise. We must inform Vibhishanan immediately.

(Here.)

Puthan: We must use the opportunity and arrest them. We wandered searching for evidence; now it has fallen into our hands. We must no longer let those poisoners go free. Tomorrow morning we must finish our task. We must tear out and cast away the venomous fangs of those deadly serpents who harm the country and society! Traitors to the nation, traitors to society, destroyers. They must be burned away! Sparing them is not wisdom! Warriors!

Warriors: We will destroy treachery!

(All raise the cry.)

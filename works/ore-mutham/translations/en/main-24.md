# Scene 24

Location: A street.

[Source-held: the exact punctuation/spacing of the scan-94 signpost/route parenthetical remains terminally source-sensitive. Secure action: a signpost points one way `To Inbapuri` and the other `To Vibhishanan's Palace`; Kumari studies it, starts along the palace road, then stops to think.]

Kumari: (To herself) It is my duty to free the Prince. Why should I worry about Ponni? Ponni—

[Source-held: scan 95 contains compact historical-vowel/punctuation decisions throughout this dense continuous monologue. The English below renders only the secure dramatic sense and does not resolve those exact Tamil decisions.]

—was the thunderbolt that fell upon my desire! The obstruction to my life of happiness. If I do not free her, the Prince's heart will belong to me. But when I free the Prince, what if she too is freed? Fine. I will go to Inbapuri itself. Let the Maharaja save the Prince.

(She turns and walks toward Inbapuri.)

Kumari: Poor Ponni—what if she has fallen into that sinner's hands? Whatever else she may be, she is a woman like me. Like me, a woman who has lost her heart to someone. Poor thing, she has only [Source-held: compact historical-type wording describing her father on scan 95]. He too died. It is that orphan I must save. (She turns.) What if my compassion becomes the weakness that steals away my love...? I will save Ponni. Those two will swim in a flood of love. I will see it. See it? Can I? I cannot! (She turns.) I cannot! I cannot attain him either. In a useless dream, should I push a life into the mouth of a poisonous snake? No. Let their love live. For them I will sacrifice my love. (She turns.) Is love a thing that can be sacrificed? Is love not the precious diamond that can demand body, possessions and life itself as sacrifice? I have heard stories of those who sacrificed for love. But sacrifice love itself? (She turns.) Let that courageous woman remain Kumari. Kumari, let your anguished heart find peace. If I save the Prince, the glory is that I saved Inbapuri! If I save Ponni, the glory is that I saved love! To buy this glory, give your love as the price. No more confusion. Let their love live. Let my love die... Hmm. It can happen only if I catch Chithra.

(Kumari goes along the road toward Vibhishanan's palace.)

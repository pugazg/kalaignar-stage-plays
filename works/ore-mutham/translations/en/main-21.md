# Scene 21

Location: Vibhishanan's palace.

Vibhishanan: If untouchables sit together with an upper-caste man, what is the punishment under imperial law?

Malayan: They are thrown into prison.

Vibhishanan: If they maintain relations with him?

Malayan: Beheading.

Vibhishanan: If they engage in prostitution?

Malayan: Torture!

Vibhishanan: You have committed all these offences.

(Malayan and Ponni are stunned.)

Vibhishanan: Using your daughter's beauty as capital, you have lured young men from respectable families and carried on a shameless trade in prostitution.

Malayan: Prostitution?

Vibhishanan: What else—business?

Malayan: God! What age is this? My daughter a prostitute?

Vibhishanan: No—a chaste wife! According to shastra, the punishment for this crime is punishment in hell. Under imperial law, it is death.

Ponni: If you want to kill us, throw us into a pit of fire and burn us if you wish. Do not kill us with these slanderous words.

Vibhishanan: Prostitute! You speak bravely. Ponni! You are speaking to the Royal Representative. Do not forget that.

Malayan: Let him be a king if he wants. Even a mendicant would not stand listening to such atrocious words.

Vibhishanan: Hmm..... then do not listen! Speaking to these creatures lowers my dignity. Take him away!

(The soldiers drag Malayan away.)

Ponni: Father! Father!...

Vibhishanan: Call “Father!... Uncle!...” (Ponni boils with anger.) In this vast empire, is there no other way to fill your span-sized bellies? Rather than rent out youth, could you not live by begging from house to house? (Ponni's fury reaches its height.) Did God create beauty and youth to be sold? Ponni! Do you have anything called honour?

Ponni: No! If I had...... I would not be standing here listening to these words.

(Vibhishanan laughs.)

Ponni: (Laughing) Do not torture me.

Vibhishanan: Torture! Aren't you the one who has torn my heart into fragments and tortured me? When I see your pitiful state, even I feel compassion. The crime you committed is terrible. The punishment you must suffer is more terrible still!

Ponni: More terrible than these words?

Vibhishanan: I do not want to cut down your beauty. Poor thing—the hardship of poverty has ruined your life. What can you do when your own father is the broker?

Ponni: Enough. My father is a mountain of gold. Do not lower him with unworthy words.

Vibhishanan: No. I intend to raise both of you.

Ponni: Raise us! Compared with these wicked words, the gallows you raise us onto would be far better!

Vibhishanan: Would I hang a body like the thumbai flower from the gallows? You can live in pleasure in my upper chamber.

Ponni: No mockery. Let the punishment be carried out.

Vibhishanan: You are a golden creeper! I will not let you wither under punishment.

Ponni: Ah!.....

Vibhishanan: Ponni! I have forgiven you. Say one word. I will forget everything.

Ponni: First forget this thought.

Vibhishanan: You are no prostitute—you are a “diamond statue!” Is that enough? Ponni, if your golden lips spill just one loving word—“Naathaa!”—that is enough. I will overflow with joy. I will thrill. Then I will not let you live in a hut. You can live among towers. I will tear down that mud house. You can roam through mansions. Why? I will make you Maharani!

Ponni: You are dreaming. You have lost your senses in a flood of lust and are babbling. Do not speak with your eyes shut merely because I have fallen into your hands.

Vibhishanan: Ponni! Your life is in my hands!

Ponni: Honour is greater—not life! I am a Tamil woman!

Vibhishanan: I too am a Tamil man.

Ponni: You.... in the Tamil people.... are Vibhishanan!

Vibhishanan: You speak without respect, stupid dog! You say honour is greater. Honour! I will show you now which is greater.

[He goes inside and drags Malayan back. As he comes—]

Malayan: Do not show me bogeymen. I will hand Ponni to the mouth of Yama. I will not make her your wife.

Vibhishanan: Fortune comes to you by force and you push it away, Malayan! Think it over.

Malayan: What is there to think about? This is the decision.

Vibhishanan: My decision will be harsh.

Malayan: We are ready to welcome any decision.

Vibhishanan: I have the authority to cut Ponni into pieces before your very eyes.

Malayan: You may cut her into pieces. Even God has no authority to stain her chastity.

Vibhishanan: Authority! See who has it.

(Vibhishanan signals. The soldiers beat Malayan. Ponni screams.)

Vibhishanan: Freedom for your father, and freedom for you too. Ponni! Say just one word.

Ponni: Both of us agree.

Vibhishanan: (Joyfully) Is that so?

Ponni: We are ready for release from this world. Cut us down with your cruel sword and pile up our bodies. Tear our bodies into pieces and throw them into the fire. Whatever atrocities your madness for power allows you to commit—commit them all.

Vibhishanan: That is exactly what I will do. Hmm..! Let it proceed.

(Malayan is attacked. Ponni faints.)

Vibhishanan: Throw her into the underground prison. (Toward Malayan.) Let this continue.

(The blows resume. His skull splits; he becomes like a madman.)

Vibhishanan: Beat him and drive him out. Let the dog run somewhere and die!

(Malayan runs away, unable to bear the beating.)

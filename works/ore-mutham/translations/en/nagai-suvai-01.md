# Comedy Section

## Scene 1

Location: Sandhegam's house.

(There is a large grain bin, a chicken, farm implements, a plough and a spade. Samayochitham is singing before the image of Panchali Amman when Sandhegam enters.)

Sandhegam: Samayochitham! Samayochitham! What is this, Samayochitham? Whenever I look, you're doing nothing but worship. Don't you like talking to your husband? At a time when we young newlyweds should be happy, if it's Panchali Amman and songs all the time, what am I supposed to do?

Samayochitham: Would everything be fine if I just kept talking with you? I am doing penance before Panchali Amman so that no harm comes to my mangalyam.

Sandhegam: What! Your mangalyam... put a slightly stronger cord on it and it'll stay safe without any harm! Is that what you're afraid of?

Samayochitham: You find it all funny. If something suddenly happens to you, won't I be left suffering without turmeric, flowers and mangalyam?

Sandhegam: Right, right! You're worried about losing the turmeric and flowers; no worry about me dying?

Samayochitham: Ayyo! You're pouring molten lead into my ears! If your life were in danger, would my life stay in this body?

Sandhegam: Would it jump into some other body then?

Samayochitham: (To the goddess) Panchali Amman! Protect my mangalyam, Mother! Mother Draupadi! You wore one mangalyam for five men. Imperishable chaste woman! I wear one mangalyam for one man. Protect my mangalyam without the slightest harm.

Sandhegam: Stop giving up your life over “mangalyam, mangalyam.” Will you say one thing to make my heart happy? I wake in the morning, drink gruel, go to the field and roast in the sun. I come back at dusk. I eat whatever I get. You begin worship. I fall asleep listening to your song. Then all I know is that dawn has come again. Do you ever spend one day laughing and talking with me, playing prettily and making me happy? Hmm! The field cracked without rain and my life are the same! I might as well hang the thali I tied on you from a tree branch.

Samayochitham: Look here, don't say such things! I am going to become Savitri.

Sandhegam: What! You're going to become Savitri? What new trouble is this?

Samayochitham: Yes. Just as Savitri took Satyavan's life back from Yama, even if you die I am going to get your life back from Yama.

Sandhegam: Ah!... (Delighted.)

Samayochitham: That is why I am doing penance to Mother and asking for the boon.

Sandhegam: Hey, Samayochitham! Don't test whether your boon can bring back a life by killing me first!

Samayochitham: Until I gain power like Savitri, I will keep worshipping like this.

Sandhegam: Fine. From now on I'll call you Savitri myself. Just stop this worship!

Samayochitham: All right. Go to the field. It's getting late.

Sandhegam: Come on... be affectionate with me once! Hmm! Come on! Won't you?

Samayochitham: Go on... my lord of life!

Sandhegam: My very life! My banyan fruit! Ah! Ah!! These loving words are carrying me straight to Indraloka!

Samayochitham: Hmm, enough! Leave for the field.

(He takes the plough and starts out, then turns back and picks up the chicken.)

Sandhegam: Samayochitham! Chicken curry today! Understand? Finish cooking quickly and bring the meal to the field.

Samayochitham: Poor thing! There's only one chicken left!

Sandhegam: So what? We can buy another. Get to work quickly! (To the chicken) Hey, chicken! Today you are going to die! You are going to enter the stomachs of this poor young couple! My beloved, shall I go?

(He waits. She begins a song as though worshipping Panchali Amman.)

Sandhegam: (To himself) Ah! What chastity, what chastity! All the great sages' wives must beg alms from her when it comes to chastity!

(He leaves.)

Samayochitham: Ah! The pest is finally gone! He wants me to stay cuddling this dirty fellow all the time. Good thing this Panchali Amman and this grain bin are here, otherwise...

Adhirshtam: “We could never escape!”

(Saying this, Adhirshtam emerges from inside the grain bin.)

Samayochitham: Poor thing! It must have been very hard all night?

Adhirshtam: No, no. It's cold season, you see; it was very snug. But your husband had gone out of town and came back at midnight. Why did he come?

Samayochitham: He simply can't stay separated from me. That's why he came running back at once.

Adhirshtam: There was a big rat inside the grain bin. It came to make love to me... look! It cut away half my hair!

Samayochitham: See how much trouble I suffer because of you?

Adhirshtam: The road of love is bound to be difficult. I lay in this prison for this beauty—is there any hardship greater than that?

Samayochitham: Yes, the moment I see you all my hardship runs away.

Adhirshtam: Your hardship runs; the moment I see you, mine flies away.

Samayochitham: The two of us...

Adhirshtam: We'll live like sugarcane and ant—like iron and splinter.

Samayochitham: What if the matter comes to light?

Adhirshtam: We'll run to another town.

Samayochitham: That's impossible. My husband is dangerous. He'll come and murder us.

Adhirshtam: Murder!... He can kill us. Can he kill our pure love? Then we can go to heaven and live happily there. Don't be afraid!

Samayochitham: What if he kills himself too and comes to heaven?

Adhirshtam: He can't. A man guilty of murder goes only to hell. We innocent lovers can revel in heaven.

Samayochitham: What luck that I got you as my lover!

Adhirshtam: My very name is Adhirshtam—Luck!

(The two sing, lost in themselves.)

Samayochitham: (Starting up suddenly) Ayyo! I forgot to cook! Even the sun has leaned west!

Adhirshtam: Do you feel hunger at all in this world?

Samayochitham: We don't! My husband lying in the field... he must be writhing by now!

Adhirshtam: You care that much about your husband?

Samayochitham: I don't care! I'm afraid! When he gets hungry he's like a demon! And on top of that he told me to cook chicken curry and bring it.

Adhirshtam: Fine! What will you do now? Somehow I'll come tonight and eat.

Samayochitham: Ayyo! He'll kill me! (Thinking) Hmm. I'll do one thing.

Adhirshtam: Even if you cook now and go, he'll say you're late and give you four blows before he leaves.

Samayochitham: Be quiet. I'll tell you.

(She pours uncooked rice into a pot, puts the live chicken in another vessel and covers it with a second vessel.)

Adhirshtam: What's this? You're pouring in raw rice?... You're covering up a live chicken?

Samayochitham: I'll tell you later. (She leaves.)

Adhirshtam: Shall I go then? I have to set out looking for a woman for Vibhishanan.

Samayochitham: Tomorrow...?

Adhirshtam: I'll come. Does a dog forget a bone?

(He leaves, looking back at her.)

# Scene 17

Location: The inquiry hall.

(Imaya and Vibhishanan are in the accused enclosure.)

Vibhishanan: One charge says tax was collected beyond measure. One charge says innocent people were massacred. One charge says there was a conspiracy to overthrow the king. I am responsible for none of these three charges.

Puthan: Yes. Are we, who have not forgotten your cruelties until now, the responsible ones?

Mahaveerar: Let Imaya speak. It is true that these atrocities took place, is it not?

Imaya: He (pointing to Vibhishanan) did not say that any of these were atrocities, Maharaja!

Vibhishanan: Yes. I said they were adharma.

Imaya: No—you said they were statecraft!

Mahaveerar: You have treated the people like lambs and carried on a fox's work, you destroyers!

Vibhishanan: A fox's work! I have never imagined such a thing even in a dream, Maharaja. The people's life and the king's welfare are the dream of my heart. I have never even spoken harshly to my beloved subjects! I learned that everyone in this land is a king.

Puthan: And therefore you imagined yourself a king too.

Vibhishanan: Slander—empty slander!

Puthan: What slander? You tortured and piled up those who would not pay tax—that is slander. You drove a spear into the heart of a young woman who would not submit to your desire—that is slander. You burned to ashes the homes of poor people who asked why—that is slander. (Looking at Imaya.)

Imaya: Yes! I thought so even then.

Mahaveerar: Do not speak. If I appoint you as Royal Representative—[Source-held: the short historical-type/joining-sensitive clause immediately following `பிரதிநிதி` on scan 65 remains unresolved at exact-source level.] Imaya! It is true that you desired this throne, is it not?

Imaya: I had no desire. He told me to desire it. So I did.

Vibhishanan: Maharaja, please listen to my words. It is true that Imaya planned to become Emperor of Inbapuri. It is true that he tormented the people. I tried in every way to stop him and showed him the paths of truth and justice. I submitted the shastras and traditions and begged him. I frightened him by showing him the law. Imaya would not listen to my words. He disregarded my advice. By God, I committed no crime. This land and the welfare of its people are my breath, Maharaja!

Mahaveerar: Very well. What about conducting the conspiracy?

Vibhishanan: I had no connection with it. It is true that Imaya conspired. I tried to stop him. He did not listen.

Puthan: Traitor!

Assembly: Traitor! Traitor!

Vibhishanan: Traitor! Think before you speak. Who is the traitor? You put an innocent man like me in the dock and speak injustice—traitors! King! I ask according to the law of this land: what evidence is there that I am a traitor?

Puthan: (Laughing) Evidence! The agreement made with Madhavan, guard of Manikkak Kottai; the written pledge Singanagan sent you; the secret routes into the fort drawn up by Ellappan. Is that enough evidence?

Vibhishanan: Sabash! A perfect fabrication! Emperor! Let the Prince show these proofs and, with that same hand, cut off my head and throw it away.

Puthan: Vibhishanan! Do not jump about for nothing. Hmm..... Kumari! (Kumari gives him the box.) Now speak with these proofs before you.

(The box is opened. Vibhishanan gives an empty, mocking laugh.)

Vibhishanan: You are showing me a bogeyman! Ha ha haa! Prince! Could you not at least have prepared two false letters?

Mahaveerar: Prince?...... (The Prince is stunned.)

Puthan: Maharaja! The letters have slipped from our hands.

Vibhishanan: Nine lies to hide one lie!

Imaya: Exactly right! What he says is exactly right.

Mahaveerar: Speak, Imaya! From today you will undergo imprisonment for life.

Imaya: Life imprisonment?

Mahaveerar: Yes. Without being fit to govern, you have treated the people like animals. Without understanding your responsibility, you have indulged in pleasures and amusements. You have also conspired against me.

Imaya: Not at all! If you want a witness, ask Vibhishanan himself.

Vibhishanan: Did I not stop you even then? Did you listen? You even planned to kill the king. If I had not been there, you would have destroyed our honourable Maharaja Mahaveerar, himself shaped like a short sword!

Mahaveerar: Great sinner!

Imaya: Me?

Vibhishanan: Yes, you. You who thought treachery against the house whose food you ate. You who ate the Maharaja's salt and thought of betraying him.

Mahaveerar: Put Imaya in chains. Vibhishanan is innocent. In Imaya's place, from today he shall be appointed Royal Representative of Mullaikadu.

Vibhishanan: Long live the Maharaja! Long live justice! Members of the assembly! Just as even sugarcane joined with neem turns bitter, by joining with Imaya I too have acquired a disgraceful name. But God knows I am innocent. Every drop of blood running in my body will be used for this land. Until my last breath I will labour for this country. Long live the prosperity of this land! Long live the king! Long live the Prince!

(The assembly disperses.)

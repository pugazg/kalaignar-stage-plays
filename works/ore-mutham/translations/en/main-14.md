# Scene 14

Location: Puthan's camp.

[Source-held: dawn-stage parenthetical on scan 52. Source-visible elements include Chithra writing and using/sending a message with a pigeon while singing, followed by Kumari and Puthan waking; several exact words remain unresolved and are not reconstructed.]

Puthan: Before that, we must put those foxes in a cage. Move out.

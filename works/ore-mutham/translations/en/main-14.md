# Scene 14

Location: Puthan's camp.

(Light. The sun has risen. Chithra gets up and prepares to depart. An ascetic passes singing about the sunrise. Before Chithra leaves, Kumari and Puthan open the box and look inside; thinking the letter-scrolls Chithra placed there are the genuine evidence they had collected, they close it. Chithra’s farewell and departure take place entirely in silence.)

Puthan: Warriors! Chithra has gone to her mother's house. Vibhishanan and Imaya will be alone. If Chithra were there, her heart would shrink from seeing her husband arrested. This is the right opportunity to arrest them.

Kumari: Yes. If Vibhishanan learns that we have seized the evidence, he will begin another scheme.

Puthan: Before that, we must put those foxes in a cage. Move out.

(They leave.)

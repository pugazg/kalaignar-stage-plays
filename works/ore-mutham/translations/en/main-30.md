# Scene 30

Location: Kumari's sickbed.

(Mahaveerar and the physician stand nearby. Kumari lies struggling to breathe, with a large bandage across her chest. Chithra and Ponni are beside her. Puthan enters, supports her and lays her across his lap.)

Puthan: Kumari! Kumari!

(She opens her eyes.)

Kumari: Aththan! Even if there was no place for me in your heart, I have at least found a place in your lap!

Puthan: You have found a place in the hearts of all the Tamils of this country. Kumari! You are one who will not die. Forgive me. The guilt of killing your love will never leave me.

Kumari: Here—the joy I am experiencing now will burn away all your guilt. Is not this joy the great final blaze of a lamp about to go out!

Puthan: Kumari!

Kumari: Aththan! Where is the sign... that I found a place in your heart? A kiss of desire... yes. One kiss! Just one kiss! Before I become a corpse...

Puthan: Kumari! You have taken an indelible place in the history of Inbapuri. You have taken an unforgettable place in the people's minds. You melted my stone heart, became the lamp of love that gave light to my very life, mingled with the flow of my blood and took your place in the casket of my heart. Here..... is the sign of that........

(Before he can bend to kiss her, she gives a sign by raising herself.)

Voice: “One Kiss”

(Along with it, “the tomb” slowly rises. They circle around it. The shadow changes. The Prince and Ponni place garlands on Kumari's tomb. A song is heard.)

O great lamp of sacrifice, nectar-maiden of sweet Tamil!

Virgin goddess who never tasted the sweetness of love!

Did you lose your precious life to set right

the injustice wrought by caste and religion?

Did you forget this earthly world?

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: closed Tamil `../../scenes/main-30.md`; the scene is source-secure.
- `அத்தான்` is retained as `Aththan` because its intimate kinship/affection register is source-specific and central to Kumari's final address.
- The title phrase `ஒரே முத்தம்` is rendered `One Kiss` in the closing voice cue.
- The four-line closing verse is translated as verse while preserving its sacrifice, Tamil-womanhood, caste/religion and death imagery.
- No secondary English witness, OCR reconstruction or later-edition wording was used.

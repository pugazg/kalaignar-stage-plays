# Scene 7

Location: Imaya's wine hall.

[Imaya and Vibhishanan.]

Vibhishanan: The invasion is next month. Mahaveerar will be imprisoned. Puthan will be tortured. Our rule in Inbapuri.

Imaya: Entering the Inbapuri fort!....

Vibhishanan: Easy, easy! Like a rat hidden inside a horse, I have our man inside the fort. He has sent me today the routes by which the fort can be besieged. What remains now?

Imaya: Nothing.

Vibhishanan: Victory is ours. No doubt.

Imaya: Yes. No doubt. Victory is ours.

Vibhishanan: What will our rule in Inbapuri be like?

Imaya: What will it be like?

Vibhishanan: An indestructible autocracy.

Imaya: Yes! That is how it will be. But Vibhishanan! What if the subjects revolt?

Vibhishanan: I will not let them.

Imaya: You will not! By imposing a repressive law......

Vibhishanan: No, no! There is another weapon.

Imaya: There will be, there will be. You will have one.

Vibhishanan: We must keep dividing the people into more and more castes.

Imaya: Yes. We must keep dividing them.

Vibhishanan: And it must be done in the name of God.

Imaya: Yes! In the name of God.

Vibhishanan: Only if friction is kept alive among them will there be no revolution. Then we can live.

Imaya: What if Puthan unites them?

Vibhishanan: A dream! A dream! The fool wants to mix toddy and arrack.

Imaya: Fool!

Vibhishanan: Then the country itself will erupt in revolution among them. The king will begin suppressing it. We can enter easily.

Imaya: Then next month I am Emperor!

Vibhishanan: Any doubt? First we must succeed in the work of treachery. Then the throne will come by itself.

Imaya: It will come by itself!

Vibhishanan: The name of this policy is.....

Imaya: The Vibhishanan Policy!

Vibhishanan: Imaya! This is the very policy of the old Vibhishana!

Imaya: A good policy. A policy the country needs.

Imaya: What is the secret?

Vibhishanan: Nothing. A personal matter.

Imaya: A secret that Royal Representative Imaya is not to know?

Vibhishanan: What Imaya wants is the crown of Inbapuri.

Imaya: Yes! The crown!

Vibhishanan: Crown! Woman! If you want them, do not speak.

Imaya: I am not speaking.

# Scene 3

Location: One side of the palace.

(The Minister and King Mahaveerar.)

Minister: All the Prince's thoughts are about the future of Inbapuri. His goal is to create the country as a realm of happiness. Whoever stands in the way of that path, he will not care who they are.

Mahaveerar: That is the quality of a true warrior. Minister! I will no longer die. As long as my son's fame lives, my name too will live.

(Puthan enters.)

Puthan: Father!

Mahaveerar: Puthan! As this year's birthday gift—(Puthan looks around)—I am going to give you a task.

Puthan: As you command.

Mahaveerar: You know our Royal Representative in Mullaikadu, do you not?

Puthan: Yes. Imaya. He did not even come to today's festival.

Mahaveerar: He will not come, that twisted-minded man. You know Vibhishanan too, I suppose.

Puthan: I know him well. Imaya's advisory minister.

Mahaveerar: I hear the two of them together are running a tyranny in Mullaikadu.

Puthan: Tyranny! A word unknown in the entire history of our dynasty!

(Ramela and Kumari, who have come that way, stop and listen.)

Mahaveerar: That is why I called you. You must go at once to Mullaikadu and set that administration right. Tyranny must not be allowed to grow.

Puthan: As you command.

Mahaveerar: I hear the injustices they commit are beyond measure. If they are caught with evidence, life imprisonment will be their reward!

Minister: Evidence!........ Then the Prince must remain incognito and discover it.

Mahaveerar: Yes. That is what must be done.

Minister: I hear brutes roam all over Mullaikadu. They too must be eliminated.

Mahaveerar: They are all said to be Imaya's men. Whatever happens, the first necessity is to seek evidence. The best course is for the Prince to go and investigate.

Minister: A dangerous task. If someone were sent to accompany him......

Mahaveerar: True. Whom shall we send?

“I will go.” (Kumari comes near as she says this.)

Puthan: No, no one is needed. I will go alone.

Kumari: The Prince speaks without thinking, Uncle!

Mahaveerar: Yes! The reports that have come about Imaya are terrifying!

Puthan: If necessary, I will take a few warriors with me.

Mahaveerar: Warriors are needed, yes. And along with them....

Kumari: Valour is important in war. But even a lion can be deceived by a fox.

Puthan: And therefore?

Kumari: We too must turn into foxes and act the part.

Puthan: Is acting such a great thing?

Kumari: Women are more skilled at it than men. To catch conspirators, a woman's daring is a good means.

Mahaveerar: True! True! Puthan! Women are trained even to enter the enemy's heart. And Kumari, above all, seeks only your welfare.

Kumari: Uncle! May I not go with him?

Mahaveerar: Is it not my wish that you should remain with the Prince?

Puthan: We have to enter the den of tyrants. Kumari is not suited for that.

Mahaveerar: You think Kumari a coward, Puthan!

Kumari: It is not that, Uncle! He thinks the entire female sex are simpletons. Uncle! Let him not take me with him. Give me permission to go alone.

Puthan: No, no. Let her come with me.

Mahaveerar: Puthan! Be careful! You are taking Kumari with you. Vibhishanan and Imaya's revelries are nothing but wine and women!

(Puthan bows and starts to leave.)

Mahaveerar: Puthan! Do not cause anyone suffering without evidence!

Puthan: Father! I swear upon justice! I will not stray from integrity.

Mahaveerar: Live!...... Triumph!

(Everyone leaves. Ramela looks knowingly at Kumari and laughs. Kumari's song of joy.)

# Scene 23

Location: Vibhishanan's palace.

[Vibhishanan welcomes Puthan.]

Vibhishanan: Prince! Is this how I should receive you? If I had known you were coming... I would have written a welcome proclamation in diamonds on a golden plate, lined up chariots, elephants, horses and infantry, surrounded you with dances, sounded thunderous fireworks—what splendour I would have received you with!

Puthan: I dislike pomp completely.

Vibhishanan: The world itself dances in pomp!

Puthan: Do not say it dances. Say it has been made to dance.

Vibhishanan: Whatever it may be! If I do not welcome a prince with pomp, there is no glory even in my authority.

Puthan: I hear that under your authority even women are sent to prison?

Vibhishanan: Yes. I apply the law properly. I deliver justice to criminals with the speed of a violent storm. I do not distinguish between man and woman.

Puthan: Let that pass. At least this way you establish equality between man and woman! Excellent rule! An egalitarian government that knows the method of justice.

Vibhishanan: Murder, theft, lies, conspiracy—no problem raises its head in the country. Complete peace prevails.

Puthan: Quite so.

Vibhishanan: Just recently I threw a prostitute into the underground prison.

Puthan: A prostitute?

Vibhishanan: Yes, a harlot. A woman who carries on prostitution with generous young men from high families.

Puthan: A prostitute! Worse than disaster. Yes—are you certain she is a prostitute?

Vibhishanan: Is there any doubt?

Puthan: Governance means thinking before doing anything! What evidence is there that she is a prostitute?

Vibhishanan: Her face itself says so!

(Vibhishanan signals the soldiers. They stand behind Puthan [Source-held: the exact final action wording of this scan-88 parenthetical remains unresolved].)

Puthan: You discover criminals from the signs on their faces. Have you studied palmistry?

Vibhishanan: Yes. The wrinkles seen on a face are lines that show the structure of a person's life. For example, merely by seeing a face [Source-held: the page-ending comparison clause on scan 88 remains unresolved].

—we say it, don't we? The Prince is young. His education in the world is not enough.

(One of the men approaching secretly points out the Prince's sword to Vibhishanan.)

Vibhishanan: Look at this. (He draws Puthan's sword from its sheath.) Merely by seeing the shine of this sword, its sharpness is known.

(Puthan is made unarmed.)

Vibhishanan: How is my explanation of line-reading?

Puthan: Vibhishanan?

Vibhishanan: Hey, Kumbakarna! Rat! You are in my trap. Do not shout! Hmm. Prince, you deliberately created a case and raised me to a higher post—would this dog forget such gratitude?

Puthan: Vibhishanan! You are playing on top of a volcano.

Vibhishanan: Playing? Is this a game? Ah-ha-ha! Chains, Prince! Chains!

(A man moves to put chains on him.)

Vibhishanan: Do not chain him yet! There is still work. Drag that prostitute here. (A man leaves.) Tie him to this pillar. (The Prince is tied.)

Vibhishanan: Why are you staring like a tiger? Why are you breathing like a storm?

Puthan: The rumbling of a volcano!

Vibhishanan: I am the earthquake that uproots even a volcano and throws it away!

Puthan: You are a hopping frog. Do not walk like a lion!

Vibhishanan: I will tie you to a raft and set you adrift in the sea. I will throw you into a pit of fire. I will tear your flesh into pieces. Puthan! Your life is in my fingertips. Measure your words.

Puthan: I am measuring them. Proud because you have tied the Prince of Inbapuri to a pillar, you are dancing in frenzy...... Vibhishanan! The result will be disastrous.

Vibhishanan: Astrologer! First look at your own horoscope. Result or harvest—on Vibhishanan's path there is nothing but victory. Puthan! You are a child. You do not know what government means. With such qualifications, you set out to investigate. Look at you—witless fool!

Puthan: Traitor! Fold your tongue.

Vibhishanan: Can one speak without folding it? I am speaking by folding it. Summit of stupidity! Frog born to a senseless king!

Puthan: Vibhishanan! You [Source-held: exact old-type insult cluster immediately after `நீ` on scan 90 remains unresolved]. You babble imagining that you have tied my waves of feeling to this pillar too. Before the flood of my feeling, this pillar is a mere splinter!

Vibhishanan: Oho! Will you take a Vishvarupa? Wait a little, little Maharaja! I will demolish and rebuild this palace to suit the Vishvarupa you intend to take. One word—tell me now the measure of the breadth of your Vishvarupa.

Puthan: (Furiously) A measure! Take the measure of the trough in which your blood will be poured! Take the measure of the bier that will be built for your corpse!

(In fury the Prince springs out hard enough for the rope to snap. At once several warriors seize him, bind him again with chains and other restraints, and fasten him tightly to the pillar.)

Vibhishanan: Ah-ha-ha! A calf leaps and runs; tie it down and it lies still.

(Ponni is dragged in wearing chains.)

Vibhishanan: Come, girl... come!

Puthan: (Seeing Ponni in astonishment) Ponni!

Ponni: Ah!.... (Ponni tries to run toward Puthan. Before she can—)

Vibhishanan: Stop, thief! (He draws his sword to stop her.)

Ponni: Your sword!.... Will it stop cowards? At least let the head you cut from me roll to his feet. Hmm. Do not put it back in the sheath! Let it happen.

Vibhishanan: First...... gag this chatterbox.

(Ponni's mouth is gagged.)

Vibhishanan: This pillar is waiting for her.

Puthan: Vibhishanan! You are playing with death.

Vibhishanan: The game has ended pleasantly. Now the work begins. Puthan! Yaaliththathar, Royal Representative of Poonthottam, is going to come here.

Puthan: Babble whatever you want.

Vibhishanan: It will sound like babbling. Vibhishanan never speaks an unconfirmed report. You yourself will learn it now. Yaaliththathar is coming precisely to see you.

Puthan: Let him come.

Vibhishanan: Then write this. (He shows a letter and orders him to write. Puthan pushes it away.) Do not embrace destruction! Write what I say. Poor thing! I do not want to ruin your beauty joined with youth.

Puthan: What compassion! The compassion of a snake for a mongoose! Vibhishanan! You speak to me with words meant for a slave. You pour on me the heroic threats meant for a coward. Remember this well. Until the final beat of my heart...... Puthan will not submit to your authority.

Vibhishanan: I will see that too. (Vibhishanan draws his sword and stands as though about to strike Ponni.) Now speak. Speak with Ponni's life as the stake! My authority—or your stubbornness?

Puthan: No! No!

Vibhishanan: Hmm... write. (Puthan takes the letter to write.)

Dear Yaaliththathar......

Hmm. Write quickly. (Puthan writes.)

An urgent summons written by Puthan. The moment you see this letter, without delaying even for the time it takes to blink, come with the warrior who brings it. Some important arrangements must be made immediately.

Hmm... sign it.

Puthan: What is this palm-leaf letter for?

Vibhishanan: For your own good.

Puthan: For my good?

Vibhishanan: Do not speak! There is an order at the sword's point. Bow to it.

Puthan: What if I do not bow?

Vibhishanan: Ponni will go to the heavenly world.

(He draws his sword and points it toward her.)

Puthan: Stop! Stop.

Vibhishanan: Hmm. Now you come to the path! Put your signature.

(Puthan signs. Vibhishanan gives it to a soldier.)

Hey, Murungai! Within three naazhigai reach the capital of Poonthottam, and within the next three naazhigai Yaaliththathar must return. (The soldier leaves.) Until then, let these two remain tied to the pillar. It seems the Prince's mouth aches from all his talking. Gag his mouth too.

(Puthan's mouth is also gagged. Vibhishanan looks mockingly at both of them. A soldier enters and bows.)

Soldier: Some people who rebelled saying they would not pay tax have been brought here.

Vibhishanan: So those troublemakers want tickets for a journey to Vaikuntha?

Soldier: We have even dragged in the man who ripped open the belly of our collection officer.

Vibhishanan: Those dogs' tongues must be torn out. I will come myself.

(He leaves rapidly.)

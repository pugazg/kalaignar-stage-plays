# Scene 22

Location: Ponni's hut.

(Puthan sits in anger. Kumari is sorrowful.)

Malayan: (Running in) Ponni! Ponni!

Puthan: Ah! What happened to Ponni?

Malayan: Ponni is gone! Ponni is gone!

Puthan: Where has she gone?

Malayan: Into Vibhishanan's prison. You saved her before. Save her now, my God! Save the lamp of my lineage from going out! (He collapses.)

Puthan: Where is Ponni being held?

Malayan: In the tiger's mouth! My Ponni has fallen into the hands of that wicked Vibhishanan.

Puthan: (To the stunned Kumari) Kumari! Look after him. I will return.

(Puthan leaves. Kumari lifts Malayan and lays him down.)

Malayan: Ponni! Ponni!

Kumari: Father! Ponni is your daughter, isn't she?

Malayan: My jewel, child. My jewel.

Kumari: Poor man. Do not worry. No danger will come to Ponni. Elder! Did you already know him before this?

Malayan: This calamity came because of him, child. He is the one who bewitched my daughter and, in the end, got her branded a prostitute. Prostitute! Prostitute! Ponni—a title unknown in our entire lineage. My dear one! Are you a prostitute? World! You tell me! O impartial sun! You tell me. Wind! You tell me. Is my daughter a prostitute? Our line has been called poor, called slave, called fool—but never has it been called prostitute! Ayyo! My belly is burning! Ayyo!......

Kumari: Do not cry! Anger has an end. We have set out to see that end. Sir! Did he tell you that he would marry Ponni?

Malayan: Child! We are of a low caste. He is of a royal family. I gave Ponni so many examples and told her the two could never match; she would not listen at all. In the end......

Kumari: The Prince will not abandon Ponni. Do not be afraid.

Malayan: I do not care even if I die. Please make sure my daughter's heart's desire is not beaten to death, child.

Kumari: Hmm. Lie down.

(She lays him down and treats his injuries.)

Malayan: To the Prince, you are......?

Kumari: His sister!

(Kumari lets out a deep sigh.)

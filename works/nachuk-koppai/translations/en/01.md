# Scene 1

Location: Garden.

(Ekambaram sings about the moon.)

(Santha covers his eyes.)

(Song: “In the Sky”)

Santha: What is this? It looks as though you have surrendered your heart to the moon's beauty!

Ekambaram: Yes—only because you were not here!

Santha: If only you did not have this gift of talk...?

Ekambaram: Then I could never escape from you. Why? Isn't that so?

Santha: Your words can deceive only me.

Ekambaram: Why? How many more people must I deceive?

Santha: Try deceiving my father a little. Let us see.

Ekambaram: The fact that the two of us are alone here is itself the result of deceiving your father, isn't it?

Santha: Yes. Did Father ask you anything yesterday?

Ekambaram: No. But he has been rather more cautious about us lately.

Santha: Your white veshti and shirt offend his eyes.

Ekambaram: You should tell him, Santha, “Even if it is only a rag, wash it and wear it.” Ah, what a brain!

Santha: That brain ought to be put in a kiln!

Ekambaram: Should one speak ill of one's father?

Santha: Only if he says something worthy can he be praised!

Ekambaram: The wonder is that you and your brother Pazhaniyappan were born so completely unlike your father!

Santha: Yes. Did your father study lessons in love like this when he was young?

Ekambaram: Santha! True, Father may not have studied them. But his forefathers understood the nature of love. They lived with the truth that “there is no difference between love and God; neither is destroyed by time.” Love is like a wave rising in the sea! No one caught in its surge can escape! Love is a captivating rainbow that makes man and woman appear as one! Just as one cannot separate the taste created when milk and sugar are mixed, one cannot separate love from the heart. But only when it is true love, Santha!

Santha: You are speaking as though you are hiding something.

Ekambaram: No, Santha! I am reminding you only so that your resolve will remain firm.

Santha: My love! Do you think my resolve is so unstable that it needs reminding?

Ekambaram: No, Santha. When you began speaking about love, my thoughts wandered somewhere else. Nothing more. Why are you so suspicious, Santha?

Santha: Is it such an easy thing to trust men's words?

Ekambaram: We shall see. There are only a few days left. Very soon we shall know whether this poor Ekambaram receives this fruit or not.

Santha: The fruit will come to you on its own. Love is without deceit; wherever it goes, it honours equality. It knows neither high nor low. I am ready to prove that love is the birthplace of courage and sacrifice.

Ekambaram: Who knows what your father is thinking...?

Santha: His mind seeks only money. He will never agree to make me your wife.

Ekambaram: Your brother Pazhaniyappan...?

Santha: I have no worry at all about Anna. He would even agree to give his life for me.

Ekambaram: Santha! The wonder is that one should be blessed with a brother like this!

Santha: Was I blessed only with a brother? Did I not also find a beloved?

(After the song ends.)

Santha: All right, it is late. If Father wakes up, he will look for me. Shall I go?

Ekambaram: All right. May I expect you tomorrow?

Santha: Can the bee forget honey? Can the moon hide the sky? I am going.

- `கந்தையானாலும் கசக்கிக் கட்டு` is translated literally enough to preserve the proverb and the joke about Ekambaram's white clothes.
- The fruit, milk-and-sugar and rainbow imagery of love is retained rather than paraphrased into modern romantic prose.

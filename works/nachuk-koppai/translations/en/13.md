# Scene 13

Location: Public meeting.

(Pazhani, Sivaguru, women, and Ekambaram, who has returned from the army, are present.)

(Song.)

Pazhani: Dear comrades! I request Sivaguru to preside over this meeting.

Sivaguru: Greetings! Dear comrades! Students! Women students! My greetings to Comrade Pazhaniyappan, who asked me to preside over this meeting. I wish to say a few words about Ekambaram. Ekambaram, who had been serving among us for some years, suffered the consequences of love, and because those consequences ended in pain he joined the army. That is a most noteworthy matter, and he has thereby rendered service to the country as well. His military training is a rehearsal for the struggle to come! Comrade Pazhaniyappan will read and present a message of welcome to him.

(It is presented.)

Welcome Address:

Pazhani: Long live Tamil! Welcome, Ekambaram! May your coming be auspicious! There are many Tamils who can drink, talk merrily, play cards, and sleep with beautiful women. But your life must not fall that way. It will not. We wish for the worth of your life. Welcome, Ekambaram! Welcome! Welcome!!

Ekambaram: Mr. Chairman! Comrades! Mothers! Greetings! Every one of us has heard of the heroic mothers who lived in our Tamil land in those days and sent their children to war. Tamil history tells of a mother who vowed that if her son died with a wound on his back she would cut off the breast that had fed him, went to the battlefield, and rejoiced greatly when she saw blood flowing from her son's chest. It was the history of Tamilagam in those days that, even on hearing that parents, kinsmen and lovers had died on the battlefield, a mother would send her son toward the direction from which the war-drum sounded, saying, “Go and face the enemy forces.” Such a Tamilagam has today become a fearful abyss.

And beyond that, it is astonishing that after hearing I ran off to the army because I lost my love, you have given me such a grand welcome. Therefore I accept it with affection. Greetings!

Sivaguru: With this, the meeting comes to an end. Greetings to everyone.

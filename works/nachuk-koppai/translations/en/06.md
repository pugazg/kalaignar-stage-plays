# Scene 6

Location: Forest.

(Ekambaram weeps while holding Santha's picture.)

(A holy man's song.)

Ekambaram: Santha! I never dreamed that you would deceive me like this! How can I show you the anguish in my heart? What wrong did I commit? I loved you with a true heart. You too poured out your love again and again. I placed my heart in your hands. But you were not deceived—I was. The feast of sweet words you spoke that day still sweetens my heart. You called me sugarcane! Today that sugarcane has become bitter neem to you! You said, “Even if earth and sky collapse, I will not forget you!”

Santha! Did you truly love me, or was your affection merely the love of prostitutes? Love this, eyes that, noses that—and a divine power besides! Is this that power? Oh!

If a young man is drawn in by a smiling face, spoken to through the eyes, bewitched by letters, tormented by words, and finally left in confusion—will the world merely stand and watch? Santha! Your own heart will pass judgment on you. You must bear the curse from the heart of Ekambaram, who trusted only you, lived in this world only for you, was deceived by you, and now runs away broken-hearted. You cannot escape it. Santha! Did you forget your beloved? Did you garland Azhagappan instead? Here I go—not out of the world, but out of this town! Santha! Would I die without first seeing the punishment the world gives you? Santha?

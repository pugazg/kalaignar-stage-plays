# Scene 17

Location: Road.

(Ayyar and Karuppan meet.)

Karuppan: What, Swami, are you only now going?

Ayyar: Ayyo! Do not speak so loudly. You will ruin my honour. Yes—who is at the arrack shop?

Karuppan: No one. Go quickly.

(Ayyar leaves.)

Karuppan: What, Swami, shall I come too?

Ayyar: There is very little chance of your coming today.

Karuppan: What, Swami? Whenever I see you, you are playing cards and all that. Yet you say there is nothing.

Ayyar: Is this the place to talk about all that? Yes. Where are you going now?

Karuppan: There is an enemy of yours, you know. I am going to teach him a lesson.

Ayyar: One enemy? The whole world is my enemy. Who is this enemy of mine?

Karuppan: That fellow, Swami! Maniyappa Mudaliar's son Pazhaniyappan. His father has paid me to beat him.

Ayyar: Maniyappa Mudaliar said that? Excellent. Keep this too. (He gives money.) Beat him so thoroughly that no one can tell whether he is alive or dead.

Karuppan: I will take care of all that. You go, Swami.

Ayyar: That is, do not tell anyone that I gave you money and told you to do it.

Karuppan: Why, sir? I have not even told anyone that you drink toddy.

Ayyar: Ayyo! Is the middle of the street the place to say all this? You... get lost! (Ayyar leaves.)

Karuppan: Hey, Pazhani! Do you think anything can stand against this Karuppan? (Song—end of scene.)

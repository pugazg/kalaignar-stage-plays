# Scene 14

Location: Maniyappa's house.

(Santha weeps. Song.)

Pazhani: My dear sister! In the speed of time and the conspiracy of a foolish world, your life has been cut apart. There are still many days in which you ought to experience happiness.

Santha: Anna! Will the world speak ill of me?

Pazhani: Let the world speak ill of you—what is that to you? The world is a jealous bogey! It is folly to fear and submit for the sake of this world surrounded by injustice and cruelty. I know that, except for your fear that the world will condemn you, your heart does not reject another marriage. Santha! Not only you—crores of widows like you are dying in fear of this world. Are not the corpses of infants found on riverbanks, in reed beds and in rubbish heaps the children born to widows? Do you at least know how many women's necks this world has broken?

Santha: Then...?

Pazhani: Seek the bridegroom your heart desires. Place the garland around him. Begin your life.

Santha: Father will not agree.

Pazhani: I explained it to him in every possible way. I even argued from the puranas he believes in. Around the fortress called orthodoxy, he has dug a moat called stubbornness.

Santha: Anna! Our family is falling into poverty. Father raves like a madman. In this condition, it does not seem right to oppose him!

Pazhani: Was it not because you decided not to oppose Father that this fate came upon you? If you had said then that you would marry no one except Ekambaram, would this have happened, Santha?

Santha: If Father agrees...?

Pazhani: He will not.

(Maniyappa enters.)

Maniyappa: What will he not agree to? What scheme are you making?

Pazhani: About Santha's life!

Maniyappa: Well, what have you decided?

Pazhani: Father! It is true that Santha's husband has died, but the loving heart within her has not died. It is true that Santha lost her married life, but she has not lost her tender young body. Santha must live again. Father! That is the decision.

Maniyappa: Hey! You are fighting a tiger.

Pazhani: Ah! What can it do to me? Tear me into pieces. That is all, isn't it?

Maniyappa: You have neither fear nor shame. Who asked you to put these ideas into her head?

Pazhani: Father! Listen to me. Arrange Santha's remarriage to the man she desires.

Maniyappa: What! Remarriage? Speak of it again and you will get a beating. Santha! Are you too thinking of remarrying? You have no honour, no shame—tch! Do not stand before me.

Pazhani: What cruel words! Is this the answer for asking whether Santha must live as a widow until she dies? I will get a beating, Father! I will establish my resolve! This is certain. Otherwise my very life will end. We shall see.

(He leaves.)

Maniyappa: Idiots! Do they think this will happen while I am here?

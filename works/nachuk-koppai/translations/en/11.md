# Scene 11

Location: Azhagappan's house.

(Everyone is present.)

Azhagappan: Uncle! I will not survive now.

Maniyappa: My boy! You need not be afraid of anything. I will go and break a coconut for Subramania Swami and come back.

(A blind man's song.)

Azhagappan: Ayyo! Santha, if I had given to a poor man unable to work the money I spent on drink and revelry, would things have come to this? I am leaving you behind in a life of suffering. Do not waste your life away! Remarry, my dear!—Santha! Santha!

(He dies.)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: closed Tamil `../../scenes/11.md`; no OCR, PDF redrafting, web text, later edition or secondary English witness was used.
- Azhagappan's final remorse links drink, waste and social responsibility; the contrast is retained directly.
- His explicit instruction that Santha should remarry is translated plainly and is important to the widow-remarriage argument developed in later scenes.

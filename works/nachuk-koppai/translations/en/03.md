# Scene 3

Location: Wedding hall.

Azhagappan, Santha, Maniyappar.

Ayyar, disciple.

(Friends and relatives are all present.)

Maniyappa: Hey, Kandha! Where is Ayyar?

Kandhan: Here he comes.

Maniyappa: Come, Swami.

Ayyar: I am coming.

Maniyappa: Swami, it is getting late. Let us get it done quickly.

Ayyar: What, Mudaliar! Tell them to bring everything. Bring the bride and bridegroom.

(Ayyar chants mantras. Then the garlands are exchanged. They go around the marriage dais.)

Ayyar: Santha! Do namaskaram. Bridegroom-sir! You too do namaskaram. Sing the nalungu song.

(Song.)

Ayyar: Mudaliar! Take the bride and bridegroom inside and give them milk and fruit. All right, shall I go?

Maniyappa: Go and come back.

Ayyar: All right. I am going. (He goes inside.)

Maniyappa: What is this, Swami, going this way and that? What is the matter?

Ayyar: Nothing. Give the dakshina.

Maniyappa: Why did you not say that at once, Swami? Here.

Disciple: Uncle! For me too.

Ayyar: Mudaliar! Give Pitchumani something too. (Money is given.)

(Pitchumani takes the money and steals a silver bowl. Kandhan catches him.)

Pitchumani: Uncle—uncle—uh...

Ayyar: What is it, boy—Mudaliar!

Maniyappa: Hey, what is it?

Kandhan: He stole this!

Maniyappa: Why, Swami, can this be done?

Ayyar: Is this what you call theft?

Kandhan: Then what is it—robbery?

Ayyar: Mudaliar! The Manu Dharma Shastra says that Brahmins may take whatever they want, wherever it may be.

Maniyappa: Is that so? Then take it.

Ayyar: No need.

(Ayyar goes inside.)

Maniyappa: Hey, Kandha! See to everything that has to be done.

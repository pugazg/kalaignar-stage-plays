# Scene 8

Location: Road.

(Chinnaiya Pillai and Kannaiya Chetti enter.)

Chinnaiya: Pazhani, where are you going?

Pazhani: There is a public meeting today. I have to speak there. That is where I am going.

Chinnaiya: You are going to speak by abusing God, aren't you?

Pazhani: I am going to speak about the scoundrels who abuse God. Yes—where are you going?

Chinnaiya: My daughter's wedding is tomorrow. I am going to break a coconut for Pillaiyar so that it will not rain.

Pazhani: Then go ahead.

(Kannaiya enters.)

Pazhani: What, Kannaiya Chetti! What brings you this far?

Kannaiya: With no rain, all the land has dried up. I am going to break a coconut for Mariamman so that at least tomorrow it may rain.

Pazhani: Chinnaiya Pillai! Did you hear what Kannaiya Chetti said? You are going to break a coconut so that it will not rain at your daughter's wedding. His fields have dried up, so he is going to break a coconut so that it will rain. Whom will God favour in this?

Chinnaiya: Why? Let him favour the good man!

Kannaiya: Does that mean I am the bad man?

Chinnaiya: We will know tomorrow.

Kannaiya: Hey! Do not boast about your greatness. God will favour whoever is as devout as I am.

Chinnaiya: Are you such a great devotee?

Kannaiya: Are you such a great devotee?

Pazhani: Sirs! The sun burning and rain falling are acts of nature. Fighting with each other over this is foolishness!

Both: Do not call it foolishness.

Pazhani: I cannot find a prettier word for it, sirs! If it rains tomorrow, will you admit that you are the rogue—or will you admit it? In the West, man is struggling with machines in his hands. You stand here with clay in your hands, moulding Pillaiyars. Is this intelligence?

Kannaiya: All right, all right. Where are you going now?

Pazhani: To the public meeting.

Both: Then we will go to the temple, break our coconuts, and come back.

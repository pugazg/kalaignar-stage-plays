# Scene 12

Location: Road.

Sivaguru: Liquor! It is liquor that has thrown this world into disorder!

Pazhani: Azhagappan is dead.

Sivaguru: Liquor! It is liquor that has thrown the condition of this world into disorder!

Pazhani: Do not stop with your tongue! Think deeply, comrade! Men who bear the name of sadhu and men who argue on political platforms alike kneel before liquor, slip on the narrow path called morality, and are destroyed. Is it not that liquor which has shattered so many families, crushed so many countries and reduced them to dumb insects? How many forms of power does liquor hold in this country! In ancient times it entered in the guise of *soma-panam* and ruined the reason of the ancient people. Everyone who drinks it becomes its slave.

Sivaguru: Liquor! It is liquor that has thrown this world into disorder! It crushed and mangled a strong part of my life—my youthful service, the goodness of friendship, the force of youth—and made me lower than a dog. Liquor destroyed my reason and made me a fool. Only when I was being buried did I awaken to new life. Pazhaniyappa, will you not take me with you too? Can you?

Pazhani: I can.

Sivaguru: Then folly will die in this country. Come. Let us go.

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: closed Tamil `../../scenes/12.md`; no OCR, PDF redrafting, web text, later edition or secondary English witness was used.
- The repeated `மது!` refrain is retained to preserve the scene's rhetorical cadence.
- `சோமபானம்` is retained as `soma-panam` because the historical/religious allusion itself is part of the anti-liquor argument.
- Sivaguru's self-indictment is kept forceful rather than summarized into a generic statement about addiction.

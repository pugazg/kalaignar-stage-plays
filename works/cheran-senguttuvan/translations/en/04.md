# Scene—4.

## Kuyilaluvam

[In the war fought between Cheran Senguttuvan and Kanaka—Vijaya, Senguttuvan is victorious.]

Cher: Villava! What is the name of this place where victory resounds?

Villavan: Kuyilaluvam.

Cher: Kuyilaluvam—mark it down! All these rocks are leaves upon which the valour of living Tamil has written! Victory alone is not enough, Villava; those useless fellows must not be allowed to escape.

Vil: They say Kanaka-Vijaya have run away somewhere in saffron robes, dressed like rishis.

Cher; Ha!...Ha!........Nothing new........That is the one habit that truly belongs to them!

[Kanaka-Vijaya are brought in dressed as holy men.]

Cher; Who are these?

Vil! Kanaka-Vijaya.

Cher: Kanaka-Vijaya, Kanaka-Vijaya. Ha! Ha! Ha....! O greatest of warriors! Fine indeed is your skill in war! Showing your backs—covering your heads—attacking from hiding—turning into holy men. Ha! Ha! Ha! Since you are dressed as sages, answer this: in the Puranas, how many days did the Deva-Asura war last?

Kan: Eighteen years!

Cher: The Rama-Ravana war?....

Kan: Eighteen months!

Cher: The Bharata war?

Kan: Eighteen days!

Cher: Warriors, remember this. The war Cheran Senguttuvan fought in the northern land ended in just eighteen *nazhigai*.

Kan: O king, we kneel. Forgive us!

Cher! Forgive you? I should forgive you? Come and kneel on the soil of Tamil Nadu; let that soil forgive you!

Vil: O king, should we not return to Tamilakam? It has been thirty-two months since we set out.

Cher: We shall depart—Villava. But before that, must we not take a stone from here to install a statue for Kannagi?

Vil: Yes,

Cher: We have found the right oxen to carry that stone. Kanaka—Vijaya, you hurled one word at the valour of the Tamils—now lift the stone!

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/04.md`; the complete 2009 published-English witness was not consulted.
- The source heading `காட்சி—4.` and setting `குயிலாலுவம்` are preserved as `Scene—4.` and `Kuyilaluvam` without external place-name normalization.
- Speaker-label distinctions are intentionally preserved: `சேர்:` → `Cher:`, `சேர்;` → `Cher;`, unusual `சேர்!` → `Cher!`, `வில்லவன்:` → `Villavan:`, `வில்:` → `Vil:`, `வில்!` → `Vil!`, and `கன:` → `Kan:`. They are not silently collapsed into normalized speaker labels.
- `ரிஷி`, `புராணம்`, and `நாழிகை` remain culturally visible as `rishis`, `Puranas`, and `nazhigai`; the traditional time unit is not converted into modern clock time.
- The sequence Deva-Asura war / Rama-Ravana war / Bharata war and its eighteen years / months / days / `nazhigai` escalation is retained exactly in dramatic order.
- Source long-dash rhetoric is retained in `We shall depart—Villava`, `Kanaka—Vijaya`, and the final `one word...—now lift the stone!` challenge.
- The closing source mark `- * -` is retained exactly.

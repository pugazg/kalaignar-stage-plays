## Pre-play framing and Scene 1

### Strong agreements

Both versions preserve:

- the `Silappathikaram` / Ilango framing;
- the Kannagi-stone episode as the reason for the northern expedition;
- the assertion of ancient Tamil history and valour;
- Kanaka-Vijaya's northern court and the `swayamvara` setting;
- the Tamil poet's antiquity claim for Tamil;
- the Ganges / Kaveri poem;
- the tiger / bow / fish emblem sequence;
- the northern rulers' ridicule of earlier Tamil victories;
- the poet's threat to report the insult to Cheran Senguttuvan.

### Material differences

| Verified Tamil control | Independent translation | 2009 witness | Comparison finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kannagi-stone framing | bringing a stone from the northern land to install a statue of Kannagi | stone from `northern India` to `carve` the statue and have it `consecrated in Tamil Nadu` | witness modernizes geography and adds carving/consecration detail not explicit in the verified line. |
| `தமிழகத்துப் பழம்பெரும் சரித்திரம்` | ancient history of Tamilakam | `golden glory of the historic past of Tamil Nadu` | published witness adds evaluative `golden glory` and modern `Tamil Nadu`. |
| opening royal military list | ornamented chariots, horses and a vast army | infantry, cavalry and chariot divisions | freer military-system rendering. |
| `பாரதந்தான்` | conservative `Bharatham` | `another Mahabharatham` | witness resolves the source-sensitive form into a specific epic comparison. |
| `கல் தோன்றி மண் தோன்றாக் காலத்தே` | before stone arose and before earth arose | before stone was formed and `sand` was formed | **material lexical divergence**: `மண்` is rendered as `sand` rather than earth/soil. |

The published witness is therefore smoother and more explanatory in Scene 1, but less conservative around source-sensitive wording and printed label distinctions.

## Scene 2 — Cheran's hall and embedded martial-family poem

### Strong agreements

Both versions preserve the broad dramatic sequence:

- Cheran and Venmal's domestic exchange;
- the turn to `Purananuru` and the martial-family story;
- the father dying in battle;
- the husband setting out to war and later dying;
- repeated war-drum / victory-report imagery;
- the widow's grief changing into renewed public-duty resolve;
- the son being summoned from his teacher and sent to the battlefield;
- the poet's interruption;
- the report that northern kings mock Tamil valour;
- Cheran's decision to respond militarily.

### Material differences

The 2009 Scene-2 verse is recognizably the same narrative, but it frequently rewrites the Tamil poem into rhyming or explanatory English and therefore cannot serve as a lexical authority for difficult Tamil forms.

## Scene 3 — mobilization and Tamil self-respect rhetoric

### Strong agreements

Both versions preserve:

- the renewed summons to Tamil warriors;
- the tiger / bow / fish insult sequence;
- Kanakan-Vijayan as leaders of northern rulers;
- the Ganges-bank challenge to the Kaveri side;
- Cheran's claim that Tamil identity and self-respect override internal Chera/Chola/Pandya rivalry;
- the long catalogue of Tamil literary/historical figures and places;
- the mobilization of forts and warriors;
- the final-kiss instruction;
- willingness to die rather than surrender;
- severed-head / torn-body imagery;
- self-respect in the blood;
- the Himalaya-directed closing call.

### Material differences

| Verified Tamil control | Independent translation | 2009 witness | Comparison finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| `வாளேந்தி` | `bearing swords` | `holding lances` | **weapon substitution** in the published witness. |
| `வில்` insult | `the bow` | `our Bow and Arrow` | witness adds `Arrow`. |
| `மண்டூக மன்னர்கள்` | `frog-kings` | `frog-headed kings` | published witness expands the insult. |
| `அத்தாணி மண்டபத்தில் சத்தான கருத்துரைக்கும் சாத்தனாரே!` | Sattanar giving weighty counsel in the Athani hall | `Satthan, who have explicated the meanings of poetry` | **material interpretive rewrite**; hall/counsel image is replaced. |
| `வேட்ட மங்கலம்` | source-spaced `Vetta Mangalam` | `Vettamangalam` | witness normalizes/compounds the verified spacing. |
| `சாமரமேந்திகள்` | `fly-whisk bearers` | `slaves holding feathered fans` | witness adds `slaves` and rephrases the courtly object. |

| repeated `இகழ்ந்தார்கள்!` | repetition retained strongly | partly compressed within fluent prose | witness reduces some rhetorical repetition. |
| final `*` | retained | not represented | source closing mark omitted in witness transcription. |

The political arc is strongly shared, but the 2009 version repeatedly makes implicit rhetoric explicit and normalizes names/places and source images.

## Scene 4 — Kuyilaluvam and the Kannagi-stone ending

### Strong agreements

Both versions preserve:

- Senguttuvan's victory over Kanaka-Vijaya;
- Kuyilaluvam as the place of victory;
- the pursuit and capture of Kanaka-Vijaya in ascetic/saffron disguise;
- the mockery of retreat, disguise and hidden attack;
- the Deva-Asura / Rama-Ravana / Bharata sequence;
- eighteen years / months / days and the short duration of Senguttuvan's war;
- the defeated rulers kneeling and asking forgiveness;
- the thirty-two-month absence from Tamilakam;
- taking a stone for Kannagi's statue;
- using Kanaka-Vijaya themselves as the humiliating carriers of the stone.

### Material differences

| Verified Tamil control | Independent translation | 2009 witness | Comparison finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| source label variants `சேர்:`, `சேர்;`, `சேர்!`, `வில்லவன்:`, `வில்:`, `வில்!`, `கன:` | preserved as distinct `Cher:`, `Cher;`, `Cher!`, `Villavan:`, `Vil:`, `Vil!`, `Kan:` | normalized to `Cheran:`, `Villavan:`, `Kanakan:` | published witness erases printed label/punctuation variation. |
| `பைந்தமிழ் வீரம் தீட்டிய ஏடுகள்` | rocks as leaves/pages written by living Tamil valour | `pages in history marked with the valour of the Tamils` | witness adds `history` and flattens `பைந்தமிழ்`. |
| `வீணர்களை விடக் கூடாது` | those useless fellows must not be allowed to escape | `We should not leave the defeated just like that` | insult is softened/generalized. |
| `முக்காடு போடுவது` / `மகானாய் மாறுவது` | covering heads / turning into holy men | hiding in clothes / disguising as sadhus | witness supplies a more specific interpretive paraphrase. |
| `பதினெட்டே நாழிகை` | `eighteen nazhigai` | `eighteen ‘nali’ [1 nali equals 24 minutes]` | **traditional-unit normalization plus explicit conversion**; witness also changes the transliteration. |
| `தமிழ்நாட்டு மண்ணில் வந்து மண்டியிடுங்கள், அது உங்களை மன்னிக்கட்டும்!` | kneel on the soil of Tamil Nadu; let that soil forgive you | come to Tamil Nadu and kneel; `Let her forgive you!` | witness removes explicit `soil` and personifies Tamil Nadu. |
| `இங்கு கல் எடுக்க` for Kannagi statue | take a stone from here to install a statue | `choose a rock fit for sculpting Kannaki statue` | witness adds `fit for sculpting`. |
| final `இப்போது தூக்கு கல்!` | `now lift the stone!` | `now carry the stone on your heads!` | witness adds the carrying position `on your heads`, not present in the verified line. |
| final `- * -` | retained exactly | not represented | source closing mark omitted in witness transcription. |

The published witness confirms the dramatic ending but is substantially less conservative with printed labels, cultural time units and the exact wording of the humiliation.

## Published alternatives useful as comparison evidence only

Some 2009 renderings are useful stylistically or interpretively without becoming automatic revisions, for example:

- `another Mahabharatham` as one possible interpretation of Scene-1 `பாரதந்தான்`;
- `speech sweeter than palmyra candy` as one possible resolution of `பனவெல்ல`;
- the time-measure explanation built around the `வட்டி` / `நாழி` passage;
- `frog-headed kings` as an expanded rendering of `மண்டூக மன்னர்கள்`;
- `pages in history` for the Kuyilaluvam rock-image;
- the bracketed modern-time explanation attached to the published witness's `nali`.

These remain **secondary evidence only**. They do not override the verified Tamil, and difficult source forms should not be retroactively normalized merely because the 2009 translator selected one interpretation.

## Result

**PASS / COMPLETE — post-translation secondary-witness comparison for `சேரன் செங்குட்டுவன்`.**

The 2009 witness confirms the complete four-scene dramatic arc and many major images, but it is consistently freer than the independent archival translation. It modernizes geography and cultural references, regularizes speaker labels, supplies confident interpretations for source-sensitive poetic words, rewrites parts of the embedded poem, compresses or expands rhetorical structures, and introduces several material substitutions. The strongest controls are the Scene-2 `சாதல் கதை`→`love story` change, `வேழப்படை`→`Tiger flags`, Scene-3 `வாளேந்தி`→`lances`, the Sattanar/Athani-hall rewrite, Scene-4 `நாழிகை`→`nali` with a 24-minute gloss, and the added `on your heads` in the final stone command.

No comparison finding by itself authorizes revision.

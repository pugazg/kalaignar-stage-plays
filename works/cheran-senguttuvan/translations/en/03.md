# Scene—3.

## Cheran's Royal Hall

Cheran: O tiger-warriors who live bearing swords and bearing fame in Tamilakam! The summons has come for all of you! A fine opportunity to establish our valour once again in the northern land! They say they mocked our emblems engraved upon the Himalaya. Some men of petty minds—the worthless fellow who has never seen a battle-front mocked the tiger. The useless man who asks a price for valour scorned the bow - the base fellow who has never even heard the tales of entering the battlefield reviled the fish. Uthiran—Visithiran—Sithiran—Sivethan—Bairavan! Do not be startled! These are only the names of men! Kanakan and Vijayan, said to be the leaders of these frog-kings, guardians living on the banks of the Ganges, have issued a challenge to the banks of the Kaveri! Comrades! We shall teach them wisdom—set out!

A Minister : Why do you alone have such anger, O king, when the Chola and Pandya do not?

Cheran: Because I am a Tamil—because my feeling of self-respect has not diminished! The Chola or the Pandya may be my enemy; for that reason I will not allow the kings of the northern land to wag their tails so long as I live! Was it not my Tamil they insulted! Wranglers! Ah.... O king who gave us the Kural, Valluvar! The northerners insulted you! O Sattanar, who gives weighty counsel in the Athani hall! They insulted you! O sea of pearls, O mullai grove! They insulted you! O sacred lion-abode that lives with drums sounding in all eight directions, they insulted you. O mother Narchonai, they insulted the victory of Senguttuvan, son of Imayavaramban Neduncheralathan! O peerless poet, Ilango! Beloved younger brother! They insulted you! O Pandyan who gave his life for justice! O Nedunchezhiyan who crossed the Aryan army! They insulted you!—O lion-bulls of unfailing refined-Tamil richness! Scholars! Poets! Masters of the arts! They insulted all of you! They insulted! Those who insulted! Those who insulted are alive in the northern land as kings! O beautiful capital Vanji, surrounded by the Porunai river and groves, they insulted you! They insulted! Men of low nature insulted you; with Minister Azhumbil Vel here, with the warrior Villavan Kothai as commander—with strength upon all your massed shoulders like a waxing crescent—some creatures fit only to loiter in the inner palace are said to have raised frenzied cries—warriors! Let us try one hand against those wanderers who do not know how to guard their tongues. Let every warrior in the forts of Thumpivadi, Thoranangkarpattu, and Vetta Mangalam set out! Young bulls, tell your beloveds that this may be the last kiss, and depart! Are we a society afraid to die? Are we a people who fear battle, to surrender to those fly-whisk bearers? No!....No!.. Your heads may fly from your bodies on the field! But let them be cut away with eyes that spit fire. Your muscles may be torn apart! But let them be torn while the heat of self-respect in your blood has not cooled. Beat the war-drum! Strike the Himalaya! The Tamil has never been defeated! Never been defeated! Comrades! ....Set out!

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/03.md`; the complete 2009 published-English witness was not consulted.
- The setting `சேரன் கொலுமண்டபம்` is rendered conservatively as `Cheran's Royal Hall`; the Tamil remains controlling.
- The tiger—bow—fish insult sequence is kept explicit because it carries the political logic of the speech.
- The source list `உத்திரனும்—விசித்திரனும்—சித்தரனும்—சிவேதனும்—பைரவனும்!` remains a five-name sequence rather than being historically normalized.
- `மண்டூக மன்னர்கள்` is retained as the source insult `frog-kings`.
- Tamil solidarity despite Chera–Chola–Pandya rivalry, `தன்மான` / self-respect, and the repeated catalogue of Tamil literary/historical figures are kept explicit rather than compressed.
- The verified source spacing `வேட்ட மங்கலம்` is retained as `Vetta Mangalam`; it is not silently compounded.
- `சாமரமேந்திகள்` remains `fly-whisk bearers`, preserving the source's contemptuous courtly image.
- The source's repeated `இகழ்ந்தார்கள்!`, `இல்லை!....இல்லை!..`, martial body imagery, `இமயத்தை முட்டுங்கள்!`, and final `*` are retained.

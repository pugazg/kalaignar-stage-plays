# Scene 7

An ornate, horse-drawn carriage shaped like a palanquin comes to a stop before the palace. Two rough-looking warriors ride as guards, one in front and one behind, swords in hand. A stout man gets down from the carriage. A silver tiger emblem is fixed to his chest. His black clothing, that silver badge, his towering body and his elephant-like gait must carry some importance. Yes—he is Vetrivelan, commander of the minor ruler of Vengai city.

The moment Vetrivelan steps down, the band strikes up. Dancing women carrying garlands run forward to receive him with gestures of welcome. The Palaiyakkarar and Sukadev stand respectfully before him, greeting him with ceremonial marks and bows. Vetrivelan enters the palace with them. In a richly decorated hall, Baladevar seats Vetrivelan in a fitting chair and sits humbly nearby.

Vetrivelan: Well, Baladevar! Is everyone well?

Baladevar: We are well! And how is the king?

Vetrivelan: Well! You received the palm-leaf message I sent, did you not?

Baladevar: I did. But I was surprised by it.

Vetrivelan: Why?

Baladevar: When there are thousands of soldiers—and when a valiant commander like Vetrivelan is there—whom does the ruler of Vengai city fear that he still wishes to enlarge the army?

Vetrivelan: Enlarging the army is not for strength! It is for protection! Baladevar, it took no more than a hundred warriors to defeat your younger sister and capture this Pazhuthur!

Baladevar: That is exactly what I mean. Vengai's warriors need not be great in number... they are not men who know defeat!—

Vetrivelan: Even so, the ruler wants the army enlarged! Siruthaiyuran lies waiting for the moment he can pounce upon Vengai city—for some pretext, some reason to create a battlefield. That is why our king has ordered that there must not be the slightest delay in the effort to expand the army.

Baladevar: I am waiting to do whatever lies within my power.

Vetrivelan: We must get at least a hundred men from this region to join the army.

Baladevar: I will try.

Vetrivelan: The ruler has ordered that no one is to be forced into it.

Baladevar: Yes—what is formed by force would not be an army! It would become a funeral bier we build for ourselves.

Vetrivelan: True! True! Your townspeople may dislike joining the Vengai army—after all, our ruler is the king who has subjugated them!

Baladevar: That is nothing. I, the foremost among the subjugated, have knelt and bowed to him. With my whole heart I pray to God that he may live long. As you expect, many will join the army! There is no doubt.

Vetrivelan: Good. I must go to my quarters.

Baladevar: Thendral Palace on South Royal Street has been arranged for you.

Vetrivelan: Excellent!...

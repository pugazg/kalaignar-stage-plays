# Scene 53

[Sumathi's house, night

Muthayi lies there alone. A voice is heard in the street. Muthayi gets up and looks outside. Vedhalam stands there disguised as a Swami.

Muthayi: Who are you, Ayya? What do you want?

Vedhalam: Who here is called Muthayi?

Muthayi: Why—what has happened?

Vedhalam: Sumathi Amma asked me to tell you—Muthan was attacked by enemies and has been carried into a house, close to death.

Muthayi: Ah! Aththan!—

Vedhalam: Are you Muthayi? If you come with me, you may see him alive.

Muthayi: Aththan! Aththan!... Ayya, where is he? Take me to him.

The deer follows the fox. Muthayi runs behind Vedhalam.

Hidden at one place, Sukadev sees this and lets out a deep sigh, not knowing what to do.

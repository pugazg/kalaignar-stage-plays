# Scene 48

[Wealthy merchant's house

The merchant enters with the child. His wife gathers the child into her arms and thanks God.

She too does not ask, “Where is Muthayi?”

But there is one man who will ask. He has the right to ask.

Merchant: If I had not gone, the child would have been finished! A tree branch broke in the wind and fell—

Muthan: Ah! Where is Muthayi?

Merchant: Ah!... In my hurry I forgot her.

Muthan: You forgot—not only my wife. Your whole class of wealthy men forgot a quality called compassion long ago!

He starts running at once. As long as the householders can see him, he runs like a lame man. Then it is no longer Ponnan who runs—it is Muthan!

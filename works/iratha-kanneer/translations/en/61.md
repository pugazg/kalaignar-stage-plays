# Scene 61

[Sumathi's house

The widowed Muthayi and her friend Sumathi are there. The entire Palaiyakkarar family stands before her—including the Swami.

Baladevar: Muthayi! Why will you not speak to us?...

Muthayi: Do you think my sighs have no power to speak?

Poongavanam: My dear Muthayi—listen to me. Come with us! I will care for you as I would care for my son.

Muthayi: Son! Do not say that again. The honour of your mansion will fall into the dirt!

Have you no shame to say now that Muthan was your son?

A mother, indeed! A mother who kept her mouth shut while her son was treated like a dog! A mother who listened with relish to the news that her son was wasting away in hunger!

It was the atmosphere of your mansion that made you hide the affection that says “my son.” It was your false honour—dignity—status—respect—and all that lump-of-mud nonsense that stopped you from calling him your child!

Was it not to prevent any stain upon the Palaiyakkarar's greatness that you treated the son you carried for ten months like a wandering stranger?

Where is that arrogance now, Mother? Have you come searching for your son only because the curtain you hung over that arrogance has been torn apart?

A mother protecting the honour of the mansion! A father fanning the frenzy of liquor!

The mother acted the role of a chaste woman! The father danced the role of a devotee!

The woman who lost her chastity became the maiden! The Swami lives as a Swami!

Why? Why? Why?

Ask the tears of blood—they will answer!

The corpse of my Muthan, hanged and fallen, mocks both false honour and false devotion!

“She is a mother”—the woman who says, “Let my son be ruined, so long as my status is not!”

“He is a father”—the man who says, “Let my son be damned, so long as public support does not fall and my name as a holy man is not forgotten!”

These great good people have now come to call their daughter-in-law!

Shameless people!

My Muthan is no longer alive to answer all of you.

If not today, then tomorrow—not only for you, but for hollow people like you—a sword is growing in my womb!

A fire that will consume false status!

A spear that will destroy the rutting elephants of religion that live by deceiving the people!

It is growing in my womb—it will answer!

An answer to tricksters!

An answer to schemers!

A burning answer—a Self-Respect answer!

Wait for that answer! Sinners! Sinners who unjustly killed my Muthan! Wait for that answer!

Now leave this place—

Unable to stand before Muthayi's eyes filled with both fire and flood, Baladevar's family and the Swami bow their heads.

# Scene 40

[Vedhalam's post house

Vedhalam is separating and arranging the palm-leaf letters. The postal runner sits nearby occupied with other work. Vedhalam hurriedly opens one letter. It says:

“To dear Thirusangu, from Sukadev.

Muthayi has been separated from me. I will not return to town until I search for her and find her. I will find her. Otherwise I will become an ascetic. You struggled so much to arrange her marriage to me, but it has failed. More later.

Sukadevan”

Taking the letter, Vedhalam sets out. His hawk-like gaze makes it clear that his scheming mind has begun working again. Where he is going so quickly—and so happily—we do not know. Let us wait and see.

# Scene 22

[Thirusangu's house

Muthayi and Sumathi are talking.

Muthayi: By now he will have gone and said that he is withdrawing from the army!

Sumathi: Even if he had not said it himself, your lovely face would have made him say it!

Muthayi: There you go again! It is almost time for Appa to come!... Here, Sumathi—be careful.

[She gives her a bag containing one hundred gold coins.]

Make sure it reaches him in the morning! You know where he is, don't you?

Sumathi: I know.

Muthayi: Where?

Sumathi: In your heart—

Muthayi: Don't play, Sumathi. He will be at the post house. Even if he isn't there, give it to Vedhalam and tell him to hand it over to Muthan!...

Sumathi: As ordered!...

Muthayi: All right, go now—Appa will be here soon.

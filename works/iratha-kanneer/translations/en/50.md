# Scene 50

[Sumathi's house

The woman who rescued Muthayi is Sumathi. The two are talking.

Sumathi: Muthayi! I am frightened even to hear your story. But—from now on you need not fear anything... both of you can stay safely in my house.

Muthayi: Sumathi... how did you come here? Won't you tell me?

Sumathi: Look here—can you tell how I came? [She shows the thali around her neck.]

Muthayi: My dear girl! Are you married? Where is your husband?

Sumathi: He is unwell. He has been admitted to the hospital. I was returning after visiting him when I found you in that condition.

Muthayi: You are newly married—the turmeric thread is still fresh! And already he has fallen ill?

Sumathi: Yes. He is a sculptor. While climbing a temple tower to do sculpture work, he slipped and fell, suffering serious injuries. He is a little better now.

Muthayi: Sumathi—that day I spoke angrily because I did not understand you. Forgive me!

Sumathi: Let us forget all that.

Muthayi: After marriage, where has all your mischievous talk run away and hidden?

Sumathi: It has not hidden anywhere. Let him recover his health—then listen to the way I talk! Muthayi—you call my speech “kurumbu,” mischief. Do you know what he calls it? “Karumbu”—sugarcane.

Muthayi: Fine! Stop talking only about your man—pay a little attention to mine too.

Sumathi: Here—I am going to that merchant's house right now.

Muthayi: His name there is Ponnan!—don't forget that!

Sumathi: All right. I will bring your husband, and on the way I will visit the hospital too. You sleep for a while. You are exhausted.

Sumathi leaves. Muthayi begins to sleep.

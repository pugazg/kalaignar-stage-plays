# Scene 46

[Muthan's hut

Muthan and Muthayi are playing with the child from the wealthy merchant's house. Listening to their affectionate words and song, the little one too smiles, showing its gums.

Muthan says to Muthayi:

Muthan: One like this for us...!

Muthayi: Wait another eight months.

Muthan: Is that so? You never told me! Then what shall we name our child?

Muthayi: We should combine our two names.

Muthan: That's right. Take the first part of your name and the last part of mine—your first part, “Muth”... my last part, “than”... Muthan!... Tch! Tch!... Ha-ha—then take the first part of my name and the last part of yours... my first part, “Muth”... your last part, “thayi”... Muthayi!... Ah-ah-ah!... It refuses to give us a new name.

Muthayi: Then let us put together the first parts of both our names—Muth! Muth! Mum-muth!

Muthan: Tham!

The two laugh together at their own wordplay.

Muthayi: All right—it is getting late. I must take the child to the seashore.

Muthan: Come back quickly! It looks as though rain is coming.

Muthayi places the child in the little chariot-carriage and pushes it away.

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: closed Tamil scene 46.md.
- The pregnancy disclosure—“wait another eight months”—is retained exactly as the scene's implication.
- The Tamil name-building joke cannot be reproduced mechanically in English spelling, so the source sound-parts Muth / than / thayi and the final mum-muth / tham play are retained transliteratively rather than reinvented.

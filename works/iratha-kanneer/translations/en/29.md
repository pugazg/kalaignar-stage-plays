# Scene 29

[In the Palaiyakkarar's palace!

Mayangatha Swamigal arrives by palanquin and comes inside. Baladevar, his wife and Poongavanam are there. Baladevar runs forward to receive the Swami. Seeing him arrive, Poongavanam grimaces. The Swami comes and sits down.

Baladevar: What brings you suddenly?

Swamigal: Yes—the Guru Puja at the monastery is approaching!—

Baladevar: What of it—we shall conduct it magnificently! What discourse is there at the monastery tomorrow?

Swamigal: I am going to speak on “The Path of Penance—and the Path of Chastity.”

Poongavanam, who dislikes standing there, begins to leave. But Sukadev's voice makes her turn back.

“Aththai! Have you heard the news? They put Muthan in fetters—paraded him through the streets—and took him away to Vengaipuram.”

Baladevar: Let him go! Let him go! What of it now—is that such great news?

Poongavanam: Yes, Anna! The suffering of the poor is never great news to you! He worked in our house like cattle. Why could we not have rescued him?

Baladevar: Are you mad, Poongavanam? Look, Swami! See who has become the object of my sister's pity! Some orphan or other!

Swamigal: Hmm! Everything happens according to fate. No one can stop it.

Poongavanam: Fine fate! A word that lazy men and cheats can grab very easily. Fate, indeed—fate!

Sukadev: Yes, Aththai! Appa being Palaiyakkarar is his fate! The Swami hating women is his fate! You wasting your years saying you want no marriage is your fate!

Poongavanam: You disgrace! That word “fate” has fallen into your mouth—that must be its fate!

Palaiyakkarar: Enough. Both of you shut your mouths. Why are you starting a war over a man who has gone away?

The Swami laughs indifferently. Poongavanam leaves. Where else would she go? She falls upon her bed! She weeps! As usual, those three figures look upon her in pity.

# Scene 18

[Muthan's room

Vedhalam: Yes—Muthan! How many more days are you going to lie here starving like this?

Muthan: Until food becomes available.

Vedhalam: Listen to me. The Palaiyakkarar has ordered that no one anywhere is to give you work. But you are a strong, well-built man. There is good strength in your body...

Muthan: So I can beat the Palaiyakkarar to pieces, is that it?

Vedhalam: Madman! If you listen to me, you will have no worries at all.

Muthan: You mean I can die?

Vedhalam: No, Appa, no.

Muthan: Death is the one thing in this world that comes running as soon as the poor call it.

Vedhalam: Muthan! Wealth for wealth. Rank for rank. Whoever sees you will fear you. Join the army.

Muthan: The army!?

Vedhalam: Don't be afraid—I will get you enrolled. The Palaiyakkarar has no authority to interfere in the affairs of the minor kingdom. So he cannot stop it either. What do you say?... Think!

[He goes inside.]

Muthan: [to himself]

A soldier!...

Yes. Muthayi! The right way to forget you!... The right way! Your father need not become a corpse because of me; nor need you become Lakshmi of poverty. Muthayi—you will be the wife of Prince Sukadev! I will be a soldier! I must not ruin you!... I am a soldier! A soldier in the army of Vengaipuram! I—a soldier in the army of the minor ruler who has enslaved Pazhuthur!—It is shame! But is there another way to forget you?... No. There is none at all. So I am a soldier! A soldier!

# Scene 11

Horse stable

Muthan is tying the horses properly and putting out their feed. Two eyes watch the scene with tenderness. Muthan, absorbed in his work, turns once and those eyes meet his. Yes—Poongavanam is standing there, looking at Muthan.

Muthan: Younger Queen! What do you require?

Poongavanam: Nothing, Muthan!... I only want to keep looking at you.

Muthan stands stunned. At that moment Poongavanam notices Thirusangu coming that way. At once she says to Muthan:

Poongavanam: Muthan! When your work is finished, come to my mansion before you leave! Don't forget! You must come.

She says this and goes. Muthan can think of nothing. The whole world seems to spin around him. His mind will not return to his work. Right where he stands, he sits down clasping his knees.

“Nothing, Muthan! I only want to keep looking at you.”

Those words make him close his eyes. His thoughts whirl.

“Come to my mansion before you leave! Don't forget—you must come.”

Those words have made him lose even his memory. He himself does not know how long he has been sitting there. At last he comes to his senses and gets up only after a kick from the hind legs of a horse.

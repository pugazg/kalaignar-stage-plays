# Scene 38

[Commander's mansion

This is Commander Vetrivelan's camp residence. Vetrivelan paces in the bedchamber, building castles in his mind: Muthayi will come, and once she comes he can forget this whole world.

“Muthayi! I have been waiting for you all day—the moonlight in the sky is waiting too. Angry girl! Maiden of cool speech! Why have you still not come?... Are those horsemen idiots? Or are the horses themselves too stupid to understand urgency? How long! How long! I said long ago that a shortcut should be built between Anandapuram and the capital! If this foolish king had listened, how quickly my Muthayi could have reached me now!”

As he wanders, babbling like this, the sound of galloping hooves reaches his ears. Vetrivelan rushes out to the front of the mansion. The horses stop. His eyes fall upon the woman sitting on one of them.

“Muthayi, have you come?”

In a single crazed rush he lifts her down and runs toward the bedchamber. Perhaps once a man believes he is carrying a woman's body, he no longer feels its weight.

He lays her on the bed, bolts the bedchamber door, and places two cups of liquor nearby.

“Muthayi, Muthayi,” he calls with overflowing affection.

She does not turn toward him. He notices that her mouth has been gagged.

“Ah—who closed the lips that pour out golden speech? Those horse-riding fools—idiots!”

He removes the gag.

That is enough. Sukadev immediately begins crying in a harsh voice.

Vetrivelan recoils and looks closely. Yes—not Muthayi! It is Sukadev!

Vetrivelan cannot contain his rage. He slaps Sukadev hard.

Sukadev: Ayya, lord! Do not hit me.

Vetrivelan: How did you come here?

Sukadev: Muthayi and I—she dressed as a man and I as a woman—we went to the riverbank to enjoy ourselves. That is when this danger happened, lord.

Vetrivelan: Fool! Get out of here without telling anyone!

Sukadev: My dear Muthayi! Thank goodness you escaped—where have you gone? Where will I search and find you?

Vetrivelan: Don't stand here—run!—

Trembling with fear, Sukadev runs from the room.

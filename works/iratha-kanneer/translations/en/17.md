# Scene 17

[Thendral Palace.

Men are being recruited into the army in Vetrivelan's presence. Vedhalam is there as well. Many recruits sit in a row. Each man is examined in turn and enrolled. Patting one young man on the shoulder—

Vetrivelan: Strong shoulders! You may soon become like me. Tell me your name—Veerappan? Excellent!—

Pointing to another man—

Vedhalam: This one's chest is very strong. In the army you will receive not only wages; prizes and medals will come in plenty.

The Man: That's right. Ayya, if I gave up the postal work and started telling fortunes, that would bring in good money too.

Vetrivelan pats him.

Vetrivelan: Excellent! A clever talker. He will have proper fame in the army!

Pointing to another and rubbing his head—

Vedhalam: Even if a shower of blows falls on this thambi, he won't break—his head is very heavy!

The Man: Then perhaps he can even become Minister of War.

Vetrivelan: Yes, yes!... All right! Now all of you have been enrolled in the army. You have signed as well. If any man who has joined decides tomorrow that he wants to leave—he must pay a penalty of one hundred gold coins.

Veerappan: Why would we ever leave?

Vetrivelan: Good. You may all go now. You may have to leave for Vengaiyur soon. Be ready.

Everyone bows to the commander and leaves. Vetrivelan looks at Vedhalam.

Vetrivelan: Vedhalam! Today you have brought ten men into the army, haven't you?

Vedhalam: Yes.

Vetrivelan: Here. [He gives him gold coins.]

Vedhalam receives them happily.

Vedhalam: Let the gold be, let the gold! Your complete favour is enough for me. Then... may I leave?

Vetrivelan: All right. Go—thank you!

Coming out through the entrance of Thendral Palace, Vedhalam says to himself:

Vedhalam: Goddess Lakshmi has begun to come to me! Vedhalam—move quickly forward on this very road! How many suitable ways there are in this world to earn wealth!

Sweet speech... gullible fools who are deceived by it... between those two lies the opportunity for men like me!

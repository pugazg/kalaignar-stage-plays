# Scene 56

[Street outside Sumathi's house

Muthan comes quickly away from Sumathi's house. A voice calls him. Startled, he turns. Sukadev is coming toward him. Muthan becomes afraid.

Sukadev: Do not be afraid, Muthan!

Muthan: What is it?

Sukadev: Vedhalam is roaming around disguised as a Swami.

Muthan: What of it now?

Sukadev: He deceived my beloved Muthayi and—I do not know what exactly—he is taking her somewhere.

Muthan: Ah!—

Sukadev: He must have taken her to his ashram. Come with me! I will show you. Please take care of Muthayi. I will immediately go and bring the guards of this town.

Muthan: First show me where Muthayi is!

Sukadev: Come, come!—

[The two run.]

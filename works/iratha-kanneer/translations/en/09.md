# Scene 9

[Thendral Palace

Commander Vetrivelan reclines at ease on a seat. Vedhalam sits opposite him with great respect.

Vetrivelan: I have heard a great deal about you. Baladevar too said you are very capable! You know why I have come. Can you help me with it?

Vedhalam: Me? That's a good one! I do not even have the strength to lift a sword...

Vetrivelan: I am not asking you to join the army. Catch hold of a few men for us. You must make the effort. How many unemployed men must there be?

Vedhalam: But people will be afraid to join the army!

Vetrivelan: Even a goat is afraid to approach the sacrificial altar. That is precisely why they sprinkle turmeric water on it—shower it with flowers—and hold food before it to tempt it!

Vedhalam: Well said! I will begin the work exactly as you order. One more request. There is a great Swami in this town. Everyone takes his words as the words of God. If we ask him to preach in favour of joining the army, our work will become easy.

Vetrivelan: Must we give him anything—to receive his grace?

Vedhalam: There is no need to put anything directly into his hand, because he will not touch and accept wealth. There is a donation box in the monastery. We merely have to put it there.

Vetrivelan: Excellent! We shall do exactly that. Vedhalam, I tell you this as well: for every man you get to join the army, you will receive five gold coins as wages! Not five in total—five gold coins for each man!

Vedhalam: As you wish. In that case, I am going to ask for an advance right now.

Vetrivelan: I will see to it. I will give you ten gold coins now.

Hey—who is there? Open the chest and bring ten gold coins!

Vedhalam can hardly contain his joy.

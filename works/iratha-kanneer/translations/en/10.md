# Scene 10

In the palace—the Palaiyakkarar's mansion. Baladevar sits stroking a puppy. His wife and Poongavanam are playing chess, while Baladevar watches with enjoyment. Sukadev enters quickly.

Sukadev: Appa! You must issue an order immediately.

Baladevar: An order?... What is it, Sukadev?

Sukadev: Muthan, the horse groom, must be dismissed from his work at once.

Baladevar: Why?...... He is a harmless donkey, isn't he?

Sukadev: You know our Thirusangu—the palace guard? Muthan grabbed his son's hand and pulled him. I condemned him for it.

Baladevar: All right... and then?...

Sukadev: On the cheek...

Baladevar: Ah... what...

Sukadev: [recovering himself] I gave him two blows. Proper ones!

Poongavanam: Thambi, Sukadev! You hit the horse. It threw you down. You hit Muthan—he kept quiet. So the man who kept quiet must be driven out of his job, while the horse gets fed grain. Fine justice this is!

Sukadev: Aththai! You need not say anything. You cannot know what happened.

Sukadev's Mother: Yes. Sukadev is right. Muthan cannot be trusted. A man who behaves wrongly with women is a very bad man.

Baladevar: All right, Sukadev. As you wish, drive Muthan out of the palace!...

Poongavanam: Poor fellow! He is an orphan without mother or father. If not with us, where will he go?

Baladevar: Poongavanam—you are right. His mother Kaveri Amma was a servant in our palace. When she died, she asked us to look after her son. That is the only reason I have tolerated Muthan all these years. For many days I have been hearing that he speaks wildly about the Swami of our monastery—and about divine matters as well. Poongavanam! I tell you too: I do not like the way you show him this kind of concern again and again...

Sukadev: Tell her that! Aththai, you dislike the whole world. You have remained a maiden saying you want no marriage at all. Why not think of God and roll your prayer beads—why involve yourself in all this trouble?

Poongavanam: Anna, did you hear how your son speaks?

Baladevar: Sukadev! Be quiet!

Poongavanam: It is a mistake for me even to stand here.

Hiding the turmoil in her eyes, Poongavanam leaves.

# Scene 59

[Prison

Muthan is confined. In a short while he is to be taken to the gallows.

A gentle breeze is blowing. After a few moments he will no longer be able to feel its touch. His beautiful eyes, now able to see that small piece of the world visible beyond the prison bars, will soon close.

Ayyo! Before that happens, will fate not allow the poor man to see once more the woman who fills his heart?

There! Muthayi has come.

It is true! Not the shadow of memory! The figure seen between the tears gathered in his eyes is not merely light playing within a diamond.

Muthayi is truly coming.

She opens her mouth to cry “Aththan!”—but cannot. Her chest tightens.

Muthan: My delight! Do not be shaken! Today is our last day! Our last meeting! I will never again see your eyes singing of love! I will never again taste the sweetness of your honey-pouring lips! Only another second or two. Beyond that, this poor Muthan cannot carry the sweet memories of you.

Muthayi: Aththan! I too will come behind you. No one can separate us!

Muthan: Mad girl! Some dreamer scribbled that a world has been built for lovers, and that dead lovers may go and live there! Do not believe that and turn yourself into a corpse too!

I who am about to give up my life ask only one promise from you—one command born of love...

Muthayi: Whatever it is, I will listen...

Muthan: You must live. You must not die.

Muthayi: I cannot live... Aththan.

Muthan: My dear! Even if I cannot see the treasury of our love growing in your womb, you will see it and rejoice. Thinking of that happy future, I draw one last breath of joy now.

Muthayi: Aththan!

Muthan: Do not cry—my dear! Do not cry! Let me see you once with a smiling face so my heart may cool. Come... my pure Tamil! Show me a smile!

Muthayi: Aththan!

Muthan: I kissed your wine-giving lips. The world did not like it—justice did not like it—law did not like it. So now I am going to kiss death.

Muthayi! I ask one last thing. Muthayi! I ask one last thing.

That love-song we used to sing together—where will I ever hear it again? Come—sing it! Sing! If you do not sing, your lover will not die in peace. Sing—my dear—sing!

Crying, Muthayi sings their love song. Muthan joins her in its final lines.

At that moment the guards open the prison door.

Muthan's final journey begins.

# Scene 39

[Ruined temple

Muthan and Muthayi sit in the hall of a ruined temple, absorbed in thoughts about the future.

Muthayi: Do you know how happy I was to hear that you were coming to free me?

Muthan: Do you know how happy I was at the thought that I was going to have you again?

Muthayi: No—my happiness was greater.

Muthan: No. More life lies in the smile of the person wearing a diamond than in the diamond's own brilliance. You are the diamond—I am the one who wears it.

Muthayi: Aththan—leave that aside. What is our next plan? Are we to live in this temple? I cannot even bear to look at these beautiful deities.

Muthan: Wherever we stay in this country, they will catch us.

Muthayi: Then where can we go?

Muthan: If we reach Siruthaiyur, we can escape. It is another country—an enemy of Vengaipuram! Vetrivelan's authority does not reach there.

Muthayi: Good—we go there. But what if the people of that country think we are spies and seize us?

Muthan: Yes—that too must be considered.

Muthayi: Then get up. Before it grows dark, let us go somewhere and find a way.

The two leave.

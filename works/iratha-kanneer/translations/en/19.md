# Scene 19

[Thendral Palace.

In the presence of Vetrivelan and Vedhalam, Muthan registers his name for the army.

Vetrivelan: Name?

Muthan: Muthan.

Vetrivelan: Father's name?

Muthan stares, unable to answer.

Vedhalam: That is not necessary.

Vetrivelan: Oh-ho!... All right. Do you at least know your mother's name?

Muthan: My mother was Kaveri—she has died.

Vetrivelan: All right—sign.

Muthan signs on a palm leaf. At that moment, thinking of his beloved, after writing “Muth” he writes “ayi”—and ends up writing “Muthayi.” Then he corrects it and writes “Muthan.”

Vetrivelan: Hmm—go on. Don't be afraid!

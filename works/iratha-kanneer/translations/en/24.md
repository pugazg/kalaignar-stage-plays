# Scene 24

[Entrance to the post house

The rough man—the postal runner—is grooming a horse. Sumathi comes there carrying the bag of gold.

Sumathi: Ayya!

Postal Runner: What do you want, girl?

Sumathi: Isn't Muthan here?

Postal Runner: Muthan? He is such a very good man. Why are you looking for him—are you in love with him?

Sumathi: You rogue! Answer only what I asked.

Postal Runner: He is at Thendral Palace. What is there for him to worry about—he is a soldier. And you are just the girl suited to him...

Sumathi: Shut your beautiful mouth, Appa!

Postal Runner: Daughter!

Sumathi: If Muthan happens to come here, tell him a girl came looking for him and has gone to Thendral Palace!

Postal Runner: Is that an order? Or a request?

Sumathi: Both—complete fool!

[She starts to go.]

Postal Runner: What, what?... Hey, little sparrow—what did you just say?

Sumathi: I spoke Tamil... hey, headache! I'm going.

Sumathi quickly leaves. The postal runner stands watching her go.

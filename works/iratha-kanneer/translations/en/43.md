# Scene 43

[Adaikkalapuri town

The front veranda of a wealthy merchant's house. The merchant and his wife stand there, the wife holding a child. Opposite them stands a young man with a large moustache, his back bent enough to be called a hunchback and walking with a limp. There is no need to say that he is Muthan. Muthayi stands beside him.

Merchant: What work can you do?

Muthayi: Whatever work is given, we will do without refusing it.

Merchant: If both of you can do household work, that will do...

Muthayi: Yes—he is lame and cannot attend to every kind of work.

Merchant's Wife: Will you raise my child safely?

Muthayi: I will, Amma!—look, even the child is reaching toward me. Come, my dear!

[She takes the child.]

Merchant's Wife: What did you say your name was—Ponni, isn't it?

Muthayi: Yes—and his name...

Muthan: Ah, let me say it myself—my name is Ponnan.

Merchant: Good. Ponni can look after the housework and the child. Ponnan! You... do whatever work you are able to do!

Ponnan: As ordered!

Merchant: There is a hut nearby—you may live there. Food will be given here. There is no separate wage!

Ponni: All right—

Ponnan: Many thanks. We will do as you say.

Merchant: Hmm—see to the work.

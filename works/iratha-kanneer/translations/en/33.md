# Scene 33

[Pazhuthur post house

Vedhalam opens the mailbag and examines the palm-leaf letters inside. Suddenly his eyes fall upon one letter.

“Ah! They are caught!” he cries, jumping up, and reads it.

The letter says:

“To dear Thirusangu, from Sukadev.

I am staying in Ananda Nagar, fifty kal from Vengaipuram. I have arranged the Glass Palace here for our marriage. Bring Muthayi here immediately and join me. I have completed all the arrangements for the wedding. Depart at once.

Sukadevan”

When Vedhalam finishes reading, delight overflows in him. At the same time, the lines forming upon his grotesque face reveal that a scheme is taking shape in his mind.

“A marriage between Sukadev and Muthayi!—according to Vedhalam's advice. Half the plan I laid for Vetrivelan's victory is already complete. Poor things—mice coming in search of the mousetrap: Sukadev—Thirusangu! Vedhalam! A proper reward is waiting for you. Muthayi in Vetrivelan's hands—a heap of pearls in Vedhalam's hands! Excellent! Excellent!”

Vedhalam springs to his feet.

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: closed Tamil scene 33.md.
- The source place form ஆனந்த நகர is rendered as Ananda Nagar here; later scenes use ஆனந்தபுரம் and are not silently harmonized.
- The source distance unit கல் is retained as kal rather than converted to a modern unit without source authority.
- Vedhalam's interception of Sukadev's private letter and his mousetrap metaphor are preserved.

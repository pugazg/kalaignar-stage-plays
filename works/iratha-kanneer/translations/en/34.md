# Scene 34

[Thirusangu's house

Muthayi sits in a corner, bathing her cheeks in tears. Thirusangu enters. In his hand is the letter written by Sukadev. He hides it.

Thirusangu: Muthayi!... Muthayi! Why are you always so sorrowful, child?...

Muthayi: You ask me the reason?

Thirusangu: It is only because I want you to be happy that I have struggled so much.

Muthayi: Appa!

[Unable to speak, she chokes and weeps.]

Thirusangu: What has happened now, child? Take comfort.

Muthayi: What use is it, Appa, to cut the root of a jasmine vine and then pour water upon its flower?

Thirusangu: I will not cut the root, child! Do not be afraid—everything will happen exactly as you wish.

Muthayi: Appa!

Thirusangu: Be cheerful! All your worries will disappear. There is also going to be a Guru Puja at the monastery. The whole town will be in celebration. You too be joyful, child!

Muthayi: Even beside light, a shadow remains dark. However many joyful events there may be, what medicine is there for my broken heart, Father?

Thirusangu: Daughter! Forget the mistakes I made. Ruined by greed for wealth, I behaved like a madman. Forgive this traitor who tried to tear apart your pure love, child!

Muthayi: Appa!

[Crying out, she clings to her father's feet.]

Thirusangu: Do not cry—I have decided to unite you and Muthan. Leave immediately!

Muthayi: Where, Appa?

Thirusangu: Listen, child! The Palaiyakkarar has learned that you love Muthan. The Palaiyakkarar does not want a marriage between you and Sukadev, so he has ordered that you be married to Muthan at once!

Muthayi: Ah! Their self-interest has wiped away my sorrow!

Thirusangu: The Palaiyakkarar has asked that Muthan be released. They too will let him go.

Muthayi: Will he come back?...

Thirusangu: He will not come here. Do you know the Palaiyakkarar's order?...

Sukadev has decided to carry you away and marry you. To escape that, the two of us must go immediately to a town called Anandapuram. Muthan will come there. Your marriage will be completed. For your welfare, the Palaiyakkarar himself is attending to every arrangement.

Muthayi: Appa!... I cannot believe myself. I bow to you for bringing this joyful news. If I have spoken harshly in my haste, forgive me!

Thirusangu: All right, child! Let us immediately make arrangements to leave for Anandapuram.

Muthayi: What other work could matter more to me than that, Appa!

They prepare to leave. A carriage arrives and stops at the entrance. Dancing with delight, Muthayi begins her journey to see her lover.

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: closed Tamil scene 34.md.
- Thirusangu's entire reconciliation is knowingly deceptive in the plot; the English preserves his statements without presenting them as narrator-confirmed facts.
- The source place form ஆனந்தபுரம் is rendered as Anandapuram, distinct from the Ananda Nagar form in Scene 33.
- Muthayi's line that the Palaiyakkarar's “self-interest” has erased her sorrow is retained because she recognizes the political motive even while believing the false story.

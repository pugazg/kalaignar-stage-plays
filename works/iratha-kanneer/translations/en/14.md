# Scene 14

[Thirusangu's house.

Thirusangu: My honour, respect and standing will all be ground into the dirt if this becomes known outside! Muthayi—no one will forgive what you have done.

Muthayi: They need not forgive me, Appa—just bless me wholeheartedly.

Thirusangu: Me? You ask me to approve it? No fool on earth would agree to walk into a cactus forest with his eyes wide open.

Muthayi: It is not a cactus forest, Appa! It is a grove of love!

Thirusangu: Shut your mouth! Love and all that are words that belong in books! Foolish girl—love for Thiruchira Narayanan? Does anyone put a hand into a snake mound asking for milk?

Muthayi: Appa! You always say my happiness is your happiness—my joy is your joy. Are you the one speaking like this? Have I no right to choose the man fit for my life?

Thirusangu: The life fit for you is appearing before you with mansions, terraces and temple towers. An iron strongbox itself is coming in search of you! And you are running after a beggar's bowl!

Muthayi: Appa, you have taken me, who grew up free, and turned me into a gambling piece for grabbing the Palaiyakkarar's wealth.

Thirusangu: Enough, Muthayi, enough! As long as I am alive I will never agree to your garlanding that penniless wanderer. If you refuse me—your wedding procession can move only after my funeral procession has gone. Yes. Remember that.

# Scene 26

[Thirusangu's house

Sumathi runs in. Muthayi has been waiting eagerly for her, and her eyes shine as she looks at her.

Sumathi: Muthayi!

Muthayi: Sumathi, what happened? Did you give it?

Sumathi: Just in time. If I had not run a little faster, they would have taken him away by now.

Muthayi: What—what?

Sumathi: Yes. They say the moment he said he was leaving the army, they put him under guard. An order has also come from the capital telling the commander to depart. They say everyone has already set out—and they have taken him too!

Don't be afraid! At that very moment I met Vedhalam and gave him the penalty gold. Otherwise they could not have released your man. That Vedhalam is a very good person.

Muthayi: Dear friend! I can never forget your help.

[She embraces her.]

Sumathi: Ayyo! Is this how you hug someone? You are practising on me the skill you learned from him!

Muthayi: Shh... you little rogue!

Sumathi: Come, let's watch the spectacle. They say Vetrivelan's procession is coming along the street! Come, come!

Muthayi: It is only Vetrivelan's procession!

Sumathi: Oh-ho—you won't like that. I want to see the procession of the two of you. Only then will delight bloom in you—your heart grow fragrant—enthusiasm pour out. All right, come, let us watch this one too.

The two come toward the doorway.

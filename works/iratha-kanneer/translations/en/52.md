# Scene 52

[Wealthy merchant's house

The wealthy merchant is sitting at the entrance to his house. Sumathi arrives.

Merchant: Who are you, girl?

Sumathi: A man called Ponnan works here, doesn't he?—

Merchant: Yes—he has gone out.

Sumathi: His wife is at my house. When he returns, tell him to come to my house immediately.

Merchant: Where is your house?

Sumathi: Number fifty, the next street.

Merchant: If he comes, I will tell him. Go!

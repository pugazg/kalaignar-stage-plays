# Scene 6

Muthan is walking along the side of the palace corridor. Poongavanam comes that way on her way to her room. She stands watching Muthan go without even blinking. Muthan, whose thoughts are wandering somewhere far away, does not notice that Poongavanam is staring at him as though she could devour him with her eyes. Why she delights so much in the movements of his body and the dignity of his walk is hard to understand.

Without her knowing it, a smile gathers on her lips. Before it can fully appear, tears begin to form in her eyes. Are they tears of joy? Or tears of grief rising at the thought of the hardships suffered in the palace by that young man who seems beauty itself?

Muthan goes away—disappears from Poongavanam's sight. But he does not seem to have disappeared from her heart. A deep, heavy sigh! Poongavanam enters her room.

What a riddle this is! The Palaiyakkarar's sister! The younger queen of Pazhuthur! She looks at Muthan the horse groom and lets out such a sigh. Hmm... what danger may be waiting for that Muthan!

# Scene 60

Baladevar, Poongavanam, the Swami, Sukadev and the elders come racing in palanquin-carriages. From their speed, it seems certain that they will rescue Muthan.

* * *

Muthan places one foot after another toward the gallows. He will never again be able to look back upon the road he has travelled.

In the distance the gallows waits with its mouth open to receive him.

Far away, Muthayi continues to sing the love song, lost beyond everything else.

Ayyo—is it truly she who sings? Or has sorrow itself taken the form of a yaazh and begun to pour out music?

Muthan walks on, surrendering his heart to that song.

His face is covered.

The world has disappeared from him.

A sound—“thadaar!”

That sound has carried away Muthan's life.

Now he need worry about nothing.

Poor Muthan has been released from this world filled with suffering.

# Scene 23

[Thendral Palace.

Muthan lies imprisoned in Thendral Palace. Like a tiger in a cage, he paces back and forth. Vedhalam enters. Muthan looks at him eagerly.

Vedhalam: Muthan!... What foolishness you have done... Couldn't you have thought before acting? Did I force you into the army? Vetrivelan is a brute... while you, poor fellow, are a very good man. By any sense of justice they should not keep you under guard! Who has ever heard of such injustice? I ran to the Palaiyakkarar and told him. He paid no attention to what I said. How can the voice of a poor man reach the master's ears?

At that moment Vetrivelan comes in.

Vetrivelan: What is this, Vedhalam? What are you spinning out? You may look at Muthan before you leave, if you wish! You have no permission to speak with him. He is now a caged bird. Step aside!

Vedhalam: Muthan lived with me, lord! Your Excellency! The affection born of familiarity is a terrible thing, is it not? Somehow forgive him...

Vetrivelan: Ha, ha, ha! Forgive him? Look at this royal order! The king has commanded me to leave immediately for the capital with all the soldiers recruited so far! We must depart today.

Vedhalam: Muthan too?

Vetrivelan: Yes! But if the hundred gold coins of penalty arrive, he can be released. Where would a man who lives in the rubbish heap find a hundred gold coins?

Vetrivelan leaves. Vedhalam pretends to look at Muthan with great sorrow. Muthan believes him.

Vedhalam: Muthan! It pains me even to look at you.

Muthan: Ayya, do me one favour.

Vedhalam: What favour? Tell me! I will see it done.

Muthan: A hundred gold coins will come to you, meant to be given to me. Take them, pay the penalty, and have me released!

Vedhalam: What work could I have more important than that?... The gold will certainly come, won't it? Muthan! There is nothing to fear now... remain without worry! Whatever happens... let the gold come—I will take care of everything.

Vedhalam leaves. The door is locked. Muthan resumes pacing inside the cage.

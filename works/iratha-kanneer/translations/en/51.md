# Scene 51

[Vedhalam's quarters

Sukadev returns from somewhere and knocks on the door. Inside, Vedhalam is fixing on his beard, while Vetrivelan sits nearby.

Sukadev: Swami! Swami!!

Vedhalam: Wait! My meditation is not yet over.

Sukadev: Open the door, Swami!

Vedhalam: Stay there, fool! I am speaking with God! About Muthayi!—

Sukadev: [to himself] Ah—he is speaking with God? We sinners cannot see Bhagavan ourselves. At least while the Swami is talking, let me secretly catch sight of Bhagavan!—

Saying this, he peers through a hole in the door.

That is enough to shock him senseless.

Sukadev: Ayyo! Ascetic! Vedhalam is climbing the murungai tree again! Good thing—I escaped! I escaped!

Crying out, he runs away.

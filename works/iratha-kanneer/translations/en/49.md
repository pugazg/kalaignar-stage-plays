# Scene 49

Muthayi lies unconscious beneath a tree. A woman comes along that way. She sees Muthayi lying there and runs over in alarm to lift her.

“Muthayi! Muthayi!” she calls.

Muthayi is unable to speak.

Fortunately, at that moment a horse-drawn palanquin carriage is passing. The woman stops it and, with the driver's help, lifts Muthayi into the carriage.

At exactly the same time as the carriage stops and the woman calls “Muthayi,” Swami Vedhalam and Sukadev arrive there.

Sukadev: There is my Muthayi!

He starts to run toward her. Vedhalam immediately stops him.

Vedhalam: Foolish young man! God has only just now shown you your beloved, and already you are leaping forward...

Sukadev: Ah—God is very good. How much trouble He has taken for me. God—thank you.

Vedhalam: Child devotee! Come—let us see where this palanquin carriage goes.

The carriage moves on. The two follow secretly, keeping themselves hidden. At last the carriage stops at the entrance of a small house. Muthayi is carried inside. Vedhalam and Sukadev watch.

Sukadev: Swami! Shall we go inside too?

Vedhalam: Wait, son!

Sukadev: I will wait.

Vedhalam: Come—let us return to our place. Now we know where Muthayi is. It is my duty to unite her with the man whom she ought to join!

Sukadev: Fulfil that duty quickly, Swami!

Vedhalam: So it shall be!—

The two leave.

* * *

Muthan searches for Muthayi beneath the fallen tree near the seashore. Unable to find her even there, he turns back broken-hearted.

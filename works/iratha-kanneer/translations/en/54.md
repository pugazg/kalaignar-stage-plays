# Scene 54

[Wealthy merchant's house

Muthan enters sadly. The wealthy merchant is chewing betel.

Muthan: I searched everywhere—my beloved is nowhere to be found.

Merchant: Everything is fine—she has been found. They say she is at house number fifty, Azhagar Street.

Muthan: Ah! Muthayi! Muthayi!

[He runs away. Hearing the unfamiliar name, the merchant stares after him.]

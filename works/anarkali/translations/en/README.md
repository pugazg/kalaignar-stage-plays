The translation preserves the work's dramatic structure, repetitions, courtly vocabulary, anti-rank rhetoric, `freedom of love` / `Delhi imperialism` contrast, live-entombment narration and final Salim lament rather than smoothing them into neutral prose.

Source-sensitive anomalies are handled explicitly. In particular, `என் திழையே!` is retained conservatively as `my thizhaiye!`; `என்னுள் உன் குடிசை பெருமையுற்றது` is translated without silently repairing the verified Tamil; and uncertain `சப்ரகூட மஞ்சம்` is kept as the *sabrakooda* couch.

The later 2009 comparison confirms broad scene/plot agreement while documenting a freer published style and several material divergences. Important examples include published smoothing of source anomalies, `அவள் அழுகை` rendered as `eternal beauty`, `நீதியில்லாத பூமியில்` rendered as `world without you`, and a changed royal-status causality in Scene 4. None of those published differences automatically controls the independent translation.

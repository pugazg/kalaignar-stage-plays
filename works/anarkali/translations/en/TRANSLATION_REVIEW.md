## Source-anomaly control

### Scene 1 — `என் திழையே!`

The verified Tamil source contains the unusual form `என் திழையே!`. The translation retains `my thizhaiye!` rather than guessing a normalized endearment. This is deliberate archival caution, not an incomplete transcription.

### Scene 2 — `என்னுள் உன் குடிசை பெருமையுற்றது`

The source phrase is grammatically unusual. The English deliberately keeps the awkward relation as `Within me, your hut has gained honour`; it is not silently repaired to an expected causal expression.

### Scene 2 — `சிம்மாசனமேறிகளை`

The verified source's unusual plural form is represented as `those who mount thrones`; the statement's anti-rank force remains visible.

### Scene 4 — `பஞ்சத்திலே பரப்ப`

Within the luxury/bedding sequence, the English uses the conservative contextual rendering `to spread upon the cotton bedding`. The Tamil source form remains untouched; the translation does not claim a source emendation.

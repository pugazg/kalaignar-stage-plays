# Scene 3

[Socrates is speaking in the marketplace.]

Soc. : O wondrous people who come to the market to buy goods! I have a rare thing that cannot be found in any of those places. A diamond whose price cannot be measured! A pearl of light drawn from bathing in the deep sea called inquiry! A commodity not found in any market stall! Knowledge! Knowledge! Come and buy this supremely sweet commodity! Come! Come!

[A Greek soldier enters.]

Soldier : Stop your sermon!........

Soc. : Even if I stop, I have many comrades to continue it!

Sold. : Don't talk gibberish! You are corrupting the young; you rave like a madman in the middle of the street!

Soc. : Go and tell those who sent you to me.... that this is not arugam grass for goats to graze down and destroy, but the teaching of those who build a new world! The light of thought is not a wild horse, brother, to become meek and submissive the moment the bridle of authority is put on it!....

Sold. : O over-preacher! Let the court hear your speech tomorrow! Come with me now!

[He pulls Socrates away.]

Crito : What injustice is this! Where are you dragging him, at such an advanced age.... our gold of knowledge—let him go, soldier, let him go!....

Soc. : No, friend, no...! Where am I going? Only to the court, isn't it? Not to the killing field!.... Let me go! So long as there is clarity in one's thought and strength in one's principle, people of wisdom need not fear inquiry or prosecution. I am going. I am going to meet directly those unjust men who drive those who show the path of knowledge toward the path of destruction, and begin battle with them.

[The soldier drags Socrates away.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/03.md`; no 2009 published-English wording was consulted.
- `அறிவு` is repeatedly retained as `Knowledge`, preserving the marketplace-as-knowledge-market metaphor.
- `அருகம்புல்` is retained as `arugam grass` rather than replaced with an unrelated generic plant.
- `கருத்தொளி` is `the light of thought`; the wild-horse / authority-bridle image is retained.
- The soldier's `அதிகப்பிரசங்கியாரே!` is rendered as `O over-preacher!` to retain its mocking force rather than smoothing it into a neutral address.
- The final contrast between `அறிவுப்பாதை` and `அழிவுப்பாதை` is retained as `path of knowledge` versus `path of destruction`.

# Socrates — Introductory Note

- Socrates, the philosopher given by Greece; the incomparable treasure of knowledge given by Athens!

- Plato, whom he produced, stands as a father to many of the political ideas of the present day.

- The philosophy of common ownership, prepared by Marx and Engels and refined like gold by people such as Lenin—Stalin, was itself first prepared in the workshop of the world of thought called Plato!

The German philosopher Hegal made use of Plato's workshop and gave a “jadai” toward the soured Nazi ideas. With that dagger, men like Hitler—Mussolini, base fellows, set out to use it on a wrong path and cut off heads!

Plato was the one who supplied the raw material for good things and evil things of this kind; and the one who produced that Plato was Socrates!

As history tells it, Socrates was not merely “the first man who did not fear death”; he was the possessor of many such qualifications!

Some may say that certain things in his speeches lie outside our views. But the movement of knowledge is not prepared to cast him aside on account of reasons equal to this dust.

Elder statesman Rajaji, in the book `‘Sogradhar’`, has made Socrates into an `astika-pazham` like Sankaracharya.

I, on the other hand, have portrayed here through this short play how he walked as a light of the world of knowledge.

Professor Sabain says that it is astonishing that, in Athens—the cradle of democracy—a great man like Socrates was put to death; he also wonders whether there might have been some politics hidden behind the curtain in this.

With this background, now begin to read!

[Short in stature—a strongly built body—eyes like lamps of light that keep turning—a flat nose—broad eyebrows full of movement—thick lips—a small beard—a single ordinary garment of silk and cotton, covering the whole body like a cloak—this is Socrates, the renowned scholar given by Greece! In the corners and byways of the city of Athens, in its lanes and alleys, he is an idealist who stops passers-by and engages them in argument. The first scene begins at the entrance to his house.]

## 2009 witness comparison checkpoint

`SECONDARY_WITNESS_COMPARISON.md` compares the locked independent translation with repository provenance records `sources/one-act-plays-2009/pages/0150.md`–`0160.md`.

The published witness contains the introductory framing and all five scenes, but it is generally freer and more normalizing. Material findings include:

- intro `பல தகுதிகளுக்கும்` rendered as `certain misdeeds`;
- source `அறிவுலக ஜோதியாக` replaced by an `intellectual who enjoyed his life` formulation;
- Scene 1 explanatory/proverb-like additions and omission of the verified closing stage direction;
- Scene 4 normalization of `சார் :` and `செடுக்கிறேனா`, plus a published `281...221` variant beside its own 281/220 vote count;
- Scene 5 conversion of `நாழிகை` to minutes, omission of the bare `: !........`, normalization of `சாச்ரடீஸ்`, and replacement of `அங்கிளிப்பியசு` / neighbouring-house `கோழிக் குஞ்சு` with the familiar `Asclepius / cock` formulation.

These are comparison findings only. No published wording was automatically imported.

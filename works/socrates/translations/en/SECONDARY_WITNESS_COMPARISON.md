The creditor line is the clearest example of why the published witness cannot be used as a backdoor authority over the Tamil archive: its `Asclepius / cock` wording is historically familiar, but the verified Tamil used for this project reads `அங்கிளிப்பியசு`, refers to a neighbouring house, and says `கோழிக் குஞ்சு`.

## Result

**PASS / COMPLETE — post-translation secondary-witness comparison for `சாக்ரடீஸ்`.**

The 2009 witness confirms the complete dramatic arc and many major images, but it is consistently freer than the independent archival translation. It normalizes unusual names/labels, adds explanatory language, converts traditional units, compresses source imagery/stage directions, and contains several material interpretive divergences. The most important controls are the intro `qualifications`→`misdeeds` shift, the altered `light of knowledge` portrayal, Scene-4 `சார் :` / `செடுக்கிறேனா` normalization and `281...221` variant, and Scene-5 `நாழிகை` conversions plus the `Asclepius / cock` creditor substitution.

No comparison finding by itself authorizes revision.

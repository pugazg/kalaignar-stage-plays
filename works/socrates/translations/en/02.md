# Scene 2

[Anytus, Meletus and Lycon are absorbed in deep thought.]

Anytus : A rumbling volcano! A raging sea! Socrates is more dangerous than these!..... A weapon of knowledge, he says..... a missile before which the whole world will bow, he says!..... If that weapon is manufactured in Greece, we will never be able to raise our heads, Meletus! What do you say, Lycon?.....

Lycon : I am a sculptor who gives a god's form to stone!..... At one time this Socrates too was a sculptor like me! But now he has become a sculptor of thought.... Never mind. Once I, who turn stone into god, Meletus—who is a poet who gives divine polish to bundles of imagination—and you, who are a political expert, have joined together.... Socrates is only a straw in the ocean, isn't he, Anytus.....!

Meletus : Bravo, Lycon, bravo.....! Only if the common people remain donkeys will they carry the bundles of lies we load on them! Only if they remain blind will they walk along the path we show them! Before that old man gives the eye of knowledge to the Greek people who have never crossed the line we drew, we must destroy him........

[The three leave with a decision.]

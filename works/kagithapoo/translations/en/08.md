# Scene 8

[At the Noble Gandhi True Workers' Association, Kannan stands before Gandhi's portrait with his hands raised in worship. The song “Long Live, Our Lord” resounds.]

Dasavatharam!: (When the song ends) Long live! Long live!

Kannan:— Well, well, come in, Dasavatharam...

Dasa.:— What, Kannan; are you well?

Kan.:— Mm... yes... Dasavatharam! What new avatar is this?

(Touches the khadi shirt.)

Dasa.:— You mean the khadi? Look here; twenty or twenty-five years ago, if you went around in khadi clothes like this, your skull would be cracked—your bones broken—and you'd be thrown into jail...... Now even a policeman bows his head at the sight of this new cloth!...

Kan.:— Dasavatharam! They say even Bhagavan took only ten avatars... looks as if there will be no counting your avatars... Instead of naming you as soon as you were born, they should have waited until your time was over... counted all the avatars you had taken... and then given you a fitting name...

Dasa.:— Enough of the teasing; I came mainly to see you...

Kan.:— Why? What do I have? A bus route... a permit,... a quota... a licence...? I've got nothing to hand out!...

Dasa.:— Not that; my boss said he wanted to see you... He's outside in the car... waiting...

Kan.:— Your boss; who?... A new boss?...

Dasa.:— No; the old one...

Kan.:— Che... che... che... It's been a long time since you left the old boss...

Dasa.:— That's him, man—our town chairman... Thiraviyam Pillai!...

Kan.:— Oh... Thiraviyam Pillai?... Chairman! Chairman! Why, Dasavatharam... I hear not a single resolution your chairman brings is passing in the municipal council....

Dasa.:— Yes...

Kan.:— I hear they're even violating government orders... and carrying on as they please......

Dasa.:— Hee hee hee...

Kan.:— I hear the councillors are coming to blows...... even *kaalanigal*...

Dasa.:— Yes... the *colony*... built for Harijans...

Kan.:— Hey... not that; *kaalanigal*.... I mean footwear! I hear even shoes are flying inside the municipal council...? 

Dasa.:— Let them fly.... let them fly...

Kan.:— I hear the government is thinking of dissolving the municipal council...

Dasa.:— Leave that alone; tell me anything, but don't say that! (At this, Dasavatharam flails his arms and legs like a man having a *kaakkaa-valippu* fit. For mischief, Kannan takes out a bunch of keys and places it in Dasavatharam's hand. Dasavatharam immediately settles down....)

Dasa.:— Ah! What's this, a bunch of keys? Did you think I had *kaakkaa-valippu*?

Kan.:— No *valippu*; only the *kaakkaa*.

Dasa.:— The one functioning here is our Congress municipal council!... Would they poke out our own eye with our own finger?....

Kan.:— Yes, yes—only other people's eyes should be poked out...

(The president of the association enters, arms spread wide as he calls to someone, “Come! Come!! Please come, great sir!”....)

President:— Kannan... Chairman Thiraviyam Pillai is coming... he was sitting in the car outside... I'll go and bring him in... Can we keep a great man waiting?...

Thiraviyam:— (Entering) It's all right... it's all right... What, Dasavatharam, you've planted yourself down here completely!...

Dasa.:— No, sir... this younger brother here... Kannan, secretary of the True Workers' Association—

Thiravi.:— I've heard of him. I've heard a great deal... a great speaker... a rare man of sacrifice... a genuine worker... I've heard it all...

Dasa.:— And this is Sathyanathan, the president of this association...

Thiravi.:— I know, man, I know... Did you tell young Kannan what we came about?...

Dasa.:— Haven't started yet... I'm just about to start...

Thiravi.:— You're “late” in everything, man... From now on we should call you “Late Dasavatharam”.... All right... all right... come to the matter...

Dasa.:— That is... respected President!... Mm... mm... President Sathyanathan, sir... and Kannan-u! Our chairman, Mr Thiraviyam Pillai, is going to stand in the coming general election...

Thiravi.:— (Angrily) Dasavatharam! Is that what you say first!... Shut your mouth!... I'll tell them!... That is... I have been working very hard in the Congress for a very long time... that is, for seven or eight months.......

Kan.:— Oh! Father! Seven or eight months! The age of Bal Gangadhar Tilak!

Thiravi.:— (Privately, in a subdued voice, to Dasavatharam) Who are all those people, man?

Dasa.:— (Softly) I don't know either.

Kan.:— Yes... when the Salt Satyagraha happened, did you take part?...

Thiravi.:— Oh... I watched it from the upper balcony of our house...

Kan.:— When the liquor-shop picketing happened—

Thiravi.:— What, man; don't you know? The picketing happened right in front of my shop...

Kan.:— Have you been to jail?...

Dasa.:— Kannan!.... You rush into questions—how can you look at him and ask whether he's been to jail?... He went to jail even last week...... Don't you know he's one of the jail visitors?

Sathyanatha.:— Why keep drilling him with questions... Today he's the one holding the Congress together..... If only you knew what we came about.......

Dasa.:— He... out of his large-heartedness... has expressed a wish to make himself a patron of this “Noble Gandhi True Workers' Association”....... And you should regard it as your good fortune...

Sathya.:— What objection could there be?... With the goodwill of great men like you, this association will flourish like a banyan tree... What do you say, Kannan!?...

Kan.:— President!... This is a camp of true volunteers! A workers' encampment functioning in the name of Noble Gandhi! I do not want to turn it into a tent of selfish men... a hunting ground for those mad for office.

Sathya.:— Kannan! Isn't welcoming the guest a Tamil virtue!...

Kan.:— True!... The pearl-oyster in the sea opens its mouth only to welcome drops of rain... The Congress may be ready to welcome sewage as well; but this Gandhi Association cannot make room for it!...

Sathya.:— Kannan!... Speak respectfully in the presence of great men!

Kan.:— Showing respect is one thing... kneeling is another... I will not agree to the second...

Dasa.:— Kannan... this isn't nothing... He's to be the patron of this association, man, the patron... money whenever we want it... facilities whenever we need them... at election time...,

Kan.:— This hall is not a stage for your divine games... Impossible!... Talk about something else!

Thiravi.:— Kannan!... Do you care for the Congress or not?

Kan.:— I do...

Thiravi.:— Should it win the election or shouldn't it?...

Kan.:— And for that.......?

Thiravi.:— It can happen only if people like me support it, can't it... my money... your labour... speech... breath... writing... together will make the Congress win... If I win, it means the Congress has won... doesn't it?...

Kan.:— Ayyo... my ears cannot bear to hear this... Wait a moment; I'll turn the Mahatma's portrait around and come back... Then you may speak.

Sathya.:— (Stopping him) Kannan! In my capacity as president of this association... I have decided to accept him as patron.

Kan.:— Ah! Very well—I will not tell you to change your decision... Even if you changed the decision for my sake... I cannot change the heart of yours that has already bent before the wealthy... So I will change my place. I'm leaving...(He starts to go.)

All three:—
Thiravi.:— Kannan!
Sathya.:— Kannan-u!
Dasava.:— Kannan!

(With passion.)

Kan.:— Become patrons! Heap up wealth!! Conduct general elections!!! You fake Congressmen! You new arrivals! Before you, I take this vow.... I will have no rest... no sleep... until I drive all of you out and turn the Congress into the camp of true workers... I'm leaving...

(Kannan leaves.)

Thiravi.:— No rest... no sleep... He left without saying, “Why, no food either”....

Dasava.:— Under our rule, that's obvious without saying it...

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/08.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- `தசாவதாரம்` is transliterated as `Dasavatharam`; the source's joke about Bhagavan's ten avatars and the character's many “avatars” is therefore preserved.
- `கதர்` is rendered as `khadi` and the source's contrast between earlier repression and present political prestige is retained.
- The `காலணிகள்` / `காலனி` joke depends on a Tamil near-homophone (“footwear” / “colony”). The Tamil forms are briefly retained in the exchange so the misunderstanding remains visible in English.
- The `காக்கை வலிப்பு` / `வலிப்பு` / `காக்கா` exchange is source wordplay with no clean English equivalent. The key Tamil terms are retained rather than replaced with an invented English pun.
- `போஷகர்` is rendered as `patron`; `பாடி வீடு` in Kannan's final vow is rendered as `camp`, retaining the source's political-military image.
- `and Kannan-u` preserves Dasavatharam's comic Tamilized English code-switching in the source.

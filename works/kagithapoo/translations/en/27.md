# Scene 27

## Ruined mandapam.

[Kannan enters carrying the flag...]

Terrifying voices:— Hey, Kanna! Stop!

Kan.:— Who?

Voice:— Put the flag down!...

Kan.:— I am a worker of the Kazhagam, descended in the line of heroes such as Kumaran—Chinnasami—Sivalingam—Aranganathan—Veerappan—men who did not fear death...

Voice:— Mm! Put the flag down!

Kan.:— First put your order in the rubbish!

Voice:— Say, “*veru pakkam*—I will not speak condemning smuggling—profiteering—the black market”; otherwise... murder!... murder!!!

Kan.:— Ha ha haa... Oh-ho... black market—smuggling... profiteering... adulteration—black-market trade—have you come with a murder weapon as representatives of all of them? Great gentlemen! Great sinners! There is the “paper flower” to protect you... go there...

Voice:— Don't go—stop!...

Voice:— Put the flag down!... Kneel and ask forgiveness!... Otherwise... a bullet from the gun will pierce your chest!... Don't die in vain...

Voice:— Don't die unjustly!...

Voice:— Don't die!

Kan.:— Ha ha ha! Madmen! In the sea of blood made by spear and sword, seeing the broken pieces of bone travelling along as though kissing the skulls of warriors floating with terrible breaths—and the shreds of severed nerves—without singing *Mukhari*, without showing one's back, without grimacing, beating the war-drum cry, “Forward, you! Forward, you!” and putting courage into the hearts of the warriors who remain; never forgetting, even by mistake, the battlefield primer that says it is not becoming of warriors, standing on the crest of death, to count the seconds wondering from which side at what moment a spear may fly, or an enemy's arrow may fly; the one who leads an army with the strength of mind that death—the bouquet given to a great warrior—is sweeter than a maiden's lips, he alone is the valiant one—I am the one who has read the special prefatory verse of the camp-epic called *Paindhamizhan*. Therefore I will not fear death; but I will not die without a cause...

Voice:— Is that so?... Ha ha ha...!

Voice:— Ha ha ha!...

Voice:— Prepare yourself for death!

(Kaveri and Maragatham come running.)

Kaveri:— Kanna! Kanna!

Kan.:— Amma!...

(A gunshot is heard.)

Kaveri:— Ah!

(When the gun is fired at Kannan, Kaveri comes between them.)

[Kaveri falls dead...]

Kan.:— Amma!...

Marag.:— Amma! Amma!!

Kan.:— Amma! Amma!... Have you gone, Amma!... O mother of a warrior lineage who commanded your son to reject what is evil and establish what is sacred!... O great lady of *Purananuru*! By giving your life and saving mine, you have lit a jeweled lamp to the glory of motherhood... But you have placed upon me the endless blame of being a son who could not save his mother; why, mother; why?...

Mother! Father's blood was used for the growth of Tamil... let your blood be used to defeat the enemies of Tamil! Let your red blood become the mark of valour for the great army of Tamils!... Let it become the mark of victory!...

Let this red of blood become the red of the crimson sky—the red that announces that the rising sun is about to emerge in the eastern direction!... Amma! Amma!...

(Kannan takes the blood flowing from Kaveri's body, applies it as a *tilak*, and makes a vow.)

(The End)

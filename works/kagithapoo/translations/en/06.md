# Scene 6

## Location: Road.

[Maragatham and her friend Mala walk along talking after the play has ended.]

Maragatham: Mala! The role of Venmal suited you, girl.... Ah! *Ansu* acted just like Anna; *sombu veeramatham*......

Mala: Maragatham! All the students are praising you for arranging a play like this... and making Tamil Martyrs' Day so special... Ever since you became secretary... the Student Forum itself has gained a new standing, Maragatham... Why, womankind itself has gained a new standing...

Maragatham: Hey.... hey.... Mala!... Don't praise me too much!... What great service have we done for the world of women?... So many women of sacrifice and fidelity have been lamps of light in this southern land. They still are!...

Mala: They still are? Who, Maragatham?

Maragatham: We have read in the *Purananuru* those heroic lines about a mother who, even after losing both her husband and her own kin in war, combed and tied the hair of her little boy and sent him to the battlefront... But there is no *Purananuru* song for the women of sacrifice I say are living now... Yet those heroic mothers do exist, Mala; they do exist.

Mala: Maragatham?

Maragatham: When all Tamil Nadu became a field of burning embers—a field of wounds—in the uprising to protect Tamil, there was a Tamil mother who gave her beloved husband alive to the field of righteous struggle and now stands in anguish, bereft of her *tali*; and she keeps kindling Tamil feeling in me!... Will you come and see that fiery spark named Kaveri! That embodiment of chastity! Will you come, Mala?

Mala: Maragatham! If I took the soil from the feet of such a Mother Tamil and placed it upon my head... even a coward's heart would gather courage! Show me, Maragatham; show me that queen of chastity!

Maragatham: Come, let's go...

(The two leave.)

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/06.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- The verified source phrase `அந்சு ... சொம்பு வீரமாதம்` is not semantically secure in the closed Tamil layer. It is therefore conservatively carried as the transliterations *Ansu* / *sombu veeramatham* instead of being silently repaired into a familiar modern reading.
- `புறநானூறு` is transliterated as *Purananuru*; the war-mother image and its martial force are retained.
- `தாலி` is retained as *tali* because the source's widowhood image depends on the culturally specific marriage emblem.

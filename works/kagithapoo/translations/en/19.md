# Scene 19

Location: Road

(Dasavatharam comes along talking to himself.)

Dasava.:— Yama, is it... what Yama is going to come anyway... what a thoroughly frightened man... Even if the sky were to fall on the crown of my head, there is no fear—no fear—there is no such thing as fear...

(Suddenly Yama stands motionless in front of him.)

Dasa.:— Ah!!... Yama...

(He closes his eyes and sits down. In the darkness Yama disappears—and a rough-looking worker stands there, twirling his moustache.)

(After staring closely at him—breathing a deep sigh.)

Dasa.:— It isn't Yama?...

Worker:— I'm a voter... I have a ballot paper in my hand...

Dasa.:— Voter!... Voter!! Oh, father, in the coming election I'm the one who's going to come as Yama for you...

(He leaves.)

Worker:— (Standing and staring after him) Yama!... Hey, fool! Where are you, man? Saviour in danger! Protector of the orphaned!...

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/19.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- Dasavatharam's opening `அச்சமில்லை` repetition is kept as a theatrical self-reassurance refrain rather than reduced to prose.
- The scene's Yama/voter inversion is retained literally: Dasavatharam first fears Yama, then boasts that he will become the voter's Yama in the election.
- `ஆபத்பாந்தவா! அனாத ரட்சகா!` is rendered closely as “Saviour in danger! Protector of the orphaned!” to preserve the worker's mock-invocation register.

# Scene,

Location: Road

(Loudspeaker announcement...)

“Congress Democratic Socialist Conference... under the chairmanship of intense man of sacrifice Thiraviyam Pillai... the torch of equality Dasavatharam will inaugurate it... famous speaker Kannan will launch the election campaign... Democratic Socialist Conference... in the procession a thousand buses... five hundred lorries... two thousand motor cars... will come in formation... Come and see the Congress Socialist Conference... Socialist Conference...”

(When the announcement stops... Dasavatharam comes majestically from that side... behind him a man comes carrying a Congress flag... there is a garland around Dasavatharam's neck...)

Dasa.:— Aha! You've come all the way to the railway station to welcome the conference inaugurator... Did only you come... didn't anyone else come?...

Flag-bearer:— Lots of people came... everybody... went to welcome that cinema actress...

Dasa.:— Let them go... let them go... at least four people to welcome the leader...

Flag-bearer:— Will four people be enough?...

Dasa.:— Add one more man and go.

(A man comes running from the opposite side, looking upward and leaping as he cries “Don't let it go! Don't let it go!”, and falls on Dasavatharam. His name is Ramu.)

Dasa.:— Hey, hey, why are you coming along looking upward and falling on people?... Huh?

Ramu:— At the hotel... he brought a dosa and put it down... the moment he switched on the fan... the dosa started flying... I'm running to catch it...

Dasa.:— Don't worry... let our Socialist Conference finish... dosas won't fly after that... then man himself will fly; into outer space. Without a rocket...

(Govindan comes along eating ration cards.)

Dasava.:— What's this, man... eating the card?...

Govi.:— A card, is it?... It's a ration card, sir!... They gave the card... no rice... not a thing; so I thought I'd at least eat the card...

Dasa.:— Hey... hey... sinner! All of you come to the conference, man; socialism is going to come... after that you won't have to eat the card.

Go.:— Then what, can we eat rats?... After that you'll tell us to eat cats!

Dasa.:— You fool!... If you eat the rat, can you avoid eating the cat?... If only the rat goes inside the stomach... it'll start stealing in there... shouldn't we send a cat to chase and catch it?... A cat to chase the rat... a dog to chase the cat... a man to chase the dog... All right... all right... everybody come to the conference... come... come...

(A broker drags along an old mendicant.)

Dasava.:— Hey, hey, where are you dragging that old mendicant, man?...

Broker:— Not for nothing... I've paid thirty rupees... for a family-planning operation...

Dasa.:— Aha, a sixty-year-old mendicant... undergoing family planning... Everyone come to the conference, let the people see this exhibition... Do you see how the Congress socialism family-planning scheme is being *amul*—implemented?...

(Running in...)

Krishnan:— Amul! Amul! Where can I get Amul... where can I get Amul baby food... tell me...

Dasa.:— Ah... you can get it at our conference... Everybody come... not bad; the crowd has gathered somehow!

(They leave.)

(Curtain)

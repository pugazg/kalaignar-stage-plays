# Scene 9

Location: Entrance of the Noble Gandhi True Workers' Association

Dasava.:— Ah, at last! Somehow we've taken over the True Workers' Association... Dug into a mountain and caught a mouse.

Thiravi.:— What's the use of catching a mouse, man...

Dasa.:— What's so lowly about a mouse? After we win the election in '67, just watch... the rat will be India's national food!

Thiraviyam:— Dasavatharam! What does it matter whether the True Workers' Association without Kannan is in our hands or not? His fiery speeches—his clear writing—his ability to run around and get work done—not only will all that be of no use to us; if he stands on a Congress platform itself and attacks me, when I'm going to stand as the Congress candidate, I won't be able to withstand it, man... Look here... whatever it costs, never mind; here's five hundred rupees! Give him an advance!... Tell him I'll give thousands and thousands... Make him our man.

Dasa.:— Hee hee hee... At the sight of five hundred, his mouth will fall open. Boss!... I'll bring him around... you stop worrying!...

Thiravi.:— Leave right now! Wherever he is, find him! Finish the job... Kannan must be in my hand!... Bring me that news of victory!....

Dasa.:— So be it... Hey, driver, bring the car for sir... I'll go and look for Kannan at her house... Jai Hind.

Thira.:— Jai Hind!!

(Curtain)

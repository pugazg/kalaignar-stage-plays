# Scene 26

## Kaveri's house

[Kaveri and Maragatham are there.]

Maragatham:— Tell me, Amma; did I fulfil what I told you or not?...

Kaveri:— Maragatham! You have removed the sorrow that I had no daughter... For Kannan, who has lost his brave father, to get a brave younger sister like you is truly the good fortune he earned.

Kaveri:— Amma! Kannan leaving the Congress... has made some people even angrier than the Congressmen themselves, Amma!...

Kaveri:— What can be done... can we fear the town when speaking the truth?... The black-marketeer, the black-market man—every one of them is Kannan's enemy. If Kannan or the Kazhagam throws dirt into their livelihood, naturally such people will become angry...!

(Mala comes running in.)

Mala:— Amma! Amma! Maragatham! Danger! Kannan is in danger!...

Kaveri:— Ah!

Mala:— Ah!... What is it, Mala?

Mala:— I overheard four or five people talking inside the ruined mandapam at the edge of our town... They've made a plan to murder Kannan at night when he returns after finishing the meeting...

Kaveri:— Ah!...

Mar.:— I even saw a gun in one man's hand... I ran here because I was afraid they might see me...

Kave.:— Ah! Kanna!... Maragatham... what shall I do?...

Marag.:— Don't panic, Amma... come, let's go to that place... Mala!... You go... inform the nearby police station...

(The three set out.)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/26.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- The source contains an unusual speaker-label sequence: the third and fourth opening speeches are both labelled `காவேரி:—`, and the line `ஆ!... என்ன மாலா?` is labelled `மாலா:—`. The English preserves those labels as `Kaveri` and `Mala` rather than silently reassigning them by dramatic expectation.
- The later abbreviated source labels `மர:—`, `காவே:—`, and `மரக:—` are retained as `Mar.:—`, `Kave.:—`, and `Marag.:—`.
- The locked livelihood phrase is translated only at its transparent dramatic sense as “throws dirt into their livelihood”; no Tamil correction is made.

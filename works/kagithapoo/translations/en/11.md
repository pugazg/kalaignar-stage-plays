# Scene 11

Location: A weaver's house.

[The weaver Murugan sits on a rope cot with his knees drawn up... His wife Meena enters...]

Meena:— Why are you sitting like this, hugging your knees? Pongal is being celebrated from house to house; is there to be nothing at all in our house?...

Murugan:— What Pongal is there in a poor weaver's house, Meena? Manickam in the mansion—the Kubera with crores—all of them celebrate Pongal! In the house of the one who has, there is ven-pongal—sakkarai-pongal; in the house of the one who has nothing, there is a pongal of tears—a pongal of sorrow.

Meena:— The child is standing in the street crying... Anandhan next door is eating sugarcane; he asks us to buy him some... Where am I supposed to go and buy it?...

Muru.:— It isn't only the child in our house, Meena; in this country, the children of many lakhs of handloom-weaver families—not only today, but every day—shed tears because they cannot get even gruel, let alone sugarcane. It's all God's doing!

Meena:— Is it enough to sit there saying it's God's doing? There must be Pongal in our house—one festival in the whole year—find some way!

Muru.:— What can I do, Meena... A government that will wipe the tears of handloom weavers still hasn't come... Can't those blessed people show to weavers who are *kathar-ing* in distress the same concessions they show to *khadi*?

Muru.:— We sold the thaali and ate! We sold the dhotis and shirts and ate! The sari you're wearing is all that's left... Give that too...

Meena:— Ayyo... Are we to sell even the sari and celebrate Pongal?...

Muru.:— If not, just keep quiet, you poverty-stricken woman! Why are you taking a man's life?

Meena:— Why are you getting angry?... My heart couldn't bear the child crying; that's why I said it... Here... if there is no other way... I'll give you this sari... Sell it and buy a Pongal pot, rice and everything... Here, I'll give it...

[She shuts the door and, through the gap, rolls up and throws out the single sari she had been wearing...]

[Murugan leaves with the sari, overflowing with sorrow.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/11.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- The source repeats Murugan's speaker label across the former physical page boundary; the English retains the two separate turns rather than silently combining them.
- `வெண்பொங்கல்` and `சர்க்கரைப் பொங்கல்` are rendered as `ven-pongal` and `sakkarai-pongal` so the repeated `பொங்கல்` rhetoric remains audible.
- The line contrasting `கதர்` (khadi) with `கதறிக்கிட்டு` (crying out in distress) is a Tamil sound-play. The English briefly retains *kathar* beside *khadi* rather than inventing a different pun.
- `தாலி` is retained as `thaali`, consistent with the marital imagery already established in the translation.

# Scene 12

Location: Road

(The weaver Murugan is selling a sari in the street.)

Murugan:— Sir... sir... do you want this sari?

A man:— Why are you selling an old sari, poor fellow!

Murugan:— There's starvation at home, sir... We have to celebrate Pongal; give me whatever you can and take this...

(They spread their hands and go away.)

(Dasavatharam enters.)

Muru.:— Sir....

Dasa.:— Che... what a lazy fellow!

Muru.:— I'm not lazy, sir; I've never lived in luxury, sir... I've worked and worked until I'm worn out. I struggled, weaving cloth, and spent my life that way. It isn't that I have no hands to work with. It isn't that my body lacks strength. But where is the way to work? If only I could get yarn... I'd turn it into cloth and earn a quarter-coin... Where do I get yarn now?... I wandered—I roamed—I asked—there is no way for me to work. What other fate is there for a poor weaver like me? The loom sleeps at home—will hunger go to sleep? What am I to do?... Sir, it's the Pongal festival—at least one day in the year we want to be without hunger—buy this sari for your housemaid... give me a little money, whatever it is, swami...

Dasa.:— Aha, how much affection you have for our housemaid!... Is my job to buy saris for the maid?... Get lost. Lazy fellows... They lie at home thinking the government will bring rice and put it into their mouths... Look at Russia. Our leader went there and came back and told us. There, *angavanum—pavanum* works, it seems...

Murugan:— Are we not working here?... You sinners!... Go tell your leader to look at the labourers on Madras Mount Road, their backbones bent, pulling cartloads in place of cattle!... Go tell your leader to see the suffering of the farmers wasting away in the fields, driving off exhaustion through fear and turning their blood into pools of sweat!... The man boiling tar—the man standing in the furnace heat—the man cleaning drains—the man carrying excreta—the man building houses—the man stitching sandals—if all these people lay down inside their homes, are they asking you for rice?.... We know the fine way you feed us—a rice queue half a mile long....

Dasa.:— Hey, hey... stop, stop.... A rice queue half a mile long! Looks like your mouth is a mile long... Came to sell a sari, did you, a sari... without selling your *pulla-kutti*..... Hey... From now on don't talk in miles; talk in kilometres!

Muru.:— You sinner... what did you say?... Sell my *pulla-kutti*?

(He leaps at him.)

Dasa.:— Touch me and you'll find out!... What did you think?... (Showing his khadi shirt.) The army itself will come... Be careful!

(He leaves.)

Muru.:— Ayyo... Meena... have I come to such a state that I must hear such cruel words, Meena...

(DMK comrades enter with a handloom-workers' song.)

Muru.:— Sir... sir... I will give...

DMK:— We know... We've been coming along hearing everything...

Muru.:— If I sell this sari...

DMK:— No... take it back and give it to your wife. Mm... first keep this money and celebrate the Pongal festival properly......

(Kannan watches from a distance.)

(The DMK men leave.)

Muru.:— (With tears of joy) Long live the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam. Long live the DMK.

(As he goes quickly and joyfully, Kannan stops him...)

Kannan:— Sir... you are?...

Muru.:— (Looking him up and down) A poor weaver, sir... Under your party's rule, it's come to the point where a man has to sell his wife's sari to celebrate Pongal...

Kan.:— Where is your house?...

Muru.:— My hut too is by the drain where all the weavers live... Why do you ask—are you coming to eat the Pongal feast?...

Kan.:— Mm....

(He shakes his head to say no.)

Muru.:— I'm going.

(He leaves.)

(Kannan walks on sorrowfully—Dasavatharam approaches from the opposite direction.)

Dasava.:— Well, Kannan... I've been wandering around looking for you....

Kan.:— Why?

Dasa.:— (Taking out money and giving it to him) Here... there's a 250-rupee advance... Thiraviyam Pillai told me to say he'll give you whatever you need whenever you ask... In the coming election you're the chief speaker... It's been decided to hand all responsibility over to you... Nobody will cross the line you draw... It's up to you to see that sir wins... Here, take it... an auspicious moment... Amirtha Yogam...

(Kannan looks at the money.)

Dasa.:— Why are you staring like that... Think they're counterfeit notes?... Shall I have it given as a cheque?....

(Kannan shakes his head.)

Dasa.:— Mm... take it...

(Kannan takes it.)

(Kannan remembers the poor weaver.)

Murugan's voice:— “A poor weaver, sir... Under your party's rule, it's come to the point where a man has to sell his wife's sari to celebrate Pongal!” Sell his wife's sari and celebrate Pongal... it's come to the point of selling his wife's sari to celebrate Pongal...

(Kannan comes to a decision.)

Dasava.:— Kannan!... Shall we go to the boss's house?...

Kan.:— Dasavatharam... I'll come in the morning...

Dasa.:— Will you definitely come?...

Kan.:— I'll come.

(He goes off quickly.)

Dasa.:— (To himself) That's it; he's taken the money... Now he won't change...

(He leaves.)

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/12.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- The verified source phrase `அங்கவனும்—பவனும்` is semantically insecure in the locked Tamil layer. It is retained as *angavanum—pavanum* rather than silently normalized.
- `புள்ள குட்டி` is retained as *pulla-kutti* in Dasavatharam's taunt and Murugan's shocked repetition because the source's colloquial force and precise referent are not cleanly reproducible with one English kinship phrase.
- `தி.மு.க.` is rendered as `DMK`, while Murugan's full slogan retains `Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam` before the abbreviation.
- `அமிர்த யோகம்` is retained as `Amirtha Yogam`, preserving the source's mock-auspicious sales pitch.
- In Murugan's remembered voice, the locked Tamil has one anomalous repeated form `விந்து` amid repetitions of `வித்து`. The English preserves the repeated meaning in the dramatic line and records the source anomaly here rather than treating it as a Tamil-layer correction.

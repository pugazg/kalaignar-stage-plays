# Scene 10

Location: Kaveri's house

[Kaveri is hanging a portrait of Anna beside her husband's portrait... Hearing a voice call “Kaveriyamma,” she turns... Dasavatharam enters.]

Dasa.:— Ayyo... sacrilege, sacrilege... what an outrage this is... you've gone and hung the Kanchipuram man's portrait here...

Kave.:— Why?... Why are you writhing as though you've stepped on fire? What if this portrait is here?

Dasa.:— Kaveriyamma... Isn't that DMK the party that left your neck without a thaali?... And isn't this Annadurai the leader of that party?

Kave.:— Sir!... Hearing your treacherous words, fire rises in my eyes like Kannagi's... But... the calm gaze of the leader from Kanchi tells me to forgive you... that is why you escape... Never forget: my thaali and my turmeric were not taken away by the army that fought to protect Tamil... They were taken away by Tamil traitors who lost their self-respect and dared to put their mother up for sale!

Dasa.:— If this man hadn't started the agitation against Hindi... unrest all over the country... destruction... riots... police firing... many people dying like your husband... would any of this have happened?... So isn't the man who started the agitation the cause of everything?

Kave.:— True! We must punish those responsible, shouldn't we?...

Dasa.:— Yes! Punish them properly!

Kave.:— Why did the anti-Hindi agitation begin?...

Dasa.:— What kind of question is that! Hindi came; so there was an anti-Hindi agitation...

Kave.:— If Hindi had not come... there would have been no anti-Hindi agitation, would there? So the cause of the agitation was those who brought Hindi. The cause of my losing my turmeric and flower was those who brought Hindi... Now tell me... whom should we punish? Whom should we punish?

Dasa.:— Leave all that aside... What is one Anna portrait? Tomorrow I'll bring you five or six more portraits of DMK leaders; hang those too... I've forgotten the work I came for and I'm talking about all sorts of things... Yes... where is your son Kannan?

Kave.:— He's gone off on useless work... He hardly comes home!...

Dasa.:— Useless work?

Kave.:— Yes... he says he's going to reform the Congress—purify it...

Dasa.:— Sabash!... You're saying exactly what I think... Purify the Congress, is it... can that ever happen?... We just have to change with the times... Look at me now... what do I care about the Congress... it's just... business... that's all! Your son simply doesn't know how to use the facilities available in the Congress now...

Kave.:— Did you come here just to teach lessons?...

Dasa.:— Lessons alone?... I've come to give an advance in cash too. Here—Thiraviyam Pillai gave it... 250 rupees, told me to give it to Kannan! I've brought the whole amount without even taking my usual commission... Mm... take it!

(Kaveri refuses the money and throws it away.)

Kave.:— (Furiously) Che!... Have you come to buy my son for a price!... Mm! Go respectfully in the direction my finger points! Don't stand there! Corruption—wastefulness—poverty—death—hunger—famine—starvation—you cannot stop any of these, yet where did you get the courage to buy people for a price?... Rising prices—a storm of suffering! A queue for rice... hardship day and night.... a head without oil—a house without a lamp—a stove where *poosna* sleeps—tears all day long—you have ruled for eighteen years... say “mm” and it's prison; ask “why” and it's Kailasam. Sinners, understand this: your faith that you can pour out sacks of money and hide all these sins and crimes will be ruined!

Dasa.:— Kaveri... you don't know the value of money... I'll take care of Kannan.

Kave.:— Kannan is the son of a heroic mother... He is no Vibhishana like you, to become merchandise that can be sold!

Dasa.:— Mm... I'm going.

Kave.:— Don't come back; go!

(Kaveriyamma goes inside.)

Dasa.:— (To himself) Ayyo... this Tamil... ka... ka-ka-ka, it surges like the sea... We've got women on our side too! Do those poor things know how to speak even one civilized word?...

(Looking at Anna's portrait.)

This is all this man's doing... Swami!... I'm going now... If you weren't here... by this time the skin on my back would've been peeled off...... Mm... there really is a calmness in the face...

(Looking around and touching the door.)

Hey, Dasavatharam; somebody might hear you!... Don't throw dirt into your own livelihood.

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/10.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- `தி. மு.க.` is rendered as `DMK`; `அண்ணாத்துரை` as `Annadurai`. The dramatic body does not add outside biographical explanation.
- `தாலி`, turmeric and flower are retained as the source's widowhood/marital-status imagery rather than replaced with a generic phrase such as “widowhood.”
- `விபீஷணன்` is retained as `Vibhishana` because the insult carries the source's betrayal imagery.
- The verified Tamil contains the semantically insecure form `பூச்ண` in Kaveri's poverty catalogue. It is retained as *poosna* rather than silently corrected from outside the locked Tamil layer.
- `ம் என்றால் சிறைவாசம் ஏன் ஏன்றால் கைலாசம்` is rendered closely as “say ‘mm’ and it's prison; ask ‘why’ and it's Kailasam,” preserving the source's compressed political rhyme rather than inventing an English equivalent.

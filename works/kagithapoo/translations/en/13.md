# Scene 13

Location: The weaver's house

(Murugan enters carrying a pot, sugarcane and other things... joyfully calling, “Meena! Meena!!”)

Muru.:— Meena... Meena! Without selling the sari... I've got ten rupees... ten rupees... Look... a new pot... sugarcane... turmeric... ginger... Meena! Meena!!...

(He searches, then looks up.)

(Meena is hanging by a noose...)

Muru.:— Ah! Meena! Ayyo... why did you die, Meena? Was it because you were enraged that this sinner had even dared to sell your sari?... O queen among women who valued honour! Have you fallen into the mouth of death? Weren't you the one who said we must celebrate Pongal, Meena! Now you've made an unending pongal of sorrow boil over... Meena! Meena...

(He beats his head.)

(Kannan comes running in.)

Kan.:— Sir... keep this money... there are 250 rupees...

Muru.:— 250 rupees! 250 rupees!!... Look over there, sir... the money that did not come when I, my wife and the child we bore were writhing in hunger... Ayyo; my beloved Meena... now that she's become a corpse... it has come for her funeral rites... it has come for her funeral rites... Meena! Money! Money!... 250 rupees!... Ha... ha... ha... ha... money... Meena! Meena!...

(He goes mad.)

Kan.:— Sir! Sir!!...

Murug.:— Vande Mataram! Jai Hind!... It is our government that's ruling... our government... no cloth to wear... no gruel even for half a stomach... it is our government that's ruling... our government... Ha ha ha......

(He scatters the currency notes.)

(The curtain falls.)

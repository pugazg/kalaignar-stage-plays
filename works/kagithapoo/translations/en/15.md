# Scene 15

Location: Yama's durbar

(With drums and percussion sounding, Yamadharman sits on the throne; a dancing woman performs. All the representatives applaud.)

Chithirabuthiran:— The daily proceedings of the Kolu Mandapam may begin, Maharaja.

(Yama falls asleep.)

My lord! My lord!

(He wakes him.)

Yama:— (Waking) What?

Chi.Bu.:— My lord! The inquiry!

Yama:— Oh! Until now I was listening to the speeches of the representatives of the House. You people ought to be in the cremation ground—why have you come to this Assembly and started taking my life?

A representative:— Point of order! Honourable Speaker! The work of taking life belongs to the king, not to us!

Chi.Bu.:— The Speaker will arrive late to the House today. So I request that the point of order be dropped!

Yama:— It's not your fault! The ghosts and demons of this country forgot that all of you are settlers, voted for you in the general election, elected you and sent you here—that's whom I should blame! Never mind! Chithirabuthirar! Read that year's budget statement before the House and get it over with!

Chi.Bu.:— I'll read it and get it over with, O king!

A representative:— (Trembling) Lord of Dharma! Since copies of the budget statement have been given to us, we shall read and understand it ourselves. Our humble request is that no time be wasted by asking Chithirabuthirar to read it.

Yama:— Fine! A useful suggestion! Accepted! I will tell you only the important points of the budget statement in brief. Last year the export of souls from our country was rather liberal. This year, because family planning has been implemented in the Indian subcontinent too, we have not been able to export in large quantities the souls that have accumulated here. Because of that, the government has suffered an indescribable loss. We must find a way to make up that loss. What can be done?

A voice:— We can impose a tax!

Yama:— Who said that? Impose a tax? This House has never heard such a word until now. Tax! Tax! Tax! What a novel word! Who said that word? Stand up, let us see!

(A figure wearing khadi clothes and a cap stands up.)

Yama:— Who are you? How did you come into this House?

Thiraviyam:— My name is Thiraviyam Pillai! I was sitting upstairs as a spectator. When you asked for a way to make the country's economy flourish, I became emotional and jumped down. Forgive me!

Yama:— You have shaken the very dignity of this House. Not only that. Without my order, without obtaining an entry permit for our country, you have trespassed into my kingdom! Perhaps you came by an illicit boat! I am not going to let you off! We shall debate the word “tax.” After that, the punishment due to you will be given!

Thir.:— Punishment?

Yama:— Guards! Arrest that living creature and keep him under protection in our Pleasure Pavilion!

Thir.:— Pleasure Pavilion? Then is it Udhagai Mandalam?

Yama:— Much the same! In your Udhagai Mandalam a cold wind blows! Here a fragrant corpse-wind blows! Experience it and see. You will know the difference between the two! Hey, take him away! I will meet him tomorrow!

(The messengers take Thiraviyam Pillai away.)

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/15.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- `கொலு மண்டபம்` is retained as `Kolu Mandapam` because the source uses it as the name of Yama's assembly setting.
- `உயிர் ஏற்றுமதி` is translated literally as “export of souls,” preserving the scene's deliberately absurd budget/economic satire.
- `கள்ளத்தோணி` is rendered as “illicit boat,” without importing a later political interpretation beyond the source wording.
- The `உல்லாச மண்டபம்` / `உதகை மண்டலம்` exchange and `பனிக்காற்று` / `பிணக்காற்று` sound-play are preserved through `Pleasure Pavilion`, `Udhagai Mandalam`, “cold wind” and “corpse-wind”; no substitute English pun is invented.

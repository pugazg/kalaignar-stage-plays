# Scene 18

Location: Thiraviyam Pillai's house

(Thiraviyam Pillai wakes from bed with a terrified cry...)

Thiravi.:— Ah... ha!... ha!... Mahatma!... Mahatma...

(Hearing the fierce cry, Dasavatharam comes running in from sleep, hastily gathering himself together...)

Dasa.:— Master... master, what is it?...

Thiravi.:— Yamaloka... blood drink... Akashvani... Mahatma Gandhi... Mahatma Gandhi... I saw them all...

Dasa.:— Did you have a dream?...

Thiravi.:— Dasavatharam! A terrible dream... a terrible dream.

Dasa.:— Sir!... At least in the dream, did you see yourself becoming a minister?

Thiravi.:— I didn't see it even in a dream... When I'm awake, Kali Devi drives me away!... When I sleep, Mahatma Gandhi drives me away!... Dasavatharam... everything is one big confusion... The reason for all this confusion...

Dasa.:— I know the reason!... You're worrying and worrying because that Kannan won't fall into your hands... and that's why you're seeing all these terrible dreams...

Thiravi.:— True! What you say is true!... Only if that Kannan comes over to my side will my mind be at peace.

Dasa.:— Don't be afraid... the fellow was dazzled the moment he saw the money... 250—

Thiravi.:— 250!... I gave 500.

Dasa.:— (Hesitating... then) I gave half of it to his mother.

Thiravi.:— Oh-ho...

Dasa.:— He said he'd come here as soon as day breaks...

Thiravi.:— Hey!... Don't sit there expecting him to come... bring him here *paayigaiyoda*!

Dasa.:— All right... you sleep...

Thiravi.:— Sleep?... I just went to Yamaloka. Where will I go next?...

Dasa.:— Legislative Assembly! Minister's chair! Opposition member's question! A fiery reply... information not available. Put a separate question... applause and uproar from the Congress side... reception... procession... garlands and honours... *poorna kumbam*...

Thiravi.:— (Delighted) Aha!...

(He covers his eyes with his own hands.)

Dasa.:— Don't wake up... just keep sleeping like that... if you wake, that's it... what has gone won't come back...

Thir.:— Dasavatharam, say it once more, man. I'll fall asleep listening to it.

Dasa.:— Is that so? All right! Don't open your eyes! If you open them, it'll vanish. Flower garlands... procession... ministerial office...

(He repeats it again and again in a musical rhythm.)

What sort of lullaby is this? A lullaby of praise!

(He leaves.)

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/18.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- The locked colloquial form `பாயிகையோட` is semantically insecure; it is retained as *paayigaiyoda* rather than silently normalized to a guessed adverbial phrase.
- The source's dream-to-political-fantasy transition is preserved as a comic “lullaby” sequence, including the legislative-answer formula `தகவல் இல்லை. தனிக்கேள்வி போடவும்` and the procession/garland imagery.
- `பூரண கும்பம்` is retained as *poorna kumbam* because the source supplies the ceremonial expression without an English gloss.
- The later source speaker label changes from `திரவி:` to `திர:`; the English preserves that change as `Thir.:—` rather than silently normalizing it.

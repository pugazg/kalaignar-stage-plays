# Scene 7

[Maragatham and Mala come to Kaveri's house—Kaveri is the wife of the lion of principle who fell to the regime's gunfire in the anti-Hindi struggle.]

Maragatham: (To Mala) Here he is; the man who is now only a picture is the one who became a corpse in the righteous struggle to protect Tamil... Even at the moment life was leaving him, this lion of principle did not fail to cry out, “Long live Tamil, born with the world!”

(She bows. Mala also bows.)

Mala: (Softly) Maragatham!... Where is Kaveri Amma?......

Maragatham: Wait...... she'll come...... (Looking inside) Amma! Amma!!

(The two stand waiting for Kaveri to come...)

(A sorrowful song about a heroic mother is heard in the background. Kaveri Amma enters.)

Maragatham: Greetings, Amma...

Kaveri: Greetings, come in, child...

Maragatham: This is... Mala...... my college friend......

(Mala bows and falls at her feet......)

Kaveri: Get up, child... Falling at my feet?... I am a woman who has committed so much sin......

Mala: Sin?...

Kaveri: Yes... Look; if I had earned merit, would I not have gone before him?......

Maragatham: What is this, Amma; have you begun to grieve again?......

Kaveri: No, Maragatham... How can I grieve? My tears have dried up... my heart has gone numb...... He smiles now, wearing a crown of fame among the heroic warriors who protected Tamil. I think of him again and again, and I am proud...

Maragatham: Amma! Was not the gold for the crown of fame your husband wears given from your own *tali*?... Were not the *mullai* buds in which you dwelt set as pearls in that crown? The jewel shining in that diadem—Mother, is it not the vermilion mark from your own forehead?...

Kaveri: Maragatham! May the tears of sympathy shed by good hearts like yours become tears that destroy the fanatics of repression. May the turmeric and vermilion I have lost... burn and consume the hardened hearts of the Tamil enemies swollen with arrogance......

Maragatham: Amma!... It will surely happen! The state kills on the day; God stands and kills later. Who is God, Amma?... God is the volcanic heart of the weeping poor—the god of destruction!... Their laughter, Amma, is the god of creation!... Their sense of duty, Amma, is the god that protects!... I came now to place a request before you, so that those madmen who do not understand this may be taught an immediate lesson.

Kaveri: What is it, Maragatham?

Maragatham: To teach wisdom to those who reduced you to this state... in the coming election... through the democratic path... we must win......

Kaveri: And for that?...

Maragatham: You must contest from this constituency;

Kaveri: What.... an election?...

Maragatham: Yes ... You must contest on behalf of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam—the camp of the honour-brigade warriors who defend Tamil......

Kaveri: Maragatham?

Maragatham: The whole student world is behind you! ... ...Every compassionate heart will support you! Your victory, Amma, will be the symbol of the anti-Hindi struggle! Please agree without hesitation...

(She pleads.)

Kaveri: What a funny girl... Your father is going to stand in this constituency for the ruling party. Am I to stand against him?...

Maragatham: I have already told my father to write it down now itself: however many lakhs he spends, this time even his deposit will not come back!

Amma! I am asking you to agree not so that you may sit in office, but so that the world may understand that all Tamil Nadu is resisting, with its whole breath, the danger that comes to destroy Tamil!....

Kaveri: So now I must agree to your request, mustn't I?....

Maragatham: Yes....

Kaveri: There is one condition; fulfil that first....... After that, I will submit to your loving command...

Maragatham: What is it, Amma?

Kaveri: You know my son Kannan, don't you?...

Maragatham: I have heard a great deal about him.... I have not met him.... He is an ardent Congress man of sacrifice, isn't he?...

Kaveri: This whole family was like that, child; Kannan's father (goes to the portrait) took part in the great Gandhi's Salt Satyagraha... When the liquor-shop picketing took place, he and I—though I was pregnant—were imprisoned for a year...... Kannan himself was born in prison!

Mala: Even the Kannan of Ayarpadi was born in prison...

Maragatham: He wandered with flute in hand... and doesn't this Kannan wander with a flag in hand?...

Kaveri: There is no flag in his hand now. His reason is that they have made the party flag and the national flag indistinguishable! That troubles him... so he does not carry a flag.

Maragatham: All right, Amma; you said there was a condition! What is it?....

Kaveri: He goes about in a frenzy saying that he is going to reform the Congress....

Maragatham: Reform the Congress? Isn't that like taking a lamp in your hand and searching for something that fell into the sea and disappeared?....

Kaveri: He must be turned away from that effort—he must be brought out of that collapsed building...... He keeps lamenting that he will rescue from evil hands the Congress nurtured by men of sacrifice like Tilak and Gandhi... that he will refine it into purified gold... Not even his father, now a corpse, has been able to change his stubbornness... He must be made to understand that not only he, but no one can reform the Congress any longer. He must understand himself—understand Tamil—understand his mother—and become a heroic son who raises his broad shoulders against the enemies of Tamil.... Can you do that?... If you can, act at once... That is my condition, Maragatham...

Maragatham: I have confidence, Amma! When we possess pure principles capable of changing even an opponent's mind, I have plenty of confidence that your son too can be changed...

Kaveri: Then I send you with my blessings... Go toward victory... bring my frenzy-driven son onto the path of those who have found the right way......

Maragatham: All right, Amma; I will fulfil your condition... And what I asked—you will?...

Kaveri: (Evading the answer) You won't forget that, will you? Ah, my dear; first reform Kannan!

(Curtain)

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/07.md`; no OCR, MD first-pass, PDF or secondary English witness was used for drafting.
- `திராவிட முன்னேற்றக் கழகம்` is rendered as `Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam`, preserving the source's proper party name rather than replacing it with an explanatory paraphrase.
- `தாலி`, turmeric and vermilion are retained as the source's widowhood/marital-symbol imagery. `தாலி` remains *tali*.
- The source's unusual `முல்லை மொட்டுக்கள்` image is carried literally as “mullai buds” rather than silently normalized into a different ornament or body-image reading.
- The proverb-like `அரசு அன்றுகொல்லும். தெய்வம் நின்று கொல்லும்.` is translated closely to preserve its parallel rhetoric.
- The Ayarpadi Kannan comparison and flute/flag contrast are preserved without importing explanatory material from outside the Tamil scene.

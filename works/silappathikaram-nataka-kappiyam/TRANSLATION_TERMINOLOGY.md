# English Translation Terminology Register — சிலப்பதிகாரம் நாடகக் காப்பியம்

This is the living register for recurring culturally, historically, politically and rhetorically loaded Tamil terms. A settled form controls later translation only where the register says so; contextual/source-ambiguous terms must still be reviewed occurrence by occurrence.

## Status key

- **locked** — use consistently unless direct contextual evidence requires documented revision;
- **preferred** — default rendering, but context may justify another form;
- **contextual** — no automatic one-to-one equivalent;
- **retained / unresolved** — preserve transliteration/source form rather than guess.

## Established scene-level decisions

- Scene 1: `வாழ்க தமிழ்! வெல்க தமிழகம்!` → `Long live Tamil! May Tamilakam triumph!`; `விற்கொடி` → `bow-banner`.
- Scenes 3–4: explicit `விதி / மதி` → `fate / reason`; `தமிழ் அறம்` retained as `Tamil aram`.
- Scene 5: `மலைவாழ் தமிழர்களே` → `Tamil people of the hills`; `மூவேந்தர்` → `three crowned kings`; `குறும்பாடு` retained.
- Scene 6: `இந்திரன் என்னும் பழந்தமிழன்` → `an ancient Tamil named Indran`; preserve `Muthamizh / iyal / isai / koothu` wordplay.
- Scene 7: `கழஞ்சு` → `kalanju`; `சுயம்வரம்` → `swayamvaram`.
- Scene 8: `மாமுது பார்ப்பார்...` → occurrence-specific `venerable ritual officiants`; `மறைவழி` → `way of the sacred lore`; `தீவலம்` → `circled the fire`.
- Scene 9: retain `oodal`; `தமிழர் வளம்` → `wealth of Tamils`; source `உன் நூல்தான் எல்லாம்...` remains unresolved and conservatively literal.
- Scene 10: repeated `ஆடுகிறாள்.` → repeated `She dances.`; `மயக்குகின்ற மாதர் வழி` retains inherited stigma without harsher modernization.
- Scene 11: `(இருபொருளில்)` → `[With a double meaning]`.
- Scene 12: `காந்தள்` retained; `சேற் கெண்டை` → `ser-kendai`; Tamil-glory climax remains explicit.
- Scene 13: `தமிழர் வழக்கப்படி` → `according to Tamil custom`; Tamil collective praise remains explicit.
- Scene 14: preserve `பிராமண / பார்ப்பன / அந்தணர் / மறையவர்` distinctions; `தமிழ் மறை` → `Tamil sacred lore`; `விமோசனம்` → `atonement` in context.
- Scene 15: preserve unusual `மனமாறிந்த`; `தமிழ்ச் சுவை` → `the taste of Tamil`; repeated `அத்தான்` retained.
- Scene 16: retain `nanniraattu`, `kurali`, and `mekalai` wordplay; `மறையவன்` → `man of sacred lore`.
- Scene 17: `மாசறு பொன்` → `stainless gold`; `தமிழரல்லவா ?...` stays explicitly `Is he not a Tamil ?...`; `என் தங்கை` → `my younger sister`.
- Scene 18: `பழந்தமிழ் மன்னன் இந்திரன்` → `Indran, the ancient Tamil king`; `மானங் காக்கும் தமிழச்சி` → `Tamil woman who guards honour`; retain `Kaanal Vari` setup.
- Scene 19: translate every quoted Kaanal Vari stanza independently from the verified Tamil; retain `Kanni`, `kaattivari`, `kankooduvari` and source-opaque `kuraivai` where secure equivalence is unavailable; keep Kovalan's lineage/sexual abuse attributable to him.
- Scene 20: `தமிழ் மறை` continues as `Tamil sacred lore`; preserve distinct `கணிகை / விலை மாது / உடல் வாணிபம்`; `மானம்` → `honour` in context; `அணுப்பொழுதும்` → `not even for the space of an atom`.

## Dravidian-movement relation rule

Preserve source-supported concepts that later carry Dravidian movement force — Tamil linguistic-cultural self-respect, Tamilakam consciousness, dignity, justice, anti-hierarchical argument, rational human agency, women's agency and Tamil historical consciousness — **without inserting movement vocabulary into dialogue when it is absent from the Tamil source**.

Through scene 20, the strongest recurring patterns are: fate versus reason/human agency; Tamil peoplehood/history; Tamil-centred maritime cosmopolitanism; language as love and collective worth; women's choice and dignity; artistic achievement against inherited stigma; Tamil custom and collective identity; practical ethical responsibility grounded in `தமிழ் மறை`; intimate `தமிழ்ச் சுவை`; literary-cultural naming; explicit `தமிழரல்லவா`; Tamil historical festival framing; Madhavi's questioning of unequal chastity expectations; exposure of birth-status/sexual stigma as dramatic violence; Kannagi's repeated refusal to define herself through hostility toward Madhavi; and her material/economic agency in offering the anklets as capital.

These relations belong in separately labelled interpretive notes unless the source itself uses later movement terminology.

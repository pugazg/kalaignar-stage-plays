# Scene 11

## The Queen of Chastity in Distress

### Kannagi's House

[Kannagi stands by the street door, watching for Kovalan's return.

The palanquin-carriage comes slowly, swaying as it moves.

She looks eagerly. Only the driver gets down......]

Kannagi : Where is he ?

Driver : He has not come!

Kannagi : Ah !...

Driver : They say he has even postponed the journey to Rome.

[Kannagi stands motionless......... The driver leaves. Then Kannagi's friend Devandhi arrives.

Seeing Kannagi standing without moving, she is startled.]

Devandhi : Kannagi!......

[Kannagi slowly turns and looks at Devandhi.]

Why ......what happened ?

Kannagi : Nothing, Devandhi!......

Devandhi : Then why are you crying?......

Where is he, your husband ?...

Kannagi : He has gone to make arrangements for tomorrow's journey....

Devandhi : Oh-ho ... a journey tomorrow ? So you are already crying just thinking of that separation! You silly girl!... Come inside !

[She takes her inside]

Devandhi : Yes........ that Madhavi kept sending person after person after your husband; what happened to all that, Kannagi?....

[With a double meaning]

Kannagi : It ended in defeat!...

Devandhi : Of course! Of course!... Would Rama leave Sita?.... Would Kovalan leave Kannagi? In between came that Madhavi, like a Surpanakha, only to be humiliated—why else?....

Kannagi : Do not insult Madhavi, Devandhi! She is one favoured by fortune; a fortunate woman.

## Translation notes

- `கற்பரசியின் கலக்கம்` is rendered as `The Queen of Chastity in Distress`, retaining Kannagi's established elevated epithet while keeping the emotional focus of the scene.
- The first right-column line in the verified Tamil, `ஏன் ......என்ன நடந்தது ?`, carries no repeated speaker label. The English likewise leaves `Why ......what happened ?` unlabelled rather than silently adding `Devandhi:`.
- The source's spaced punctuation in `அவர் எங்கே ?`, `ஆ !...`, and `வா உள்ளே !` is reflected in the English staging without regularizing the Tamil archive.
- `அடி அசடு!` is rendered as the affectionate `You silly girl!`, preserving Devandhi's familiar teasing rather than making it a harsh insult.
- `(இருபொருளில்)` is translated as `[With a double meaning]`, because Kannagi's `தோல்வியில் முடிந்தது!` carries more than one implication in the immediate situation.
- Devandhi's Sita–Rama–Surpanakha comparison is retained directly. The translation does not soften her insult to Madhavi, because Kannagi's following correction depends upon its sharpness.
- Kannagi's `அவள் கொடுத்து வைத்தவள்; பாக்கியசாலி` is rendered as `She is one favoured by fortune; a fortunate woman`, retaining the doubled idea of fortune rather than compressing it to a single adjective.
- No published English edition was used in drafting or reviewing this scene.

## Dravidian movement resonance — interpretive note

This note is **interpretive context, not translated source text**.

Scene 11 contains no overt rationalist or political declaration, so the movement-context reading must remain restrained. The strongest source-supported feature is Kannagi's refusal to preserve her own dignity by degrading Madhavi. Devandhi reaches for a familiar epic analogy that casts Madhavi as a Surpanakha-like intruder; Kannagi immediately stops her: `Do not insult Madhavi`.

That refusal to build one woman's honour upon another woman's humiliation can resonate with a broader Dravidian/Self-Respect concern for human dignity, especially when read alongside Kalaignar's earlier presentation of Madhavi as artist, woman of choice and person burdened by inherited stigma. But this scene itself states no movement doctrine, and the translation does not add one.

Kannagi also conceals Kovalan's delay from Devandhi by saying he has gone to prepare for the journey. The scene therefore preserves her emotional complexity—hurt, dignity, loyalty and generosity—without reducing her to either passive suffering or ideological symbolism.

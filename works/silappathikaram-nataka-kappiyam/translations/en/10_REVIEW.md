Kalaignar's repetitions, emotional accumulation, visual metaphors and theatrical sound are preserved.

## Key fidelity decisions

- `ஆடுகிறாள். / ஆடுகிறாள். / ஆடிக்கொண்டே இருக்கிறாள்.` → `She dances. / She dances. / She goes on dancing.`
- `“தடால்!”` → `“THADAAL!”`, retaining theatrical sound.
- `விரகதாபம்` → `anguish of separation`.
- `மகிழ்ச்சி பூத்துக் குலுங்கும் தமிழகம்` retains `Tamilakam` and the blossoming/swaying image.
- the repeated `வேண்டேன்` rhetoric remains repeated `I want no...` rather than being compressed.
- `மயக்குகின்ற மாதர் வழி வந்தவள்` is translated as inherited social stigma, not as a generic sexual insult.

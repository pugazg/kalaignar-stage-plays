## Gate review

1. **Coverage — PASS**
   - Kovalan's palm-leaf voice-over, Maasathuvan's reaction, Madhavi's arrival with Manimekalai and gold, the return-of-wealth exchange, forgiveness request, daughter-in-law declaration and closing child scene are all represented.

2. **Semantic fidelity — PASS**
   - The title's `துறவு` is not expanded into an invented religious rite.
   - Madhavi's act is presented as relinquishing wealth and rejecting material benefit; her self-blame remains present.
   - Maasathuvan's refusal to take the gold and his acceptance of Madhavi as daughter-in-law remain distinct actions.

3. **Kalaignar voice/cadence — PASS**
   - `பாவி`, `பசும் பொன்`, `உதிர்ந்த மலர்`, `அன்புச் செல்வம்`, and the repeated `பாரம்மா! பாரம்மா!!` emotional structure are retained in force and imagery.

4. **Terminology — PASS**
   - `மாதவி துறவு` → `Madhavi's Renunciation`, with explicit note that this scene contains no formal ascetic initiation.
   - `மாற்றுக் குறையாத பசும் பொன்` → `pure gold that loses nothing under assay`.
   - `உதிர்ந்த மலர்` → `fallen flower`.
   - No caste/religious label is imported into the scene.

5. **Stage timing — PASS**
   - Kovalan's voice-over transitions naturally into Madhavi's entrance; the final movement from gold to child to tears remains intact.

6. **Dravidian-context separation — PASS**
   - Interpretive note relates Madhavi's rejection of profit/status and Maasathuvan's dignity-conferring acceptance to anti-hierarchical/Self-Respect resonances.
   - Madhavi's own self-condemnation is not erased or rewritten as later ideological speech.

7. **Secondary witness — PASS**
   - No published English edition was used.

## Review conclusion

Scene 23 is accepted as `translation-reviewed` / `passed`. The translation preserves the scene's tension between Madhavi's self-blame and Maasathuvan's refusal to define her by that blame, while avoiding the false implication that `துறவு` here is necessarily a formal religious conversion or ascetic ceremony.

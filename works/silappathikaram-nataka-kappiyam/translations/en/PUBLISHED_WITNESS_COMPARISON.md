# Published-English Secondary-Witness Comparison

Status: **comparison framework established**

## Purpose

This phase compares the repository's independently produced, reviewed English translation of `சிலப்பதிகாரம் — நாடகக் காப்பியம்` with the verified published-English witness in the 2009 volume *Tale of the Anklet and One Act Plays*.

This is **not** a replacement translation and **not** a correction authority.

## Authority hierarchy

1. controlling Tamil scan `TVA_BOK_0016473_சிலப்பதிகாரம்_நாடகக்_காப்பியம்.pdf`;
2. verified Tamil page records and assembled Tamil scenes;
3. independently produced English translation, already individually reviewed and final-consistency reviewed;
4. 2009 published-English edition — **secondary comparison witness only**.

The published witness was not used in drafting or reviewing the independent translation.

## Comparison rules

- never copy a published rendering into the independent translation merely because it differs;
- never alter verified Tamil to agree with the published English;
- compare meaning, rhetorical force, repetition, stage action, names, culturally loaded terminology, humour, political/literary register and omissions/additions;
- treat differences as evidence about translation choices, not automatically as errors;
- if a comparison exposes a possible issue in the independent translation, return first to the verified Tamil and controlling source before considering any change;
- any future change must pass the existing translation-review controls and be documented explicitly;
- protect the permanent terminology distinctions, especially `பிராமண`, `பார்ப்பன`, `பார்ப்பார்`, `அந்தணர்`, `மறையவன் / மறையவர்`;
- keep Dravidian-movement interpretation outside dialogue unless stated in the Tamil.

## Witness mapping limitation

The independent translation is organized as **38 numbered Tamil scenes plus an unnumbered closing tableau**. The 2009 published witness is organized by its own English section structure across physical scans 1–130. The two structures must therefore be aligned by dramatic content and source sequence rather than assumed to have a one-to-one scene-number correspondence.

## Comparison record format

For each aligned unit, record:

- independent translation file / Tamil scene provenance;
- published-witness physical scan range and section heading;
- material agreement in dramatic action and meaning;
- significant lexical/register differences;
- repetition/rhetorical differences;
- culturally or politically loaded terminology differences;
- apparent omission/addition/restructuring in either English rendering;
- decision: `no action`, `Tamil recheck required`, or `independent translation review required`.

## Baseline findings already established by source processing

The published witness visibly uses a distinct translator/editorial register and preserves many edition-specific English forms and roughness. Its section titles and segmentation do not duplicate the repository's Tamil-scene organization. It must therefore remain analytically separate from the canonical independent translation.

The repository's independent translation has already passed scene-level review and a final English consistency review, with no published English edition used during those gates. Accordingly, comparison findings are advisory unless independently confirmed against Tamil.

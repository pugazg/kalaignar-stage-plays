## Coverage and voice

The review confirms complete coverage of:

- the opening garland/song sequence;
- the `ஊடல்` explanation and Kural reference;
- Rome/pearl-trade departure argument;
- Kannagi/Kovalan separation poetry;
- Tamil-language and Tamil-wealth metaphors;
- the Vasanthamalai messenger sequence;
- Kannagi's decision to send Kovalan to explain matters to Madhavi;
- the final carriage departure.

Kalaignar's rapid movement between humour, erotic metaphor, economic pride, poetic anguish and stage melodrama is retained rather than normalized into one register.

## Key fidelity decisions

- `ஊடல்` remains `oodal` because the source itself explains the term.
- `தமிழர் வளம்` remains `the wealth of Tamils`.
- `செந்தமிழ்` remains rhetorically present in the lovers' imagery.
- `கொல்லாமற் கொல்லாதீர்` retains the paradox `Do not kill me without killing me`.
- `எண்ணிரண்டு பதினாறு கிழமை` remains `Two eights—sixteen weeks`.
- `பிரியவில்லை / பிரியமில்லை` is preserved semantically as the turn from `part` to `love`, with the unavoidable English sound-play loss documented.
- `உன் நூல்தான் எல்லாம்...` is not silently corrected; the conservative `your thread is everything...` is retained as an acknowledged source ambiguity.

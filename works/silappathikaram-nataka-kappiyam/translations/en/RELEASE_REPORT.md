## Translation principles retained

The release preserves Kalaignar's rhetorical force, repetitions, humour, direct address, emotional escalation, dramatic timing, Tamil historical/literary register and source-supported roughness. It does not silently modernize the play into generic prose or modern legal language.

The permanent terminology discipline remains part of the release. In particular, `அந்தணர்` is not automatically rendered `Brahmin`, and the distinctions among related Tamil social/ritual terms remain protected.

Interpretive Dravidian-movement resonance is separated from translated dialogue unless the source itself states the concept. Scene 37's explicit `சுயமரியாதை` is translated directly as `self-respect`.

## Protected source issues

The release continues to document rather than conceal unresolved or source-specific material. Most importantly, the closing tableau on scan 88 contains a later library/accession stamp that obscures the leading characters of two lines. Those characters remain unresolved in both Tamil and English; no contextual reconstruction has been substituted.

During Scene 37 translation, direct source-pixel reinspection established three Tamil corrections before the English was finalized: `தலைவர்களாம்`, `அன்னை நற்சோணையே`, and `ஈடற்ற புலவனே`. These corrections are documented in the archive and handover materials.

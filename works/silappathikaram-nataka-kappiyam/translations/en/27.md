# Scene 27

## Introduction to the Aychiyar

### Border of Madurai

[Kavundhi Adigal, Kannagi and Kovalan arrive... The sound of a song sung by boys and girls driving cattle and goats is heard.... Among that young group comes a beautiful girl named Aiyai, leading them. Seeing Kavundhi Adigal, she says...]

Aiyai : Adigal... greetings! Greetings!

Kavundhi : Live long... live long... Aiyai! Where is your mother Madhari, child?

Aiyai : Amma... she has gone to prepare milk-rice for the Jain ascetics... Come, let us go home!

Kavundhi : Kovalan!... Come. Madhari, a woman of the Aayar people, is a very good woman... I shall introduce you to her.

[All of them move away from there with the herd.]

### Madhari's House...

[Kavundhi, Kannagi, Kovalan, Aiyai,...]

Aiyai : There, Amma has come too.

...Amma... Amma...

Madhari : Ah! Is that Kavundhi Adigal!... You must come... you must come...

Kavundhi : Madhari! These are... Kovalan and Kannagi... If the people of Madurai merely heard the names of their parents, they would run here and invite them as guests into their own homes.... Such is the distinction of their families! Until their work in Madurai is finished, keep them in your own house and...

Madhari : Am I going to object? Mother... let them stay freely. Amma... Kannagi! Here is my daughter Aiyai; she will remain by you as a companion who does not leave your side!

Kavundhi : My duty is done. Kovalan!... Kannagi... my dear child! I shall take leave now...

Kannagi : Mother... having you with us gave us so much comfort!

Kavundhi : Madhari is here in my place, is she not... Madhari! I have entrusted Kannagi to you. She is now under your protection! That is all I shall say...

## Translation notes

- `ஆய்ச்சியர்` is retained as **`Aychiyar`** in the title rather than forced into a modern caste/community label. The scene itself supplies the related `ஆயர்குல மங்கை`, translated contextually as **`a woman of the Aayar people`**.
- `சமணத் துறவிகளுக்குப் பாற்சோறு` becomes **`milk-rice for the Jain ascetics`**. No further ritual explanation is added beyond the source.
- `பசக் கூட்டத்தோடு` is rendered by its immediate stage sense as `with the herd`; the protected Tamil wording remains untouched.
- The internal location heading `மாதரி வீடு...` is retained inside scene 27 rather than treated as a new numbered scene.
- Madhari's `தடையா சொல்லப் போகிறேன்?` keeps its colloquial warmth as **`Am I going to object?`** rather than being formalized.
- `அவள் உன் அடைக்கலம்!` occurs immediately after Kavundhi says she has entrusted Kannagi to Madhari. English therefore renders the relational force as **`She is now under your protection!`**, without turning `அடைக்கலம்` into an abstract theological term.
- The lower illustration on scan 70 is uncaptioned; no identity or dialogue is invented from it.
- No published English edition was used in drafting or reviewing this scene.

## Dravidian movement resonance — interpretive note

This note is **interpretive context, not translated source text**.

The scene builds dignity through hospitality and chosen responsibility rather than through rank. Kavundhi, identified in the previous scene as a Jain ascetic, trusts Kannagi to Madhari's household; Madhari accepts her immediately, and Aiyai is offered as a constant companion. The dramatic emphasis is on care, protection and human relationship.

The Aayar/Aychiyar setting is also allowed to remain culturally specific without being translated into a modern hierarchical label. That restraint matters for a Dravidian-movement reading: the scene can resonate with anti-hierarchical dignity and Tamil social-cultural plurality precisely because the translation does not overwrite Kalaignar's own vocabulary with a later caste taxonomy.

The movement resonance therefore lies in relational equality, hospitality and Tamil social memory. No later ideological slogan is inserted into the characters' speech.

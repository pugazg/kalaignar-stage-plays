## Findings

The translation preserves the scene's deliberate contrast with scene 12, Maasathuvan's fish imagery, Kannagi's refusal to have Kovalan humiliated, the Ramayana/Bharata comic exaggeration, the explicit `தமிழர் வழக்கப்படி`, and Maasathuvan's `தமிழ்க் குலத்தின் பெருமைக்கு நீயொரு சான்று` praise.

Kannagi's extended defence of Madhavi was reviewed with special care. The English retains the difficult source claim that Madhavi is `greater than I am... even in love`, and preserves Kannagi's explanation that she can remain alive in separation while Madhavi would wither like a flower. The translation does not editorially correct, condemn or sentimentalize that argument.

The final three repetitions of `யாரும் இங்கே வராதீர்கள்` remain three separate appeals, preserving Kalaignar's dramatic crescendo.

The interpretive note identifies Tamil collective self-description and a dignity-based refusal to degrade Madhavi, while also acknowledging that the scene remains deeply shaped by chastity, marital endurance and self-sacrifice. No modern ideological vocabulary is inserted into the dialogue.

No Tamil source change was required during translation review.

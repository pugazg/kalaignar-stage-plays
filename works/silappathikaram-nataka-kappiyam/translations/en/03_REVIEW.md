## 3. Wordplay / untranslatable feature review

The Tamil `பொய்க் காது / பொய்க்காது / உண்மைக் காது` cluster cannot be reproduced fully in English because it depends on Tamil sound structure.

The chosen English—`ears made for falsehood`, `may never sound false`, `an ear for truth`—preserves the semantic targets and repeated ear/truth contrast. The translation notes explicitly record the unavoidable loss rather than inventing a different joke.

PASS.

## 4. Kural and epic-reference review

The quoted Kural is translated from the Tamil source passage itself and not replaced by a standard published English version.

The source's `பதினெட்டு வருடம்` remains `eighteen years`. No attempt was made to harmonize the play with another Ramayana chronology.

Ilango's argument is preserved as argument, not converted into narrator commentary: he rejects the use of an old epic as proof that fate is unalterable and answers by declaring that he will create a new epic.

PASS.

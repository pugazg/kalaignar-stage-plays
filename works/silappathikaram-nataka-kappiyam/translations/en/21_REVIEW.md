## Gate review

   - All dramatic text from scan 61 is represented: Madhavi/Vasanthamalai exchange, Madhavi's address to Manimekalai, Chitrapathi intervention, Hunchback exchange, the wealth-plunder accusation, Chitrapathi's admission and Madhavi's final lament.

   - Chitrapathi's `கட்டிய கணவன்` taunt remains a social-status taunt attributable to her.

3. **Kalaignar voice/cadence — PASS**
   - Repetition (`போதும்`, `போய்விட்டார்`, `ஆதரவில்ல`) and emotional accumulation are preserved.
   - `முத்தமாரி`, `தமிழ்க் கனி`, `செந்தமிழ்மழலை`, `சுடுநெருப்பு`, and the Ramayana hunchback comparison retain their rhetorical imagery.

4. **Terminology — PASS**
   - `கலைமகள்` → `The Muse` for this title.
   - `தமிழ்க் கனி` → `fruit of Tamil`.
   - `செந்தமிழ்மழலை` → `baby-lisp of refined Tamil`.
   - No caste/ritual term is normalized or imported.

5. **Stage timing — PASS**
   - The shift from Vasanthamalai to Chitrapathi to the Hunchback, then back to Manimekalai, remains theatrical and immediate.

6. **Dravidian-context separation — PASS**
   - The separate interpretive note identifies dignity, resistance to economic exploitation and intimate Tamil-language identity.
   - No later movement terminology is inserted into Madhavi's dialogue.

7. **Secondary witness — PASS**
   - No published English edition was used.

## Review conclusion

Scene 21 is accepted as `translation-reviewed` / `passed`. The English preserves Madhavi's grief as grief, her anger as anger, and the scene's explicit economic exploitation without turning either the dialogue or the Ramayana comparison into a modern explanatory essay.

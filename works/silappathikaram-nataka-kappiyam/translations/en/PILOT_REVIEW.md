# English Translation Pilot Review — காட்சி-1

Source Tamil: `../../scenes/01.md`  
English pilot: `01.md`

## Result

**PASS — pilot convention accepted.**

`translations/en/01.md` may remain `status: "translation-reviewed"` / `translation_review: "passed"`.

The pilot succeeds as a source-faithful dramatic translation while retaining Kalaignar's public-oratory cadence and keeping Dravidian-movement interpretation outside the translated dialogue.

## 1. Coverage review

All literary elements in the verified Tamil scene are represented:

- scene title;
- opening stage direction;
- `முரசறைவோன்` speaker turn;
- all proclamation slogans;
- repeated `கொட்டுவோம், கொட்டுவோம்` call;
- final `[முரசு முழங்குகிறது]` direction.

No source visual layer is inserted into the English dramatic text.

## 2. Line / rhetorical-unit review

### `வஞ்சி மூதூரில் முரசறைதல்`

Pilot: `Drum Proclamation in Vanchi, the Ancient City`.

Review: preserves the public-announcement function of `முரசறைதல்`, the proper name `Vanchi`, and the antiquity conveyed by `மூதூர்` without inventing a modern location.

### Opening direction

The English preserves:

- Vanchi as the Chera capital;
- Senguttuvan's birthday announcement;
- the elephant-mounted announcer;
- the bow emblem on both cloth layers;
- the elephant's swaying movement;
- `புதிய எழுச்சி` as `a new awakening`;
- `பூரிப்போடு` as exultant public proclamation.

The direction remains performable stage prose rather than academic historical description.

### `வாழ்க தமிழ்! வெல்க தமிழகம்!`

Pilot: `Long live Tamil! May Tamilakam triumph!`

Review: **accepted**. It retains slogan form, personifies Tamil as in the source, and keeps `Tamilakam` rather than replacing it with modern `Tamil Nadu`.

### `இமிழ் கடல் வேலித் தமிழகம்`

Pilot: `Tamilakam, girdled by the sounding sea`.

Review: **accepted**. `girdled` retains the enclosure image of `வேலி`; `sounding sea` preserves the elevated acoustic quality of `இமிழ் கடல்` better than neutral `surrounded by the sea`.

### Chera historical clause

Pilot identifies Nedun Cheralathan as the great Chera warrior/leader who went to the Himalaya and planted the bow-banner, and Senguttuvan as his beloved son and mighty warrior.

Review: semantic relation is preserved without simplifying the dynastic rhetoric.

### `கொட்டுவோம், கொட்டுவோம் முரசம்!`

Pilot: `Let us beat, let us beat the drum!`

Review: **accepted**. The inclusive first-person call and exact rhetorical repetition are retained. Replacing it with a single `Beat the drum!` would lose Kalaignar's mobilizing cadence.

### Closing slogans

Pilot:

- `Long live Prince Senguttuvan!`
- `Long live the Chera land!`
- `Long live the homeland of the people of refined Tamil!`

Review: preserves the escalation from ruler → Chera land → Tamil cultural homeland.

`the people of refined Tamil` is intentionally slightly marked English because a smoother `Tamil people` would erase the lexical elevation of `செந்தமிழர்`. Keep this as the pilot default, but re-evaluate globally if later occurrences show a more natural consistent solution that preserves the distinction.

## 3. Kalaignar voice review

PASS.

The English retains:

- parallel `Long live...` slogans;
- collective inclusive `Let us...`;
- repetition rather than stylistic deletion;
- Tamil historical geography and Chera symbolism;
- the movement from image → awakening → proclamation → collective acclaim.

The scene remains speakable aloud and does not become museum-caption English.

## 4. Dravidian movement relation review

PASS WITH CORRECT SEPARATION.

The interpretive note identifies defensible resonances in Kalaignar's framing:

- Tamil language as a collective public subject;
- `Tamilakam` as historical/civilizational geography;
- `தாயகம்` / homeland;
- public awakening (`எழுச்சி`);
- invocation of ancient Tamil dynastic achievement;
- mass-mobilizing repetition and drum rhetoric.

These can be related to Dravidian movement themes of Tamil linguistic-cultural self-respect, Tamil historical consciousness and public mobilization.

Crucially, the translation does **not** insert `self-respect`, `social justice`, `rationalism`, `anti-caste`, `Dravidian`, or party-political terminology into scene 1, because those expressions are not present in this scene. Interpretation is kept in a separately labelled note.

This separation is now mandatory for later scenes.

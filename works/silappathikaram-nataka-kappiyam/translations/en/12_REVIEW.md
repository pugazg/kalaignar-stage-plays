## Findings

The translation preserves the scene's deliberate contrastive title, the intimate bedchamber register, Madhavi's garland action, Kovalan's long cumulative praise and Madhavi's witty answer about men `stringing` descriptions from words.

The long praise was checked for every major source image: kanthal fingers; golden body; the difficult source form `சேற் கெண்டை`; milk-nectar at the lips; black-bee/eyelid-casket image; dark `மழைக் கூந்தல்`; peacock-plume length; honey/jackfruit tongue image; `மொழி வளம்`; and the `படைப்புத் தலைவன்` climax.

No speculative correction was made to protected Tamil. `சேற் கெண்டை` is retained conservatively as `ser-kendai` in the English image, and unusual `அசிவதுபோல்` is translated only from its immediate movement context.

Most importantly for Kalaignar's voice, the climax `தமிழுக்கு அல்லவா பெருமை உண்டாகிறது!` remains an explicit declaration that Tamil itself gains glory through the act of describing Madhavi. The interpretive note relates this to Tamil linguistic-cultural pride but does not turn the scene into a political speech.

No Tamil source change was required during translation review.

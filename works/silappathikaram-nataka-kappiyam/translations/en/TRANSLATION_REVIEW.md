## Review gates

A scene may be marked `translation-reviewed` only after:

1. complete Tamil-source coverage check;
2. semantic fidelity check;
3. Kalaignar-voice/cadence check;
4. rhetoric/repetition/stage-timing check;
5. terminology-register check;
6. Dravidian-context separation check — interpretive movement notes must not leak into translated dialogue;
7. secondary-witness disclosure check.

## Accepted conventions now controlling

- retain `Tamilakam` rather than modernizing it to `Tamil Nadu`;
- distinguish `தமிழ்நாடு / தமிழ்நாட்டு` contextually from `தமிழகம்`;
- preserve slogans, repetition, direct address, poetic accumulation, humour and theatrical sound;
- preserve source ambiguity rather than silently correcting verified Tamil;
- retain cultural/technical terms when English substitution would erase taxonomy, wordplay or source uncertainty;
- translate quoted classical material independently from the verified Tamil scene unless a secondary witness is explicitly introduced and disclosed;
- keep Dravidian-movement interpretation outside translated dialogue;
- do not collapse `பிராமண`, `பார்ப்பன`, `பார்ப்பார்`, `அந்தணர்`, `மறையவன் / மறையவர்` into one undifferentiated `Brahmin` label;
- socially degrading language remains attributable to the character who speaks it, not translator narration;
- do not infer formal religious rites from a title when the scene itself does not depict one;
- do not add narrator foreshadowing before Kalaignar's stage directions disclose an action;
- protected Tamil punctuation/bracket anomalies may be mirrored or explicitly documented rather than silently repaired.

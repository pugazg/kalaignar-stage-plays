## Accepted conventions now controlling

Unless later scene evidence requires an explicitly documented revision:

- retain `Tamilakam` rather than modernizing it to `Tamil Nadu`;
- distinguish source `தமிழ்நாடு / தமிழ்நாட்டு` contextually from `தமிழகம்`;
- preserve slogans, repetitions, direct address, poetic accumulation, humour and theatrical sound;
- retain named cultural/technical terms when English substitution would erase the source taxonomy (`Muthamizh`, `iyal`, `isai`, `koothu`, `pann`, `thalaikkol`, `kalanju`, `swayamvaram`, `oodal`);
- preserve explicit Tamil peoplehood, Tamil economic/cultural worth and historical geography;
- preserve source ambiguity rather than silently correcting verified Tamil;
- keep movement-context commentary outside translated dialogue;
- do not automatically translate `அந்தணர்`, `பார்ப்பார்`, `பார்ப்பன`, or `மறையவர்` through one undifferentiated `Brahmin` label.

### Scene-14 terminology precedent

Scene 14 is now a controlling example of why Kalaignar's vocabulary must remain distinct:

- explicit `பிராமண மாது / பிராமணமாது` → `Brahmin woman`;
- source `பார்ப்பனமாது` → retained as `paarppana woman` in that direction;
- `அந்தணர் / அந்தணன்` → **`Antanar`**, not `Brahmin`;
- `மறையவர்` → `man of sacred lore`;
- `தமிழ் மறை` → `Tamil sacred lore`.

This is not a claim that all occurrences have one fixed English equivalent; future uses still require context review.

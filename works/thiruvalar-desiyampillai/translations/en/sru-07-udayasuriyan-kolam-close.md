Desiyampillai comes out to the front entrance. He calls the driver and tells him to bring the car around quickly. The car comes and stops. His daughter is drawing a kolam in the street.

“Kamala! Kamala! You must sing at this evening's public meeting!” he calls out loudly.

“Oh, I'll sing—which meeting, appa?” Kamala, who had been bent over, straightens up. The kolam she has drawn at the entrance catches Desiyam Pillai's eye. It is not the kolam Kamala usually draws! A new kolam!

## Rising Sun Kolam

Desiyampillai stands stunned.

“Tell your mother that father is going to Ooty!”

Having said this, he gets into the car. The car leaves.

Kamala stands looking at the kolam. Her mother comes outside. The daughter tells her what her father said.

“What can we do, daughter! The time has come for the Congress Party to climb the hill!”

—Desiyam Pillai's wife gives this reply and stands for a long time looking in the direction the car has gone.

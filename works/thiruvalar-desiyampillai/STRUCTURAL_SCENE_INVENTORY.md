**Verdict:** `உதயசூரியன் கோலம்` is a **centered descriptive/intertitle attached to Kamala's kolam inside the final dramatic unit**. It must not be promoted to a source scene title or used to invent a numbered scene. In assembly it should remain a visibly separate source line inside the final unit.

# Scene 25

[The Minister, Ariharanathar and Gurunathar are together.]

Minister: How was my idea! You saw how I made him act exactly like a real madman!

Gurunathar: Extraordinary, Minister! If only he had not fallen into our hands at the right moment, the Assembly of Nobles would have suspected all three of us!

Ariharanathar: Yes — if the suspicion had grown stronger, the religious seat...

[He points to Gurunathar.]

The seat of power...

[He points to the Minister.]

The propaganda-seat!

[He points to himself.]

All three seats would have been shaken!

Minister: My brain is working powerfully now precisely to keep these three seats from shaking!

Gurunathar: Even this puppet king cannot be trusted for long, Swami! Somehow we must make him sign as though he were the king and transfer all authority into my name!

Gurunathar: What?

[He suppresses his inner disturbance.]

Minister: Yes — only then can we do exactly as we please. The right opportunity is about to arise. When word spreads that the king is coming to the foundation-stone ceremony, all the people of the country will gather. What do they know — that this fellow is mad? Right in front of them I shall make him declare that he transfers all authority into my name. What do you say, Gurunathar? How is the idea?

Gurunathar: A splendid idea!

[He does not reveal his bitterness.]

Minister: So, Ariharanathar! Put a report in our newspaper saying, `The king's madness has disappeared through Gurunathar's grace.` Only then will the people believe that the king, in full possession of his mind, is transferring authority into my name!

Ariharanathar: Yes! Yes!

[With bitterness.]

Minister: Then I shall attend to what must be done! I take leave, Gurunathar!

Gurunathar: Very well, Minister. May there be happiness!

[The Minister leaves. Once he has gone, Gurunathar and Ariharanathar look at each other.]

Gurunathar: Ariharanathar — did you hear him?

Ariharanathar: I heard, Swami! I heard.

Gurunathar: He says he will use our support and our prestige as the foundation and build a palace of power on top of it. Shall we allow that?

Ariharanathar: No! No!!

Gurunathar: Gunaseelan, who used to lie at our feet, plans to take up the sceptre using that puppet king! We must find a crooked path to stop this!

Ariharanathar: Yes, Swami!

Gurunathar: Murder! Murder!! There is no other way but murder! When that crooked fellow comes in the king's costume to the temple foundation-stone ceremony — he must be killed! The Minister hopes to seize authority through the mouth of this counterfeit king; that counterfeit king must be murdered. What do you say?

Ariharanathar: Not only must the king be killed; we must accuse Minister Gunaseelar of having made him impersonate the king and eliminate him too. Then the religious seat will rule Manimagudam!

Gurunathar: Exactly! One man to kill the king — one sharp sword! Let us prepare both! Gunaseela! Blunt-witted Minister! You think you can deceive me and rule the country? Trickster against trickster — with a madman in between! I shall see whether you win or I win! Ha...! Deceiver who stretches your tail before the demon called religion! Thick-headed Minister! We shall see who is going to win — we shall see who wins!

Ariharanathar: History has never recorded the prayer-beads being defeated, Swami!

Gurunathar: Prayer-beads — staff and kamandalam — they are not merely objects of devotion, Editor. Look here! They can also be weapons of vengeance — look!

[He removes a part of the staff; a sharp knife is concealed inside.]

Ariharanathar: Ah — the sword is ready! Now only the man is needed to bring our conspiracy to a successful end!

Gurunathar: Ha... ha... ha! He too will be ready soon!

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/25.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The three institutional `பீடம்` terms remain visibly linked as `religious seat`, `seat of power`, and `propaganda-seat`.
- The Minister's attempted transfer of authority and Gurunathar/Ariharanathar's separate murder conspiracy are kept distinct in sequence.
- `ஜெபமாலை`, `தண்டு`, and `கமண்டலம்` are retained as prayer-beads, staff and `kamandalam`; the concealed blade in the staff is source stage action, not an added inference.

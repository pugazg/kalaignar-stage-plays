# Scene 9

[*People's Servant* is being sold in the streets of Manimagudapuram.]

Newspaper Seller: The king has signed the resolution of the Assembly of Nobles! Intensive preparations to build the temple — Minister's statement! A temple tax will be imposed! With a divine article written by Gurunathar, *People's Servant*, price two panam! *People's Servant*, price two panam!

[He cries out and sells the paper. Alli, who comes that way, buys a copy and reads it. She is startled and turns back quickly.]

[The newspaper report spreads throughout the country.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/09.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The scene preserves the source's newspaper-vendor rhythm and its central false claim that the king signed the Assembly resolution.
- `பணம்` is retained as `panam` in the newspaper price rather than converted into a modern currency value.
- *மக்கள் தொண்டன்* remains *People's Servant* and `சீமான்கள் சபை` remains `Assembly of Nobles`, following the established terminology controls.

# Scene 3

[People's Forum — the same night. Alli, the volunteers and the Poet are present.]

He sings the Bharathidasan song that continues:

“Beloved ones, elders, mothers who bore me, fine young lions!”

Alli: A wonderful poem, Poet!... Who reclaimed the barren land where hordes of snakes fought? What a question filled with awakening! If this question rises in the hearts of the people of Manimagudapuram — if the question mark “Who?”, “Who?” appears everywhere, in every place — in a single day Manimagudam will become the People's Crown!

Poet: Alli! The child of poetry born in a poet's heart must be cradled and raised by valiant women like you!

Alli: Cradled? Oh no.. I will not lull children of poetry filled with awakening to sleep. I will let them crawl through the courtyards of the people's hearts. I will put a spear in their hands and send them forth saying, “Go and win!” Poet — keep writing and piling up such epics of feeling, songs of rights that give life to the poor, lines of verse that turn even a coward into a warrior of principle!... Let the bowed-down spirit of freedom rise bearing a flag! Let the hunchback straighten! Let the blind gain sight — let the mute speak — let rights prevail! Let the town and the world flourish!

[At that moment Ponnazhagan, Puthumaippithan and Black Rose arrive.]

Pon.: Alli — do you know him? His name is Puthumaippithan.

Alli: Greetings.

Puthu.: Greetings.

Pon.: I met him by chance. But the meeting has proved very useful! He holds revolutionary ideas — he shares my principle that the seat of power must pass into the hands of the people, and your principle that the people's minds must be developed, for only then will they be fit to govern.

Alli: Why not say it briefly? Altogether, he holds my principles.

Black Rose: Forgive me — the leader failed to introduce me — no, he forgot. I am Black Rose! More or less a man of your principles. If I too could become a member here...

Pon.: I already told you! It will take another six months before you can become a member. Until then, you may stay here if you wish. Alli! I have no objection of any kind to making Puthumaippithan a member of our Forum...

Alli: Then what remains? You have explained the Forum's rules and regulations, haven't you?

Puthu.: Everything is finished. Only the signature... remains...

[Alli brings a book and holds it out for him to sign. Puthumaippithan takes the pen in his hand.]

Alli: Please take the pledge first...

Puthu.: What pledge?

Alli: I will work for the welfare of the people of Manimagudapuram and for their liberation.

Puthu.: I will work for the welfare of the people of Manimagudapuram and for their liberation.

Alli: I will carry out, according to the orders of the People's Forum, every task that must be done for the people.

Puthu.: I will carry out, according to the orders of the People's Forum, every task that must be done for the people.

Alli: For the liberation of the country, I will not hesitate even to give my life.

Puthu.: For the liberation of the country, I will not hesitate even to give my life.

Alli: Mm — sign it.

Puthu.: Mm — sign it.

Alli: [Laughing] No — I am telling you to sign it.

Puthu.: Forgive me. [He signs.]

# Scene 42

[People's Forum. Everyone is present except Kalarani. Ponnazhagan speaks in agitation.]

Ponnazhagan: I had rejoiced that our People's Forum had grown to such magnificent strength that even our opponents would be bewildered! I had proudly believed that no one would dare destroy this Forum! Joy — pride — exultation — circumstances are forming that could grind all of them into dust! Not from the opposing side! Destructive thoughts are rising among us ourselves. Situations in which stabbing and cutting may arise inside the fort itself are coming toward us! A flood of betrayal that no dam can stop! Yes, comrades! Fifth columns are right here! The king's agents, capable at any moment of bringing the life of this Forum to an end, are moving among us!

[Alli, understanding nothing.]

Alli: Fifth column? An agent of the king? Who are they? Name them! We will act immediately!

Ponnazhagan: Whom am I to name, Alli? I cannot even trust myself! Even you may be a traitor!

Alli: Ah!

Ponnazhagan: I am only giving an example! The king might even make me his overseer! He may already have done so! Even you may have been bewitched by the king's beauty! You may have surrendered your heart to his palace — splendour — status! Perhaps that is why you even saved his life?

Alli: What are you saying?

Ponnazhagan: I already told you — it is an example! Who can trust whom, Alli? We guard even our eyes with such care — we give them first place among all our organs — even when we sleep we close them with the doors of our eyelids, embracing and protecting them so dearly that we do not wish to let even a speck fall in and trouble them! Yet if even a tiny disorder arises inside that eye — ayyo! Can its torment be endured? Is it easy for a person to escape it? Perhaps that is why eyes and women are compared with each other? Both are beautiful! And likewise both are full of danger!

Alli: I will not say that your speech is meaningless. Nor am I prepared to believe that it is merely “for example”! You are hiding something! Tell me without hiding it!

Ponnazhagan: Why should I hide anything? Only if one were born a woman and fell in love with a king would one need to hide it during an inquiry before the People's Forum!

Alli: Oh! Are you speaking about Kalarani? Yes — have we made her a member of the Forum? Is she not a prisoner in our Forum? How do “fifth column” and “traitor” apply to her?

Ponnazhagan: I said something, that is all! Whoever it fits may fit it to themselves! But one thing, Alli! No one should dream that fifth-column work can destroy this Forum! The People's Forum may be destroyed today! Ponnazhagan and his comrades may one day have to swing from the gallows while raising the cry of rights. But if some power-drunk man builds castles in his mind that tomorrow, the day after, and thereafter no People's Forum will ever arise, he will certainly be deceived! Alli! If you ever get another chance to speak to that foolish king, do not forget to remind him of this!

Alli: What? Am I to meet the king?

Ponnazhagan: Yes! Did you not say that on the day you saved him he was speaking with you?

Alli: You keep reminding me that I saved the king! You seem to consider it a great crime! If it was wrong, I am ready to bow my head to the Forum's judgement and punishment!

Ponnazhagan: Neither I nor the Forum considers that wrong. What of saving the king? Even if someone told me you sang a love song with him, I would not consider that wrong!

Alli: I understand, Leader — I understand! I understand the reason for your strange new conduct! I respectfully ask that leaders like you should not vent personal anger in public places like this!

Ponnazhagan: What are you saying, woman...? Do not forget that you are speaking to the leader of the People's Forum! Not to the king; remember that!

Alli: I remember! I remember very well, Leader! What is the mystery behind hurling conspiratorial words at me, whom you treated like a sister? What crime have I committed? Tell me! For the welfare of the people, this Alli has not strayed even by a sesame seed from her resolve to march in ranks under Ponnazhagan's leadership! A scaffold? A king's command? Whatever it may be, this Alli has not retreated by an atom from the revolutionary principle of smashing it and raising the battle flag! Then why, Leader, these monstrous accusations against me? These unjust stains? No — do not speak like this! [She cries.] Your sister cannot bear attacks like these, Leader — she cannot bear them!

Ponnazhagan: You ask me to trust you, Alli? I did trust you! More than anyone else in this world, I trusted you. You have left the man who trusted you stranded in midstream, Alli!

Alli: I understand what you are talking about, Leader! From the day I met you, I have regarded you only as a brother!

Ponnazhagan: Yes! Somehow I alone failed to know that! I failed to know that a conspirator who regards Ponnazhagan as her brother and the king who rules the earth as her lover had become my sister!

Alli: Ah! Ayyo! Me — the king's lover! An outrageous accusation! Take those words back immediately!

Ponnazhagan: I cannot!

Alli: Why can you not? What evidence do you have for telling this unjust lie connecting me with the king?

Comrades: Yes. What evidence does the Leader show that Alli is guilty?

Ponnazhagan: I saw it myself!

Comrade: And the witness?

Ponnazhagan: I myself am the witness!

Comrade: There is no court anywhere in which the complainant himself comes as the witness! We reject this accusation deliberately levelled against Alli!

All Comrades: Yes! We cannot permit a conspiracy charge without proper evidence! We remind you that once before, by suspecting Puthumaippithan in this same way, our Forum lost his close association!

Alli: Leader! Call Alli a foolish girl — I will listen! Call me arrogant — I will not be angry! Call me stubborn — I will not make much of it! Simpleton — fool — one possessed of a demon's nature — not a woman but a fiend — say all of that! But do not say that I, who worked in this Forum like a dog, am unworthy of trust! Rather than crown me with the title of traitor, drop a stone on me while I sleep and kill me! Me — a traitor? Me, who dedicated my life to making this Forum strong and radiant — a traitor? After bearing blows from stones and words, never forgetting duty, dignity and discipline even in dreams, valuing life as mere straw and standing in formation with you — is this unfortunate Alli a traitor? [Looking at everyone.] You tell me, comrades — is Alli a traitor? Is Alli a traitor? Alli, who roams day and night without cease holding the battle flag beside you — is Alli a traitor? Is Alli a traitor? Is Alli a traitor?

Everyone: No! No!! No!!!

Ponnazhagan: Hmm... never mind! For the present Alli has won! Let the time come — the black darkness will disappear... the sun of truth will certainly arise!

Poet: [entering] Before the sun arises, our fate itself will be ruined!

Ponnazhagan: Poet!

Poet: The People's Forum has been banned — declared illegal!

Ponnazhagan: What?

Alli: Ah — a ban on the People's Forum?

Poet: Not only that. In the Assembly of Nobles, which has met secretly in the dead of night, an order has been issued to arrest all of us tomorrow morning!

Ponnazhagan: Ah!

Alli: An order to arrest us?

Poet: It does not end there — a durbar of repression! My heart bursts merely to think of it — arrangements have even been made to give a reward to whoever brings Puthumaippithan's corpse!

Alli: Ah!

Poet: Anyone who sees that `kaama veeran` may shoot him wherever he is found!

Alli: Ayyo! Him? To shoot him? Poet, we must act urgently at once. Let the comrades state their opinion.

Ponnazhagan: What opinion is there? The flood has risen above our heads! Now the king's head itself must be plucked off and thrown away!

Comrades: Yes, that is right!

Poet: The right idea! Before the emergency law is issued in the morning, news of the king's death must be issued! The red of the eastern sky must be defeated by the blood-red of the king who has been plucked away! On a bed spread with jasmine, the king's crimson blood must lie scattered like hibiscus petals! Hmm... begin writing the `sirappayiram` of the chapter of revolution!

Ponnazhagan: We may begin writing — but we must know who will first take up the stylus. The king must become a corpse tonight! Who will do the deed? Must we not know that?

Poet: Why not draw lots, as usual? Whose name comes out — that person must kill the king!

Ponnazhagan: [turning to Alli] What does the Leader say to that?

Alli: I am ready! Draw lots! If the king does not die today, Puthumaippithan must die tomorrow — and then we must die. If both we and he are to escape, it is better that the king die! Poet, prepare the lots!

Poet: Here they are.

[He brings them.]

Ponnazhagan: Hmm... shake them and draw!

[The poet shakes them. One man takes a slip, opens it, and the poet reads it. Terrifying music.]

Poet: Alli! Alli must kill the king!

[The comrades applaud.]

Alli: I am ready this very moment! Comrades, give me leave! Poet — I will return victorious! Leader — I have immediately received a worthy opportunity to clear your suspicion of me! I take leave — greetings!

Ponnazhagan: Hmm... go and return, Alli! Here!

[He takes out a pistol and gives it to her. She takes it and leaves.]

Ponnazhagan: [to the comrades] Comrades — my suspicion of Alli has still not gone! Come; we too will follow her! If Alli deceives us, we ourselves must finish the king tonight! Let us go!

[They leave.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/42.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- `ஐந்தாம் படை` remains `fifth column`, continuing the established control while preserving that the accusation is Ponnazhagan's character perspective.
- `கங்காணி` is rendered contextually as `overseer`; no outside institutional meaning is imported.
- `காம வீரன்` is retained as `kaama veeran` because the source epithet is compressed and context-sensitive.
- `சிறப்பாயிரம்` is retained as `sirappayiram`; the literary term is not replaced by an unsupported narrower English category.
- The Forum's decision to kill the king, the drawing of lots, and Alli receiving the pistol are represented as plot actions exactly within the source scene, without narrator endorsement.

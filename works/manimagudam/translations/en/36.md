# Scene 36

[People's Forum. Alli is alone. Black Rose comes and gives her a letter.]

Alli: Who gave it to you?

Black Rose: Puthumaippithan.

Alli: I don't see a signature?

Black Rose: Ask him all this when you see him in person. I am just an ordinary messenger, that's all!

Alli: What is it, Black Rose — why are you speaking in such a strange way?

Black Rose: What else, madam! Your association refused to take me in!

Alli: Oh, is that the anger? All right, all right — go! I will get you admitted soon!

Black Rose: If you are gracious enough, fine... hm... the letter is waiting — read it. If I remain here, you will feel shy. I am going!

[He leaves. Alli reads the letter. As she finishes, Kalarani, who has come there, notices her and watches from hiding. Alli tucks the letter into her waist and goes out. Without her noticing, the letter falls to the floor. Kalarani sees it, reads it and keeps it safely. Ponnazhagan now enters.]

Ponnazhagan: Kalarani, where is Alli?

Kalarani: Always Alli, Alli, Alli... don't you even see that I am here?

Ponnazhagan: What are you saying now?

Kalarani: I am here, ready to wilt at a touch, while you are setting a ladder against a fruit you cannot reach — that's what I am saying!

Ponnazhagan: I have never seen a woman speak this openly! Your kind seems to be a class apart! Shouldn't one know how to speak with a man?

Kalarani: In the same way, men should know how to speak with women! One must not act in haste — place a hand on her head, touch her to see whether she has a fever, stroke her hair, catch her hand and pull her when she runs — one should not indulge in things like that!

Ponnazhagan: How do you know all this? Did Alli tell you?

Kalarani: I saw it myself! I enjoyed it! I laughed!

Ponnazhagan: All right, all right! I will have to send you away from here!

Kalarani: Don't send me anywhere except `Saajur`!

Ponnazhagan: Kalarani! However many times I tell you, you still do not understand! You tell me a way — a way to forget Alli!

Kalarani: Think of me...

Ponnazhagan: I have to forget her first before I can think of you!

Kalarani: She does not think of you at all!

Ponnazhagan: Can you decide that already? Alli is imprinted on my mind like an indestructible painting! Can I forget her? I cannot! I cannot!

Kalarani: I will give you an idea to decide whether you can forget her or not! Leave right now for the garden beside the palace — you will see Alli there!

Ponnazhagan: What are you saying, Kalarani!

Kalarani: A man is waiting there to drink Alli in by the handful — Alli has gone there!

Ponnazhagan: Is that true?

Kalarani: Why doubt it? Go and you will know!

Ponnazhagan: I am going right now. I will find out to whom Alli belongs. Kalarani! If this alone turns out to be a lie, then Ponnazhagan will become a storm in your life — beware!

Kalarani: Become a tiger later; first go and find out what the matter is with the spotted deer!

Ponnazhagan: Hm... I am going right now!

[He leaves. Kalarani breathes a deep sigh.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/36.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- Kalarani's discovery of the dropped letter is retained as stage action; the translation does not invent the letter's wording because the Tamil scene does not print it.
- `தொட்டால் துவள ... எட்டாத கனிக்கு ஏணி` keeps the source contrast between a woman close at hand and an unreachable fruit.
- The enlarged-source form `சாஜூரைத் தவிர` is intentionally preserved as `Saajur` rather than silently corrected or assigned an unsupported meaning.
- The closing tiger / spotted-deer exchange is kept literally enough to preserve the source animal-wordplay.

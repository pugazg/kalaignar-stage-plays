# Scene 28

[The pavilion for the foundation-stone ceremony. Gurunathar arrives; Ariharanathar follows him. A gleaming knife shines in the hand of the traitor arranged to kill the king. Gurunathar signals encouragement to him with his eyes. The man nods back, indicating that he will complete the task successfully.]

At that moment...

Voices: `Do not destroy Kodikkal Nagar!`

[Alli, Ponnazhagan and others enter with volunteers, raising the slogan.]

Gurunathar: What is this new shouting?

Ponnazhagan: Not shouting — a battle-flag raised to protect Kodikkal Nagar!

Gurunathar: O God! Your grace! Young people... do not provoke the king's anger. If the cavalry is unleashed... you will be smashed to dust.

Alli: We do not care... we have come prepared for death! Here — let your king shoot us dead, step across our corpses and then conduct his foundation-stone ceremony... Hm, comrades... begin the blockade!

[Everyone lies down across the way.]

Ariharanathar: Ayyo — this is the route the king will come by. You are going to die for nothing...

Alli: We are not of the breed that fears death like you... You move aside! Let the God who is going to occupy a temple in Manimagudapuram first behold the display of our corpses — the corpses of those who labour for the people of that land! Then let him consume the prasadam you offer.

[Standing and addressing those lying down.]

Alli: Comrades! There — surrounded by troops carrying guns, with traitors whirling ceremonial fans and maidens dancing, King Manimara, the Lord with ornamented ears, comes to conduct the foundation-stone ceremony. Sound the drum of struggle!

Voices: `Do not destroy Kodikkal Nagar!`

[Those below repeat the cry.]

Gurunathar: [To Ariharanathar] This too is good! Once the king is dead, we can put the blame on these people.

Ariharanathar: Yes! Yes! Everything is turning out for the best!

[The king arrives in splendour. Gurunathar signals to the traitor. The man lunges at the king. On seeing this, Alli suddenly throws herself directly in the path of the traitor's knife. She is badly wounded in the shoulder. Meanwhile the traitor runs away. Total confusion. Alli faints. Ponnazhagan and the others are shocked. Gurunathar slips away with Ariharanathar. Confusion grows. Gunfire increases and the people scatter. Minister Gunaseelar too runs away.]

King: [To the guards] Hm! Carry her to my palace — immediately!

[Ulagappan and the guards carry Alli away.]

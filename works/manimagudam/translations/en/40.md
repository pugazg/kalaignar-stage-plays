# Scene 40

[Assembly of Nobles. The king, Gurunathar, Ariharanathar and the Minister are present. The people are on one side; the poet is among them. Gurunathar is speaking.]

Gurunathar: It was that Puthumaippithan — Ponnazhagan's group — that tried to kill the king! Puthumaippithan is a greater terrorist than Ponnazhagan! Not only has he deceived the very eyes of the government and created a great propaganda force against the government, he is also drawing up plans to overthrow this government completely! The *Manimagudam* newspaper appears as if by magic — and is sold as if by magic! Who is the schemer who can outwit even Kannan? Who is the destroyer who came to turn Manimagudapuram into a land of atheism? The sinner who mocks devotees — the frog that ridicules religion — the scoundrel who asks for reasons behind God's commands — the wrongdoer who spins stories that there is nothing beyond rationalism — the wicked creature who incites the common people with words like innovation — revolution — common ownership — Puthumaippithan! Puthumaippithan!! Terrorist Puthumaippithan! Puthumaippithan, who tried to kill the crowned king, must be shot down immediately! He must be shot wherever he is found! It must be proclaimed by drum that a reward of ten thousand mohars will be given to whoever brings the corpse of that utter atheist. Those `maathenmaththargal` tried to push into the pit of death none other than the divine incarnation — the nationally renowned King Manimaran! God saved the king! We must destroy the wrongdoers completely. Otherwise God will keep being forced into the urgent work of saving the king again and again. Let us put an end to this trouble. I demand that this honoured Assembly of Nobles immediately order the arrest of Ponnazhagan's group — and order Puthumaippithan to be shot dead wherever he is found! This is the command of the divine — the will of God!

Minister: Nobles! You have heard everything Gurunathar said! Considering the welfare of Manimagudapuram and the safety of the king, I request that a resolution be passed ordering the arrest of Ponnazhagan's gang and banning the People's Forum.

Noble: On behalf of the nobles, I welcome it!

[The nobles raise slogans.]

Minister: Good — the resolution banning Ponnazhagan's group and ordering their arrest has been passed unanimously! The right to order the killing of an individual belongs to the king. Therefore I request the king himself to issue the order concerning Puthumaippithan!

Gurunathar: Is the king going to refuse? Your Majesty, say it without hesitation — order those arrogant men to be placed before the gun!

Ariharanathar: Yes — that is right!

Minister: True! It is just that he be eliminated! The law of the government must eliminate the man who came to eliminate the king! The Assembly awaits the king's command!

King: Nobles — according to your wish, and according to the wishes of Gurunathar and the Minister — I order Puthumaippithan to be shot dead.

[The nobles applaud. The poet leaves in fury.]

King: I also order it proclaimed by drum — as Gurunathar said — that whoever brings Puthumaippithan's corpse will receive a reward of ten thousand mohars!

[The nobles applaud.]

Gurunathar: Sabash, sabash! Manimagudam has been saved! The king has been saved! The enemies of religion have been destroyed! Eradicated! Long live religion! Long live God! Long may they flourish!

[At that moment Ulagappan runs in with several soldiers.]

Ulagappan: The conspirator who came to kill the king has been caught.

Minister: What?

Ulagappan: The man who threw the knife at the king during the foundation-stone ceremony! We have caught that murderer — we have locked him in the prison!

Gurunathar: Has that `chandalan` been caught? [Surprise and fear.]

Ulagappan: Yes, Swami — he has been caught and imprisoned.

Gurunathar: Members of the Assembly — do you see? He must be one of Puthumaippithan's men. Wait; I will go at once and, by divine power, draw out from that fellow who he is — and at whose instigation he came to kill the king! I will return in a moment!

[Gurunathar leaves. The Assembly is quiet. The king rises from his seat and looks at the Minister.]

King: Minister, please — you and just two more nobles must come with me.

Minister: Your Majesty! [Unable to understand, startled, but at the same time with an air of indifference.] What are you saying?

King: Please do not ask anything — you and the nobles follow me. [As the king walks away.] Do not dissolve the Assembly — let it continue until I return.

[The king leaves. Understanding nothing, the Minister and two nobles follow him.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/40.md`; verified scans 137–140 were used for source-sensitive forms; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- Gurunathar's speech is kept as propaganda spoken by the character: accusations of terrorism, atheism, rationalism, revolution and common ownership are not converted into narrator claims.
- `மோகராக்கள்` remains `mohars`, preserving the source denomination.
- The enlarged-source form `மாதேன்மத்தர்கள்` is deliberately retained as `maathenmaththargal`; the verified page record explicitly warns against normalizing it.
- `சண்டாளன்` is retained as the source slur `chandalan` rather than silently replacing it with a different social category.
- The king's order to shoot Puthumaippithan and the ten-thousand-mohar corpse bounty are represented exactly as actions within the plot; the poet's angry departure and the later capture of the actual knife attacker remain distinct events.

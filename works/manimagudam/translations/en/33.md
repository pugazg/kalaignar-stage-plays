# Scene 33

[People's Forum. The same night. Ponnazhagan sits sorrowfully. Kalarani comes there.]

Kalarani: If you sit here sleepless and grieving like this, will Alli come back?

Ponnazhagan: Kalarani — don't trouble me as well. Go and sleep!

Kalarani: I have taken it as my duty to end your sorrow — and you call it trouble?

Ponnazhagan: You are bothering me without knowing the time!

Kalarani: Who would call nighttime an unsuitable time for this?

Ponnazhagan: What are you saying now?

Kalarani: What new thing am I going to say? From the day I came here, haven't my every movement told you what I think and what I yearn for? Must I still say all of it aloud?

Ponnazhagan: The palace kind really shows itself! A comrade who lived and moved among us is missing — we do not know what has happened to her — and only now you come to sing a love-song.

Kalarani: Do you think I am not worried about Alli?

Ponnazhagan: How would you feel concern for her? Don't you think of her as your rival?

Kalarani: No! It does not look as though she is competing with me by loving the very man I love!

Ponnazhagan: Did Alli tell you that?

Kalarani: Must she tell me...? One woman can easily understand another woman!

Ponnazhagan: Wrong calculation! Alli is mine! No one can change that!

Kalarani: Then you hate me! That is the truth, isn't it?

Ponnazhagan: I do not hate you, Kalarani! But the first person my thoughts went to was Alli! Can that be changed? Is there such an exception in the grammar of love? Please forgive me. Kala... go... go to sleep! I am going to sleep too!

[He turns down the lamp and lies down. Kala leaves. A little later, Alli comes in and turns up the lamp. Ponnazhagan starts awake and cries, `Alli!`]

Ponnazhagan: Alli! You made it back? I was beside myself wondering what had happened! Ayyo, is the wound serious?

[He touches the wound to examine it — and naturally places his hand upon her.]

Alli: It is not serious — I escaped!

Ponnazhagan: I sent all our people out looking for you! Yes — where were you hidden?

Alli: I was shut up in the palace!

Ponnazhagan: What?

Alli: Yes. If Puthumaippithan had not come, I could not have escaped!

Ponnazhagan: Puthumaippithan?

Alli: Yes — he is the one who secretly got me out!

Ponnazhagan: Appa! This Puthumaippithan really is a man of great ability! He enters the enemy's very fortress and returns wearing the garland of victory... Alli, where is Puthumaippithan now? I feel like seeing him.

Alli: It seems he has no rest at all! And the hurt he felt toward us has still not gone away!

Ponnazhagan: Poor man — we are the ones who made the mistake! By suspecting a good man, we weakened the People's Forum. If revolution can take human form, that form belongs to Puthumaippithan!

Alli: He says the same about you!

Ponnazhagan: Is that so? I am happy. Hm — Alli, you are exhausted. We can talk about everything in the morning — sleep first!

Alli: Hm, all right! Yes... did anyone say anything... wrongly, because I saved the king?

Ponnazhagan: They did! I answered all of it! Our poet gave an entire sermon saying that you protected the People's Forum from blame!

Alli: Where is the poet?

Ponnazhagan: As usual, he has gone out to gather intelligence! We can talk about the rest in the morning. Sleep, Alli..! Why should you lie down here...? Go on, sleep. I will lie outside where there is more air..!

[Alli lies on the bed.]

Ponnazhagan: Don't strain yourself! You must not move your arm too much either! You need plenty of rest...

[As he speaks, he draws the blanket over her legs.]

Ponnazhagan: Your face looks so worn... you don't have a headache, do you?

[He touches her to check.]

Alli: Mm-mm!

Ponnazhagan: Hm, sleep. Don't talk — sleep!

[He strokes her hair. After seeing a few such untoward gestures, Alli gets up.]

Alli: I will go inside and sleep beside Kalarani. She will be alone...

[She rises.]

Ponnazhagan: Why, Alli... why?

Alli: Nothing. You sleep...

Ponnazhagan: Alli! [He catches her hand.]

Alli: Let me go!

[She leaves. Ponnazhagan is confused. Kalarani has been watching all of this from hiding.]

Ponnazhagan: [To himself.] Chi! I was hasty — far too hasty!

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/33.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- Kalarani's direct pursuit of Ponnazhagan and his repeated insistence that Alli is the first object of his love are kept explicit.
- Ponnazhagan's admiration for Puthumaippithan after learning of Alli's escape is retained without importing knowledge of the king's concealed identity.
- The caregiving sequence is translated as staged: wound-checking, blanket, head-touching and hair-stroking lead Alli to withdraw; the English does not add motives beyond the source.
- The unusual verified source expression around `... ஜாதி` is rendered conservatively as `kind` rather than silently regularizing the Tamil form.

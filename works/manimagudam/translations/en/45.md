# Scene 45

[A part of the palace. Ulagappan is on guard. He sees someone coming and speaks to himself.]

Ulagappan: Oh-ho — is that Alli? So she has started coming all the way into the inner palace looking for her lover? Yes — since when has this been going on? Has the king told her the truth? Otherwise would madam come here herself? All right... all right... why should I stand here as an obstacle? I will move aside!

[He leaves that place. Alli slowly passes through and enters toward the palace — terrifying music.]

# Scene 24

[After the king, pretending to be mad, has been taken away by Ulagappan.]

Gurunathar: Honourable nobles! Wealthy men blessed by Bhagavan! The total of sinful acts in this empire of Manimagudapuram has grown vast! The number of people who live by abusing those who wear saffron has also increased! What we saw with our own eyes a little while ago in this sacred Assembly is the result of that!

The sins committed by the people fall upon the head of the king. Is not the king the leader of the people? The fire of anger that has seized our Lord is the reason the sovereign's madness has ripened. If we continue to permit those who speak atheism and those whose tongues are hardened by such speech in this kingdom, the disease now afflicting the king will next seize and torment each noble — and after that it will seize and torment every citizen! Why stop there? God himself will become the matted-haired deity who burned the three cities and destroy the entire empire of Manimagudapuram — except me!

Noble 1: It is true — what Gurunathar says is true! We must immediately perform the foundation-stone ceremony for the temple and calm God's anger!

Noble 2: Yes! I second it.

All Nobles: We all second it!

Gurunathar: The seventh day of Margazhi — a good day dear to my heart! On that day, the foundation-stone ceremony for the temple works!

Noble: Excellent! The king himself must come and conduct the foundation-stone ceremony.

Gurunathar: Gladly — we shall bring the king himself to the ceremony. But by then his illness must be cured! Nobles! To protect this country from every harm, I have decided that tonight I shall permit my inner spirit to speak with God. So that God may come and speak with me quickly, I ask each of you, who care for the welfare and prosperity of Manimagudapuram, now to praise his sacred name in this Assembly!

Minister: All resolutions are carried unanimously. The prayer may now begin!

[All the nobles conduct bhajan.]

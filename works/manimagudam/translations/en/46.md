# Scene 46

[The king is asleep. A mosquito net is tied around the bed. Alli enters. She trips below and falls face down. The pistol falls to the floor. The king wakes and gets up. As she hurriedly rises and goes to take the pistol in her hand, he catches her hand. Though the pistol is in her hand, she cannot fire; her hand is caught in his.]

King: Alli! Why have you come here?

Alli: I do not know! I came to kill you!

King: To kill me? Why?

Alli: How long can you live by killing us? Must we not show that the instrument of killing can turn toward the `kolu-mandapam` too? That is why!

King: Kill me! I am not afraid! But tell me what profit there is in killing me!

Alli: There may be no profit! But at least there will be no loss, will there? If we leave you alive, tomorrow morning an order will arise to arrest Ponnazhagan's group — a law will be born to shoot down Puthumaippithan — the People's Forum will be banned. We decided to kill you in order to prevent all those things from happening!

King: I myself have stopped every one of the things you named! Tomorrow morning I am going to hand Manimagudapuram over to the people. The resolution has just been passed in the Assembly of Nobles.

Alli: I understand — king who rules the earth — I understand your trick! Do you think you can escape by telling lies? I will not return without extinguishing the life that dams and blocks the new flood of rights!

King: You would extinguish my life, Alli — if you knew who I am, you would embrace this very body!

[He releases Alli. Alli points the pistol directly at his chest.]

King: Alli — shoot! Shoot and kill me! But before you shoot, learn who I am!

[He turns toward the bureau.]

Alli: Stop!

King: I am not going to take a gun! I am going to show you `intha thuppaakkiyin yaar` — the source's strange “who of this pistol”! Wait a moment! [He takes a beard and other things from the bureau.] In one second you will know who I am! Here! Look, Alli! Look at me! Look at whom you came to shoot!

[He stands as Puthumaippithan.]

Alli: Ah! Puthumaippithan?

King: Puthumaippithan himself! The lover of the revolutionary Alli! The man who, by mistake, happened to be born in this golden house! Alli! Are you going to shoot me? You will shoot!! You are a strange fire — from a distance you burn; when one comes near you, you cool!

[Alli stands bewildered, unable to understand anything. Only Ponnazhagan's voice is heard.]

Voice: “Yes — somehow I failed to know that a conspirator who regarded Ponnazhagan as a brother and the king who rules the earth as her lover had come to stand beside me — betrayal — a flood of betrayal no dam can stop — fifth columns are right here — the king's agents who can at any moment finish the life of this Forum are moving among us.”

[Alli trembles.]

Alli: I am a fifth column! I am a traitor! Ponnazhagan's accusation is true! I am the woman who lay in a lap at the palace entrance! I am the woman caught in the embrace of the palace man!

King: No, Alli! Not at all! I am not the heartless man you imagine! Nor am I a magician who deceived you! I proudly say that I am a child running at the forefront of democracy! I am the same Puthumaippithan whom you loved! A tiger may roam in a deer's skin — but I became a deer forced to roam in a tiger's skin! I became a man forced to live in the king's disguise! I am not responsible for this wrong! My treasure — believe me! I thought that only by bearing the king's role for a few days could I do some permanent good for the people — and as I thought, I have succeeded in the work! Alli! Will you still not believe me? Tomorrow morning people's rule is going to blossom! The king is going to renounce the crown! The Assembly of Nobles is going to be dissolved! Believe me, Alli! Believe me!!

Alli: Uh-huh... I must carry out the command of the People's Forum! And I must wash away, with your blood, the mark of shame that says I am the king's lover!

Puthumaippithan: Then — carry out the command! Alli! Kill your lover and go! If the love and affection that have grown between us all these days have not created compassion in your heart — what can I do? Carry out the order! Take the life of the man who regarded you as his very life! Aim and shoot into the broad chest against which you rested after imprinting your beautiful face! No — no! Do not shoot my chest! My beloved Alli is there! The bullets may strike her! Aim at my head and kill me! My love! My very life! My precious medicine! Finish the man you desire in a single moment! Hmm, shoot! Wait, wait — here I come! Do not shoot Puthumaippithan! He is your lover! You must not shoot your lover! Your enemy is the king! Shoot the king! Shoot him down!

[Puthumaippithan removes his disguise and stands as the king. Alli stands lost to herself.]

King: Hmm... shoot... Alli... here is your enemy — the king of Manimagudapuram! Not the Puthumaippithan who bewitched you — he is gone! He will never again appear before your eyes! Shoot — do not weaken! Fire again and again and shoot me down! Wait a little... Alli! Let me remember you once to my heart's content! Let me hold in my mind's eye how we played and sang! Let me give one joyful sigh! Flower-handed woman — queen who fills my heart — beloved — Alli — beloved, beloved — shoot... shoot!

[He speaks with his eyes closed. A gunshot. Terrifying music. He opens his eyes — he has not died. Alli has shot herself and lies on the floor.]

King: Ah... Alli...!

[He is stunned. At that moment Ponnazhagan runs in, pointing a pistol.]

Ponnazhagan: Stop! Unjust king! You sinner! Have you killed Alli? Revenge for this — now!

[He shoots the king. The king falls. Guards run in and surround Ponnazhagan and the others.]

King: [rising with difficulty] Stop — guards! No one touch them! I sought this ending myself! Ponnazhagan — you acted too quickly! I did not kill Alli! She shot herself! There is no guilt on you either! Some hasty people must have told you only the first resolution of the Assembly of Nobles. You do not know what the final resolution was! As I breathe my dying breaths, I am going to tell you joyful news — from tomorrow morning people's rule is going to dawn in Manimagudapuram! I came to sleep only after that decision had been taken in the Assembly of Nobles — you yourselves have arranged my long sleep; I am glad! I have received the proper fruit for the labour I have suffered for so many days! I have cultivated the minds of the people — enough for them to administer the new government! Ponnazhagan, I take leave of you with the pride that I have opened the way for a people's government in which caste — religious divisions — doctrines of God — superstitions are all abolished! What could not be accomplished through violence — and even if done that way could not endure — I have accomplished peacefully; my principle of an intellectual revolution, followed by political and economic revolution, has succeeded! Why are you startled? Are you looking at me and thinking, “What is this — it sounds like Puthumaippithan's doctrine”? Not only Puthumaippithan; it may even sound as though the editor of the *Manimagudam* newspaper is speaking! Here, look, comrades! Look carefully! Look at your comrade! [He changes into Puthumaippithan.] I was born a king — by the rule laid down by religion and authority! I lived as a servant of the people — here I die as a servant of the people! Comrades! From tomorrow — a new government — a new flag — a new life — a new general election! Contest that election! Victory is on our side! Let the messengers of poor people become members who rule! Let the old `paththaambasali` rule perish! Let the rule of rationalism dawn! Forget violence from this day onward — let it congeal with my blood! I take leave, comrades — I take leave. Alli... Alli! [embracing her]

King: We take leave! The two lovers of the new age take leave of you! Ponnazhagan! Where are you? Raise a cry of greeting once! Long live the People's Forum! The People's Forum... long live!

Everyone: Long live!

King: Long live, long live! Long live the homeland! Long live self-respect!! Long live equality!! Long live...

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/46.md`, with verified page records for scans 163–169 used for the long cross-page continuities; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- Source-confirmed `கொலுமண்டபத்துப்` is retained as `kolu-mandapam` rather than silently normalized; the line's turn-of-violence image remains visible.
- The extinguish/embrace double sense of `அணை` is preserved in `extinguish my life / embrace this body`.
- Scan 164 explicitly verifies the anomalous source wording `இந்தத் துப்பாக்கியின் யார் என்று காட்டப் போகிறேன்!`; the English deliberately exposes the anomaly as `intha thuppaakkiyin yaar` instead of repairing it into a smoother sentence.
- `ஜனநாயகத்தின் முன்னோடும் பிள்ளை` is translated closely as `a child running at the forefront of democracy`, without replacing the source image.
- Ponnazhagan's offstage accusation remains a remembered voice and not narrator fact.
- Alli's self-shooting, Ponnazhagan's shooting of the king, and the king's dying political speech are kept in source order; no extra death direction is inserted after the final `Long live...` because the Tamil scene supplies none.
- `பத்தாம்பசலி` is retained as `paththaambasali` rather than assigned an unsupported modern political label.
- In the dying line `மக்கள் தொண்டனாக`, the phrase is translated as the common-noun `servant of the people`; the newspaper title remains separately controlled as *People's Servant* elsewhere.

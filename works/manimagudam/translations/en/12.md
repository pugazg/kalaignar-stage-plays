# Scene 12

[The garden behind the palace — night. Kalarani and Vanji come that way.]

Kalarani: Vanji! You stay here. I will go and see the king and come back.

Vanji: Don't come running back quickly, without even finishing the conversation, because you are worried that I am waiting! I will stay here talking to the peacock, the cuckoo, the mangoes and the flowering plants. Take your time and come back with a good result!

Kalarani: I am going with a good decision — won't I return with a good result?

Vanji: Yes, don't forget that. Like Menaka who conquered Vishwamitra, return with a victorious smile! I will pluck flowers here and prepare a victory garland to place on you. I need some work until you return, don't I? Kala! As soon as you go, don't begin talking casually to the king. First bow with the respect due to a king! Even as you bow, ensnare him with your eyes! Fold your hands so that the beauty of your kantal-like fingers can be seen! Don't stand in some dark place where your face cannot be seen at night; stand near a lamp where this full-moon face can shine clearly while you speak!... Cast glances from time to time! After that, the king will be dust before you!

Kalarani: Are you giving me lessons?

Vanji: The alphabet-primer of love, Kala! Why — do you know it without being taught?

Kalarani: I need no primer at all. Don't go anywhere. Stay here; I will be back presently.

[Kala leaves. Vanji plucks flowers and begins stringing a garland.]

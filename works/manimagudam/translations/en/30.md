# Scene 30

[Elsewhere in the palace. The king and Ulagappan are together.]

King: What, Ulagappa... why did you call me away so soon — at such a delicious moment?

Ulagappan: There was nothing delicious about it, my lord — you were only standing at a distance and talking!

King: Even if I stood at a distance, it was not distant talk — and not empty talk either!

Ulagappan: Sugar-candy talk, then! Your Majesty! The Minister sent his servant Vanji to see whether you are here!

King: Why has he sent her?

Ulagappan: When the town is in confusion, will the Minister come outside? So she has come. Show her your face! Hey, Vanji, Vanji, come here!

[She enters.]

Ulagappan: See — here he is! The puppet king!

King: Why have you come, girl? The Minister-master sent you to have a look and return, did he?

Vanji: Yes!

King: Go tell him I'm here! Tell the master not to step outside — the whole town is in uproar.

Vanji: Very well.

King: Fine. I am going to pour two pots of water over my head — I haven't bathed for four days — my whole body itches! I must change these clothes too — do these clothes suit me?

Ulagappan: Go bathe, bathe!

[The king leaves. Ulagappan moves closer to Vanji.]

Vanji: Fine, fine! Don't smile at me at all — and don't burn my heart either!

Ulagappan: Am I burning your heart? What is it — a stove? Or firewood for me to burn?

Vanji: I am standing here with so much worry! Do you have even a little concern for it?

Ulagappan: It isn't that I have no concern, beauty — there is only a little obstruction on this shore, that's all!

Vanji: Is it enough to speak as sweetly as sugar?

Ulagappan: Don't make the tangle grow — tell me the news quickly...

Vanji: Last night I waited and waited at the garden gate until my legs broke and my eyes flowered over.

Ulagappan: Ah — legs broken, eyes flowering! Ayyo, then you cannot walk — and you cannot see either! Vanji, a disease in which flowers fall into the eyes must not be allowed to worsen — your sight may disappear altogether!

Vanji: You are teasing me. Only God knows how I stood suffering beside that fence for the lover who is going to tie the thali around my neck.

Ulagappan: Ayyo! If God knew, he would not have kept quiet! He would have come there in the incarnation of Kannan!

Vanji: Even if he had come like that, I would not have minded...

Ulagappan: What?

Vanji: I would have sent him himself as my messenger to you!

Ulagappan: Ah, of course! He is the rescuer who went as messenger for Arjuna, after all... My dear Vanji... give up your worry... [He moves closer.]

Vanji: Hm... don't touch me!

Ulagappan: Shall I sing, `O Vanji-deer who came seeking me... do not make a fuss in vain`?

Vanji: Will sulking end through song?

Ulagappan: Through dance?

Vanji: Hm-hm.

Ulagappan: Through union?

[Vanji closes her eyes and smiles.]

Ulagappan: Ah! White-pearl smile that sends its glance through the gaps between your fingers! Beauty — graceful one — come, give me those palms red as lotuses!

[He takes her hand. Vanji turns her face away.]

Ulagappan: Turn your face! [She turns.] Ah! Hair like hair! Eyebrows like eyebrows! Eyes like eyes! A face like a face!

Vanji: What is this new kind of description?

Ulagappan: This is natural description. I don't like that work of saying `eyebrows like a bow — eyes like arrows — face like a shield` and turning the whole face into an armoury!

Vanji: By talking and talking like this, you are making my dizziness grow... the moment I see you, I feel as though a snake has bitten the crown of my head!

Ulagappan: Ayyo! So much poison?

Vanji: No — so much intoxication. You are both the snake that causes the intoxication and the magician who draws out the poison!

Ulagappan: Ah, my sugar-candy who speaks the language of love in a new way! My bouquet of flowers!

Vanji: See! Sugar-candy, flower-bouquet — you have already begun speaking about melting and being crushed! Old descriptions!

Ulagappan: Fine — my sugar-candy that does not melt, my flower-bouquet that does not crumple! Is that correction enough? My honey! [With a deep sigh.]

Vanji: [With a deep sigh] I cannot stand any longer. I am going somewhere, my beloved!

Ulagappan: Ah... where are you going? Vanji! Where are you going?

Vanji: [Swooning as she collapses] I am going to the world of bliss!

Ulagappan: Here — I have come too! Take me with you! Man or woman, if one goes alone, there is no admission ticket to the world of bliss...

[She falls unconscious.]

# Scene 20

[Kodikkal Nagar. Government soldiers enter the huts of the poor and commit violence. Roofs are pulled apart. Walls are demolished. The people of Kodikkal Nagar stand in the middle of the street with their children.]

Old Man: [Staggering] What injustice is this, sons? Without even giving advance warning, you leave us in the middle of the street with our children like this. Would even God accept this?

Commander: It is precisely to build a great mansion for God that we are telling all of you to run away. Hey, old man! Do you know what will happen if you speak against us?

Old Man: What more is there to happen than this? You sinners! Where are we to go and stand stranded now?

Commander: Chi, you old dog! Move aside — standing in the way while we are working! Get out! Go!

[A young man, his wife and child are driven out of one house. A soldier throws away the child's doll along with the other belongings.]

Child: Father — oh, my doll!

[The child cries.]

Father: Chi — the whole family is being destroyed and you care about a doll, a doll...!

[He strikes the child; the child cries. Another householder is dragged out while being beaten. His wife follows behind weeping.]

Wife: Sir — please leave him alone, sir. We will vacate the place as you ordered.

Commander: Chi, don't talk and talk until you kill us with it. This brute spoke against us... has he that much arrogance?

[They beat him. Blood runs all over his body.]

Beaten Man: Why are you beating me? You have thrown earth into our livelihood, struck us in the stomach and left us without even a place to live — must you beat us too? Ayyo, Amma!

[Whip blows fall. He collapses. At that moment People's Forum volunteers led by Alli and Ponnazhagan run in.]

Alli: Stop! [She stands directly in front of the man and seizes the whip.] Cowards who run riot against the poor who do not know how to resist — stop!... Why are you beating him?

Commander: Who are you to ask the reason? The seat of power of Manimagudapuram has issued the order!

Alli: You cannot, brute! Commander of the wise men who burn a thousand huts and build a temple! Is it only people like you, who have thrown away their hearts, who receive high posts in Manimagudapuram's army? Are you human beings or machines? The order was only to empty the huts of the poor. Who ordered you to empty their skeletal bodies too and light a worship-lamp for God inside those skeletons? Who said it? Who?

Commander: Gurunathar's order! The Minister's approval! The king's signature! Excellent prey for the tips of our whips!

Alli: You will hunt, soldiers, you will hunt — as long as people remain like cattle! As long as water instead of fire rises in the coward's eyes! Until the iron hand of hunted humanity rises against your domination, you will hunt! Blind men who destroy living temples to build one temple for Gurunathar! Worthless men of stunted minds! Remember in your hearts that all the destroyers who hunted people because they knew no path for governing a country have sprouted and lie like thorn forests in the ravines of history!

Commander: What? Before a formation of manly soldiers, does a petty woman have this much nerve?

Alli: [Laughing] Wrong calculation, commander — a very wrong calculation! Women are not merely queens of the bridal bed. Once a storm rises in our hearts, we will not let go until the authority of men like you flies away like cotton! This hand has not learned only to rock a cradle; it has learned to break the orders of madmen too! Women are not merely beautiful maidens who display art and portray the state of the country. When cruel men like you raise their heads, women know how to smash those heads too. Only your Gurunathar — the madman who says a woman is illusion — will forget that "women of courage are the victory-lamps of a nation." You know how to hold a sword — do not forget justice! Shame! Shame!

Commander: Enough of your sermon!

Ponnazhagan: Friend — of course it sounds like a sermon. The cry rising from the wounded heart of those pushed backward will sound like a sermon. To whom? To jungle kings whose eardrums have been torn! To pleasure-loving dandies whose eyes have grown flesh from seeing nothing but golden jeweled beds and women speaking nectar-sweet words, the angry voice of the poor and the cry for rights from the fallen will sound only like a sermon.

Soldier 1: What is this fellow babbling?

Soldier 2: He isn't babbling — he is shouting!

Ponnazhagan: A donkey does not know the fragrance of camphor, my man! Sermon — babble — shouting — abuse — you cannot keep blocking the arrows of liberation for long with shields like these.

Soldier: Fine. Why have you come here now? What do you intend to do?

Alli: We have come to protect our own people — elder brothers, younger brothers, elder sisters, younger sisters, fathers and mothers. We have come to free a thousand households from suffering. Shall I put it briefly? We have come to stop `kurappuligal` like you who pounce upon these people already bent and crushed.

Soldier: Against the government?

Alli: No... against injustice!

Soldier: Against God?

Alli: No... as loving comrades!

Soldier: You cannot stop us! Hm... let it proceed!

Alli: We shall see that too.

[Alli stands opposite them with her arms spread wide.]

Alli: Turn all of us into corpses first, then drive those poor people away. Hm! Carry on with the noble work you have undertaken!

Ponnazhagan: Soldiers! Turn back. Tell the king that the people refuse to leave! Let the king understand how fiercely the plan to destroy Kodikkal and raise a temple is opposed. Let the seat of power understand it!

Commander: Impossible. Why are you standing there? [To the other soldiers.] Beat these people too and drive them away.

[Ponnazhagan, Alli and the others are beaten. Blood flows from Alli's head. Puthumaippithan watches all this from one side. He has a small pistol in his hand. One soldier comes from behind to stab Ponnazhagan. Puthumaippithan shoots the soldier down, saves Ponnazhagan and disappears. The soldiers are shocked and lift the fallen soldier. The confusion grows. Ponnazhagan and Alli are attacked more severely by the soldiers and fall. The huts are destroyed. People run. The soldiers laugh terrifyingly.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/20.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The eviction violence, blood, whipping, destruction of huts and assault on the Forum volunteers are retained without sanitizing the dramatic action.
- Alli's recurring contrast between `people` and `cattle`, living human beings and `living temples`, and cradle-hand versus resistance-hand imagery is preserved.
- `ஆட்சிப் பீடம்` remains `seat of power`, consistent with earlier scenes.
- The source-specific insult `குரப்புலிகள்` is retained conservatively as `kurappuligal` rather than assigned an unsupported modern equivalent.
- Puthumaippithan's armed intervention is kept exactly limited to the source event: he shoots the soldier who is about to stab Ponnazhagan from behind, saves him, and disappears.

# Scene 8

[The king's palace. At the entrance a bodyguard is pacing about. Short in stature and sharp-eyed, he notices someone approaching and stands alert. Minister Gunaseelar arrives. The bodyguard comes forward.]

Gunaseelar: Tell the king I wish to see him!

Bodyguard: Officially... or...?

Gun.: Officially, of course...

Bodyguard: I will run and tell him at once...

[He goes inside and returns.]

Bodyguard: He says he will come immediately. Please be seated here.

[The Minister sits.]

Bodyguard: What new matter might it be...?

Gun.: You are the king's bodyguard, aren't you?

Bodyguard: Yes...

Gun.: Then stick to that job! Do not poke your head into all this and get yourself into trouble! What does it matter to you what business brings me here?

Bodyguard: Sacrilege — forgive me — kushimikka vendum! No... I meant, about building that temple...

Gun.: Yes — that is what it is about. Are you going to build it yourself...? [Angrily.]

Bodyguard: Me? Oh no! There are masons to build all that; there are Gurunathars to move in!

Gun.: Hey, Ulagappa! More and more you are standing upside down... behave respectfully.

Bodyguard: Oh no, my lord, I never stand upside down — except when I do a headstand!

Gun.: Behave respectfully!

Bodyguard: Kneel down like Hanuman?

[He assumes such a pose.]

Gun.: The arrogance of thinking you serve the king — just wait! I will tell her and have you properly...

Bodyguard: No... no... my lord! Forgive me and take me under your protection... I only said it in a little petty mischief. The king likes these jokes I make very much. I thought perhaps you too liked something and...

Gun.: What do I like, you devil? A ghost?

Bodyguard: No, sir... Whatever I say, you take it the wrong way. Forgive me, my lord! Do not blow up my job, master! Sacrilege! Sacrilege... I am your humble little servant — I will not err again!

[He strikes his cheeks in apology.]

Gun.: I forgive you. Get lost! Do not behave like this again! Go and see whether the king is coming!

Bodyguard: At your command...

[Mocking the Minister to himself, he goes inside. The Minister waits. The bodyguard returns.]

Bodyguard: My lord! The king is coming!

[The Minister rises. The king enters. King Manimaran has a handsome budding moustache and a majestic figure. His appearance closely resembles Puthumaippithan. The Minister bows to the king, who walks in proudly. The king returns the greeting and sits.]

King: Gunaseelar! What is the special matter? Is everyone well? Is Kalarani well?

Gun.: She is well, Your Majesty! She has been longing and longing to see you.

King: Is that so? Mm! How are the affairs of the realm proceeding? Are there any new resolutions in the Assembly of Nobles...?

Gun.: Yes! I have come to obtain your signature on one of them!

King: What resolution?

Gun.: To build a temple...

King: Then it must be built! A most commendable undertaking... What the people urgently need is a temple! Who is the originator of this divine idea?

Gun.: Gurunathar!

King: I thought as much. Who else could understand the necessities required by the Lord? — Splendid! To build a temple will require a great deal of money!

Gun.: There is some in the treasury; if it is not enough, we also intend to impose a temple tax!

King: Excellent!... Minister!... Wasn't it said that because a certain river has no dam, floods occur frequently and cause damage to the people and villages? Has that been attended to?

Gun.: No.

King: Yes, that is not urgent now. If we build the temple and satisfy the Lord, perhaps he himself will stop the flood and even dry up the river! All right... Minister! Where will this temple be built?

Gun.: In Kodikkal Nagar.

King: And the people of that town!

Gun.: The resolution is to clear the town and build the temple there.

King: Oh! So the resolution is to drive out human beings and build a palace for Maheswaran!

Gun.: Your Majesty!.. [Somewhat astonished.]

King: And I am expected to sign the resolution?

Gun.: Yes! That is why I came... [He extends the book.]

King: [Taking it] Even if I do not sign it, the Minister has special authority to bring the resolution into force, does he not?

Gun.: He does, but the Assembly of Nobles, without your signature...

King: Minister! I do not want my signature on this particular resolution. If possible, try to have this resolution withdrawn!

Gun.: The Assembly has already passed it. Would withdrawing it not be humiliating, Your Majesty?

King: Then use the Minister's special authority... I will not sign only the resolution to build the temple... I will sign the others.

[He signs the other resolutions.]

Gun.: Please, this resolution too...

King: No! I will not stain my signature... You ask for an explanation! Use your special authority and build the temple!

[The king goes inside. The bodyguard goes with him. The Minister stands deep in thought.]

Gun.: [To himself] “He did not sign! Use the special authority!” If I become angry with the king now, the Kalarani matter will not end successfully. But for that reason I cannot abandon the temple resolution either! Very well. I will spread the lie that the king has signed it. We do have *People's Servant*, after all! There is no other way..! No other way..!

[The bodyguard enters.]

Bodyguard: Why is there no way? My lord, here is the way — you want the way out, don't you, my lord?

Gun.: Pah... fool! He has come to show me the way — a thoroughgoing fool!

[He leaves quickly.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/08.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The verified source's unusual `குஷிமிக்க வேண்டும்!` is retained visibly as `kushimikka vendum!` rather than silently repaired to a familiar Tamil expression; its comic stumble remains part of the exchange.
- The king's remarks about the dam, floods, temple and divine intervention are translated in their dramatic sequence without adding an editorial label such as `sarcastically` that the source does not print.
- `விசேஷ அதிகாரம்` is consistently rendered `special authority`, preserving the later plot point that Gunaseelar can implement the resolution without the king's signature.
- The source-visible physical resemblance between King Manimaran and Puthumaippithan is retained without importing an explanation beyond this scene.
- The final `way / way out` joke is preserved as closely as English permits.

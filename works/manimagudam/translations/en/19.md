# Scene 19

[Gurunathar's monastery. The Minister, Gurunathar and Ariharanathar are present.]

Ariharanathar: [Holding a copy of *Manimagudam*.] We must not allow this situation to grow! Who is this Puthumaippithan? We must find out where he is. How did he learn that the king did not sign? This must be attended to urgently and necessarily, Minister! Otherwise the whole country might as well commit suicide!

Gurunathar: Ariharanathar! Proprietor of a newspaper — should a newspaper editor panic like this? You, who have learned to twist so many lies and hide so many truths, should you be afraid of this?

Minister! I tell you: use your special authority and clear Kodikkal Nagar immediately! The resolution to build the temple must also be put into effect.

Minister: That is the right idea! If the resolution is not put into effect, the people will believe the report in *Manimagudam* is true. I am issuing the order now. Kodikkal Nagar must be cleared — preparations for the temple must begin.

Ariharanathar: Bravo! Bravo! *People's Servant* has obtained an important front-page story.

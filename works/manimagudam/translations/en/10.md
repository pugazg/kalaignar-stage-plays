# Scene 10

[People's Forum. Ponnazhagan, the poet, Alli and others are present.]

Ponnazhagan: The Minister gets everything he wants done. The king has signed the resolution — next we will hear that he is going to marry the Minister's daughter tomorrow! Once the Minister and the king become connected by marriage, whatever the Minister lays down will become law! Manimagudapuram will become Gunaseelan's exclusive property!.... We failed to stop the temple resolution; at least that marriage must be stopped immediately!

Poet: There are only two ways to stop the marriage! One — abduct the king — or abduct the Minister's daughter Kalarani.

Ponnazhagan: Hiding Kalarani is easier than hiding the king! — Yes! That is what we must do. Comrades! Who among you will come forward to bring Kalarani here? Will you volunteer yourselves — or shall we, as usual, write names on slips and draw lots?

Poet: That is the better method! Let us draw lots — write the slips!

[At that moment Puthumaippithan enters. A complete silence settles over the Forum — no one welcomes him. Black Rose comes with Puthumaippithan. The two look at each other in bewilderment, and finally...]

Puthumaippithan: What is this, such complete silence? I suppose you have seen the report that the king has signed! Somehow I do not believe that report. Did you read it? Look here — [turning the newspaper pages] — it says, “The king has signed the resolution of the Assembly of Nobles”; but it does not say whether he signed the resolution to build the temple or not. This is a fraud committed by the Minister, Gurunathar and Editor Ariharanathar! Do not believe it. If we too had a newspaper at this moment, we could immediately publish a denial and establish the truth. What do you say, Alli? Shall we start a daily newspaper called *Manimagudam* on behalf of the People's Forum?

Ponnazhagan: Starting a newspaper can wait, friend! First, a critical situation has arisen in which you must secure your own position as a member of the People's Forum!

Puthumaippithan: What?

Ponnazhagan: Members of the Forum have begun to suspect you!

Puthumaippithan: Suspect me? Ah, that will split our association apart!

Ponnazhagan: A conspiratorial nature is more dangerous than suspicion! It will pierce the strength of the association with holes like a sieve!

Puthumaippithan: A mistaken thought — it is wrong, friend, wrong! I am not a man who deserves suspicion! I am not a conspirator! For the advancement of the people of Manimagudapuram, I am ready even to give up my life. I swear upon my mother! I swear upon my father! I swear upon my pure language! — Believe me — I am innocent! A man devoted to principle! A man with a heart capable of sacrificing anything for it!

Ponnazhagan: The secret meeting of the Forum has already resolved that you are a man under suspicion! But let one thing be my personal condition. I will set you a severe test. If you pass it, you can free yourself from suspicion.

Puthumaippithan: A severe test!

Ponnazhagan: Yes... a test necessary for you to establish your integrity! You must secretly bring Minister Gunaseelar's daughter Kalarani here without anyone knowing.

Puthumaippithan: Kalarani? Why?

Ponnazhagan: To prove that you are not a government spy and, at the same time, to prevent the marriage that is to take place between Kalarani and the king, this command is given to you on behalf of the People's Forum. And it is also a sign that the affection I have for you has not yet vanished completely!

Poet: Splendid! The suspicion will be cleared and the marriage will be stopped! Two mangoes with one stone!

Puthumaippithan: No, Poet! Two spears in one heart! Suspicion and a trial! I must bear both — an impossible task!

Ponnazhagan: If you carry out the command, the door of the People's Forum will always be open to you — otherwise...

Puthumaippithan: It will be closed — and my hopes will be shut away with it!

Ponnazhagan: What can I do? I too must obey the Forum's order, must I not, comrade? Return victorious! I will take my leave!

[Ponnazhagan leaves, and the others follow him. Puthumaippithan, Alli and Black Rose remain alone.]

Puthumaippithan: Alli... I am going!

[Alli stands troubled.]

Alli: Win the test and return soon.

Puthumaippithan: Why do you want me to succeed?

Alli: Only because the People's Forum must not lose a fine genius.

Puthumaippithan: A genius, me?

Alli: Not merely a genius — a scholar — a poet — an orator — a master of words — a sculptor of thought — you are all of them!

Puthumaippithan: A suspected conspirator — a man unworthy of trust.

Alli: That is how they think of you here. But I...

Puthumaippithan: You do not think so, Alli?

Alli: No. There is not even the slightest shadow of deceit on your face.

Puthumaippithan: Alli... your words are enough for me. It is enough that you know the truth... I am not a conspirator, am I? Say it, Alli! Say it! Say it once more with your own lips!

Alli: No! You are not a conspirator! You are the embodiment of noble truth! [She chokes with emotion.]

Puthumaippithan: Ah... Alli! I feel a joy as though Manimagudapuram itself has won liberation. Give me leave; I will go!

Alli: I will give you leave if you promise to return.

Puthumaippithan: [Astonished] In the way you speak...

Alli: What are you imagining?

Puthumaippithan: Nothing...

Alli: Tell me!

Puthumaippithan: My heart trembles... no, let it be...

Alli: My heart trembles too.

Black Rose: My whole body is trembling — that someone may come in! I will go stand guard outside. [He leaves.]

Puthumaippithan: Alli, I am going!

Alli: In the attempt to bring the Minister's daughter...

Puthumaippithan: Are you too commanding me in the same way?

Alli: Not a command; an affectionate request born from the wish to see you return.

Puthumaippithan: An affectionate request... [Repeating the words with delight] Alli!

[He turns back and approaches her. She covers her face and moves away sorrowfully to another side. Puthumaippithan leaves, distressed.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/10.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The scene's distinction between the newspaper's vague claim that the king signed `the resolution of the Assembly of Nobles` and the specific temple resolution is preserved exactly as Puthumaippithan argues it.
- The proposed newspaper title `மணிமகுடம்` is retained as *Manimagudam* rather than converted into an English title.
- `ஒரே கல்லில் இரண்டு மாங்காய்` is retained as `two mangoes with one stone` rather than replaced with an English bird idiom; Puthumaippithan's answering image `ஒரு இருதயத்தில் இரண்டு வேல்கள்` remains `two spears in one heart`.
- Alli's praise sequence `மேதை - அறிஞர் - கவிஞர் - நாவலர் - சொல்லின் செல்வர் - சிந்தனைச் சிற்பி` is kept as a rising rhetorical list rather than compressed.
- The closing emotional exchange and Black Rose's interruption retain the source's shift from political suspicion to personal affection without adding an explicit romantic interpretation not printed in the scene.

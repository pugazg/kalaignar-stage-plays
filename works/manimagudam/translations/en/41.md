# Scene 41

[Prison. Gurunathar's man has been imprisoned. Gurunathar comes there.]

Henchman: Swami! Save me, Swami!

Gurunathar: You fool! Why did you let yourself get caught?

Henchman: What could I do? That Ulagappan chased me and chased me, and finally arrested me. Somehow save me, Swami!

Gurunathar: I will save you. Do not be afraid! But one thing: during the criminal inquiry, you must behave exactly as I tell you.

Henchman: All right!

Gurunathar: Admit that you were the one who came to stab the king!

Henchman: Ayyo!

Gurunathar: Listen! Along with that, say that Puthumaippithan sent you with orders to kill the king!

Henchman: They'll ask, “Impossible — impossible! Whoever told you, why did you leap at the king to kill him?” and hang me!

Gurunathar: Do as I say — otherwise you are finished!

Henchman: Gurunathar! You are scheming to save yourself! I will not agree!

Gurunathar: You disrespectful wretch! Do not die for nothing! Say what I tell you! Put the blame on Puthumaippithan — admit that you are his man!

Henchman: Even if I say that, is it enough? Will my head be spared afterward? I am going to say that you were the one who ordered me to kill the king! Is that all right?

[The king, the Minister, nobles and Ulagappan stand aside and listen.]

Gurunathar: You sinner — what have you done? How much money did I give you! Hey, listen to this! Admit the crime yourself! Even if some terrible punishment is imposed on you, I am here to take care of your family!

Henchman: What will happen to me, Gurunathar?

Gurunathar: Say what I tell you! I will save you too!

Henchman: If I say what you tell me — not you, even if your grandfather himself came, he could not save me!

[Gurunathar thinks. He opens a medallion-like container on his rudraksha garland.]

Gurunathar: Very well — here. A weapon used in times of danger. God's sacred-vibhuti prasadam. Eat this — no punishment will be able to do anything to you!

Henchman: Is that true? Then give me that miraculous vibhuti, Swami!

[He gives it.]

Gurunathar: Pour it straight into your mouth!

[The henchman smells it and becomes suspicious.]

Henchman: Is this vibhuti? No — poison! Poison!! You sinner! Are you trying to kill me by giving me poison? Here, you eat it yourself! [Gurunathar takes it.] Wait, wait! Tomorrow I will expose all your conspiracies directly before the emperor!

Gurunathar: So you are going to betray me?

Henchman: Yes! Yes!

Gurunathar: What?

[He begins strangling him. At that moment the king and the others enter.]

King: Gurunathar, stop! Stop!

Gurunathar: Ah — who?

Nobles: Who? Do you not know? We were listening to everything! We now understand who plotted to kill the king! [To the guards.] Come — arrest this false `vedari` immediately!

Gurunathar: Arrest me? That will never happen as long as my father, God, exists! Never!

[He pours the poison in his hand into his mouth.]

Gurunathar: Ha... ha... ha! No one can arrest me! When I say “me”, whom do I mean? This body? Ha... ha... ha... No! This soul! I say you cannot arrest this! Before the spirit leaves this empty shell, Gurunathar will finish the work he has to do! Noble, you called me a false `vedari`! I am a false `vedari`! But I alone am not a false `vedari` — here, here... this... [his speech slurs] this king standing here — he... he... [he points and writhes, unable to stand] I dreamed that religion would rule Manimagudam — that religion must rule it... that dream has not come true! If not today, tomorrow... someone like me is bound to arise! Do not imagine that you can destroy religious gurus like me! The more you destroy us, the more we will keep sprouting! We will keep sprouting!! One word before I go! False `vedari`... false `vedari`... Noble — I am not the only false `vedari`! Here is this false `vedari` whom the Minister makes dance... Why the shock? Ha... ha... why the laughter? Vedari. Ha... ha... why the smile? Ha... ha... ha... why the scorn? Shall I explain? He... he is not the king! Do you know who he is...? One day he was *Manimagudam*... *Manimagudam*....

[He falls and dies. The nobles look at the Minister; the Minister looks at the king.]

King: Even at the time of death, no good words come from the mouths of these `chandalargal`! Hmm — Minister, nobles... come, let us return to the Assembly. Let him come too... to the Assembly... [pointing to the prisoner] Hmm, Ulagappa... this corpse...

Ulagappan: I will throw it into the sea!

King: Hey! [sharply] It must be put on display tomorrow.

Ulagappan: Why? Even crows and vultures will not come near enough to look at it!

King: Hmm... do as I said!

Ulagappan: All right!

[The king and the others leave.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/41.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- `போலி வேதாரி` is retained conservatively as `false vedari`; the source form is not silently reassigned to a narrower religious category.
- Gurunathar's poisoned dying speech intentionally breaks down and repeats; that disintegration is preserved rather than smoothed into polished prose.
- `சண்டாளர்கள்` is retained as `chandalargal`, a source slur spoken by the king, rather than silently mapped to a modern social category.
- `காட்டுக்கு வைக்க வேண்டும்` is rendered contextually as putting the corpse on public display; Ulagappan's crow/vulture joke is retained.

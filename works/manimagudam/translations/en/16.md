# Scene 16

[The People's Forum. A lamp is burning. Alli is singing a song of people's liberation written by the poet; the poet listens appreciatively.]

Let us make a new world — let us uproot
this evil world that wages war.
Let us spread the principle of common ownership in all eight directions;
let us guard that sacred principle as our very life.
Let us soak every heart in the river of love;
let us cast away the cruelty that says, "This is mine."
In the fire called consciousness let us burn away weariness;
let us laugh at the person who says, "This thing is mine alone."
If nature's wealth refuses to yield benefit, let us all go hungry;
if there is enough to give, let everyone eat. [New...]
— Bharathidasan

[When the song ends.]

Poet: Alli! However many ideas a poet may pour into a poem, only when it comes forth through the voices of those filled with feeling can it become a feast of awakening and a medicine that relieves the suffering of the poor!

[A knock is heard at the Forum door.]

Poet: Who could it be? Perhaps Ponnazhagan...

Alli: No, he said he would come only in the morning — perhaps Puthumaippithan...

Poet: Why would he come here? Would he be mad enough to come here again?

[The door is knocked again.]

Poet: Alli, don't open it... what if they are government men?

Alli: Whoever they are, let us see that too.

[Alli opens the door. Puthumaippithan and Black Rose enter carrying Kalarani. Alli and the poet are astonished. Puthumaippithan lays Kalarani on a plank there. She is still unconscious.]

Poet: Who is this?

Puthumaippithan: Poet! Still in doubt? This is the Minister's daughter Kalarani — the object of the test set up so that I might prove I am not a conspirator! Alli! At least now, will the stain placed upon me be removed?

Alli: Forgive me... you yourself know whether I ever suspected you.

Poet: Puthumaippithan! I am the one who must be forgiven.

Puthumaippithan: I will not forgive you, Poet — I am going to punish you properly. From now on you must write at least two poems where you once wrote one!

Poet: Spirited young man! I called a pure deed treachery; that statement will keep piercing my heart — how am I to write poetry now?

Puthumaippithan: I will not say you were wrong to act cautiously for the good of the People's Forum. But the test given to me was very severe! I have completed even that. I have removed the stain upon me. I take leave.

Alli: Where are you going?

Puthumaippithan: Alli! Where would I go? Wherever I can stand and work for the life of Manimagudapuram, I will go there. I will labour for the people. Religion, social restraints, the cruelty of monarchy, the Minister's domination — I will attack them all!

Alli: Standing alone?

Puthumaippithan: No — standing as a branch of this People's Forum! Alli, do you say it cannot be done? The work I have undertaken is work I can carry through even by myself. I told you earlier that a newspaper called *Manimagudam* should be started; I am going to undertake that effort. That one paper will be as though we had volunteers throughout the country! Until the guru-seat, the seat of power and their propaganda-seat — all three seats — are smashed to dust, until Manimagudapuram passes into the hands of the people, Puthumaippithan will not disappear.

Poet: Isn't the People's Forum doing that same work? Why should you alone separate yourself and fight?

Puthumaippithan: The work is the same — but the method is different! Look at this Minister's daughter. I say she can rise and walk only after her unconsciousness clears. But the People's Forum says it is enough to lift her upright without clearing the stupor and she will begin walking!

Poet: I don't understand.

Puthumaippithan: Think of Manimagudapuram in place of the Minister's daughter and you will understand! Only after the stupors of religious frenzy, caste frenzy, god-frenzy and the like have cleared can people's rule take firm root. That is what I say in *Manimagudam*.

Poet: We agree too.

Puthumaippithan: But you do not say it — you do not demonstrate it in action. I am going to say it. I am going to write it. I am going to show it in action. I am going to prepare the people for people's rule. It is enough if the People's Forum supports my work. I take leave.

Alli: I am telling you — you must not go. Change your decision. I will take responsibility for making the People's Forum accept your principles soon!

Puthumaippithan: If it does, no one could be happier than I... Alli! Bring changes within the People's Forum! Spread the light of knowledge! Conduct righteous struggles! The *Manimagudam* I run will give wholehearted support to all of it! My *Manimagudam* is for the people — to transform the people! But do not ask only that I remain here, Alli. Once I have become a man under suspicion, it is no longer right for me to stay here...

Alli: However many times I ask, is this always your answer?

Puthumaippithan: Forgive me... Tell friend Ponnazhagan my decisions. If possible, we will meet soon. No one should wake Kalarani until her unconsciousness clears and she rises herself. One more thing: it is true that the Minister is trying to arrange a marriage between Kalarani and the king. To stop that marriage, keep her secretly locked here. Be careful, Poet. Greetings! Alli... bolt the door!

[Alli is dejected. She follows behind Puthumaippithan to bolt the door.]

Puthumaippithan: Why, Alli, so much sorrow?

Alli: Ask the poets who write about when an alli flower wilts.

Puthumaippithan: Alli... [Turns back.]

[She covers her face sadly and turns back inside. He and Black Rose leave.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/16.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The Bharathidasan song is translated only from the source-visible lines in this scene; no additional poem text is imported.
- The scene's central double meaning of `மயக்கம்` — Kalarani's physical unconsciousness and society's ideological stupor — is preserved explicitly.
- `குருபீடம் - அதிகார பீடம் - ... பிரச்சார பீடம்` is kept as a three-seat rhetorical structure: guru-seat, seat of power, propaganda-seat.
- `மதவெறி`, `ஜாதிவெறி`, and `கடவுள் வெறி` remain distinct forms of frenzy rather than being merged into a generic `superstition`.
- The departure of Puthumaippithan from the Forum and the founding intention for *Manimagudam* are preserved as a strategic difference in method, not as a break in ultimate political aims.

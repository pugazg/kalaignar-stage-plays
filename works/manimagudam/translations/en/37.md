# Scene 37

[Garden. Alli and Puthumaippithan in a love duet.]

Alli: It is getting late, dear — shall I leave?

Puthumaippithan: How did you know that time had passed?

Alli: See? We took a solemn pledge that `we must not drown completely in the sea of love and forget our duty`... and now we ourselves have thrown all that to the wind!

Puthumaippithan: No, my joy! I too want love and duty to remain intertwined with one another. I have not forgotten!

Alli: When will the day come when the two of us can openly live as husband and wife — as a couple carrying out our duty?

Puthumaippithan: That blessing will come soon!

Alli: Kannaala!

Puthumaippithan: Alli — queen of my heart!

Alli: My king... king of my heart! Shall I leave?

Puthumaippithan: Ask instead, `Shall I take your life away with me?`

Alli: Shall I leave my life here and go?

[Calling each other `dear` and `jewel`, the two embrace and part. As Alli leaves, Ponnazhagan watches from hiding.]

Ponnazhagan: [Muttering.] It is Alli — it really is Alli.

[Alli leaves.]

Ponnazhagan: Who is he? Who is that new man? She called him `king` — who is that king of love? I will not turn back until I find out...

[He follows Puthumaippithan.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/37.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The scene keeps love and political duty explicitly intertwined, matching the preceding garden scene.
- `ராஜா / காதல் ராஜா` remains `king / king of love`, because Ponnazhagan's literal hearing of that address drives the misunderstanding that follows.
- `கண்ணே, மணியே` is represented by simple endearments rather than expanded into unprinted dialogue.

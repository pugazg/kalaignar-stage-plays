# Scene 15

[The king's palace. The king is walking to and fro, smiling. Ulagappan runs in.]

Ulagappan: Your Majesty!

King: Well, is it done? Where is Kalarani?

Ulagappan: I finished it successfully... I gently bewitched her...

King: Hey! Did you give her the drug that makes one unconscious? [Angrily.]

Ulagappan: No, Your Majesty! That was another kind of bewitching! [Grinning.] Hm... I cleverly led her away, and our men showed Kalarani the drug and brought her to the marble pavilion. Kalarani still has not regained consciousness!

King: Good. Get ready, Ulagappa! Change your clothes. I will be back presently too...

[The king goes inside. Ulagappan removes his bodyguard's clothes, puts on different clothes and stands transformed as Black Rose. The king, who went inside, comes out transformed as Puthumaippithan!]

Puthumaippithan: Ulagappa...

Black Rose: Your Majesty!

Puthumaippithan: Do you know what I am going to do now?

Black Rose: How would I know? Everything you do is something new, isn't it?

Puthumaippithan: That is why I gave myself the name Puthumaippithan.

Black Rose: Did you stop with that? [Pointing to himself.] Haven't you transformed this Ulagam too into Black Rose?

Puthumaippithan: [Laughing] Black Rose! Come, come! It is getting late. The work must be done before Kalarani's unconsciousness clears.

[The two leave stealthily.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/15.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- The scene's identity reveal is preserved explicitly: the king is Puthumaippithan and the bodyguard Ulagappan is Black Rose.
- Ulagappan's joke turns on two senses of `மயக்கி`: charming/bewitching and rendering unconscious; both senses are retained.
- His joke that the king transformed `this Ulagam` into Black Rose preserves the source play on his name `Ulagappan` / `Ulagam` rather than normalizing it away.

# Scene 17

[A street. A vendor walks along crying out the newspaper *Manimagudam*. People eagerly buy it. A group gathers around one copy and reads it.]

One man reads:

"MANIMAGUDAM"

"Editor — Puthumaippithan"

"Daily newspaper — price one panam"

"Is it true that the ruler signed the order to destroy Kodikkal and build a temple?

Can the Minister, Gurunathar or the Assembly of Nobles deny the report of *Manimagudam*'s agent: that the king did not consent to the atrocious scheme of destroying a thousand huts and building a temple there, and that publishing the claim that he had signed was an open fraud by the government's propaganda-seat?"

[Alli stands nearby listening happily as this item is read. The group is delighted. Alli runs toward them.]

Alli: Sir... sir... will you give me that newspaper?

They: No... no... we have to read it.

[They read quickly.]

"Gurunathar's Ferocious Dance — a short story"

[Without looking at Alli, the reader answers her and continues reading. Then he looks up, sees her and exclaims in surprise.]

Reader: Good heavens — it's you! Here, take it!

[He hands her the paper, still reading as he gives it.]

"The Minister's Daughter — a serial story — full of mysteries!"

Alli: All right, all right — you read it. I will buy another copy.

[She hurries away excitedly.]

## Translation notes

- Immediate authority: verified Tamil `../../scenes/17.md`; no secondary English witness was consulted.
- Newspaper headings, price, editor credit and nested article/serial titles are preserved as text being read within the scene.
- `மணிமகுடத்தின் ஒற்றன்` is rendered conservatively as `Manimagudam's agent`; no outside identity is assigned to that source figure.
- The established `பிரச்சார பீடம்` → `propaganda-seat` control is retained from Scene 16 to preserve the recurring political metaphor.

# Scene 31

[In the palace, Alli lies awake, unable to sleep. Puthumaippithan comes in and turns the lamp up a little. Alli starts up in alarm.]

Alli: Who is it?

[Puthumaippithan runs to her and covers her mouth.]

Puthumaippithan: Shh... don't make a sound.

[Alli gently removes the hand covering her mouth, lingering over it with pleasure.]

Puthumaippithan: Alli — this is the moment. Come, let us escape from the palace.

Alli: How did you get in?

Puthumaippithan: I will tell you all that later... come!

Alli: There are so many guards!

Puthumaippithan: Don't worry! The king is asleep — the palace itself is asleep. The only two who are awake are you and I! Come... come!!

[He takes her hand and leads her away.]

Alli: Running away like this...

Puthumaippithan: [Hesitating.] Is indeed a crime! But don't you have to escape tomorrow from being charged with murder? Come quickly. Hm...

[The two leave.]

- The scene preserves the contrast between the sleeping king / palace and the two wakeful fugitives without adding an inferred security detail.

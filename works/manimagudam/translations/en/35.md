# Scene 35

[The Minister's house. Ariharanathar, the Minister and Gurunathar are together.]

Minister: It was that same group that abducted my daughter — and that same group that tried to kill the king — we must not let them go free!

Ariharanathar: After doing all this, their newspaper says it was Gurunathar who plotted the conspiracy! We must not spare that Puthumaippithan either, Minister!

Gurunathar: Ruin has come upon the country and upon us! Father! Lord!

Minister: There is no fear! There is no need for fear at all! Before the authority of the country is transferred into my hands, this revolutionary group must be crushed! Ponnazhagan's group must be arrested on sight! That Puthumaippithan must be shot down! I will immediately convene the Assembly of Nobles and issue an emergency order.

Gurunathar: Good! Do what is good! And do it today itself, Minister!

Ariharanathar: I too will write it in our newspaper. I will put in banner letters that Puthumaippithan is a lover of violence! I will write for the whole town to know the truth that it was he who tried to kill the king!

Minister: Let us attend to what must be done — first convene the Assembly of Nobles!

Gurunathar: Joy! Joy!

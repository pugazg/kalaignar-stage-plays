## Translation / terminology safeguards retained

The completed translation layer preserves source-specific political, rhetorical, comic, lexical, and structural features. Standing controls include:

- `மக்கள் மன்றம்` → `People's Forum`;
- `சீமான்கள் சபை` → `Assembly of Nobles`;
- newspaper `மக்கள் தொண்டன்` → *People's Servant*;
- common-noun `மக்கள் தொண்டன்` → `servant of the people` where context requires;
- `ஆட்சிப் பீடம்` → `seat of power` where abstract authority is meant;
- `கொடிக்கால் நகரம்` → `Kodikkal Nagar`;
- `விசேஷ அதிகாரம்` → `special authority`;
- newspaper title `மணிமகுடம்` → *Manimagudam*;
- `மார்கழி` → `Margazhi`;
- `கல் நாட்டு விழா` → `foundation-stone ceremony`;
- `கொடி நாட்டு விழா` → `flag-raising ceremony`;
- `மோகராக்கள்` → `mohars`;
- `ஐந்தாம்படை` → `fifth column`.

Source-sensitive forms and wordplay were retained or transliterated where direct English substitution would erase the mechanism or where a modernized interpretation was not securely supported. The standing safeguard that `அந்தணர்` is not automatically rendered as `Brahmin` without contextual justification remains in force.

## Final dramatic / thematic fidelity gate

The completed Tamil→English review records PASS for:

- speaker and stage-direction coverage;
- dramatic-structure coverage;
- political and rhetorical fidelity;
- humour, wordplay, and repetition coverage;
- source-sensitive-form handling.

The translation preserves distinctions important to the work's dramatic argument:

- propaganda accusations remain attributed to the characters who make them;
- the People's Forum's own coercive and violent actions remain visible;
- the king/Puthumaippithan identity is not disclosed before the Tamil source discloses it;
- the intertwined struggles around monarchy, religious authority, ministerial power, propaganda, caste hierarchy, repression, rationalism, common ownership, and popular government remain source-bounded;
- dissolution of the Assembly of Nobles, renunciation of the crown, people's rule, and the proposed general election remain as framed by the play;
- the final rejection of violence and the stated intellectual → political/economic revolution sequence are preserved without importing an outside ideological gloss.
